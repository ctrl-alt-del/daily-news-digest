VeriLocc：基于 LLM 的跨架构寄存器分配
register allocation LLM compiler GPU
VeriLocc is a framework that fine-tunes an LLM to translate compiler intermediate representations into target-specific register assignments, aided by static analysis and a verifier-guided regeneration loop. On GEMM and multi-head attention it reaches 85-99% single-shot accuracy and near-100% pass@100, and in one case outperformed rocBLAS by over 10% in runtime.
