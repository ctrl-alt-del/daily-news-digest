Uncertainty-Aware Diffusion Guided Refinement of 3D Scenes
不确定性感知的扩散引导 3D 场景细化
3D reconstruction diffusion models Gaussian splatting uncertainty estimation novel view synthesis
The paper addresses single-image 3D scene reconstruction, where novel views are often blurry and incoherent, by using a pre-trained latent video diffusion model to iteratively refine a coarse scene represented by Gaussian parameters. It adds Fourier-style transfer to match the input image's style and a semantic uncertainty module that guides refinement using per-pixel entropy, reporting better novel-view synthesis on RealEstate-10K and KITTI-v2.
