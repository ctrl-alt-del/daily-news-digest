Naturally Computed Scale Invariance in the Residual Stream of ResNet18
ResNet18 残差流中自然计算的尺度不变性
mechanistic interpretability ResNet computer vision scale invariance
This study examines how ResNet18 builds invariance to scale, focusing on its residual stream, a component that the previously studied InceptionV1 lacks. The author finds scale-invariant channels in intermediate blocks produced by summing scale-equivariant residual representations, and uses ablation experiments to probe a causal link to recognition behavior.

MetaHarm：由领域专家、GPT-4-Turbo 与众包工人标注的有害 YouTube 视频数据集
content moderation dataset multimodal YouTube

通过对称性感知训练目标捕捉语言模型中的对称与反对称关系
relational reasoning benchmark contrastive learning NLP

论恶意软件检测中 GNN 解释的一致性
malware detection graph neural networks explainability
The paper proposes a malware-detection framework that dynamically builds control flow graphs, embeds node features with a mix of rule-based encoding and autoencoder embeddings, and classifies them with a GNN. arxiv:2504.17813 arXiv ✓
CLOC: Contrastive Learning for Ordinal Classification with Multi-Margin N-pair Loss
CLOC：基于多间隔 N-pair 损失的序数分类对比学习
ordinal classification contrastive learning medical imaging
CLOC is a margin-based contrastive learning method for ordinal classification that learns ordered representations by optimizing multiple margins with a new multi-margin N-pair loss, allowing flexible boundaries between adjacent classes. Experiments on five real-world image datasets and a synthetic dataset simulating clinical decision bias show it outperforming existing ordinal methods.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2504.16315 arXiv ✓
SignX: Continuous Sign Recognition in Compact Pose-Rich Latent Space
SignX：在紧凑的姿态丰富潜空间中进行连续手语识别
sign language recognition pose estimation computer vision inference efficiency
SignX is a framework for continuous sign language recognition that encodes heterogeneous pose formats into a compact latent space, extracts that representation from raw video with a ViT-based video-to-pose module, and models sequences entirely in the latent space. The authors report state-of-the-art accuracy on recognition and translation with roughly 50-fold speedup over pixel-space baselines.
