A Post-Processing-Based Fair Federated Learning Framework
基于后处理的公平联邦学习框架
federated learning fairness post-processing
The paper defines and evaluates a two-stage framework for group fairness in federated learning: standard training such as FedAvg followed by fully decentralized local debiasing. Tests on four tabular, signal and image datasets show fairness gains with little or no accuracy loss, especially under heterogeneous data.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2501.15348 arXiv ✓
ReInc: Scaling Training of Dynamic Graph Neural Networks
ReInc：扩展动态图神经网络的训练
graph neural networks dynamic graphs distributed training caching
ReInc is a system for efficient, scalable training of dynamic graph neural networks that reuses intermediate results and incrementally computes aggregations across graph snapshots. It adds a two-level cache and a distributed training strategy, reporting up to an order-of-magnitude speedup over state-of-the-art frameworks.
→ arxiv.org

VR privacy network traffic machine learning
Researchers collected network traffic from 25 VR applications on a Meta Quest Pro and trained machine learning models that identified the app with 92.4% accuracy and user activity with 91% accuracy. Less than 10 minutes of traffic per app was enough to exceed 90% and 88% accuracy respectively.

mixture-of-experts model pruning LLM efficiency
