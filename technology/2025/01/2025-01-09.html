Kite: How to Delegate Voting Power Privately
Kite：如何私下委托投票权
DAO governance zero-knowledge proofs voting privacy Ethereum
Kite is a protocol for private delegation of voting power in DAOs that lets members delegate, revoke and re-delegate without revealing their delegate, and hides delegators even from the delegate. It is implemented as an extension to Ethereum's Governor Bravo contract, with zero-knowledge proofs making delegation take 7 to 167 seconds on a consumer laptop depending on the privacy level.
