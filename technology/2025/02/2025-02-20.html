Quantize What Counts: More for Keys, Less for Values
量化该量化的部分：键多给精度，值少给精度
KV cache quantization LLM inference transformer memory efficiency
The paper proposes two theorems for mixed-precision KV-cache quantization, arguing that key projections carry higher information density than value matrices and that prioritizing key precision reduces quantization error. Experiments show key-favored allocations such as 4-bit keys and 2-bit values retain up to 98.3% accuracy.
→ arxiv.org

The authors propose a post-training static quantization method for OpenSora, a video diffusion transformer, aimed at deployment on resource-constrained AI processors. They report video quality comparable to FP16 and dynamically quantized ViDiT-Q methods on CLIP and VQA metrics.

UPCORE：面向均衡遗忘的效用保持核心集选择
machine unlearning coreset selection LLM model utility
UPCORE is a method-agnostic data-selection framework that aims to reduce collateral damage when unlearning information from LLMs by pruning outliers from the forget set.
