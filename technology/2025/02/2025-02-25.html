无需奖励模型与人类偏好数据的生成式大模型判别式微调
fine-tuning discriminative learning LLM alignment preference optimization
The paper introduces Discriminative Fine-Tuning (DFT), a variant of supervised fine-tuning that raises the likelihood of positive answers while suppressing negative ones. It avoids preference data and reward models, and experiments show it beats SFT and is comparable to or better than SFT followed by preference optimization.

MPO：混合多样偏好对齐的高效后处理框架
RLHF preference alignment LLM reinforcement learning
The paper proposes Mixing Preference Optimization (MPO), a post-processing framework that combines existing single-objective policies log-linearly instead of training multi-objective RLHF from scratch. arxiv:2503.00043 arXiv ✓
VOILA: Evaluation of MLLMs For Perceptual Understanding and Analogical Reasoning
VOILA：评估多模态大模型感知理解与类比推理的基准
multimodal LLM benchmark analogical reasoning visual reasoning
The paper introduces VOILA, an open-ended benchmark that asks multimodal models to complete visual analogies between image pairs without predefined choices. Experiments show current models struggle with inter-image relational reasoning: the best text-answer accuracy is 13% for LLaMa 3.2 on hard cases and 29% for GPT-4o on easier ones, versus 70% for humans.

FairGen：通过自适应潜空间引导控制扩散模型敏感属性
diffusion models bias mitigation fairness text-to-image

federated learning differential privacy privacy budget machine learning
