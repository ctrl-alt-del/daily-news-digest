Knowledge Distillation with Training Wheels
带辅助轮的知识蒸馏
knowledge distillation reinforcement learning Path Consistency Learning language models inference
The paper frames knowledge distillation as entropy-regularised value optimisation and combines Path Consistency Learning with constrained reinforcement learning so a student model can also request teacher help at test time under set rules. Experiments on translation and summarisation report operating points that the authors say speculative decoding does not reach.

IBURD is an image-blending pipeline that generates synthetic underwater debris images and pixel-level annotations to train detectors for autonomous underwater vehicles. It uses Poisson editing and style transfer to blend objects, including transparent ones, into varied marine backgrounds.

RSPO: Regularized Self-Play Alignment of Large Language Models
RSPO：大语言模型的正则化自我博弈对齐
self-play alignment large language models regularization preference optimization
RSPO is a framework for regularised self-play alignment of large language models that unifies earlier methods and supports plug-in regularisers while preserving convergence to a Nash equilibrium. The authors report gains in length-controlled win rate on AlpacaEval-2, for example from 28.5% to 35.4% with Mistral-7B.
