ChartLens: Fine-grained Visual Attribution in Charts
ChartLens：图表中的细粒度视觉归因
multimodal LLM chart understanding visual attribution benchmark
ChartLens is a chart attribution algorithm that uses segmentation-based techniques to identify chart objects and set-of-marks prompting with multimodal LLMs to attribute a response to fine-grained chart elements. The authors also present ChartVA-Eval, a benchmark of synthetic and real charts, and report 26-66% improvements in fine-grained attribution.

arxiv:2505.19349 arXiv ✓
DECA: A Near-Core LLM Decompression Accelerator Grounded on a 3D Roofline Model
DECA：基于三维 Roofline 模型的近核 LLM 解压缩加速器
LLM inference near-memory computing HBM sparsity computer architecture
DECA is a near-core accelerator that offloads de-sparsification and dequantization of compressed weight tiles from the CPU for in-core GeMM engines, paired with a new ISA extension that allows out-of-order invocation. In simulations of a 56-core Xeon server with HBM, it accelerates compressed GeMMs by up to 4x and cuts next-token generation time for Llama2-70B and OPT-66B by 1.6x-2.6x.
→ arxiv.org

decision transformer reinforcement learning zero-shot reach-avoid
The paper introduces RADT, a decision transformer for offline, reward-free, goal-conditioned reinforcement learning that encodes goals and avoid regions as prompt tokens, so any number of avoid regions can be specified at evaluation time. It generalizes zero-shot to out-of-distribution avoid-region sizes and counts, and is applied to cell reprogramming in biology.

ASTRA: Communication-Efficient Acceleration for Multi-Device Transformer Inference
ASTRA：面向多设备 Transformer 推理的通信高效加速
transformer inference distributed inference quantization bandwidth
ASTRA is a communication-efficient framework for multi-device Transformer inference that combines sequence parallelism with mixed-precision attention, transmitting non-local token embeddings as low-bit vector-quantized codes. It reports up to 2.64x speedup over single-device inference and up to 15.25x over prior multi-device baselines at bandwidths as low as 10 Mbps, and remains robust on Llama-3-8B under packet loss.

通过互信息重新审视大语言模型的理解能力

The paper proposes an information-theoretic framework based on mutual information to measure how well an LLM's sentence embeddings preserve input information, deriving a computable lower bound from Fano's inequality.
