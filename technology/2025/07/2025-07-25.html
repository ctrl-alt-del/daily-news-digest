KD-GAT：结合知识蒸馏与图注意力 Transformer 的 CAN 总线入侵检测系统
intrusion detection knowledge distillation graph attention automotive security
KD-GAT is a CAN bus intrusion detection framework that pairs graph attention networks with knowledge distillation; its student model is only 6.32% the size of the teacher and reached 99.97% and 99.31% accuracy on the Car-Hacking and Car-Survival datasets. The authors note that class imbalance in can-train-and-test reduced performance for both models.
