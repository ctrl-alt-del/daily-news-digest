面向区块链上开源语言模型基准测试的透明公平性评估协议

CoEx -- Co-evolving World-model and Exploration

LLM agent world model planning neurosymbolic
CoEx is a hierarchical agent architecture in which LLM planning co-evolves with a dynamically updated world model stored as a neurosymbolic belief state of textual inferences and code-based symbolic memory. Experiments on ALFWorld, PDDL and Jericho show it outperforming existing agent paradigms in planning and exploration.

Meaning-infused grammar: Gradient Acceptability Shapes the Geometric Representations of Constructions in LLMs
意义驱动的语法：梯度可接受度塑造 LLM 中构式的几何表示
LLM interpretability linguistics representation learning
The study examines English double-object and prepositional-object constructions in Pythia-1.4B using 5,000 sentence pairs varied by human-rated preference. Weighted Conditional Flow Matching
加权条件流匹配
flow matching generative modeling optimal transport
The paper proposes Weighted Conditional Flow Matching, which modifies the standard CFM loss by weighting each training pair with a Gibbs kernel inspired by entropic optimal transport. It shows the weighting recovers the entropic OT coupling up to marginal bias and matches minibatch OT in the large-batch limit, with comparable or better sample quality on synthetic and real datasets.
