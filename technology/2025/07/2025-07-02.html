基于深度学习的酒店 KPI 预测：全球城市市场的跨城市分析
LSTM forecasting hospitality

Manchester and Mumbai showed the highest predictive accuracy, while Dubai and Bangkok were more variable due to seasonal and event-driven influences.

Data Diversification Methods In Alignment Enhance Math Performance In LLMs
对齐中的数据多样化方法提升 LLM 数学能力
alignment math reasoning preference optimization
The paper evaluates temperature sampling, chain-of-thought prompting and Monte Carlo Tree Search for generating diversified preference data, and introduces Diversified-ThinkSolve (DTS). The best approach gained 7.1% on GSM8K and 4.2% on MATH over the base model with only 1.03x computational overhead, while MCTS was nearly five times more costly.

角色扮演智能体是否言行一致？LLM 人类信任模拟中的信念与行为一致性

ESTR-CoT：面向可解释、准确的事件流场景文字识别的思维链推理
event-based vision scene text recognition chain-of-thought Vicuna

Discrete Diffusion Models for Language Generation
面向语言生成的离散扩散模型
diffusion models language generation D3PM
The thesis evaluates the Discrete Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Model (D3PM) against autoregressive language models. The best D3PM model reached a bits-per-token of 5.72, while the autoregressive model had a lower mean BPT of 4.59, but D3PM processed up to 3.97 batches per second, suggesting potential for parallel generation.

Extended Differential Cryptanalysis of Kuznyechik
Kuznyechik 的扩展差分密码分析
cryptography Kuznyechik cryptanalysis
The authors analyze a multiplicative input-side relation as an extended differential for block ciphers, then compute uniformity values of 64, 33 and 21 for exceptional inverse classes of the Kuznyechik S-box.
