XYEval: Agents say yes to bad advice

事实核查 形式化方法 信息完整性 可审计性
This systematization of knowledge organizes 121 works on automated fact-checking by what is formalized rather than by pipeline stage, yielding five levels: the claim, the reasoning, the checking system, the information ecosystem, and the regulatory obligation.
