Scaling of Capability and Efficiency at Inference Time in Large Reasoning Models
大型推理模型在推理阶段的能力与效率缩放规律
推理模型 测试时计算 规模定律 思维链
The paper uses hierarchical Bayesian models to evaluate capability and efficiency of the DeepSeek-R1-Distill family across four classes of arithmetic and algorithmic reasoning problems. It finds that the probability of a correct solution decays approximately exponentially with problem size, with a decay scale that grows sublinearly in model size, while output length grows as a power law whose parameters do not vary systematically with model size, suggesting capability gains diminish with scale and efficiency improves little.

Learning Risk Scores Robust to Unobserved Confounders
学习对未观测混杂稳健的风险评分
风险评分 因果推断 分布鲁棒优化 资源分配
The paper proposes a method for learning risk scores from observational data under unobserved confounding that, since propensity weights cannot be reliably estimated, treats them as an uncertainty set determined by observable data and domain-informed estimates of confounding, combining causal sensitivity analysis with Wasserstein distributionally robust optimization and reformulating the problem as an exponential cone program. On semi-synthetic data derived from UCI datasets it improves calibration by up to 29.2% over traditional benchmarks and up to 11.1% over the state of the art.

变化检测 遥感 状态空间模型 扩散模型
The paper presents BMD-CD, which converts deep bitemporal features into region tokens arranged in explicit temporal partitions for bidirectional state-space propagation with linear sequence complexity, adds orthogonal feature disentanglement and multiscale decoding, and refines coarse change logits with a five-step conditional diffusion decoder operating in logit space. It reports F1 scores of 93.7%, 96.0%, 97.8%, and 99.0% on four standard benchmarks plus boundary-F1 gains, at 32.09 GFLOPs and 47 ms per 256x256 image pair.

我们需要复杂的拓扑控制吗？稀疏多智能体辩论中异质对等随机路由提升成本效率
多智能体辩论 通信拓扑 成本效率 大语言模型推理
The paper examines sparse multi-agent debate and finds that a simple random-without-replacement routing policy, in which each agent debates with two distinct and newly sampled peers every round, is a surprisingly strong baseline that consistently improves the accuracy-cost trade-off. The paper introduces RECAP, which assigns step-level credit from two signals: structural responsibility, obtained by propagating credit backward from the final-answer node through an outcome-independent, LLM-annotated semantic dependency graph, and step efficacy, measured by changes in gold-answer log-likelihood as each step is added, then reshapes rollout-level GRPO advantages into step-specific updates without a process reward model.
