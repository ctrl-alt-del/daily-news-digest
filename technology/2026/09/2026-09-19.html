The paper fuses U.S. SEC financial fundamentals with facility-level EPA greenhouse-gas registries to build a mathematically guaranteed baseline of physical corporate emissions, then uses gradient boosting and Mondrian conformal prediction to quantify the gap with self-reported data as a Conformal-Weighted Continuous Divergence metric. SoK: Formal Methods for Fact-Checking and Information Integrity
SoK：面向事实核查与信息完整性的形式化方法
事实核查 形式化方法 信息完整性 可审计性
This systematization of knowledge organizes 121 works on automated fact-checking by what is formalized rather than by pipeline stage, yielding five levels: the claim, the reasoning, the checking system, the information ecosystem, and the regulatory obligation. It finds that most relevant formal machinery already exists but was built for other domains, that the gap is widest for verifying the checking system itself, and that writing a claim in checkable form and correcting a published verdict lack stated correctness criteria.
