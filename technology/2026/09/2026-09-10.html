EAR: Entity-Aware Partitioning Approach for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Development
EAR：面向检索增强生成开发的实体感知划分方法
retrieval-augmented generation corpus partitioning entity anchors multiple-choice QA
EAR is an entity-aware partitioning approach for retrieval-augmented multiple-choice QA that extracts normalized surface anchors from the question, answer options and corpus, then retrieves local windows around matching anchors and can attach a larger parent passage via an extractive summary. On a cleaned 153-question MMLU-style subset with Mistral, Gemma and DeepSeek, entity-window retrieval cut retrieved words by 37.5-40.2% relative to chunks, while accuracy changes were mixed and not statistically significant.

面向基于模型强化学习的摊销低秩适配
world models LoRA hypernetworks model-based reinforcement learning

The study trains continuous (TD3) and discrete (DQN) deep reinforcement learning dosing policies on a high-dimensional heterogeneous tumor model and benchmarks them against a Pontryagin's Maximum Principle open-loop controller.
