GameSmith: Scaling Zero-Sum Self-Play with Synthesised Games
GameSmith：用合成游戏扩展零和自博弈
self-play game synthesis LLM post-training reinforcement learning reasoning transfer
GameSmith uses an LLM to synthesize games inside fixed templates, verifies each by seed-paired replay and an independent re-implementation, and expands them into 26 new games in 29 families and 336 variants from three SPIRAL templates. The student is distilled from a larger Qwen3-32B teacher's self-play and then trained with self-play RL using CRAB, an advantage estimator defined on every variant; on Qwen3-4B-Base distillation raises the 13-benchmark mean pass@1 from 45.5 to 62.5 and self-play with CRAB reaches 64.9, above the three original games at the same budget.
→ arxiv.org

What Must a World Model Distinguish for Planning?
世界模型为规划必须区分什么？
world models planning robotic planning decision sufficiency model-based RL
The paper formalizes a hierarchy of mechanism, response, and decision sufficiency to argue that what a world model must preserve depends on the planning query, the candidate set, and the planner, and studies these effects in a collision system, nonlinear dynamics, and robotic planning. Query-conditioned models that jointly generate actions and outcomes achieve lower regret on seen objectives but lose most of that advantage on unseen ones, motivating a modular design where the query determines where to look and an action-conditioned model predicts what happens.
→ arxiv.org

Model-Aware Data Selection from In-and-Out Information Interplay
基于内外信息交互的模型感知数据选择
data selection LLM post-training representation analysis data efficiency counterfactual signals
The authors observe a rank equilibrium in which hidden-state rank follows a U-shape across layers while weight rank follows an inverted U, and interpret intermediate activations as carrying mainly what the weights cannot supply. Building on this, they propose CAP, a counterfactual data selection method that reports 35.4% greater average improvement over the base model than the strongest baseline across math, code, and science, and matches or beats full-pool training with only 10% of the data on math and science.
→ arxiv.org

The Epistemics of Agent Memory: Measuring, and Governing, the Consolidation Decision in Long-Horizon LLM Agents
智能体记忆的认识论：度量与治理长程 LLM 智能体的整合决策
agent memory memory consolidation benchmark AI governance long-horizon agents
This four-phase research program studies how long-horizon LLM agents decide what to keep, compress, abstract into reusable skills, or forget, moving from learned episodic boundaries and promotion policies to the ConsolidationBench benchmark and a governed-consolidation phase with poison-resistance, reversibility, and auditability guarantees. It reports a verified +22.7% task-success gain with 7x compression, shows governance quality is statistically distinct from consolidation quality (r-squared = 0.43), and finds that the quality score does not predict real transfer accuracy.

Relative Generalization Invariance of LLM Pretraining
LLM 预训练的泛化相对不变性
pretraining generalization optimizers training data scaling
The paper introduces Relative Generalization Invariance (RGI), the invariance across models of the validation-loss difference between any two tokens, and shows it approximately holds across a wide range of optimizers and moderate architectural variations but not when the training data stream changes. The authors show RGI is not explained by neural tangent kernel or mean-field regimes alone and prove it can emerge in an overparameterized quadratic model.
→ arxiv.org

消费级 AI 智能体世界中的信任与任务完成
AI agents trust task completion agent evaluation consumer AI
The authors build an evaluation that scores trust and task completion on the same runs in a simulated world of businesses with their own websites, inboxes, and phone lines, pairing every trap with a matched control where acting is correct.
