ARCagent: An Adaptive Retrieval Calibration Agent for Clinical Question Answering
ARCagent：面向临床问答的自适应检索校准智能体
clinical question answering retrieval-augmented generation conflict-aware retrieval medical guidelines LLM-as-judge
ARCagent is a clinical question-answering agent for ME/CFS that pairs a 1,706-chunk, 10-source knowledge base and a structured inter-guideline conflict registry with a conflict-aware retrieval calibration pipeline that re-ranks evidence using query-specific focus and conflict signals. On a benchmark scored by LLM-as-Judge, which the authors say avoids a 10.1-percentage-point underestimation from keyword matching, ARCagent achieves 95.3% and outperforms all base LLMs.

arxiv:2609.36368 arXiv ✓
AdaKerNet: Neural Kernel Decoding for Task-Adaptive Prediction with Multimodal Large Models
AdaKerNet：面向多模态大模型任务自适应预测的神经核解码
multimodal learning neural kernels frozen representations low-label learning MLLM adaptation
AdaKerNet is a task-adaptive neural kernel decoder that is agnostic to an MLLM's parameters and learns from its frozen multimodal representations using learnable Lipschitz-controlled features, a reference kernel as a soft structural prior, and a lightweight nonlinear predictor trained jointly. Across BLIP-2, LLaVA-1.5, Qwen2.5-VL, and Gemini Embedding 2 with text, audio, image, and tabular inputs, it reports average error reductions of up to 41% over MLP, attention-, autoencoder-, and kernel-based decoders in scarce-label regimes.

arxiv:2609.36374 arXiv ✓
OTT3R: Multi-View 3D Reconstruction and Fast Dataset Generation at 1% Compute
OTT3R：仅需 1% 算力的多视图三维重建与快速数据集生成
3D reconstruction knowledge distillation pseudo-labeling dataset generation compute efficiency
OTT3R distills the 959M-parameter pi^3 model into a 102M-parameter student for feed-forward 3D reconstruction, reporting 9.4x compression, up to 7x faster inference, and training at 1.6% of VGGT's compute, plus an integrated pseudo-label pipeline that generated dense point maps and camera poses for a 667K-image corpus in 3.5 hours on two commodity GPUs. The general student tracks the teacher in-distribution and beats COLMAP zero-shot on 7-Scenes and DTU completion but does not replace the teacher on out-of-distribution multi-view geometry; a domain-specialized student is 4x more accurate than COLMAP on 7-Scenes at 980x throughput.

LEGO-Anything: Coding Agents for 3D Scene Reconstruction
LEGO-Anything：用编程智能体进行三维场景重建
3D scene reconstruction coding agents Blender benchmark program synthesis
LEGO-Anything is an image-to-code framework in which a coding agent iteratively writes and executes Blender code, inspects scenes and renderings, and revises the program, so that a single image becomes an editable, queryable scene program. The authors introduce LEGO-Bench (208 images from 104 indoor and outdoor scenes) and report GPT-6-astra as the strongest agent at 53.4% indoor and 39.6% outdoor, while a training-free harness plugin improves all six evaluated models by up to 62.7% relative.

Support-Set Target Leakage in Relational Foundation Models during In-Context Learning: Impact, Detection, and Mitigation
关系基础模型上下文学习中的支持集目标泄漏：影响、检测与缓解
in-context learning data leakage relational foundation models integrated gradients tabular data
The paper formulates support-set target leakage, where target-derived columns appear only in the labeled support set of relational in-context inference while queries remain clean, and constructs 14 synthetic leaker types across 20 columns spanning different noise levels, coverage, modalities, and relational distances. Evaluated on held-out RelBench databases, target-table leakers cause the clearest degradation while one- and two-hop leakers are not consistently used, and Integrated Gradients ranks target-table leakers highly and partially recovers performance where leakage has the largest effect.

面向高效机器学习工程智能体的奖励率策略梯度
