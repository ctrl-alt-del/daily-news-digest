HaikuS2S: A Cascaded System For Responding In Verse
HaikuS2S：用于以俳句作答的级联系统
语音合成 俳句生成 韵律建模 级联系统
The paper presents HaikuS2S, a cascaded system combining automatic speech recognition, LLM-generated haiku, and text-to-speech fine-tuned on both prose and custom haiku datasets to produce speech that respects haiku's 5-7-5 structure and line-ending pauses. Evaluation on emotion similarity, speech quality, and prosody alignment shows significant prosody and tonal alignment gains for the fine-tuned systems, especially the one trained on both general poetry and haiku, while emotion similarity stays similar across systems.

XYEval: Agents say yes to bad advice

智能体评测 XY 问题 谄媚 人机协作
The paper introduces XYEval, a meta-evaluation framework that transforms existing benchmarks into XY-problem tests of whether agents resist plausible but misleading user suggestions and explain their reasoning, evaluated across five models and six benchmark suites. The paper studies time-incremental continued pretraining on FineWeb-Edu dumps drawn from after each model's knowledge cutoff across six open-weight models from the OLMo2, Llama-3.1/3.2, and Gemma-3-1B families at 1B-8B scale. Increasing Skill Level Recruits Deeper Attention Layers in a Frozen Chess Transformer
棋力提升会调动冻结的国际象棋 Transformer 中更深的注意力层
机制可解释性 Transformer 注意力头消融 国际象棋
The paper ablates every attention head of the Maia-3 chess transformer at every Elo from 700 to 2500, exploiting the model's skill input to vary conditioning without changing weights. It finds that higher skill monotonically pushes the causal center of mass of computation deeper for every piece and move type measured, that the migration is much larger for specific tactics such as knight forks, and that it consists of deeper heads being recruited for specialized computations while one shared shallow head keeps a roughly constant contribution.

Echo State Network (ESN) for Signal Recovery in RF-Impaired IBFD MIMO Systems

回声状态网络 全双工 MIMO 自干扰消除 无线通信
The paper proposes a two-stage echo state network scheme for in-band full-duplex MIMO in which a frozen ESN is trained offline to characterize the static self-interference path and an adaptive ESN, updated online via recursive least squares on sparse pilot symbols, tracks the time-varying signal-of-interest path under RF impairments. Simulations show the approach outperforms blind source separation, LSTM, and GRU baselines in both efficiency and signal-of-interest recovery.
