arxiv:2609.32081 arXiv ✓
Toward Interactive Understanding of Code APIs
迈向代码 API 的交互式理解
code APIs tool understanding benchmark exploration LLM agents
The paper introduces PAU, a benchmark that gives models only black-box, API-level access to Python snippets, so they must probe the code with exploratory inputs and infer its functionality, framing this as unsupervised tool understanding. Frontier models struggle, with Claude-4-Opus failing to understand over 45% of the test set, and the authors trace this to overconfidence and insufficient exploration; post-training with an Asymmetric Actor Critic approach lets an AAC-tuned Qwen3-8B match GPT-5-mini.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.32093 arXiv ✓
GameBoyWorlds: A Testbed for Self-Improvement in Embodied Video Games
GameBoyWorlds：具身电子游戏自我改进的测试平台
self-improvement embodied agents video games benchmark exploration
GameBoyWorlds is a testbed for agentic self-improvement in video games: its Execution split spans 5 game series and 500 tasks where agents get training games but no demonstrations, documentation, or rewards, while its Playthrough split tests end-to-end completion of two fan-made Pokémon games. Out-of-the-box frontier models complete fewer than 50% of the execution tasks, and the authors show that world modelling and autonomous skill discovery fail while a curiosity-driven guide-writing strategy achieves only partial success; a sophisticated agentic pipeline with multimodal memory and hierarchical subgoals fails to reach the first major milestone in either playthrough.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.32056 arXiv ✓
Graph Forward Distribution Matching for Molecular Inverse Design
用于分子逆向设计的图前向分布匹配
molecular inverse design graph diffusion reinforcement learning distribution matching polymer generation
GraphFDM is an online reinforcement learning paradigm for graph diffusion that optimizes through the forward process, using valid generations to define a reward-tilted target distribution jointly over graph size and molecular structure for each property condition without storing reverse trajectories. In multi-conditional polymer and small-molecule generation it reports the lowest MAE on every target property, with reductions of up to 53.0% relative to the strongest baselines and chemical validity above 0.99.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.32060 arXiv ✓
Depth Laws for the Precision Floor of Trained Neural Networks: Amplification, Residual Scaling, and a Quantization-Aware Training Paradox
训练神经网络精度下限的深度定律：放大效应、残差缩放与量化感知训练的悖论
quantization neural network theory depth scaling quantization-aware training numerical precision
The paper derives a first-order theory in which the precision floor at which accuracy falls halfway to chance is set by a predictive amplification factor that grows linearly with depth, and tests it in MLPs, CNNs, Vision Transformers, and nine pretrained language models under post-training quantization and quantization- or noise-aware training. The predicted depth-exponent equality holds in twelve of thirteen architectures and in GPT-2 from 12 to 48 layers, and the authors report a QAT paradox in which noise-aware training's benefit decays with depth, steepening the depth law.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.32082 arXiv ✓
mu-bench: A Multilingual Utterance Transcription Benchmark
mu-bench：多语言话语转写基准
speech recognition multilingual benchmark ASR evaluation voice agents word error rate
The authors release mu-bench, 4,270 caller utterances from 250 phone calls to an AI banking agent in English, Spanish, Turkish, Vietnamese, and Mandarin, centered on form-field inputs such as names and email addresses, along with an LLM-judged Utterance Error Rate metric calibrated against human raters. UER agrees with annotators at kappa = 0.78 versus 0.53 for normalized exact-match WER, and on a leaderboard of six commercial providers the best reaches 11.9% UER, with Mandarin hardest for all six.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.32083 arXiv ✓
When Should a Human Take Back Control? Optimal Delegation under Turbulent AI Risk
人类何时应收回控制权？动荡 AI 风险下的最优委托
human oversight AI delegation reinforcement learning Hawkes processes risk management
The paper formulates adaptive human oversight of AI as a continuous-time stochastic control problem in which failures cluster through self-exciting dynamics, jointly deciding monitoring effort and switching between human-AI operation and full delegation. It proposes Hawkes-PPO, a policy-gradient method using a bank of exponential filters of incident times, which in a synthetic environment attains a higher risk-adjusted objective than either fixed regime and approaches an approximate full-information oracle.
