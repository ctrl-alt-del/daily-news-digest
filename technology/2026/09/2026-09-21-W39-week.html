Continuous Optimization for p-adic Models
p 进模型的连续优化
p 进数 梯度下降 优化器 Berkovich 直线
The paper presents a method for native continuous gradient descent on machine learning models with p-adic parameters by working over the Berkovich affine line, a path-connected metric-tree expansion of the p-adic numbers that admits interpretable points and local derivatives. It formulates gradient descent plus momentum and Adam variants, shows linear models with p-adic coefficients can learn modular arithmetic, and demonstrates linear regression and classification on binary-encoded hierarchies.
→ arxiv.org
MiniMax arxiv:2610.00254 arXiv ✓
Sharp Oracle-Regret Tradeoffs for Projection-Free Online Convex Optimization
无投影在线凸优化的紧致 oracle-遗憾权衡
在线凸优化 遗憾界 无投影方法 线性优化 oracle
The paper characterizes minimax expected regret for online convex optimization when the feasible set is accessible only through an exact linear optimization oracle, giving a dimension-free bound of Theta(GD max{sqrt(T), T/(1+min{Q,BT})^{1/4}}) for G-Lipschitz convex losses with diameter D, total budget Q, and per-round cap B, with a matching counted approximate-gradient method. Total-budget and strict per-round guarantees follow as special cases, including the T^{3/4} rate with one call per round and the quadratic total budget needed for sqrt(T) regret.
→ arxiv.org

universal adversarial perturbations intrusion detection deep reinforcement learning explainable AI probabilistic robustness

RAG-NAROK: Retrieval-Aware Knowledge Corpus Poisoning in RAG with Source-specific Refutation
RAG-NAROK：具备来源特定反驳的检索感知型 RAG 知识库投毒
RAG 安全 知识库投毒 对抗攻击 大语言模型
The paper introduces RAG-NAROK, an adaptive knowledge-base poisoning framework that exploits RAG pipeline transparency to first extract the legitimate source identities retrieved for a query, then generates Anchor-Specific Refutation documents that explicitly name and devalue those sources while using recency and authority biases to steer generation toward a target answer. It reports that the attack significantly outperforms static poisoning baselines across diverse domains.

arxiv:2609.27155 arXiv ✓
The Like Trap: Multi-Stage Poisoning against Agents in Similarity-based Recommendation Systems
点赞陷阱：针对相似度推荐系统中智能体的多阶段投毒
推荐系统安全 智能体安全 投毒攻击 社交媒体
The paper theoretically analyzes how the like-score mechanism in OASIS can be exploited, characterizes the conditions under which a multi-stage chain of poisoned posts can steer an agent's recommendation feed, and develops an algorithm for crafting realistic poisoned posts. Experiments support the analysis and show that the attack makes the recommender select poisoned posts even when their user-post similarity falls below the retrieval threshold.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.30614 arXiv ✓
Subjects, Not Authors: The Authorship Hazard in Agentic Dataspaces
是主体，而非作者：智能体数据空间中的作者身份风险
AI governance agentic dataspaces authorization policy authoring LLM agents
The paper names an authorship hazard in agentic dataspaces: agents that both author and are subject to policy can write the norms that bind them, and it argues that an agent should be a subject of the governance plane, never an author of it. Across 90 preregistered edits evaluated on 344,512 requests, it finds that six edits which only reclassify a field all change authorization and narrow a duty without touching policy text, and that compiled tool-call constraints kept protected fields from reaching the model in 105 of 105 cases.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.33016 arXiv ✓
Relative Generalization Invariance of LLM Pretraining
LLM 预训练的泛化相对不变性
pretraining generalization optimizers training data scaling
The paper introduces Relative Generalization Invariance (RGI), the invariance across models of the validation-loss difference between any two tokens, and shows it approximately holds across a wide range of optimizers and moderate architectural variations but not when the training data stream changes. The authors show RGI is not explained by neural tangent kernel or mean-field regimes alone and prove it can emerge in an overparameterized quadratic model.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.34002 arXiv ✓
T-SNN: Temporal Simplicial Neural Network for EEG Decoding
T-SNN：用于脑电解码的时间单纯形神经网络
EEG decoding simplicial neural networks emotion recognition brain-computer interfaces graph learning
T-SNN represents EEG recordings as sequences of evolving simplicial complexes and combines simplicial convolutions with recurrent updates to jointly learn higher-order interactions among brain regions and their temporal evolution. On the seven-class SEED-VII emotion recognition task it outperforms convolutional, recurrent, graph-based, and Transformer methods in both trial-wise and cross-subject evaluations, and incorporating eye-movement features yields further gains.
