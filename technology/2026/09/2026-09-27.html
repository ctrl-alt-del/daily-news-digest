Fisher-Informed Recalibration for Feedback-Based On-Policy Self-Distillation of LLMs
面向 LLM 反馈式同策略自蒸馏的 Fisher 信息重校准
self-distillation LLM fine-tuning Fisher information training stability feedback learning
FIRE is a dual-branch framework that stabilizes feedback-based on-policy self-distillation by replacing self-distillation with re-weighted on-policy SFT for correct responses and recalibrating the feedback-conditioned target for incorrect ones, using a token-level radius derived in part from a softmax Fisher trace. Experiments report substantially more stable self-distillation while maintaining strong downstream performance, particularly where standard feedback-conditioned distillation becomes unstable.
→ arxiv.org

基于状态空间模型的三维点跟踪
3D point tracking state space models monocular depth robot navigation autonomous driving
The method composes frozen dense optical flow and monocular metric-depth front-ends and learns only the residual depth with a compact Mamba-3 state space model conditioned on DINOv3 appearance features, targeting metric 3D point tracking on a single commodity GPU without poses. On the TAPVid-3D minival benchmark its best configuration attains the highest absolute metric accuracy among methods evaluated under identical conditions (mean metric Average Jaccard 0.256), and a companion analysis explains why several published trackers lose most of their accuracy under the same budget.

忠实的激活言语化：减少 LLM 表示解释中的幻觉
activation verbalization interpretability hallucination direct preference optimization representation decoding
AVPO is a two-stage framework that first reconstructs source text from a hidden activation and then evaluates the reconstruction with a separate frozen question-answering model, yielding an inspectable intermediate readout, with the inverter optimized by DPO using rewards for semantic recoverability and lexical fidelity. privacy screen-share assistants PII redaction content mediation evaluation methodology

T-SNN：用于脑电解码的时间单纯形神经网络
EEG decoding simplicial neural networks emotion recognition brain-computer interfaces graph learning
T-SNN represents EEG recordings as sequences of evolving simplicial complexes and combines simplicial convolutions with recurrent updates to jointly learn higher-order interactions among brain regions and their temporal evolution.
