Continuous Optimization for p-adic Models
p 进模型的连续优化
p 进数 梯度下降 优化器 Berkovich 直线
The paper presents a method for native continuous gradient descent on machine learning models with p-adic parameters by working over the Berkovich affine line, a path-connected metric-tree expansion of the p-adic numbers that admits interpretable points and local derivatives. It formulates gradient descent plus momentum and Adam variants, shows linear models with p-adic coefficients can learn modular arithmetic, and demonstrates linear regression and classification on binary-encoded hierarchies.
→ arxiv.org

arxiv:2609.25490 arXiv ✓
SAM-V: Geometry-Aware Segment Anything for Multi-View Instance Segmentation
SAM-V：面向多视图实例分割的几何感知分割一切模型
多视图分割 实例分割 3D 感知 基础模型
The paper proposes SAM-V, which instead of post-hoc mask matching integrates features from the feed-forward geometry model VGGT directly into SAM and trains end-to-end for cross-view instance prediction, using prompt fusion with view-specific camera tokens and local geometry features plus a mask decoder attending to dense 2D and 3D features. On the IGGT 3D tracking benchmark it improves overall IoU by 5 points and frame-level recall by 12 points on the ScanNet++ split over the state-of-the-art multi-view instance segmentation baseline, and leads on all metrics on the zero-shot ScanNet split.

RAG-NAROK: Retrieval-Aware Knowledge Corpus Poisoning in RAG with Source-specific Refutation
RAG-NAROK：具备来源特定反驳的检索感知型 RAG 知识库投毒
RAG 安全 知识库投毒 对抗攻击 大语言模型
The paper introduces RAG-NAROK, an adaptive knowledge-base poisoning framework that exploits RAG pipeline transparency to first extract the legitimate source identities retrieved for a query, then generates Anchor-Specific Refutation documents that explicitly name and devalue those sources while using recency and authority biases to steer generation toward a target answer. It reports that the attack significantly outperforms static poisoning baselines across diverse domains.

arxiv:2609.25474 arXiv ✓
Spectra: A Rules-Driven LLM Pipeline for Automated KYC Document Processing
Spectra：面向自动化 KYC 文档处理的规则驱动 LLM 流水线
KYC 合规 文档处理 规则引擎 大语言模型应用
The paper restructures KYC onboarding from a four-party to a two-party workflow to remove handoffs, then introduces Spectra, a platform that combines a rules engine encoding compliance policy as a queryable database with LLM-based classification, extraction, and validation agents decomposed into isolated, auditable stages. On real KYC documents it reports 100% classification accuracy, 89.4% extraction accuracy, and a 96% reduction in human review burden.

Terminal Shrinkage Averaging Reveals a Schedule-Estimator Interaction in LLM Pretraining
终端收缩平均揭示大模型预训练中的学习率调度与估计器交互
预训练 学习率调度 检查点平均 优化
The paper proposes Terminal Shrinkage Averaging, which interpolates between the raw final iterate and the average of recent checkpoints to balance late-training optimization progress against terminal variation, and analyzes how it changes the preferred terminal learning-rate schedule under a local quadratic approximation. Controlled NanoChat experiments show the schedule-estimator interaction, the gains transfer to depth-22 NanoChat, and a qualifying time-to-GPT-2 run finishes faster than the public baseline used in the experiments.
