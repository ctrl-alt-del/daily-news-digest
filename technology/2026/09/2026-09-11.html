Same Patient, Different Order: Action-Level Reliability of Clinical LLM Agents Under Repeated Runs
同一患者，不同医嘱：重复运行下临床 LLM 智能体的动作级可靠性
clinical LLM agents evaluation reliability MedAgentBench reproducibility
The authors introduce a "same-input rerun" protocol that replays a task with every input held fixed and compares the resulting orders rather than the score, and apply it to 1000 MedAgentBench runs across 50 tasks with two quantized open-weight models below ten billion parameters at two temperatures. They find action-level divergence across identical runs that often passes unrecorded by the benchmark score, arguing for repeated-run evaluation and action-level stability reporting.
→ arxiv.org

FLoKD: Adaptive Knowledge Distillation for Federated Low-Rank LLM over Wireless Networks
FLoKD：无线网络下联邦低秩 LLM 的自适应知识蒸馏
federated learning knowledge distillation LoRA wireless networks
FLoKD is an adaptive knowledge-distillation framework for federated LoRA fine-tuning of LLMs over wireless networks that communicates intermediate LoRA activations as the distillation signal instead of logits or full parameters. It adds transformer-block importance scoring and two dataset selection strategies, and reports 50-65% lower communication overhead with rapid convergence to competitive perplexity on WikiText-103, PTB and Dialog.

面向完整出院小结生成：基于抽象语义表示的方法

arxiv:2609.13601 arXiv ✓
Mind the Gap: Detecting Description-Execution Mismatch Attacks in DAO Governance
留意差距：检测 DAO 治理中的描述-执行不一致攻击
DAO governance smart contracts LLM security proposal attacks
The paper presents a framework for detecting Description-Execution Mismatch (DEMI) attacks, in which a DAO proposal's benign-looking natural-language description hides code that transfers funds or seizes control, using a DAO-agnostic simulation that derives execution behavior and an LLM evidence-mapping paradigm that locates per-action textual justifications instead of issuing holistic judgments. On real Ethereum DAO governance data the detector reports 81.7% mean precision and 98.3% mean recall under stratified cross-validation and defends against most adaptive attacks in a red-team/blue-team evaluation.

arxiv:2609.13611 arXiv ✓
In the Blind: Building Pseudo-References for MT Evaluation
盲评之下：为机器翻译评测构建伪参考译文
machine translation evaluation quality estimation pseudo-references WMT
The paper describes how pseudo-references were built for 10 reference-free language pairs in the WMT26 General MT task plus six pairs with some human references: seven models translate 3,277 documents under up to five prompt conditions, three reference-free quality estimation models score candidates, and a per-document selector picks one translation that GPT-5.5 post-edits where needed. Adding a confidence-scaled language-identification penalty drove wrong-language selections to zero, and the released human evaluation shows the references match the strongest participating systems when the selector kept a frontier-model candidate.
→ arxiv.org

AttnFuse: A Composable DSL for Compiling Attentions to Fused GPU Kernels
AttnFuse：将注意力编译为融合 GPU 内核的可组合 DSL
GPU kernels attention mechanisms compiler DSL RoPE
AttnFuse is a small domain-specific language for attention that makes pre-multiplication transformations such as RoPE first-class operations, letting researchers compose ten building blocks into a variant that compiles to a single fused GPU kernel. It reports a 2.10x speedup over flex_attention on the RoPE+causal pattern on an RTX 3090 and runs a full Llama-3-8B training step within 5% of PyTorch's hand-tuned backend on an H100.
