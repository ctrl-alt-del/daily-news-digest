arxiv:2609.30614 arXiv ✓
Subjects, Not Authors: The Authorship Hazard in Agentic Dataspaces
是主体，而非作者：智能体数据空间中的作者身份风险
AI governance agentic dataspaces authorization policy authoring LLM agents
The paper names an authorship hazard in agentic dataspaces: agents that both author and are subject to policy can write the norms that bind them, and it argues that an agent should be a subject of the governance plane, never an author of it. Across 90 preregistered edits evaluated on 344,512 requests, it finds that six edits which only reclassify a field all change authorization and narrow a duty without touching policy text, and that compiled tool-call constraints kept protected fields from reaching the model in 105 of 105 cases.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.30625 arXiv ✓
Audio LLMs Know When They Can't Hear You
音频大模型知道自己听不清你说话
audio LLMs transcription reliability speech recognition uncertainty estimation clarification
The authors study whether an audio LLM can recognize when its own transcription of degraded speech is unreliable, finding that prompting the model to judge itself works poorly, as do speech-quality predictors, generation uncertainty, and transcript-conditioned WER estimation, while reliability is strongly represented in the frozen audio encoder. A lightweight predictor trained on those encoder representations reaches 81.10% in-domain and 78.09% cross-domain macro-F1, beating the strongest baselines by 10.33 and 11.93 points, and can trigger a clarification request without modifying the underlying model.
→ arxiv.org
