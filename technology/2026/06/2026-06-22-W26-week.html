Attributable by Construction: Claim-Anchored Provenance for Multi-Document Summarization
构造即归因：多文档摘要的声明锚定溯源
multi-document summarization attribution provenance annotation dataset
CAMS decomposes source documents into atomic claims tied to verbatim quotes and token spans, clusters equivalent claims and flags conflicts, then rewrites a selected subset so that every summary sentence carries claim identifiers resolving to source spans. They release CORE-Bench v1.1 and an out-of-distribution suite, and a small randomized experiment finds human-agent collaboration roughly twice as fast.
