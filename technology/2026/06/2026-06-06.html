Forward-Free Diffusion Language Models with BPTT-Free Looped Refinement
无前向过程的扩散语言模型：免 BPTT 的循环精炼
diffusion language model FReDA distribution refinement BPTT
FReDA eliminates the hand-designed forward corruption process in diffusion language models, treating model-generated drafts as intermediate states that a refinement model recursively improves, and trains without backpropagation through time across refinement passes. In sub-8B evaluations, FReDA-4B outperformed larger diffusion baselines on reasoning and coding benchmarks with absolute gains of up to 15% and a 1.5-1.8x average speedup.
→ arxiv.org

Benchmarking Open-Ended Multi-Agent Coordination in Language Agents
语言 Agent 开放式多智能体协作基准测试
multi-agent benchmark alem LLM agents
The authors introduce alem, a JAX benchmark built on Craftax-like dynamics that tests long-horizon coordination among language agents, and report that 13 modern LLMs evaluated zero-shot average only about 6% normalized return. Communication was the largest contributor to coordination, and Gemini-3.1-Pro-High approached MARL agents trained for a billion steps on the hardest setting while GPT-5.4-High showed strong base-task reward but much lower coordination reward.
→ arxiv.org

CATPO：批评增强的树策略优化
RLVR tree policy optimization CATPO Qwen2.5-Math
CATPO scores tree-structured rollouts for informativeness, uses natural-language critiques to repair trees in which every branch fails, and weights the loss by that score. On Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B it reached 37.5% macro accuracy across AIME24, MATH-500, OlympiadBench and MinervaMath, 1.9% above TreeRPO and 4.8% above GRPO.

SoK：合成表格数据上的重构攻击（来自赢得 NIST CRC 的洞见）

GENERIC-FNO: Embedding Energy Conservation and Entropy Production into Fourier Neural Operators
GENERIC-FNO：把能量守恒与熵产生嵌入傅里叶神经算子
neural operator Fourier neural operator thermodynamics PDE
GENERIC-FNO embeds the metriplectic (GENERIC) degeneracy structure of nonequilibrium thermodynamics into a neural operator, learning energy and entropy functionals and building reversible and irreversible operators as diagonal Fourier multipliers flanked by rank-one projections. The degeneracy conditions hold to machine precision by construction, and over 200-step rollouts the model stayed bounded where every unconstrained model tested diverged or collapsed, at half the parameters but 4-10x the compute and with accuracy losses on pure transport and the smallest 1D backbone.

协变量依赖到达下自适应同伴推荐招募的生成式前沿规划
generative planning respondent-driven sampling public health
Generative Frontier Planning is a model-based planner for allocating referral resources in peer-referral recruitment, modeling referral capacity and the covariates of new recruits as conditioned on the referrer. A deterministic backup over a latent covariate-coverage surrogate makes planning tractable and supports a greedy allocation with a (1-1/e) approximation; in a simulation calibrated to a real respondent-driven sampling dataset it outperformed random, reinforcement-learning and i.i.d. dynamic-programming baselines across four discount factors.
