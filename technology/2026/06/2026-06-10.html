psychometric evaluation LLM behavior Theory of Planned Behavior alignment

Normative Robustness as a Frontier for Non-Verifiable Reasoning in LLMs
规范鲁棒性：LLM 不可验证推理的前沿
moral reasoning sycophancy LLM evaluation
The authors propose measuring 'moral robustness' with an adversarial multi-turn framework, simulating 48,000 moral deliberations across four frontier LLMs. They find models shift their reasoning by up to 6.5% toward the user's stated moral view, with premise order and conversation length altering judgments in 13–22% and 10–24% of cases respectively—a failure mode they call moral deliberative sycophancy.

differential privacy DP-SGD privacy auditing
The paper studies one-run empirical privacy auditing for differentially private machine learning, showing that canary-aligned signals in white-box DP-SGD form random variables whose normalized sum is asymptotically Gaussian. That perspective yields a framework for tighter privacy lower bounds from a single training run.

面向含 Dirac Delta 源项的 PDE：物理信息神经网络与径向基函数
PINNs partial differential equations radial basis functions groundwater modeling
Treating PINNs as residual least squares methods allows Dirac delta terms in PDEs to be handled directly through weak-form integration, the authors show.
