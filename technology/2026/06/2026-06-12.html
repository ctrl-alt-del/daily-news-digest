AI Engram：寻找人工智能中的记忆痕迹
AI engram memory traces interpretability closed-form estimator
The paper introduces a geometric framework that formalizes neuroscientific criteria—specificity, reactivation, sufficiency, and necessity—as a constrained inverse problem to identify “AI engrams” in neural networks.
