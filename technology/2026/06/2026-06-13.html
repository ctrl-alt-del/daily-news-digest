LLM4RTL: Tool-Assisted LLM for RTL Generation
LLM4RTL：面向 RTL 生成的工具辅助 LLM
RTL generation Verilog VerilogEval dataset curation
LLM4RTL proposes a judge-renew-check-renew-check pipeline that uses a hierarchy of commercial LLMs to filter and refine public Verilog/RTL samples into a higher-quality training set, then adds pre-processing tools that help models infer logical relations from tabular data. It reports gains on VerilogEval and performance comparable to GPT-4O with a much smaller model.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2606.15474 arXiv ✓
Who Drifted: the System or the Judge? Anytime-Valid Attribution in LLM Evaluation Pipelines
Who Drifted：是系统还是评审？LLM 评估流水线中的随时有效归因
LLM evaluation judge drift statistical testing monitoring
The paper introduces a fixed human-labeled anchor set, a second betting e-process and a guard-window rule to decide whether an LLM evaluation score moved because the system changed or because the judge did. It reports correct attribution of a silent judge version bump in 60 of 60 runs and of a strict-prompt change in 110 of 120 runs, while the rolling z-test false-alarmed on 75% of drift-free streams.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2606.15479 arXiv ✓
Bayesian 3D Steerable CNNs: Enabling Equivariance and Uncertainty Quantification Simultaneously
贝叶斯 3D 可导向 CNN：同时实现等变性与不确定性量化
equivariant CNN Bayesian deep learning uncertainty quantification calibration
The authors put posterior distributions over the basis coefficients of a 3D steerable CNN, keeping SE(3) equivariance exact while yielding epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty. The model reports an expected calibration error of 0.0263 and up to 6.17% better accuracy than its deterministic counterpart under additive Gaussian noise shift.
→ arxiv.org

Evaluative Judgement in Teaching AI-based Translation: A Class-room Case Study of AI-Mediated Translation and Post-Editing
AI 翻译教学中的评价性判断：AI 辅助翻译与译后编辑的课堂案例研究
machine translation post-editing translation teaching adequacy evaluation
Using 23 anonymized student projects from a fourth-year machine translation and post-editing course, the study examines how comparing general-purpose LLMs with online MT systems builds evaluative judgement. Students often chose outputs that diverged from automatic metric rankings, justifying them on adequacy, fluency, terminology, naturalness and expected post-editing effort.
→ arxiv.org

ST-DiffEye: Diffusion-based Continuous Gaze Generation via Joint Scanpath-Trajectory Modeling
ST-DiffEye：联合扫描路径与轨迹建模的扩散式连续注视生成
gaze modeling diffusion models scanpath CRPS
ST-DiffEye models gaze as a stochastic process that jointly captures continuous eye-tracking trajectories and discrete scanpaths, adding both as an extra input channel without architectural overhead. ToolMenuBench：面向可靠高效 LLM 智能体的工具菜单过滤策略基准
LLM agents tool selection benchmark risk exposure
ToolMenuBench evaluates how tool-menu construction shapes multi-step LLM agents, varying menu size, distractor type, task structure and risk exposure. AthDGC：带印欧语平行语料的开放历时希腊语树库
