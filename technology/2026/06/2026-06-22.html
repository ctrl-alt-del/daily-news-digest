Fast and Slow Variational Continual Learning
快慢结合的变分持续学习
continual learning optimizer variational inference LLM fine-tuning
Researchers propose Continual IVON (CoVON), an optimizer that implements fast and slow adaptation within the variational continual learning framework by merging past posteriors and using the merged result as a prior, reporting gains over existing VCL optimizers and weight-regularization strategies in domain-incremental learning, continual pre-training and LLM fine-tuning.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2606.23989 arXiv ✓
Attributable by Construction: Claim-Anchored Provenance for Multi-Document Summarization
构造即归因：多文档摘要的声明锚定溯源
multi-document summarization attribution provenance annotation dataset
CAMS decomposes source documents into atomic claims tied to verbatim quotes and token spans, clusters equivalent claims and flags conflicts, then rewrites a selected subset so that every summary sentence carries claim identifiers resolving to source spans. The authors report multi-source attribution accuracy rising from 38% to 64%, a 3.4x cut in human verification time per claim, and the release of about 320K claim-quote-span annotations.
→ arxiv.org

Learning to Trigger: Reinforcement Learning at the Large Hadron Collider
学习触发：大型强子对撞机上的强化学习
reinforcement learning Large Hadron Collider trigger system high-energy physics
Researchers cast online trigger-threshold tuning at the Large Hadron Collider as a sequential decision problem, adapting Group-Filtered Policy Optimization so an agent adjusts thresholds to keep background rates in tolerance while maximizing signal efficiency. On real collision data they report the first demonstration of RL-based trigger control at the LHC, with in-tolerance time improving 56% for the total transverse energy (HT) trigger and 28% for an anomaly-detection trigger.
