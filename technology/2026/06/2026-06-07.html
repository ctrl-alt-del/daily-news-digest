RKSC: Reasoning-Aware KV Cache Sharing and Confident Early Exit for Multi-Step LLM Inference
RKSC：面向多步 LLM 推理的推理感知 KV Cache 共享与置信早退
KV cache LLM inference early exit vLLM
RKSC is a training-free inference framework that removes two structural redundancies in multi-branch LLM reasoning: it shares a prefix KV cache across semantically similar branches and gates early exit on generation confidence or per-layer entropy, while bounding cache growth. Across five 7B-10B model families and 1,000 problems, the authors report a mean 3.008x speedup over a no-KV baseline with an early-exit error rate of 0.37%.
→ arxiv.org

sGPO: Trading Inference FLOPs for Training Efficiency in RLVR
sGPO：在 RLVR 中以推理 FLOPs 换取训练效率
RLVR reinforcement learning compute efficiency policy optimization
The paper proposes sorted Group Policy Optimization, which spends a small batch of parallel samples under the initial policy to estimate each query's difficulty, then sets rollout group size to the inverse success rate and uses the same pass to filter data and build a curriculum. It matches or exceeds baselines while cutting total training compute by a factor of three, including the profiling cost.
