Right Knowledge, Wrong Answer: Characterizing Parametric Temporal Conflict in Open-Weight Language Models
知识正确，答案错误：刻画开放权重语言模型中的参数化时间冲突
parametric knowledge temporal conflict activation patching open-weight models
The authors introduce Parametric Temporal Conflict, a setting where a model encodes both an outdated fact and its newer replacement but the default forward pass prefers the outdated one, and release a benchmark of 8,746 verified Wikidata position-holder transitions. Date-prefix prompts recovered the newer fact in 61–81% of cases, and activation patching flipped predictions in 72–85%, pointing to a localized representational preference rather than missing knowledge.
