Reflective VLA: In-Context Action Consequences Make VLAs Generalize
Reflective VLA：引入动作后果上下文，提升 VLA 泛化能力
VLA embodied AI robot learning generalization
Reflective VLA conditions each robot decision on a context of observation-action-consequence triplets, so the policy sees not only what was observed and executed but how the scene changed afterward, rather than relying on a single observation. It keeps in-distribution performance on LIBERO and SimplerEnv-Bridge and improves average success rate by 5.4 and 4.2 percentage points over a matched reactive baseline on LIBERO-Plus and LIBERO-Plus-Hard.

ASAP：以实际耗时为中心的自动超参优化智能体-系统协同设计
hyperparameter optimization LLM agents KV cache AutoML

Structuring Sparsity: Block-Sparse Featurizers Capture Visual Concept Manifolds
结构化稀疏：块稀疏特征器捕捉视觉概念流形
block sparsity interpretability visual representations diffusion models
The paper argues that block sparsity, which groups directions into blocks, matches a model in which a representation is a sparse sum of low-dimensional manifolds, and implements three block-sparse featurizers. A minimum-description-length analysis shows all three describe activations more compactly than direction-based featurizers, with recovered concepts typically two- to four-dimensional; the authors use them to reinterpret InceptionV1 curve detectors, find shadow and lighting manifolds in DINOv3, and steer image generation in SDXL.

基准饱和之后：CORE-Bench 案例研究
