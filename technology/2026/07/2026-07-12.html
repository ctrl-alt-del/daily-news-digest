Edge Physical AI Deployment of Vision Transformers on Heterogeneous Edge GPU Targeting Autonomous Vehicles
面向自动驾驶的异构边缘 GPU 视觉 Transformer 物理 AI 部署
edge AI deployment vision transformer autonomous driving NVIDIA DLA frame scheduling
The paper presents Heterogeneous Frame Dispatch Scheduling (H-FraDS), which routes frames across the GPU and two DLA cores at fixed dispatch ratios and adapts accelerator-incompatible transformer components, including tensor reshaping, a tanh approximation of ERF and bounded tanh layer normalization. MiniMax arxiv:2607.10936 arXiv ✓
Bandit PCA with Minimax Optimal Regret
具有极小极大最优遗憾的 Bandit PCA
online learning principal component analysis bandit feedback regret bounds quantum tomography
The paper studies bandit-feedback online principal component analysis, where an adversary selects a rank-r symmetric gain matrix with spectrum in [0,1] each round and the learner chooses a unit vector, and establishes a minimax regret of order r times the square root of dT up to polylogarithmic factors, improving on both prior bounds. The upper bound comes from an algorithm combining online mirror descent on the density-matrix spectrahedron with multiscale exploration, and the lower bound from an adaptive adversary.
→ arxiv.org
MiniMax arxiv:2607.10959 arXiv ✓
WSqD: A Horizon-Free Learning Rate Schedule for Large Model Training
WSqD：面向大模型训练的免视界学习率调度
learning rate schedule LLM pretraining optimization training efficiency
WSqD replaces the constant stable phase of warmup-stable-decay with a shifted inverse-square-root base schedule while keeping a final linear cooldown, making the base schedule horizon-independent so the horizon is needed only to decide when cooldown begins. It is proved to attain the minimax-optimal O(1/sqrt(T)) last-iterate rate in the stochastic convex setting, and in SlimPajama language-model pretraining it matches or outperforms tuned WSD and other baselines across horizons while reusing a single peak learning rate.
→ arxiv.org

ChipVerilog：基于 OpenCores 的大规模 LLM Verilog RTL 生成基准
Verilog RTL generation LLM code generation benchmark construction equivalence checking
ChipVerilog is a description-to-Verilog benchmark built from OpenCores IP and core designs, containing 64 generation targets across OR1200, a double-precision FPU, MIPS-16, I2C and CORDIC, including cross-module targets and several exceeding 1,000 lines of Verilog. arxiv:2607.10930 arXiv ✓
The Singularity Space: A Generative Diffusion Framework for Signal Representation
奇点空间：一种用于信号表示的生成式扩散框架
diffusion models signal representation inverse problems pole-residue representation scientific signals
Singularity Space represents signals through complex-plane pole-residue singularities instead of dense amplitude grids, learning a latent space of physically constrained per-signal singularity configurations with a transformer-based diffusion model that predicts singularity coordinates subject to geometric constraints during sampling. On 1D Burgers shocks, 32 predicted singularities per shock replace a 1024-point grid, preserving structure under unseen observation noise and reporting 4.2x lower reconstruction error than a grid-based baseline in zero-shot sub-resolution generalization.
→ arxiv.org

ARMOR-IMC: Adaptive Resource Mapping for Operational Robustness via Secure In-Memory Computing
ARMOR-IMC：通过安全存内计算实现运行鲁棒性的自适应资源映射
in-memory computing SOT-MTJ devices side-channel attacks process variation DNN accelerators
The paper presents a post-training framework that hardens analog in-memory computing accelerators based on SOT-MTJs against process variation and power side-channel attacks simultaneously, using a Variation Impact Score to guide the mapping of Fault Observation Windows and a Leakage Per Inference metric to quantify input-dependent power variability. Implemented in the IMAC-Sim simulator, it is reported to restore near-baseline accuracy where process-variation faults degrade accuracy by more than 50%, while mitigating correlation-based power analysis.
