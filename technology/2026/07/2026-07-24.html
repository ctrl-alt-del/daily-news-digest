Discrepancy-Rounded Fair Bandits with Static and Time-Varying Exposure Floors
具有静态与时变曝光下限的差异舍入公平老虎机
fair bandits exposure constraints rounding algorithms regret bounds recommender systems
The paper formulates stochastic bandits with exact exposure floors as a rounding problem and proposes BDQ-UCB, a blockwise algorithm that satisfies every time-varying floor deterministically with regret governed by the nonmandatory budget rather than the horizon. It provides matching lower bounds and extends the approach to overlapping group floors using Beck-Fiala null-space rounding.

Not All LLM Reasoning is Visible in the Chain-of-Thought
并非所有大模型推理都能在思维链中看到
chain-of-thought AI safety interpretability filler tokens frontier models
The authors show that frontier models can use semantically irrelevant filler tokens to improve accuracy on synthetic reasoning tasks, with gains of up to 13 percentage points across 13 models. arxiv:2607.22926 arXiv ✓
SAGE: Safety-First Defense-in-Depth Guardrails for Verified Lifecycle Control of High-Impact Generative AI
SAGE：面向高影响力生成式 AI 生命周期受控验证的安全优先纵深防御护栏
AI safety guardrails lifecycle control formal verification risk management
SAGE is an authorization-separated architecture that gates release of high-impact generative AI on catastrophic-risk admissibility, combining signed release manifests, detectors, risk envelopes, output checking, audit chains, and rollback, with formal verification of authorization separation. A vendor-symmetric study of 840 calls across GPT, Claude, and Gemini snapshots found low harmful-compliance estimates, with variation arising mainly from benign utility and safe redirection.

arxiv:2607.22927 arXiv ✓
Hidden Boundary Motion in Transformer Optimization: Function-Space Orthogonalization of Affine Weight and Bias Updates
Transformer 优化中的隐藏边界运动：仿射权重与偏置更新的函数空间正交化
transformer optimization weight updates AdamW function space optimization dynamics
The paper shows that weight updates in affine layers contain a sample-independent component functionally indistinguishable from a bias update, termed boundary motion, and measures it dominating explicit bias updates in a four-layer Transformer trained on IMDb. A diagnostic optimizer, SBO-AdamW, improves validation accuracy in a single-seed experiment but suffers bias-coordinate drift, so the authors present it as evidence for the mechanism rather than a final optimizer.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2607.22947 arXiv ✓
Let AI Agents Translate Networks, Not Reason About Them
让 AI 智能体翻译网络，而非对其推理
network verification formal methods LLM agents root-cause analysis program translation
The authors argue that LLMs should be confined to translating network artifacts into formal logic while a solver handles reliable long-horizon reasoning, since such translation can be formally verified. They build TypoNet, which constructs and validates a symbolic model of an emulated production-scale WAN, and report faster, cheaper, and more reliable operational answers than an LLM, plus improved fault localization when used as a tool.
