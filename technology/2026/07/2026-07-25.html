论结果奖励下无偏且长度不变策略优化的不可能性
policy optimization GRPO reinforcement learning length bias reward design
The paper proves an impossibility theorem showing that, under standard outcome rewards and GRPO-style training, no length-based weighting scheme can be simultaneously gradient-unbiased and length-invariant. GRPO and Dr. GRPO occupy opposite ends of this tradeoff, which the authors characterize with the family f_alpha(L) = L^(alpha-1).

自信地犯错：前沿大模型规则评估中的异常链崩塌
neuro-symbolic AI rule evaluation LLM reliability regulatory compliance SMT solving

UltraViT：面向大型视觉语言模型的延迟优化端侧视觉编码器

VClare: Resolving Imperfect Specifications in LLM-Based Verilog Generation
VClare：解决基于大模型的 Verilog 生成中的不完美规格
Verilog generation hardware design LLM code generation specification repair electronic design automation
The paper studies how vague, contradictory, or incomplete natural-language specifications degrade LLM-generated Verilog and proposes VClare, which repairs specifications either at the text level through LLM-driven inconsistency mining or at the simulation level through behavioral clustering. On two new benchmarks with injected specification defects, repair raises the pass rate by 12.7% for single-module tasks and 13.7% for multi-module tasks.
