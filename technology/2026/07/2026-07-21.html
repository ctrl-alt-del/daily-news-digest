Scaling Laws for Hypernetwork-Based Knowledge Injection in Large Language Models
基于超网络的大语言模型知识注入缩放定律
hypernetworks knowledge injection scaling laws LoRA adapters out-of-distribution generalization
The paper studies train-time factual knowledge injection by training a hypernetwork to generate a fixed LoRA adapter that lets the target model answer questions about a large fact corpus. The Mechanism Matters: When Knowledge Graphs Help Reinforcement Learning
机制至关重要：知识图谱何时有助于强化学习
knowledge graphs reinforcement learning reward shaping action masking sample efficiency
A controlled study independently varies the RL task, the knowledge-graph injection mechanism (state features, action masking, or potential-based reward shaping), and graph quality in MiniGrid environments. It finds that structural KG guidance improves sample efficiency and solve reliability on compositional sparse-reward tasks, while hard action masking is brittle when the graph is incomplete or corrupted.

From Bit-Position Sensitivity to Unequal Error Protection for DNN Inference Memory
从比特位敏感性到 DNN 推理内存的不等错误保护
fault tolerance error-correcting codes DNN accelerators bit sensitivity energy efficiency
The authors characterize per-bit-position fault sensitivity across 16 ML workloads and three floating-point formats, finding that low-order fraction bits are largely inconsequential while bits at the exponent-mantissa boundary cause catastrophic collapse. They derive per-data-type safe-bit floors and use them to design an unequal error protection codec that reduces ECC area and read energy.

HypEMBER: Hypernetwork-based Ensemble for Robust Policy Learning of Parametrized Dynamical Systems
HypEMBER：面向参数化动力系统鲁棒策略学习的超网络集成方法
reinforcement learning hypernetworks ensemble learning robust control uncertainty quantification
HypEMBER combines hypernetworks with ensembling so that policy and value functions are conditioned on a system's physical parameters, enabling generalization across dynamical regimes. The ensemble quantifies epistemic uncertainty, and experiments on the Kuramoto-Sivashinsky equation and a gyre-flow navigation task show improved training stability and robustness to measurement noise and parameter misspecification.
