Compositional Generalization via Structural Identification in a Category-Theoretic Framework
范畴论框架下通过结构辨识实现组合泛化
compositional generalization category theory COGS benchmark Kan extension
Represents sentences as functors from syntactic addresses to lexical tokens and uses selective collapses that induce Kan extensions to ask which structural or lexical identifications make held-out COGS examples admissible given the structures observed in training. Across 21 COGS generalization types, admissibility follows distinct identification profiles while residual failures separate unsupported structural templates, all without training a predictive model.

Subgraph Filtering for Fair Graph Neural Networks
面向公平图神经网络的子图过滤
graph neural networks algorithmic fairness edge filtering message passing
Proposes SF-GNN, a lightweight architecture-agnostic framework that identifies bias-prone edges by combining sensitive homophily with structural propagation amplifiers such as hub participation and triadic closure, then applies stochastic edge filtering at each message-passing step while adding a statistical-parity regularizer with a warm-up schedule. Experiments on five benchmark datasets show consistent fairness improvements with competitive predictive performance, yielding a better fairness-accuracy trade-off than recent fairness-aware GNN baselines.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.26442 arXiv ✓
Don't Overthink, Don't Underthink: Toward Adaptive Reasoning in Agentic AI
别过度思考，也别思考不足：迈向智能体 AI 的自适应推理
adaptive reasoning agentic AI inference-time compute reasoning failure modes
Argues that fixed or pre-allocated reasoning controls are poorly suited to agentic systems, where reasoning demands evolve through planning, tool use, memory retrieval, and agent-to-agent interaction, and characterizes over-reasoning and under-reasoning as recurring failure modes of misallocated reasoning. Evaluating on MATH-500 and the GAIA public validation benchmark, the authors find over-reasoning cases incur higher computational cost without proportional accuracy gains, while under-reasoning cases are consistently associated with incorrect or incomplete solutions.

Vowel Signs Are Not Letters: A Pre-tokenization Ceiling on Multilingual Tokenizer Fertility
元音符号不是字母：多语言分词器“生育率”的预分词上限
tokenization multilingual NLP byte-level BPE abugida scripts tokenizer fairness
Formalizes a training-free lower bound on tokenizer fertility caused by the GPT-2-derived letters-only word regex, which splits abugida words at every combining vowel sign so that BPE merges can never rejoin them regardless of vocabulary size or corpus mix. Across 26 languages all 17 abugidas are affected (1.47× for Tibetan to 9.02× for Thai), three matched 268M models show the fixed tokenizer gaining 4.43% in held-out Nepali bits per byte, and a census finds the pattern in 63.3% of the most-downloaded text-generation models.
