物理信息神经网络 预条件优化 偏微分方程 二阶优化 科学计算
Introduces SS-ESOAP, which augments SOAP-style Kronecker-factored preconditioning with a scalar secant-energy correction adapted to Kronecker geometry plus an adaptive basis update and variance-state downscaling, aimed at ill-conditioned physics-informed neural network training. Across eight PDE benchmarks it attains the lowest final residual on six, and on Boussinesq reaches a 10^-5 residual in 4.1 hours where Adam does not within 14 hours.
