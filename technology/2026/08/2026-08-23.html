Q-Learning with Stable Infinite-Dimensional Linear Function Approximation
具有稳定无穷维线性函数逼近的 Q 学习
Q-learning function approximation stochastic approximation convergence guarantees
The paper develops a stable infinite-dimensional linear function approximation framework for Q-learning from a single Markovian behavior-policy trajectory, using reconstruction and compression operators whose nonexpansiveness yields a contractive latent Bellman map with a unique fixed point. It proposes two stochastic approximation algorithms with sup-norm convergence bounds whose leading term is of order O(n^{-1/2}), controlled through covering numbers of the latent space rather than its dimension.
