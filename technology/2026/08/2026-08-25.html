面向早期、原理图设计前的嵌入式系统设计：LLM 驱动、数据手册感知的硬件兼容性自动验证
hardware compatibility embedded systems datasheet understanding design automation LLM verification
Proposes an LLM-driven framework that builds a design graph from hardware datasheets and high-level connectivity descriptions, retrieves only the engineering properties required by explicit verification criteria, and generates deterministic scripts that check interface compatibility before schematic simulation or implementation. On seven embedded-system designs covering 34 datasheets it reports 97.5% compatibility-verification accuracy and an 8.6× reduction in input context size compared with upload-and-query workflows.

FLARE: Verifying MILP Reformulations with LLM-Based Theorem Proving
FLARE：基于 LLM 定理证明的 MILP 重构验证
MILP reformulation theorem proving Lean proof assistant optimization modeling formal verification
Introduces a constructive definition of MILP reformulation that can be formalized in Lean and machine-checked, plus FLARE, an LLM-based agent paired with the Lean proof assistant that verifies proposed reformulations against a reference formulation. On the new FormulationBench (20 problems and 109 formulations) FLARE reports 100% accuracy on the NP-hard subset and produces a machine-checkable certificate for every reformulation it accepts, while a cheaper LLM-only proxy, FLARE-NL, matches its accuracy without certificates.
→ arxiv.org
