Toppling the Hierarchy in Byte-level Language Modeling
推翻字节级语言建模中的层级结构
字节级语言模型 层级结构 字符操作 注意力机制 模型架构
Examines why byte-level models fail to perfectly manipulate characters, finding that the hierarchical byte-to-word-to-byte design itself limits character-level understanding, with pure byte-level models consistently outperforming hierarchical variants on character manipulation tasks. Layer ablations further indicate byte-level attention as the primary mechanism, framing a trade-off between computational efficiency and fine-grained character understanding.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.00474 arXiv ✓
Exploring Collaboration between a language and a non-language agent
探索语言智能体与非语言智能体的协作
多智能体协作 表征内化 语言模型 国际象棋 智能体编排
Introduces LLAMIA-Bench, six collaborative chess tasks spanning behavioral imitation, state assessment and natural-language explanation, to test whether verbalizing a non-language agent's continuous representations into text creates a bottleneck. It proposes latent state internalization, which projects those representations into the LLM token stream as learned state tokens; a 14B model trained this way matches or exceeds task specialists and frontier models including GPT-5.1 with tool access, and generalizes out of distribution.
→ arxiv.org

A hybrid quantum-classical neural network for learning to route
一种用于学习路径规划的量子-经典混合神经网络
量子神经网络 组合优化 车辆路径问题 模型压缩 神经组合优化
Asks whether small quantum neural networks can replace parameter-heavy modules inside a competitive attention-based routing model for the capacitated vehicle routing problem. Encoder feed-forward replacement reduces model parameters by 56.6% while staying close to the classical neural baseline at small and medium instance sizes, though the gap grows for larger instances; the authors report no quantum advantage or solver dominance.

Context Window Failures in Relational Foundation Models
关系型基础模型中的上下文窗口失效
关系型深度学习 基础模型 上下文窗口 高基数数据 表格数据
Introduces Animus, a synthetic financial dataset in which predicting customer income requires aggregating up to tens of thousands of transactions, exposing the constrained neighborhood budgets of relational foundation models. Three recent models achieve R^2 of at most 0.18 on the raw representation, while a single routine temporal pre-aggregation step recovers R^2 up to 0.65, questioning readiness for high-cardinality real-world data.

Does Reasoning Mitigate Backdoor Attacks? A Neuro-Symbolic Perspective
推理能缓解后门攻击吗？一个神经符号视角
神经符号 AI 后门攻击 对抗鲁棒性 可信 AI 符号推理
Provides the first systematic evaluation of backdoor attacks against neuro-symbolic models, comparing DeepProbLog with baseline neural networks across eight backdoor settings and four reasoning tasks.
