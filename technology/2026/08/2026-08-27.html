Load-Bearing Context: The Question Damage Score for Evaluating Context Reliance in Linguistic Reasoning
承重上下文：用于评估语言推理中上下文依赖的问题损伤分数
context reliance LLM evaluation abstention linguistic reasoning memorization
Introduces a diagnostic framework that deletes single context examples from 53 UK Linguistics Olympiad puzzles, either uniformly at random or targeted at a structurally load-bearing example, and scores the impact with a Question Damage Score that classifies puzzles as fragile or robust. Evaluating three frontier LLMs under instructions to abstain when information is insufficient, the authors find the models rarely abstain and often still produce correct answers after load-bearing context is removed.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.27757 arXiv ✓
Beyond Search-Imitation: Prior-Directed Exploration for Searchless Chess
超越搜索模仿：无搜索国际象棋中先验引导的探索
searchless chess reinforcement learning exploration KL regularization self-play
Fine-tunes a searchless chess network with self-play reinforcement learning, replacing the usual entropy bonus with a forward, mass-covering KL divergence toward the network's own MCTS prior, paired with an entropy-adaptive sampling temperature set by the value head's outcome uncertainty. In about two thousand steps it raises puzzle accuracy from 93.9% to 94.9% and mate-in-four accuracy from 77% to 81% while holding searchless strength at or slightly above the base, and a matched-compute sweep shows tactical accuracy and playing strength dissociate.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.27760 arXiv ✓
Informational Antilocality and the Locality Bias in LLMs
信息反局部性与大语言模型中的局部性偏置
locality bias transformers formal languages learnability
Constructs k-antilocal languages, which carry no mutual information across any span of k contiguous symbols, and trains transformer language models on them for increasing k. The models reach comparable cross-entropy loss regardless of antilocality but converge more slowly on more antilocal languages, supporting the view that non-local dependencies are harder to learn while locating the evidence in learning speed rather than learning success.

Memorization Is Not Extraction: Tight Differential-Privacy Bounds and Audit Blind Spots
记忆不等于提取：紧致的差分隐私界与审计盲区
differential privacy memorization extraction attacks machine unlearning model auditing
Pins down exact differential-privacy constants for counterfactual memorization and adaptive extraction under f-DP, showing that the two measures do not control each other and that DP uniformly controls extraction only up to a threshold in how well the secret can be guessed a priori, with min-entropy certifying that baseline distribution-free. It further derives a closed-form staircase bound for duplicated copies and constructs mechanisms that are either memorized yet unextractable or fully extractable yet invisible to loss-based scores, exposing a two-sided blind spot for loss-based auditing and unlearning verification.

Compositional Failure in Audio-Visual LLMs: Late-Layer Prior Dominance Under Cross-modal Conflict
音视频大模型的组合性失败：跨模态冲突下的后层先验主导
audio-visual LLMs compositional generalization cross-modal conflict mechanistic interpretability
Uses audio-visual conflict as a compositional generalization test, finding that VideoLLaMA 2-7B-AV stays near chance on the scored exact-string Yes/No subset of AVHBench across three alignment configurations, while off-the-shelf InternVideo2 shows a 32.3% accuracy decrease and a 17.3% instruction-following failure specifically under cross-modal conflict. A mechanistic analysis attributes this prior dominance to late-layer commitment around 25.5 ± 1 layers, and stronger temporal alignment changes answer bias without improving conflict resolution.

What Can Low Resource Languages Learn From Each Other?
低资源语言之间能相互学到什么？
low-resource languages optical character recognition multilingual adaptation task arithmetic model merging
Investigates OCR adaptation in extreme data-scarce regimes (under 10K real and under 250K synthetic images), observing that conventional fine-tuning reaches a ceiling and that specialized models diverge mainly in higher layers while lower layers learn redundant, highly similar features. The proposed PSMC framework derives language-specific experts from a high-resource base model and fuses them with task arithmetic, reporting roughly a 2% average Word Recognition Rate improvement across 10 Indian scripts (20+ languages) over individual specialists without increasing parameter count.
