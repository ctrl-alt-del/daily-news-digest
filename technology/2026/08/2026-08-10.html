Toward a Theory of Value in AI Alignment
走向 AI 对齐中的价值理论
AI value alignment theory of value preference-based alignment automated evaluation
The authors annotate 94 value alignment papers to surface the implicit theory of values the field relies on, finding that most do not define values and instead substitute preferences, which risks collapsing culturally situated concepts into binary choices. They argue that the shift toward synthetic data and automated raters may foreclose alternative ways of contesting and enacting values in foundation models, and call for making these philosophical commitments explicit.

Space as an Interventional Invariant: Cross-Modal Predictive Geometry for Stratified Cities and Em-Spaced Intelligence
空间作为干预不变量：面向分层城市与具身空间智能的跨模态预测几何
spatial representation causal identifiability cross-modal geometry urban systems modeling embodied intelligence
The paper defines space as an interventional invariant — the minimal relational structure preserved under admissible actions — and builds a cross-modal predictive geometry combining local state spaces, modality-specific observation maps, an action groupoid, and a predictive-state quotient. Its main result shows identifiability of the latent space up to the centraliser of the intervention group, and the framework is extended to stratified urban systems via sheaf-valued representations and tested in synthetic noisy experiments.

arxiv:2608.10291 arXiv ✓
MRIComp4Flow: Compression of 3D Brain MRI for Training Multi-Modal Generative Models
MRIComp4Flow：面向多模态生成模型训练的三维脑部 MRI 压缩
medical image compression 3D generative models flow matching brain MRI storage and I/O cost
The study asks whether standard image codecs can compress brain tumor MRI volumes while still supporting training of a 3D generative model, compressing each volume with JPEG2000 or a near-lossless JPEG-LS pipeline before training a Wavelet Flow Matching model conditioned on BraTS sequences. At a 20:1 compression ratio, synthesis quality was statistically equivalent to training on uncompressed data within a pre-specified margin, supporting JPEG2000 as a practical route to scalable 3D MRI generative modeling.

Site Is Decodable Before Pretraining: Negative Controls for Probing Frozen Brain-MRI Foundation Models
站点信息在预训练之前即可解码：探测冻结脑部 MRI 基础模型的阴性对照

Probing three frozen 3-D brain-MRI models across two multi-site cohorts shows that acquisition site is far more decodable than any clinical or demographic target, and that untrained models and even 12x12x12 raw voxel inputs predict site nearly as well. Cracks in the Foundation: Seemingly Minor Architectural Choices Impact Long Context Extension
地基中的裂缝：看似微小的架构选择会影响长上下文扩展
long context extension transformer architecture pretraining ablations grouped-query attention sliding window attention
The authors show that four seemingly minor architectural choices — each used by at least one of the Olmo, Llama, or Qwen families — compound to hurt long context extension, with three or more combined dropping downstream performance by up to 47%, an effect invisible in short-context loss.
