arxiv:2608.16733 arXiv ✓
GoalEvolve: From Handcrafted Algorithm Priors to Goal-Driven Evolution of Physical Design Algorithms
GoalEvolve：从手工算法先验到目标驱动的物理设计算法进化
物理设计 LLM 布局布线 OpenROAD
GoalEvolve is a goal-driven framework that makes physical design algorithm evolution accountable for the final quality of results: it converts unmet requirements into normalized target gaps, identifies the dominant bottleneck stage, and uses an LLM-based Teacher with parallel Student agents to validate hypotheses through full-flow evaluation. Across eight ASAP7 designs it improves post-route TNS by 30.67% on average and reduces leakage power by 21.18% versus default OpenROAD.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.18170 arXiv ✓
CTTE: An Open Dual-Protocol RISC-V Trace Encoder for N-Trace and E-Trace
CTTE：支持 N-Trace 与 E-Trace 的开源双协议 RISC-V 追踪编码器
RISC-V 追踪编码器 开源硬件 形式化验证
CTTE is an open SystemVerilog trace encoder with a protocol-agnostic front end and selectable N-Trace/Nexus or E-Trace back ends, released under CERN-OHL-S-2.0. It has been integrated with six RISC-V cores from five suppliers and evaluated on 64-bit Linux-booting systems; trace cost ranges from 0.21 to 4.90 bits per retired instruction depending on workload.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.17139 arXiv ✓
RENESIS: Energy-Aware Synthesis of Adiabatic Logic from Irreversible Netlists
RENESIS：从不可逆网表到绝热逻辑的能耗感知综合
电路综合 绝热逻辑 低功耗 开源
Renesis is an automated synthesis tool that maps ordinary irreversible netlists to verified, technology-mapped energy-recovery (adiabatic) circuits, using energy rather than area or delay as the optimization criterion and equivalence-checking every transformation. On a held-out set of 20 circuits, 15 of 19 are improved with a best-arm median of 0.91 of default energy; the tool, benchmarks, and run records are released open source.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.16336 arXiv ✓
Beyond Binary Priorities: Multi-Tier SLA Scheduling for Large Language Model Serving
超越二元优先级：LLM 服务的多级 SLA 调度
LLM 推理调度 SLA Llumnix
The authors extend Llumnix's LLM serving scheduler from a two-level priority model to an arbitrary number of tiers, evaluating the extension with the Vidur simulator under uniform, Gaussian, and enterprise priority distributions. Four priority tiers yield the best cost-effectiveness tradeoff, achieving prefill mean speedups of up to 8.3x and P99 speedups of up to 3.1x over INFaaS with cost-per-latency improvements of 46-68%.
