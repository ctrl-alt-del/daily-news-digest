GateTruth: Auditing the Rigor of RTL Design Benchmarks via Mutation Testing
GateTruth：用变异测试审计 RTL 设计基准的严谨性
RTL-design mutation-testing benchmark-auditing LLM
GateTruth applies mutation testing to audit whether RTL-design benchmark testbenches actually catch broken logic: it certified 46 of 60 testbenches in the author's own suite (killing at least 95% of injected mutants), while 72% of 46 auditable designs in the external RTLLM v2.0 benchmark fell below that floor and three scored 0%. Spec Sheets Are Not Kernels: An ISA- and Source-Level Audit of INT8 Availability on NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra
规格表不是内核：NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra INT8 可用性的 ISA 与源码级审计
NVIDIA Blackwell-B300 INT8 LLM-serving
This audit traces INT8 (W8A8) support on NVIDIA's Blackwell Ultra GPU (B300), whose spec sheets show a roughly 30:1 FP8-to-INT8 dense-compute ratio versus the 1:1 of H200 and B200, through the PTX ISA, CUTLASS, vLLM and SGLang. It finds a consistent layered withdrawal: PTX never exposes the fifth-generation tensor-core integer path (tcgen05.mma .i8) on sm_103a, CUTLASS skips INT8 UMMA generation for 103a, vLLM fails with a hard runtime error on the first INT8 forward pass, and SGLang's INT8 GEMM stops at Sm90 — leaving a nominally supported format effectively undeployable on B300 by default.

arxiv:2608.12500 arXiv ✓
Lonic: Algorithm-Hardware Co-Design for Energy-Efficient Fully Local Online SNN Training with INT4 Precision
Lonic：面向 INT4 精度全本地在线 SNN 训练的算法-硬件协同设计
SNN INT4 online-training co-design
Lonic is an algorithm-hardware co-design for energy-efficient fully local online supervised SNN training at INT4 precision, introducing reconfigurable multiplier-free integer PE arrays, a dual-optimization zero-gating strategy, temporal prefix-accelerated local learning dataflow and low-precision weight movement. Compared with Apple M4 and NVIDIA V100 GPUs it reports average energy-efficiency gains of 17.44x and 66.28x respectively (plus 15.95x over an ASIC TPU-like accelerator and 14.64x area efficiency over H2Learn), with code released on GitHub.

HBF Sucks? A Full-Stack Characterization of High-Bandwidth Flash for KV-Centric LLM Serving
HBF 行不行？面向 KV 密集型 LLM 服务的高带宽闪存全栈评测
HBF KV-cache LLM-serving storage
Using an extended TokenSim, four two-hour Qwen-Bailian production traces, five MoE/dense models and H100/B200 profiles, this study finds that swapping High-Bandwidth Flash (HBF) into an SSD-style Mooncake KV-offloading stack backfires: average end-to-end latency rises 2-5.5x and maximum SLO goodput falls 1.1-2.7x, since writes outnumber reads and the tier hits thermal limits. The authors conclude HBF earns its place in LLM serving only when used selectively with reuse-aware placement, write budgeting and thermal coordination.
