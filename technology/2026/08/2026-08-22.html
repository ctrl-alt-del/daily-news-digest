DeepMind arxiv:2608.22117 arXiv ✓
TANGO: Treating Tokens as Operators
TANGO：将 token 视为算子
Transformer architecture attention mechanisms gating language modeling
TANGO computes a nonlinear feature-wise gate at each source token, averages these gates per destination, and uses the average to modulate a linear projection of the destination, forming the diagonal core of a source-conditioned linear operator. At matched parameters and training data, full-prefix TANGO has the lowest mean test negative log-likelihood in all 16 settings, and a narrower three-set variant reaches 3.26 to 3.45 times its throughput.
→ arxiv.org

How Weight Encoding Affects Language Model Placement and Performance on the Apple Neural Engine
权重编码如何影响语言模型在 Apple Neural Engine 上的部署与性能
Apple Neural Engine weight compression Core ML quantization inference latency
Using the public Core ML deployment path, the study combines compiler device plans, synchronized memory-controller measurements, and compute-unit exclusion controls to examine how dense fp16, int8, and ternary weight encodings affect where language-model checkpoints execute. It finds encoding-dependent placement, with a smaller fp16 export running on the CPU while compressed counterparts show ANE activity, and reports a 1.9x reduction in warm forward latency for the int8 export.
→ arxiv.org
