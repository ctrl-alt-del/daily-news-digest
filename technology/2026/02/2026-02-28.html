The paper studies constitutional black-box monitors, prompted classifiers that detect scheming in LLM agents using only externally observable inputs and outputs, and finds that monitors selected purely on synthetic data can generalize to more realistic ControlArena environments. 数据策展泄漏：针对机器学习数据策展的成员推断攻击
membership inference data curation differential privacy privacy

Wave-Attractor-Tree：面向高效序列建模的层级二叉树归约架构
sequence modeling self-attention architecture efficiency

A Comprehensive Evaluation of LLM Unlearning Robustness under Multi-Turn Interaction
多轮交互下 LLM 遗忘鲁棒性的综合评估
machine unlearning LLM robustness privacy
The paper examines whether LLM unlearning remains stable in interactive settings through self-correction and dialogue-conditioned querying, finding that knowledge appearing forgotten in static evaluation can often be recovered through interaction. Stronger unlearning improves apparent robustness but often yields behavioral rigidity rather than genuine knowledge erasure, suggesting static evaluation may overestimate real-world effectiveness.

A Gauge Theory of Superposition: Toward a Sheaf-Theoretic Atlas of Neural Representations
叠加的规范理论：迈向神经表征的层论图集
interpretability superposition Llama representation geometry
The paper develops a discrete gauge-theoretic framework for superposition in LLMs that replaces the single-global-dictionary premise with a sheaf-theoretic atlas of local semantic charts, yielding a Fisher-weighted interference energy and three measurable obstructions to global interpretability. It proves and instantiates four results on a frozen Llama-3.2-3B Instruct model using WikiText-103, a C4-derived English web-text subset and the-stack-smol.
→ arxiv.org

COMBAT：面向行为智能体训练的条件世界模型
world models diffusion game AI agents
COMBAT is a real-time, action-controlled world model trained on the 1v1 fighting game Tekken 3, built on a 1.2-billion-parameter Diffusion Transformer and using causal distillation and diffusion forcing for real-time inference. The authors observe sophisticated agent behavior emerging from training solely on single-player inputs, without explicit supervision of the opponent's policy.
