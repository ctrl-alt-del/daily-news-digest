MentalBlackboard：用数学变换评估空间可视化能力
benchmark spatial reasoning vision-language models
The authors introduce MentalBlackboard, an open-ended benchmark for paper-folding and hole-punching tests that probes spatial visualization in vision-language models through prediction and planning tasks. Models struggled with symmetric transformations and rotations; Claude Opus 4.1 scored 10% on planning, while o3 reached 71.6% on a generalization task but only 25% on text-based prediction.
→ arxiv.org

LLMs Can Learn to Reason Via Off-Policy RL
LLM 可通过离策略强化学习学会推理

The authors propose OAPL, an off-policy RL algorithm that embraces the off-policy data produced by distributed training instead of correcting it. It outperforms GRPO with importance sampling on competition math and matches DeepCoder on LiveCodeBench with 3x fewer generations, tolerating lags of more than 400 gradient steps.
→ arxiv.org

The authors hypothesize that fixed “golden layers” in LLMs can achieve near-optimal knowledge-editing performance comparable to sample-wise optimal layers, and present empirical evidence plus Layer Gradient Analysis, a gradient-attribution method for finding them without repeated trial-and-error.
