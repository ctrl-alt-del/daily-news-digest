explainable AI feature attribution whitening benchmark
The paper studies whether data whitening, a common decorrelation preprocessing step, can reduce feature-attribution errors in explainable AI. VLM-Guided Iterative Refinement for Surgical Image Segmentation with Foundation Models
VLM 引导的基础模型手术图像分割迭代优化
surgical segmentation vision-language model robot-assisted surgery SAM3
The authors propose IR-SIS, an iterative refinement system for surgical image segmentation that accepts natural language descriptions, using a fine-tuned SAM3 for initial segmentation, a vision-language model to detect instruments and assess quality, and an agentic workflow to pick refinement strategies. Generalizing GNNs with Tokenized Mixture of Experts
用 Token 化混合专家提升 GNN 的泛化能力
graph neural networks mixture of experts robustness benchmark
The paper proposes STEM-GNN, a pretrain-then-finetune framework combining a mixture-of-experts encoder, a vector-quantized token interface and a Lipschitz-regularized head to improve graph neural network robustness under distribution shifts and perturbations. Across nine node, link and graph benchmarks, it reports a stronger three-way balance of clean-graph performance, robustness and stability.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.09268 arXiv ✓
Rethinking Global Text Conditioning in Diffusion Transformers
重新思考扩散 Transformer 中的全局文本条件
diffusion transformers text conditioning text-to-image training-free
The paper examines whether modulation-based text conditioning is necessary in diffusion transformers, finding that pooled text embeddings contribute little in conventional use and that attention alone is generally sufficient to propagate prompt information. It shows the pooled embedding can provide gains when used as guidance for controllable shifts, a training-free approach applicable to text-to-image and video generation and image editing.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.09263 arXiv ✓
Atlas: Enabling Cross-Vendor Authentication for IoT
Atlas：面向 IoT 的跨厂商认证
IoT security authentication X.509 mutual TLS
Atlas is a framework that extends the web public-key infrastructure to IoT by issuing X.509 certificates through vendor-operated ACME clients and vendor-controlled DNS namespaces, enabling mutual TLS across administrative domains without hardware changes. Prototyped on ESP32 and Raspberry Pi, it provisions certificates in under 6 seconds per device and adds about 17 ms of latency.
→ arxiv.org

测量交互中的包容性：面向人机协作学习的包容性分析
human-AI collaboration inclusion education discourse analysis
The paper introduces inclusion analytics, a discourse-based framework for studying inclusion as a dynamic interactional process in collaborative problem solving, along three dimensions: participation equity, affective climate and epistemic equity.
