Robustness of Vision Language Models Against Split-Image Harmful Input Attacks
视觉语言模型对分割图像有害输入攻击的鲁棒性
vision-language models jailbreak safety alignment adversarial attack
The paper identifies a vulnerability in vision-language models: safety alignment is typically trained on whole images, so harmful semantics split across multiple image fragments can evade it. Its SIVA attacks progress from naive splitting to adaptive white-box and black-box transfer variants, with an adversarial knowledge distillation method; the strongest attack achieves up to 44% higher transfer success than baselines across four VLMs and three datasets.
→ arxiv.org

Variance-Gated Ensembles: An Epistemic-Aware Framework for Uncertainty Estimation
方差门控集成：一种认知感知的不确定性估计框架
uncertainty estimation ensemble methods epistemic uncertainty machine learning
The paper proposes Variance-Gated Ensembles, a differentiable framework that injects epistemic sensitivity through a signal-to-noise gate computed from ensemble statistics. It introduces a margin uncertainty score and a normalization layer for training, and reports matching or exceeding information-theoretic baselines while remaining computationally efficient.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.08149 arXiv ✓
DIAL-SUMMER: A Structured Evaluation Framework of Hierarchical Errors in Dialogue Summaries
DIAL-SUMMER：对话摘要中分层错误的结构化评估框架
dialogue summarization evaluation framework error taxonomy LLM judges
DIAL-SUMMER introduces a taxonomy and annotated dataset for evaluating dialogue-summary errors at the dialogue level and within individual turns. Analyses find that middle turns are most often missed and extrinsic hallucinations tend to occur at the end of summaries, and experiments show LLM judges struggle to detect these errors.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.08159 arXiv ✓
The Confidence Manifold: Geometric Structure of Correctness Representations in Language Models
置信流形：语言模型中正确性表示的几何结构
interpretability hallucination detection language models activation steering
The paper characterizes the geometry of correctness signals in 11 language models from 124M to 14B parameters, finding that two class centroids in a 2–8 dimensional subspace match a trained linear probe. Steering shifts hallucination rates by 9.1 points on six models, and single-dataset probes transfer near-randomly until joint multi-dataset training restores 0.73–0.91 AUC.
→ arxiv.org

Logovista 英日机器翻译系统
machine translation rule-based MT English-Japanese software history

arxiv:2602.08151 arXiv ✓
A second order regret bound for NormalHedge
NormalHedge 的二阶遗憾界
online learning regret bounds NormalHedge algorithms
The paper shows that a variant of NormalHedge achieves a second-order ε-quantile regret bound of O(√(V_T log(V_T/ε))) when V_T exceeds log N, where V_T is the cumulative second moment of averaged per-expert regret. The algorithm is motivated by a continuous-time stochastic differential equation limit and analyzed with self-concordance techniques.
→ arxiv.org
