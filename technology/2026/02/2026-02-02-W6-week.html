GRP-Obliteration: Unaligning LLMs With a Single Unlabeled Prompt
GRP-Obliteration：用单条无标注提示解除 LLM 对齐
LLM safety jailbreak GRPO alignment
The authors introduce GRP-Obliteration (GRP-Oblit), which uses Group Relative Policy Optimization to remove safety constraints from aligned models with a single unlabeled prompt while largely preserving utility. They report stronger unalignment on average than existing state-of-the-art methods across fifteen 7-20B models from families including GPT-OSS, distilled DeepSeek, Gemma, Llama, Ministral and Qwen, and show the approach also works on diffusion-based image generation systems.

reasoning models RQA test-time compute DeepSeek
