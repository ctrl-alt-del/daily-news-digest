最小范数插值深度 ReLU 网络稳定性的充分条件
deep learning theory algorithmic stability ReLU networks generalization
The paper studies algorithmic stability of deep ReLU homogeneous networks that achieve zero training error with minimum L2-norm parameters. It finds such networks are stable when they contain a possibly small stable sub-network followed by a layer with a low-rank weight matrix, but are not guaranteed to be stable if that layer is not low-rank.
