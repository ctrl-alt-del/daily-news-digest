DeepSeek arxiv:2602.13517 arXiv ✓
Think Deep, Not Just Long: Measuring LLM Reasoning Effort via Deep-Thinking Tokens
深度思考，而非只是更长：用深度思考 token 衡量 LLM 推理投入
LLM reasoning chain-of-thought test-time compute DeepSeek
The paper argues that raw token counts are unreliable proxies for reasoning quality and proposes measuring "deep-thinking tokens" whose internal predictions undergo significant revision in deeper layers before convergence. Across AIME 24/25, HMMT 25 and GPQA-diamond with GPT-OSS, DeepSeek-R1 and Qwen3, the deep-thinking ratio correlates positively with accuracy, and the authors introduce Think@n, a test-time scaling strategy that matches or exceeds self-consistency while reducing inference cost through early rejection of unpromising generations.

$γ$-weakly $θ$-up-concavity: A Unified Framework for Non-Convex Optimization Beyond DR-Submodular and OSS Functions
γ-弱 θ-上凹性：超越 DR-子模与 OSS 函数的非凸优化统一框架
non-convex optimization submodular functions approximation guarantees
The authors introduce γ-weakly θ-up-concavity, a first-order condition that strictly generalizes both DR-submodular and one-sided smooth (OSS) functions, and show that such functions are upper-linearizable, yielding unified approximation guarantees and static and dynamic regret bounds. The framework recovers the optimal approximation coefficient for DR-submodular maximization and improves existing coefficients for OSS optimization over matroid constraints.

AI agents privacy web agents benchmark
