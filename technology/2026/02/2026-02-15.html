DeepMind arxiv:2602.14351 arXiv ✓
WIMLE: Uncertainty-Aware World Models with IMLE for Sample-Efficient Continuous Control
WIMLE：面向样本高效连续控制的不确定性感知世界模型
model-based RL world models uncertainty estimation sample efficiency humanoid
WIMLE extends Implicit Maximum Likelihood Estimation to model-based RL so that stochastic, multimodal world models can be learned without iterative sampling, with ensembles and latent sampling used to estimate predictive uncertainty and synthetic transitions weighted by confidence. Across 40 continuous-control tasks spanning DeepMind Control, MyoSuite and HumanoidBench, it reports better sample efficiency and competitive or stronger asymptotic performance than model-free and model-based baselines.
→ arxiv.org
DeepSeek arxiv:2603.09998 arXiv ✓
Automated evaluation of LLMs for effective machine translation of Mandarin Chinese to English
自动评估 LLM 在中译英机器翻译中的表现
machine translation LLM evaluation DeepSeek Mandarin Chinese GPT-4o
The paper applies an automated framework combining semantic and sentiment analysis to Mandarin-to-English translations from Google Translate, GPT-4, GPT-4o and DeepSeek across news articles and modern and classical literature. It finds the LLMs perform well on news translation but diverge on literary texts, with DeepSeek better preserving cultural subtleties and grammar while cultural details, classical references and figurative language remain open problems.
→ arxiv.org

面向鲁棒强化学习控制的共形信号时序逻辑：案例研究
reinforcement learning formal methods safety shield aerospace conformal prediction

arxiv:2602.14338 arXiv ✓
Train Less, Learn More: Adaptive Efficient Rollout Optimization for Group-Based Reinforcement Learning
少训练、多学习：面向组式强化学习的自适应高效 rollout 优化
GRPO reinforcement learning LLM post-training compute efficiency
The paper introduces Adaptive Efficient Rollout Optimization (AERO), an enhancement of GRPO that prunes rollouts selectively and maintains a Bayesian posterior to avoid zero-advantage groups in LLM post-training. Across Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B, Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, it reports about 48% less total training compute and roughly 45% shorter wall-clock time per step on average while matching or improving Pass@8 and Avg@8.

arxiv:2602.14344 arXiv ✓
Zero-Shot Instruction Following in RL via Structured LTL Representations
以结构化 LTL 表示实现强化学习中的零样本指令遵循
reinforcement learning linear temporal logic zero-shot generalization instruction following
The paper learns structured task representations for zero-shot instruction following in multi-task reinforcement learning by conditioning the policy on sequences of Boolean formulae built from a finite automaton of the task. A hierarchical neural architecture encodes the logical structure of the formulae, and an attention mechanism lets the policy reason about future subgoals, with experiments reporting strong generalization across complex environments.
