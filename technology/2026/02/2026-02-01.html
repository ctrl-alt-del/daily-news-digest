arxiv:2602.01459 arXiv ✓
Understanding vision transformer robustness through the lens of out-of-distribution detection
从分布外检测视角理解视觉 Transformer 的鲁棒性
vision transformer quantization out-of-distribution detection robustness
The authors investigate how quantized DeiT, DeiT3 and ViT models behave on out-of-distribution datasets, finding that 4-bit models trained on ImageNet-22k can degrade sharply, with DeiT3 dropping 17% from quantization error. OOD detection shows ImageNet-22k pretrained ViT and DeiT3 had average quantization deltas of 15.0% and 19.2% in AUPR-out, leading the authors to suggest large-scale pretraining may hinder low-bit robustness and that data augmentation may be more beneficial.
