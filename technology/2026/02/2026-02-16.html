BindCLIP：面向虚拟筛选的统一对比-生成表征学习框架

Closing the Distribution Gap in Adversarial Training for LLMs
弥合 LLM 对抗训练中的分布差距
adversarial training LLM robustness diffusion LLM
The authors argue that adversarial training for LLMs leaves models vulnerable to simple in-distribution attacks, such as rewording prompts in the past tense or translating them, because training does not cover the data distribution well. Their Distributional Adversarial Training uses diffusion LLMs to generate diverse high-likelihood prompt-response samples and reports substantially higher robustness than prior methods.
