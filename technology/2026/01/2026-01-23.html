超越结果验证：面向结构化推理的可验证过程奖励模型
process reward models reinforcement learning verifiable rewards medical evidence
The authors introduce Verifiable Process Reward Models (VPRMs), a reinforcement-learning framework in which deterministic, rule-based verifiers check intermediate reasoning steps instead of neural judges. 重述、奖励、重复：叙事理论指导的故事重述强化学习
reinforcement learning storytelling LLM post-training

SpecBridge: Bridging Mass Spectrometry and Molecular Representations via Cross-Modal Alignment
SpecBridge：通过跨模态对齐连接质谱与分子表示
mass spectrometry molecular representation cross-modal alignment foundation models
SpecBridge is an implicit alignment framework that fine-tunes a self-supervised spectral encoder (DreaMS) to project into the latent space of a frozen molecular foundation model (ChemBERTa), then retrieves candidates by cosine similarity. Across MassSpecGym, Spectraverse and MSnLib it improves top-1 retrieval accuracy by roughly 20-25% over strong neural baselines while keeping trainable parameters small, and the code is released on GitHub.

NewPINNs：用传统求解器为神经网络注入物理约束
physics-informed neural networks PDE solvers scientific computing
NewPINNs is a physics-informing framework that integrates conventional numerical solvers into the training loop and defines learning objectives through solver consistency rather than residual-based loss terms. The authors report it mitigates common physics-informed neural network failure modes such as optimization pathologies, loss-weighting sensitivity and poor performance in stiff or nonlinear regimes.

transformer FPGA particle physics jet tagging
JetFormer is an encoder-only Transformer for particle jet tagging at the Large Hadron Collider, designed for scenarios from high-accuracy offline analysis to ultra-low-latency online triggering. diffusion models uncertainty quantification inverse problems materials characterization
