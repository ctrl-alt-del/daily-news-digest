Medical SAM3：通用提示驱动的医学图像分割基础模型
medical imaging segmentation foundation model SAM3

arxiv:2601.11663 arXiv ✓
Activation Sensitivity as a Unifying Principle for Post-Training Quantization
以激活敏感度统一理解训练后量化
quantization LLM model compression post-training
The paper shows that activation-aware scaling, GPTQ-style error compensation, diagonal Fisher quantization, block reconstruction and Hadamard rotation all reduce to minimizing one loss-derived quadratic form governed by gradient-weighted activation sensitivity. It derives a sensitivity-weighted Cholesky method, SAQ-GPTQ, and reports simulations where it keeps over 99% top-channel saliency recall and cuts INT3/INT4 excess loss by roughly 3x versus unweighted GPTQ.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2601.10896 arXiv ✓
DialDefer: A Framework for Detecting and Mitigating LLM Dialogic Deference
DialDefer：检测并缓解大模型对话中的顺从偏差
LLM evaluation bias dialogue calibration
The paper documents dialogic deference: LLMs judge the same claim differently when it is framed as a statement to verify rather than attributed to a speaker, with a mean absolute shift of 15.9 percentage points across five models while accuracy moved less than 2 points. It introduces the DialDefer framework and a Dialogic Deference Score, and finds that mitigation can over-correct into skepticism.
→ arxiv.org

serverless AI security FaaS cloud security

Action Shapley：强化学习世界模型的数据选择指标
