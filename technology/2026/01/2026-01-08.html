Large Language Models Are Bad Dice Players: LLMs Struggle to Generate Random Numbers from Statistical Distributions
大语言模型不会掷骰子：LLM 难以按统计分布生成随机数
LLM random sampling probability distributions benchmark
The authors run a large-scale audit of native probabilistic sampling in 11 frontier LLMs across 15 distributions, finding a 7% median pass rate for batch generation and near-total failure for independent requests, where 10 of 11 models passed no distribution. They conclude that current LLMs lack a functional internal sampler and need external tools when statistical guarantees are required.
→ arxiv.org

面向胸部 X 光图像检索的多任务跨模态学习
BiomedCLIP chest X-ray medical imaging image retrieval contrastive learning
The authors fine-tune BiomedCLIP with a lightweight MLP projector head and a multi-task loss combining binary cross-entropy, supervised contrastive and CLIP objectives for chest X-ray image-text retrieval. They report more balanced, clinically meaningful performance than pretrained BiomedCLIP and general-purpose CLIP, with t-SNE plots showing clearer clustering of normal and abnormal cases.

含噪 LLM-as-a-Judge 评估的高效推断
