Layer-Parallel Training for Transformers
面向 Transformer 的层并行训练
transformer parallel training neural ODE scalability
The authors propose a multilevel, layer-parallel training method for transformers built on a neural ODE formulation, which parallelizes across layers and detects when gradient bias hurts convergence so training can fall back to serial or raise accuracy. Experiments on BERT, GPT-2, ViT and machine translation models show parallel speedups with accuracy comparable to serial pre-training.
