arxiv:2601.12178 arXiv ✓
Federated Learning for the Design of Parametric Insurance Indices under Heterogeneous Renewable Production Losses
异构可再生能源产量损失下参数化保险指数设计的联邦学习
federated learning parametric insurance renewable energy solar farms
The paper proposes a federated learning framework for calibrating parametric insurance indices when renewable energy producers face heterogeneous production losses, with producers modeling their losses locally using Tweedie generalized linear models. In an experiment with up to 121 solar farms in Germany, federated learning remained valid as the pool grew and was more than 250 times faster than an approximation-based method that became non-computable beyond 50 farms.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.11165 arXiv ✓
Assessing LLM Reliability on Temporally Recent Open-Domain Questions
评估大语言模型在时效性开放域问题上的可靠性
LLM evaluation benchmark open-source models paraphrase
The authors introduce RECOM, a benchmark of 15,000 recent Reddit questions from September 2025 paired with community-derived reference answers, and evaluate four open-source LLMs on it. They report a semantic-lexical paradox — over 99% cosine similarity despite under 8% BLEU-1 overlap — and find that model scale did not predict performance, with Mistral-7B outperforming GPT-OSS-20B across all metrics.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.11166 arXiv ✓
Small Updates, Big Doubts: Does Parameter-Efficient Fine-tuning Enhance Hallucination Detection ?
小改动，大疑问：参数高效微调能提升幻觉检测吗？
PEFT hallucination detection LLM uncertainty
The study examines how parameter-efficient fine-tuning affects hallucination detection across three open-weight LLM backbones and three fact-seeking QA benchmarks, using seven unsupervised detectors. It reports that PEFT consistently improves detection, raising AUROC across a wide range of detectors, and that the effect comes mainly from reshaping how uncertainty is encoded rather than from adding factual knowledge.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2601.12179 arXiv ✓
Tolerance Principle and Small Language Model Learning
容忍原则与小语言模型学习
language acquisition BabyBERTa transformer cognitive science
The authors trained BabyBERTa, a transformer optimized for small datasets, on artificial grammars that varied in size and in the proportion of rule-following versus exception examples, in order to test Yang's Tolerance Principle. They found that, unlike human infants, BabyBERTa's learning dynamics do not align with the principle.
→ arxiv.org

VeRVE: Versatile Retrieval for Videos via Unified Embeddings
VeRVE：通过统一嵌入实现通用视频检索
video retrieval multimodal LLM LoRA embeddings
VeRVE is a multimodal-LLM-based video retrieval framework that handles corpus-level retrieval, moment localization, and composed multimodal queries within a single architecture. Its embedding model, trained with LoRA on 700K paired visual-text samples, beats other MLLM-based methods on zero-shot video retrieval, and with reranking it approaches specialized state-of-the-art systems.
