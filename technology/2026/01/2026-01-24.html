稀疏子空间—专家共享：面向任务无关的持续学习
continual learning catastrophic forgetting mixture of experts LoRA
SETA addresses catastrophic forgetting in continual LLM learning by decomposing parameters into task-specific and shared sparse expert modules, sharing parameters only when a new task passes subspace-agreement and sign tests and protecting shared knowledge with adaptive elastic anchoring. The authors report gains of 1.1 to 1.7 points on TRACE and up to 5.5 points on O-LoRA over the strongest baseline, with less forgetting than MoE-CL and LoRA baselines.

Conduit: Programmer-Transparent Near-Data Processing Using Multiple Compute-Capable Resources in Solid State Drives
Conduit：利用固态硬盘多种计算资源的程序员透明近数据处理
