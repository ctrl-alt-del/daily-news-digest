面向奶牛场高效电力负载调度的预测感知深度强化学习

Semantic Gravity Wells: Why Negative Constraints Backfire
语义引力井：负面约束为何适得其反
negative constraints instruction following mechanistic interpretability LLM
The paper investigates why negative constraints fail in LLMs, introducing “semantic pressure” and finding that violation probability follows a logistic relationship with it across 40,000 samples. It attributes failures to priming, where naming a forbidden word activates it, and override, where late-layer feed-forward contributions overwhelm suppression, with layers 23–27 found to be causally responsible.

MemoBrain：作为推理代理大脑的执行记忆
agent memory long-horizon reasoning tool use context management
MemoBrain is an executive memory model for tool-augmented agents that builds a dependency-aware memory over reasoning steps, pruning invalid steps and folding completed sub-trajectories to keep a compact reasoning backbone within a fixed context budget. It reported consistent improvements over strong baselines on GAIA, WebWalker and BrowseComp-Plus.

Limits of n-gram Style Control for LLMs via Logit-Space Injection
Logit 空间注入下 LLM n-gram 风格控制的局限
style control logit manipulation n-gram LLM
The paper tests steering a frozen LLM with n-gram style priors injected in logit space at decoding time, sweeping the control parameter and style corpora on TinyLlama-1.1B. It finds a single narrow regime (Don Quixote, lambda=0.1) where style and base-model perplexity improve, while other settings degrade style and fluency, and concludes the approach is outperformed by prompting and LoRA.
