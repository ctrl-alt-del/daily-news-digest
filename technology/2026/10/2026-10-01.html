Jev 式类型化决策模型中标签压过定义

The paper studies open-weight typed decision models and finds that predictions mostly follow option labels rather than the written definitions, an effect it calls option-label bias. Removing all definitions left accuracy roughly unchanged, while renaming options to A and B raised it by 0.1511, and the authors trace the behavior to prompt rendering.
