目标图像即控制目标：面向机器人操作的空间锚定目标图像合成
robotic manipulation goal-image synthesis CALVIN FLUX.1 Kontext image editing

It introduces SGE-Goal, which predicts initial and final edit-region masks before synthesis, and reports roughly seven times the success rate of the best proprietary editor on five-step chains.
