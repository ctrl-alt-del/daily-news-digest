multiple-choice evaluation selection bias order sensitivity LLM benchmarking
The paper links selection bias and order sensitivity in multiple-choice evaluation through a pooled standard deviation of option selection rates, which it shows factors into a flip rate and a positional skew. Across 41 checkpoints and 12 benchmarks, prior correction mainly reduces positional skew while aggregation mainly reduces flip rate, and the authors recommend reporting both alongside accuracy.
