尺寸泛化的无维缩放定律：指数级分离
scaling laws size generalization sample complexity minimax theory
The paper develops a statistical theory of size generalization for models on variable-size inputs such as sets and graphs, proving a sharp exponential separation in sample complexity between in-domain learning and transfer across input sizes. ViMoLa: Discrete Motion Tokens for Language-Guided Video Generation
ViMoLa：用于语言引导视频生成的离散运动 token
