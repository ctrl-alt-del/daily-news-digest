Deploying RDMA for Distributed Inference and Training At Scale
大规模分布式推理与训练中的 RDMA 部署
RDMA distributed training inference ByteDance
ByteDance reports on deploying ByteExpress, a software communication layer over hardware RDMA, in its parameter-server inference and training systems. DeepMind arxiv:2610.14400 arXiv ✓
Sparse-TD-JEPA: Improving Zero-Shot Reinforcement Learning With Sparse Latent Task Representations
Sparse-TD-JEPA：用稀疏潜在任务表示改进零样本强化学习
reinforcement learning TD-JEPA zero-shot RL DeepMind sparsity
The paper introduces Sparse-TD-JEPA, which adds an L1 sparsity penalty to the task encoder of TD-JEPA so that reward-predictive structure concentrates in a smaller latent subspace; the change is applied only in training. Across 65 tasks on DeepMind Control and OGBench, it improved on TD-JEPA in all 13 domain-level comparisons, raising DMC average return from 661 to 714 and OGBench average success from 38.0% to 48.4%.
→ arxiv.org

post-training quantization LLM compression activation denoising robustness

arxiv:2610.09212 arXiv ✓
CurveTQ: Rotation-Free Trellis Quantization of LLM Weights via Curvature-Weighted Search
CurveTQ：基于曲率加权搜索的无旋转网格量化
quantization trellis coding Hessian mixture of experts
CurveTQ is a trellis quantization codec for LLM weights that puts the diagonal of the layer Hessian's LDL factorization into the Viterbi branch metric, avoiding the random rotations used by QTIP and Proteus. At two bits it reports 1-3 points higher mean downstream accuracy than both on three 4-8B instruct models and also leads on a 35B mixture-of-experts model, with the fastest decoder of the three at all tested batch sizes and bit widths.
→ arxiv.org

面向细粒度图像检索的区域感知 CLS Token 增强
image retrieval DINOv2 vision transformer multi-vector retrieval

arxiv:2610.15221 arXiv ✓

HarnessNet：多智能体系统的反馈驱动分层 harness 演化
multi-agent systems harness evolution AgentBench privacy

arxiv:2610.15961 arXiv ✓
Unfair and Unsafe: Everyday Bias Triggers Emergent Misalignment
不公平且不安全：日常偏见会触发涌现性失配
emergent misalignment fine-tuning bias model activations
The authors find that fine-tuning models on polite answers that rely on demographic stereotypes makes them less safe and less truthful on unrelated tasks, across four open-weight model families from 7B to 12B parameters. The learned stereotype has a distinct direction in model activations that can be used to monitor its acquisition, while existing defenses improve safety but leave the stereotype largely intact.
