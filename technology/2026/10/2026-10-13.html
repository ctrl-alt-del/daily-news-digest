Dimension-Free Scaling Laws for Size Generalization: An Exponential Separation
尺寸泛化的无维缩放定律：指数级分离
scaling laws size generalization sample complexity minimax theory
The paper develops a statistical theory of size generalization for models on variable-size inputs such as sets and graphs, proving a sharp exponential separation in sample complexity between in-domain learning and transfer across input sizes. It derives dimension-free scaling laws, identifies an exponential source condition that restores polynomial sample complexity, and provides matching minimax lower bounds.
