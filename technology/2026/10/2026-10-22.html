Amplifying Latency and Cost of LLM Inference via Rowhammer Attack
利用 Rowhammer 攻击放大 LLM 推理延迟与成本
Rowhammer Triton LLM inference GPU bit flip
Researchers present LLVMhammer, a Rowhammer attack that flips a bit in Triton's compilation pipeline so it produces a functionally identical but suboptimal GPU kernel, which is cached on disk and slows later runs. On NVIDIA RTX 4090 and AMD RX 7900 XTX GPUs it raised single-GPU inference latency by 4-16% and could also corrupt outputs.
