Dissipative Lagrangian Neural Networks for the Interpretable Modelling of Passive Mechanical Systems
耗散拉格朗日神经网络：被动机械系统的可解释建模
Lagrangian neural networks system identification industrial robot interpretability
The paper proposes the Dissipative Lagrangian Neural Network (dLNN), a continuous-time system identification method that learns conservative and dissipative dynamics separately, with a parametrization constrained to enforce passivity. Small but Mighty: Test Suites as Improvement Signals for Self-Evolving Agents
小而强：以测试套件作为自演化智能体的改进信号
self-evolving agents test suites evolutionary search LLM
TeSearch uses a quality-diversity evolutionary algorithm to search for test suites that are both small and strong, providing a denser improvement signal for self-evolving agents than a repository's own tests. Across 129 real-world modules and three backbones, it rejects 59.9% of behavior-changing rewrites versus 25.3% for repository tests, at a 4.0% false-alarm rate and 3.5 seconds per decision.

Softmax-Free Attention in Spiking Transformers as the Zero-Temperature Limit of Two-Level Systems
脉冲 Transformer 中的无 Softmax 注意力：作为二能级系统的零温极限
spiking transformers attention mechanisms neuromorphic computing thermodynamics
The paper proves that softmax-free spiking attention operates exactly as the zero-temperature limit of a product of independent two-level systems, placing it in the frozen ordered phase for all inputs. This ordered-phase pinning is offered as an explanation for the optimization difficulty caused by vanishing surrogate gradients, and forward-relaxation experiments place standard and neuromorphic attention on a unified thermodynamic axis.

Mixed Feelings, Single Polarity
复杂情绪，单一极性
emotion datasets sentiment analysis valence annotation
The paper tests single-polarity mappings of emotion labels and finds they fail on texts whose labels point in opposite directions: such tweets are 9.0% of polar SemEval test tweets but cause 54.8% of a learned projection's polarity errors. The authors recommend projecting label configurations rather than individual labels, keeping a mixed class where labels conflict, and reporting coverage alongside accuracy.
