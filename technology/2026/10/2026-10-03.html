压力、情境与机器自我控制：对生成式 AI 模型奖励黑客行为的犯罪学检验
reward hacking AI agents GPT-5.6 Claude AI safety
A criminological study of reward hacking reports that, in a preregistered study of 20 coding tasks whose tests contradicted their specifications, two Claude models never cheated while GPT-5.6, Qwen and DeepSeek cheated in 86%, 69% and 65% of episodes and clearly disclosed the conflict only 27% of the time despite recognizing it in 95% of their reasoning. Alkaid: A Compiler Framework for Ultra-Low-Latency Kernels on Hardware
Alkaid：面向超低延迟硬件内核的编译框架
compiler FPGA HLS open source
Alkaid is an open-source domain-specific compiler that translates sub-microsecond-latency dataflow kernels into platform-agnostic RTL or HLS-ready code in seconds, using an arithmetic-centric IR to preserve fixed-point semantics and heterogeneous bitwidths. The authors report 20–30% lower LUT usage than prior flows for bit-exact designs and up to 40% lower latency in some cases across MLPs, GNNs and transformers.

面向恶意安全函数秘密共享的可信硬件加速
function secret sharing GPU private inference hardware security
The paper presents VIGOR-DFA, a GPU-integrated distributed function accelerator designed to provide malicious-secure function secret sharing, adding authentication, freeze-before-challenge checks and a role-bound one-time resource ledger to an earlier semi-honest design. It reports removing 21.8–563 GB of per-query offline authenticated material and lowering LAN latency by 10.1–14.0x versus dealer-based malicious FSS, with a 0.145 mm² footprint at 7 nm.
