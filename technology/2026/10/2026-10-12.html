The paper presents Resolve--Express--Act (REA), a framework for following safety-guard judgments through parsing and fallback decisions to the actions an application ultimately takes. Structure-preserving fidelity diagnostics for partial differential equations
偏微分方程的结构保持保真度诊断
PDE surrogates scientific machine learning numerical methods fidelity diagnostics
The paper proposes structure-preserving fidelity diagnostics that test whether a learned PDE surrogate honors known physics — conserved quantities, symmetries or Hamiltonian structure — without needing the training data or the governing equation. In tests over five systems, the scores followed the true-dynamics error with a rank correlation as high as 0.94, whereas test loss was uncorrelated or inversely correlated with it.

arxiv:2610.17466 arXiv ✓
Efficient Fréchet Distributional Decoder Alignment for Latent Generative Models
面向潜变量生成模型的高效 Fréchet 分布解码器对齐
generative models Fréchet distance decoder alignment post-training
The paper proposes Fréchet Distributional Decoder Alignment (FDDA), which leaves the latent generator frozen and post-trains the decoder on latents produced at generation time with a Fréchet distributional objective. Across 11 models, FDDA reduced gFDr^6 by 34.0–59.6% and delivered a further 10.2–57.2% reduction after generator-side FD post-training, while cutting wall-clock time by 3.6× to 328.7× relative to generator-side post-training.
