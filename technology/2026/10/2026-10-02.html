对 LLM 风险偏好中自我报告与行为一致性的表征控制

Using activation steering across four open-weight LLMs, the authors find that self-report and risk-taking behavior are moved by near-orthogonal directions: trait-description vectors shift self-report but leave behavior at chance, while directions derived from the model's own choices on the task show the opposite pattern.
