压缩方法很重要：LLM 提示压缩中随基准而变的输出动态

NerVE: Nonlinear Eigenspectrum Dynamics in LLM Feed-Forward Networks
NerVE：LLM 前馈网络中的非线性特征谱动态
LLM feed-forward networks eigenspectrum interpretability
NerVE is a lightweight framework for tracking eigenspectrum dynamics in LLM feed-forward networks through four metrics: spectral entropy, participation ratio, eigenvalue early enrichment, and Jensen-Shannon divergence. The authors find that FFN nonlinearities reinject variance across eigenmodes, that optimizer geometry modulates this effect, and that the resulting spectral signatures correlate with generalization and extend to MLP-Mixer architectures.
