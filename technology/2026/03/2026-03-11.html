FedACT: Concurrent Federated Intelligence across Heterogeneous Data Sources
FedACT：跨异构数据源的并发联邦智能
federated learning device scheduling participation fairness distributed systems
FedACT is a resource-heterogeneity-aware device scheduling approach for multiple concurrent federated learning jobs, using alignment scoring and participation fairness to minimize average job completion time. Experiments report up to 8.3x lower average JCT and 44.5% higher model accuracy than state-of-the-art baselines.
→ arxiv.org

DriveXQA：面向恶劣驾驶场景理解的跨模态视觉问答
autonomous driving visual question answering multimodal LLM sensor fusion
The authors introduce DriveXQA, an autonomous-driving multimodal VQA dataset with 102,505 QA pairs spanning four visual modalities, five sensor failure cases and five weather conditions. They also design MVX-LLM, a token-efficient architecture whose Dual Cross-Attention projector improves performance in hard conditions, scoring 53.5 GPTScore in fog versus 25.1 for the baseline.

TimeSqueeze: Dynamic Patching for Efficient Time Series Forecasting
TimeSqueeze：面向高效时间序列预测的动态分块
time series forecasting tokenization state space models
TimeSqueeze is a dynamic patching mechanism for time series transformers that gives short patches to information-dense regions and long patches to smooth segments, using a lightweight state-space encoder for point-wise features. The authors report up to 20x faster convergence and 8x higher data efficiency in large-scale pretraining versus point-token baselines.

Teleodynamic Learning：可解释 AI 的新范式
interpretability learning dynamics information geometry self-organization
The authors propose Teleodynamic Learning, which frames learning as a constrained dynamical process in which representation, parameters and resources co-evolve, and instantiate it in DE11. On standard benchmarks DE11 reports 93.3% accuracy on IRIS, 92.6% on WINE and 94.7% on Breast Cancer while producing interpretable logical rules.
