The paper shows that invertible logic can be implemented with simple spiking neural networks based on stochastic computing, and presents a CMOS design methodology for invertible stochastic gates. From Actions to Understanding: Conformal Interpretability of Temporal Concepts in LLM Agents
从行动到理解：LLM 智能体时序概念的一致性可解释性
LLM agents interpretability conformal prediction reward modeling
The paper introduces a conformal interpretability framework that combines step-wise reward modeling with conformal prediction to label an LLM agent's internal representations as successful or failing at each step. Linear probes on ScienceWorld and AlfWorld show the temporal concepts are linearly separable, and preliminary results suggest steering along successful directions can improve performance.

RealBirdID: Benchmarking Bird Species Identification in the Era of MLLMs
RealBirdID：MLLM 时代的鸟类物种识别基准
benchmark multimodal LLM fine-grained recognition abstention
RealBirdID asks systems to either identify a bird species or abstain with an evidence-based rationale, pairing curated unanswerable examples with answerable ones. Unsupervised Behavioral Compression: Learning Low-Dimensional Policy Manifolds through State-Occupancy Matching
无监督行为压缩：通过状态占用匹配学习低维策略流形
reinforcement learning policy compression state occupancy
The authors propose Occupancy-based Policy Compression, which replaces immediate action matching with long-horizon state-space coverage to compress reinforcement learning policy parameter spaces. The method uses an information-theoretic uniqueness metric for dataset generation and a differentiable objective that minimizes divergence between occupancy distributions, validated on continuous-control benchmarks.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.27048 arXiv ✓
MOOZY: A Patient-First Foundation Model for Computational Pathology
MOOZY：面向计算病理学的患者优先基础模型
computational pathology foundation model whole-slide imaging medical AI
MOOZY is a pathology foundation model that treats the patient case, not individual slides, as the unit of representation and uses a case transformer to model dependencies across slides. Pretrained on 77,134 public slide feature grids and supervised over 333 tasks from 56 datasets, it reports gains over PRISM on 16 held-out tasks with 85.77M parameters, 14x smaller than GigaPath.
→ arxiv.org

speech corpus low-resource languages Whisper Pashto

Fine-tuning Whisper Base on MCV20 reached 13.4% WER on its test split, against a published 99.0% zero-shot WER for Whisper Base on Pashto.
