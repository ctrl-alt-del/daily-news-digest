arxiv:2603.20537 arXiv ✓
From Language Models to Control Policies: Search, Selection, and Verification
从语言模型到控制策略：搜索、选择与验证
LLM industrial control program synthesis formal verification safety
The paper studies LLM-driven synthesis of interpretable Python control policies for hot steel rolling, using simulator feedback to propose and refine controllers. Across 52 independent runs, moderate-length searches matched the hindsight-optimal budget allocation, a per-job portfolio outperformed the best single heuristic, and an audit pipeline combining static analysis, Z3 proofs, and property-based testing proved safety properties for all 174 synthesized heuristics.
→ arxiv.org

Understanding Behavior Cloning with Action Quantization
理解动作量化下的行为克隆
behavior cloning action quantization robotics sample complexity
The paper provides theoretical foundations for behavior cloning with quantized actions, analyzing how quantization error propagates over the horizon and interacts with sample complexity. It shows that behavior cloning with log-loss achieves optimal sample complexity under stable dynamics and a probabilistic smoothness condition, and proposes a model-based augmentation that improves the error bound.
→ arxiv.org

Towards Practical Multimodal Hospital Outbreak Detection
迈向实用的多模态医院疫情暴发检测
outbreak detection machine learning public health electronic health records
The paper explores MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry, antimicrobial resistance patterns, and electronic health records as rapid alternatives to whole-genome sequencing for hospital outbreak detection, using a machine learning approach that integrates these modalities. It also proposes a tiered surveillance paradigm and identifies potentially high-risk contamination routes tied to clinical procedures.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.22332 arXiv ✓
Large Language Models for Missing Data Imputation: Understanding Behavior, Hallucination Effects, and Control Mechanisms
大语言模型用于缺失数据插补：行为、幻觉效应与控制机制
LLM data imputation benchmark tabular data
A benchmarking study compares five LLMs with six imputation baselines across 29 datasets under MCAR, MAR, and MNAR mechanisms at missing rates up to 20%. Leading LLMs, particularly Gemini 3.0 Flash and Claude 4.5 Sonnet, performed better on real-world open-source datasets, while traditional methods such as MICE outperformed them on synthetic data, and LLMs came with significantly higher computational time and monetary costs.

当否定成为视觉语言模型中的几何问题
CLIP vision-language models negation representation engineering
The paper examines why CLIP-style vision-language embedding models fail at negation, critiques retrieval-based evaluation, and uses multimodal LLM judges to assess negation understanding. It finds evidence of a negation direction in CLIP embedding space that can be manipulated at test time through representation engineering without fine-tuning.

DeEscalWild：基于小模型开展自动缓和冲突训练的真实世界基准
benchmark small language models Qwen law enforcement dataset

Permutation-Consensus Listwise Judging for Robust Factuality Evaluation
面向稳健事实性评估的排列共识列表评判
LLM-as-judge factuality evaluation RewardBench
The paper introduces PCFJudge, an inference-time method that reruns a factuality-first listwise prompt over multiple candidate orderings and aggregates scores, ranks, and uncertainty into one consensus decision. On RewardBench 2 Factuality, the seven-permutation aggregate improved top-1 accuracy from 86.00% to 91.33% with GPT-5.4 and from 86.33% to 89.67% with Claude Sonnet 4.6.
