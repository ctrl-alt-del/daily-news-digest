Widespread Gender and Pronoun Bias in Moral Judgments Across LLMs
大语言模型道德判断中普遍存在性别与代词偏倚
LLM bias fairness ETHICS dataset
A controlled study built 14,850 counterfactual sentences from the ETHICS dataset and found statistically significant biases across six model families (Grok, GPT, LLaMA, Gemma, DeepSeek and Mistral), with singular third-person statements more often judged fair, second-person statements penalized, and gender markers producing the strongest effects. The authors conjecture that the patterns reflect distributional and alignment biases learned during training.
