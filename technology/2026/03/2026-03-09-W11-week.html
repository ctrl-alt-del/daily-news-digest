An accurate flatness measure to estimate the generalization performance of CNN models
一种精确的平坦度度量：评估 CNN 模型的泛化性能
CNN generalization Hessian flatness
The authors derive a closed-form expression for the Hessian trace of the cross-entropy loss with respect to convolutional kernels in CNNs that use global average pooling followed by a linear classifier, then specialize relative flatness to convolutional layers. Experiments on standard image-classification benchmarks suggest the measure can help assess generalization and guide architecture and training choices.
