MiniMax arxiv:2604.07658 arXiv ✓
Optimal Decay Spectra for Linear Recurrences
线性循环模型的最优衰减谱
linear recurrent models Mamba-2 long-context retrieval MiniMax
The paper traces the weak long-range memory of linear recurrent models to their decay spectra and introduces PoST, an architecture-agnostic framework that enforces geometrically spaced log-decay rates and adapts the spectrum to the actual dependency range. Tested at 180M-440M scale on Mamba-2, RWKV-7, RetNet, Gated DeltaNet and Gated Linear Attention, it reports zero-shot language-modeling improvements and stronger long-context retrieval for Mamba-2.
→ arxiv.org

VSAS-Bench: Real-Time Evaluation of Visual Streaming Assistant Models
VSAS-Bench：视觉流式助手模型的实时评测基准
VSAS-Bench vision-language models streaming benchmark Apple
Apple researchers propose VSAS-Bench, a benchmark for visual streaming assistants built on more than 18,000 temporally dense annotations, with synchronous and asynchronous protocols that also measure proactiveness and consistency. They report that ordinary VLMs can be adapted to streaming without extra training and can outperform dedicated streaming models, with Qwen3-VL-4B beating Dispider by 3% under the asynchronous protocol.
→ arxiv.org

The xPU-athalon: Quantifying the Competition of AI Acceleration
xPU 大竞技：量化 AI 加速器的竞争
AI accelerators Cerebras TPU energy efficiency GPU
The paper benchmarks AI accelerators — Cerebras CS-3, SambaNova SN-40, Groq, Gaudi and TPUv5e — against NVIDIA A100/H100 and AMD MI-300X GPUs on latency, throughput, power and energy efficiency. It finds the best platform varies with batch size, sequence length and model size, and that Cerebras, SambaNova and Gaudi draw 10–60% more idle power than NVIDIA and AMD GPUs.

相机视角选择中的覆盖优化
active view selection 3D reconstruction Nerfstudio Fisher information

arxiv:2604.07650 arXiv ✓
A Statistical Framework for Auditing Behavioral Dependence and Induced Bias in LLM Judges
审计 LLM 评判器行为依赖与诱导偏差的统计框架
LLM-as-a-judge model entanglement evaluation bias
The authors introduce a statistical framework for auditing hidden behavioral entanglement among black-box LLMs, which can arise from shared pretraining data, distillation or alignment and undermine LLM-as-a-judge and ensemble verification setups that assume independent signals. Across 18 models from six families they find significant entanglement associated with judge over-endorsement bias, and show that de-entangled verifier reweighting beats majority voting in accuracy and precision.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.08810 arXiv ✓
R2G: A Multi-View Circuit Graph Benchmark Suite from RTL to GDSII
R2G：从 RTL 到 GDSII 的多视图电路图基准套件
EDA graph neural networks benchmark RTL-to-GDSII
R2G standardizes five stage-aware circuit-graph views with information parity across 30 open-source IP cores and supplies an end-to-end DEF-to-graph pipeline, loaders, splits and baselines.
