Improving Feasibility via Fast Autoencoder-Based Projections
基于快速自编码器投影提升可行性
constrained optimization autoencoder feasibility projection reinforcement learning
The authors propose a data-driven amortized approach that trains an autoencoder with an adversarial objective to serve as an approximate projector, correcting infeasible neural network predictions by projecting their latent representations onto a simple convex shape before decoding. Tests on constrained optimization and reinforcement learning problems with nonconvex constraints show effective constraint enforcement at low computational cost.

Cultural Authenticity: Comparing LLM Cultural Representations to Native Human Expectations
文化真实性：比较 LLM 文化表征与本土人类期望
cultural alignment LLM evaluation Gemini 2.5 Pro GPT-4o Claude 3.5 Haiku
The paper introduces a human-centered framework that compares model-derived Cultural Representation Vectors with human-derived Cultural Importance Vectors built from open-ended survey responses across nine countries, applying it to Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Haiku. It finds Western-centric calibration in some models, with alignment declining as a country's cultural distance from the US increases, and highly correlated systemic error signatures (ρ>0.97) across all models.

Resource-Conscious Modeling for Next- Day Discharge Prediction Using Clinical Notes
基于临床笔记的次日出院预测资源节约型建模
clinical NLP discharge prediction LoRA LightGBM
The study compares 13 models for predicting next-day discharge from postoperative clinical notes in elective spine surgery, including TF-IDF with XGBoost or LightGBM and compact LLMs such as DistilGPT-2 and Bio_ClinicalBERT fine-tuned with LoRA. TF-IDF with LightGBM gave the best balance, with an F1 of 0.47 for the discharge class, 0.51 recall and 0.80 AUC-ROC, while transformer-based and generative models underperformed overall.
