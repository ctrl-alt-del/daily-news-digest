Anthropic arxiv:2604.21131 arXiv ✓
Cross-Session Threats in AI Agents: Benchmark, Evaluation, and Algorithms
AI 智能体的跨会话威胁：基准、评估与算法
AI agent security cross-session attacks benchmark Anthropic
The paper argues that AI-agent guardrails judge each message in isolation, letting an adversary spread a single attack across many sessions so that only the aggregate carries the payload. It introduces CSTM-Bench, with 26 executable attack taxonomies and two 54-scenario splits, and reports that both a session-bound judge and a full-log correlator lose roughly half their attack recall on the cross-session split, while a bounded-memory Coreset Memory Reader keeps its recall on both.
→ arxiv.org
NVIDIA arxiv:2604.23466 arXiv ✓
Evaluating CUDA Tile for AI Workloads on Hopper and Blackwell GPUs
在 Hopper 和 Blackwell GPU 上评估 CUDA Tile 的 AI 工作负载表现
CUDA Tile NVIDIA Blackwell Triton FlashAttention-2 GPU kernels
An independent cross-architecture evaluation of NVIDIA's CUDA Tile finds that results depend heavily on workload and GPU: on B200 it reached up to 1007 TFLOP/s for fused attention, 2.5x FlashAttention-2 with 60 lines of Python, but the same kernel achieved only 53% of FlashAttention-2 throughput on RTX PRO 6000. By contrast, Triton delivered 62–101% of cuBLAS speed on every GPU tested, without tuning per architecture.

Dynamic Governance of Multi-LLM Agent Systems for Collaborative Conversational Outcomes
多 LLM Agent 系统的动态治理：优化协作对话结果

The paper examines two LLM agents with structurally opposed objectives in a simulated financial-services setting, where the absence of a shared goal function leads conversations to collapse rather than compete.
