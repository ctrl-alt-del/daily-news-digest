Don't Make the LLM Read the Graph: Make the Graph Think
别让 LLM 读图，让图去思考
belief graphs multi-agent reasoning Theory of Mind Hanabi
A study runs more than 3,000 controlled trials across four LLM families in the cooperative card game Hanabi to test whether explicit belief graphs improve multi-agent reasoning. It finds that graphs help mainly when they gate action selection rather than serve as prompt context, identifies a model-specific failure called "Planner Defiance," and reports that inter-agent conventions outperform single-agent interventions.

vision-language-action models reinforcement learning robot manipulation fine-tuning
Researchers introduce an "RL token," a compact readout representation from a pretrained vision-language-action model, and train a small actor-critic head on it to make online reinforcement learning sample-efficient. Across four real-robot tasks, the method improved speed on the hardest part of each task by up to 3x and raised success rates within minutes to a few hours of practice.
