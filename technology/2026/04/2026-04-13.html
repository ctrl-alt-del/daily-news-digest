Retrieval-Augmented Generation Must Move Beyond Factual Grounding to Represent Diverse Opinions
检索增强生成必须超越事实依据，以呈现多元观点
RAG opinion diversity uncertainty quantification Wasserstein distance
This position paper argues that RAG systems assume queries have correct answers, creating a factual bias that ignores aleatoric uncertainty in opinion-rich content and risks erasing minority voices or enabling opinion manipulation. The authors propose Opinion-Aware RAG, which adds LLM-extracted, entity-linked opinion metadata before indexing; it reduced Wasserstein distance to corpus sentiment distributions by 18–48% on two datasets, and human evaluators preferred its responses 79.2% of the time.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.12129 arXiv ✓
Aethon: A Reference-Based Replication Primitive for Constant-Time Instantiation of Stateful AI Agents
Aethon：面向有状态 AI 智能体的近常数时间实例化复制原语
AI agents copy-on-write agent infrastructure memory model
Aethon is a reference-based replication primitive that represents each stateful AI agent as a compositional view over stable definitions, layered memory and local context instead of materializing full objects. The authors say this decouples creation cost from inherited structure and supports lighter instantiation for multi-agent orchestration and enterprise governance.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.12119 arXiv ✓
Beyond Perception Errors: Semantic Fixation in Large Vision-Language Models
超越感知错误：大型视觉语言模型中的语义固着
vision-language models benchmark semantic fixation activation steering
The paper introduces semantic fixation, the tendency of vision-language models to retain a default interpretation even when a prompt specifies an alternative, equally valid mapping, and builds VLM-Fix, a benchmark over four abstract strategy games with paired standard and inverse rules. Across 14 open and closed VLMs, accuracy consistently favored standard rules; neutral alias prompts narrowed the inverse-rule gap while semantically loaded aliases reopened it, and late-layer activation steering partially recovered degraded performance.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.12113 arXiv ✓

PR-MaGIC：利用掩码解码器梯度流优化上下文分割提示
image segmentation SAM prompt refinement test-time adaptation
PR-MaGIC is a training-free test-time framework that refines prompts for in-context segmentation using gradient flow derived from SAM's mask decoder. A top-1 selection strategy stabilizes the method, and the authors report consistent segmentation gains across benchmarks without additional training or architectural changes.

arxiv:2604.12116 arXiv ✓
The A-R Behavioral Space: Execution-Level Profiling of Tool-Using Language Model Agents in Organizational Deployment
A-R 行为空间：组织部署中工具型语言模型智能体的执行层画像
LLM agents AI safety behavioral profiling autonomy
The study proposes an execution-layer measurement approach for tool-using LLM agents based on a two-dimensional A-R space of Action Rate and Refusal Signal, with Divergence capturing coordination between them. Evaluating models across four normative regimes and three autonomy configurations, it finds that execution and refusal are separable behavioral dimensions, and that reflection-based scaffolding often shifts behavior toward more refusal in risky contexts, with patterns varying by model.

arxiv:2604.12133 arXiv ✓
Towards Platonic Representation for Table Reasoning: A Foundation for Permutation-Invariant Retrieval
面向表格推理的柏拉图表示：排列不变检索的基础
table representation learning permutation invariance RAG CKA
The paper argues that linearizing tables into sequences discards geometric and relational structure, leaving representations brittle to layout permutations, and proposes permutation invariance as a requirement for robust table latents. Using CKA-based metrics, it shows that minor layout changes cause disproportionate shifts in LLM table embeddings, then presents a structure-aware encoder that enforces cell-header alignment and shows greater geometric stability.

arxiv:2604.12140 arXiv ✓
XANE(3): An E(3)-Equivariant Graph Neural Network for Accurate Prediction of XANES Spectra from Atomic Structures
XANE(3)：从原子结构精确预测 XANES 光谱的 E(3) 等变图神经网络
XANES graph neural network equivariance materials science
XANE(3) is an E(3)-equivariant graph neural network that predicts X-ray absorption near-edge structure spectra directly from atomic structures, trained with a composite objective matching spectral values and first and second derivatives. On 5,941 FDMNES simulations of iron oxide surface facets, it reaches a test mean squared error of 1.0 × 10⁻³ and reproduces the main edge structure, peak intensities, pre-edge features and post-edge oscillations.
→ arxiv.org

ViLL-E：面向检索的视频 LLM 嵌入
video retrieval VideoLLM embeddings contrastive learning
ViLL-E is a unified VideoLLM architecture with an embedding mechanism that can spend more computation on complex videos and stop early on easy ones, trained in three stages combining generative and contrastive learning. The authors report average gains of 7% in temporal localization and up to 4% in video retrieval over baselines, plus zero-shot improvements of 5% in composed video retrieval and 2% in retrieval from long text.
