Cross-Session Threats in AI Agents: Benchmark, Evaluation, and Algorithms
AI 智能体的跨会话威胁：基准、评估与算法
AI agent security cross-session attacks benchmark Anthropic
The paper argues that AI-agent guardrails judge each message in isolation, letting an adversary spread a single attack across many sessions so that only the aggregate carries the payload. It introduces CSTM-Bench, with 26 executable attack taxonomies and two 54-scenario splits, and reports that both a session-bound judge and a full-log correlator lose roughly half their attack recall on the cross-session split, while a bounded-memory Coreset Memory Reader keeps its recall on both.
