保护并保留抗议动态，以支持负责任的分析
synthetic data privacy protest analysis conditional image synthesis
The authors propose a responsible-computing framework that replaces sensitive protest imagery with labeled synthetic reproductions made by conditional image synthesis, so collective protest dynamics can be analyzed with lower privacy risk. They also assess demographic fairness in the generated data and describe the approach as harm-mitigating rather than offering absolute privacy guarantees.

The paper extends TabDDPM with lightweight temporal adapters, context-aware embedding modules, windowed sequences and timestep embeddings to generate temporally coherent synthetic time-series. 相机视角选择中的覆盖优化
active view selection 3D reconstruction Nerfstudio Fisher information
The authors introduce COVER, a lightweight coverage-based metric for active camera view selection that approximates Fisher Information Gain and avoids expensive transmittance estimation. Integrated into Nerfstudio, it improved reconstruction quality over state-of-the-art active view selection methods across real datasets in fixed and embodied settings.
