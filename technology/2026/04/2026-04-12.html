The xPU-athalon: Quantifying the Competition of AI Acceleration
xPU 大竞技：量化 AI 加速器的竞争
AI accelerators Cerebras TPU energy efficiency GPU
The paper benchmarks AI accelerators — Cerebras CS-3, SambaNova SN-40, Groq, Gaudi and TPUv5e — against NVIDIA A100/H100 and AMD MI-300X GPUs on latency, throughput, power and energy efficiency. It finds the best platform varies with batch size, sequence length and model size, and that Cerebras, SambaNova and Gaudi draw 10–60% more idle power than NVIDIA and AMD GPUs.

Task2vec Readiness: Diagnostics for Federated Learning from Pre-Training Embeddings
Task2Vec 就绪度：基于预训练嵌入的联邦学习诊断
federated learning Task2Vec embeddings diagnostics
The authors derive readiness indices from Task2Vec embeddings to gauge how a federated learning federation will perform before training, using cohesion, dispersion and density metrics computed from client embeddings. Across CIFAR-10, FEMNIST, PathMNIST and BloodMNIST with 10–20 clients, some indices correlate with final performance at values often above 0.9.

arxiv:2604.10837 arXiv ✓
Immune2V: Image Immunization Against Dual-Stream Image-to-Video Generation
Immune2V：抵御双流图生视频生成的图像免疫
image-to-video deepfakes adversarial immunization
Immune2V protects images from being animated by image-to-video models by enforcing temporally balanced latent divergence at the encoder and steering intermediate representations along a collapse-inducing trajectory. Experiments show stronger, more persistent degradation than adapted image-level baselines under the same imperceptibility budget.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.10827 arXiv ✓
Know Thy Reasoner: Not All Language Models Explore Alike
了解你的推理器：并非所有语言模型都以相同方式探索
LLM reasoning compute scaling exploration
The paper argues that the right compute-scaling strategy for LLM reasoning depends on a model's diversity profile and formalizes this by decomposing reasoning uncertainty. It finds that low-diversity aligned models benefit from depth-based refinement, while high-diversity base models are often harmed by it and need breadth or stronger signals.

Transformers Learn Latent Mixture Models In-Context via Mirror Descent
Transformer 借镜像下降在上下文中学习潜在混合模型
transformers in-context learning mirror descent attention
The paper frames token-importance estimation as in-context learning under a Mixture of Transition Distributions, with a latent variable setting how much past tokens influence the next. It shows transformers can implement Mirror Descent to learn the mixture weights, giving an explicit three-layer construction that is a first-order approximation of the Bayes-optimal predictor.

diffusion models sampling query lower bounds
The paper proves the first score query lower bounds for diffusion sampling, showing that d-dimensional distributions with polynomially accurate score estimates require on the order of √d adaptive score queries. The authors say this explains why multiscale noise schedules are necessary in practice.
