MiniMax arxiv:2604.07658 arXiv ✓
Optimal Decay Spectra for Linear Recurrences
线性循环模型的最优衰减谱
linear recurrent models Mamba-2 long-context retrieval MiniMax
The paper traces the weak long-range memory of linear recurrent models to their decay spectra and introduces PoST, an architecture-agnostic framework that enforces geometrically spaced log-decay rates and adapts the spectrum to the actual dependency range. Tested at 180M-440M scale on Mamba-2, RWKV-7, RetNet, Gated DeltaNet and Gated Linear Attention, it reports zero-shot language-modeling improvements and stronger long-context retrieval for Mamba-2.
