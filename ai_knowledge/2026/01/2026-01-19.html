knowledge graph antonym discrimination semantic drift synonym clustering polysemy resolution

Can LLMs Compress (and Decompress)? Evaluating Code Understanding and Execution via Invertibility
大语言模型会压缩（与解压）吗？通过可逆性评估代码理解与执行
code reasoning round-trip consistency self-reflection benchmark design
RoundTripCodeEval assesses code understanding through round-trip consistency across four lossless compression algorithms, using execution-free exact-match scoring of bijection fidelity. The authors report that models often pass forward and backward tasks separately yet fail the combined round trip, and that supervised fine-tuning and self-reflection saturate after one revision round without repairing fundamental misunderstandings.

semantic relations low-resource languages FastText embeddings dataset generation retrieval accuracy
The authors describe a three-phase protocol that combines FastText embeddings with agglomerative clustering, Gemini 2.5-Flash classification, and curated dictionaries to build a Turkish semantic-relations corpus. The resulting 843,000 unique pairs across synonyms, antonyms, and co-hyponyms represent a tenfold scale increase at about $65, and downstream embedding and classification models reach 90% top-1 retrieval accuracy and 90% F1-macro.

arxiv:2601.13300 arXiv ✓
OI-Bench: An Option Injection Benchmark for Evaluating LLM Susceptibility to Directive Interference
OI-Bench：评估大语言模型受指令干扰程度的选项注入基准
directive interference benchmark robustness multiple-choice QA social compliance
OI-Bench adds a misleading directive as an extra option to multiple-choice questions, creating 3,000 questions across knowledge, reasoning, and commonsense tasks with 16 directive types such as social compliance, bonus framing, threat framing, and instructional interference. Evaluating 12 LLMs reveals substantial and heterogeneous vulnerabilities, and the authors examine inference-time prompting and post-training alignment as mitigations.
