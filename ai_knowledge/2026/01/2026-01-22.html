Learning to Discover at Test Time
在测试时学习发现：TTT-Discover
test-time training reinforcement learning continual learning scientific discovery open models
TTT-Discover performs reinforcement learning at test time so an LLM can keep training on experience specific to the problem at hand rather than prompting a frozen model. Using the open gpt-oss-120b model, it reports new state-of-the-art results on Erdős's minimum overlap problem, an autocorrelation inequality, a GPUMode kernel competition, past AtCoder contests and a single-cell denoising problem, at a cost of a few hundred dollars per problem.

Design and Empirical Study of a Large Language Model-Based Multi-Agent Investment System for Chinese Public REITs
面向中国公募 REITs 的大语言模型多智能体投资系统设计与实证研究
multi-agent investment system LLM trading agents Chinese REITs backtesting
The paper builds a multi-agent LLM trading framework for Chinese public REITs in which announcement, event, price-momentum and market agents feed a prediction agent and a decision agent. In an October 2024–October 2025 backtest, both agent-based strategies outperformed buy-and-hold on cumulative return, Sharpe ratio and maximum drawdown.

Grounding Large Language Models in Reaction Knowledge Graphs for Synthesis Retrieval
将大语言模型锚定于反应知识图谱以进行合成路线检索
reaction knowledge graph Text2Cypher exemplar selection embedding-based retrieval synthesis planning
The authors cast reaction-path retrieval over a reaction knowledge graph as a Text2Cypher generation task and compare zero-shot prompting with one-shot variants using static, random and embedding-based exemplars. One-shot prompting with aligned exemplars performed best, while a checklist-driven self-correction loop mainly improved executability in zero-shot settings.

Inference-Time Diversity in RL-Trained Lean Theorem Provers: A Diagnostic Study
RL 训练 Lean 定理证明器的推理时多样性：一项诊断研究
inference-time diversity Lean theorem proving tactic skeletons mode collapse
The study finds that RL-trained Lean provers mode-collapse at inference time: for DeepSeek-Prover-V1.5-RL, doubling the sampling budget from k=32 to k=64 solved no additional miniF2F-test theorems. A fixed schedule of 15 tactic skeletons recovered a 45% relative improvement at k=16, while the SFT-trained Goedel-Prover did not benefit.

SPARC-RAG: Adaptive Sequential-Parallel Scaling with Context Management for Retrieval-Augmented Generation
SPARC-RAG：面向检索增强生成的自适应串行-并行扩展与上下文管理
retrieval-augmented generation multi-agent RAG context management inference-time scaling multi-hop QA
SPARC-RAG is a multi-agent RAG framework that coordinates sequential depth and parallel width under a shared context-management mechanism, generating complementary sub-queries and regulating when branches should exit. Across single- and multi-hop QA benchmarks it reports an average +6.2 F1 improvement over prior RAG baselines at lower inference cost.

arxiv:2603.02212 arXiv ✓
GLEAN: Grounded Lightweight Evaluation Anchors for Contamination-Aware Tabular Reasoning
GLEAN：面向污染感知表格推理的轻量级评测锚点
tabular reasoning contamination-aware evaluation error attribution retrieval diagnostics
GLEAN is a lightweight evaluation protocol for small tabular models that combines contamination-aware probes, retrieval-reasoning diagnostics and structured L0–L4 error attribution under a 16GB GPU budget. Using Squall gold SQL as an executable anchor, it finds distinct error profiles for TAPEX and TAPAS and shows that retrieval Recall@K can saturate while end-to-end accuracy remains limited.

Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning Under Constrained Communications
受限通信下的多智能体深度强化学习
