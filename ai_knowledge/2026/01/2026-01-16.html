POLARIS: Typed Planning and Governed Execution for Agentic AI in Back-Office Automation
POLARIS：后台办公自动化中 Agentic AI 的类型化规划与受治理执行
typed planning governed execution agent orchestration enterprise automation anomaly routing
POLARIS is a framework for controlling how LLM agents are orchestrated in enterprise back-office tasks, treating automation as the synthesis of typed plans that are then executed only after validation. It reports 0.81 micro F1 on SROIE and 0.95 to 1.00 precision for routing anomalies on a controlled synthetic suite, along with full execution traces.

arxiv:2601.11344 arXiv ✓
How Much Would a Clinician Edit This Draft? Evaluating LLM Alignment for Patient Message Response Drafting
临床医生会如何修改这份草稿？评估 LLM 在患者消息回复起草中的对齐
clinician alignment retrieval-augmented generation preference optimization evaluation framework patient messaging
The authors develop a taxonomy of thematic elements in clinician responses and an evaluation framework for assessing clinician editing load of LLM-drafted patient portal messages at content and theme levels. Testing thematic prompting, retrieval-augmented generation, supervised fine-tuning and direct preference optimization, they find substantial epistemic uncertainty in aligning drafts with individual clinicians, with particular difficulty in question asking to elicit further information.

MHA2MLA-VLM: Enabling DeepSeek's Economical Multi-Head Latent Attention across Vision-Language Models
MHA2MLA-VLM：将 DeepSeek 的经济型多头潜在注意力引入视觉语言模型
multi-head latent attention KV-cache compression vision-language models parameter-efficient fine-tuning
MHA2MLA-VLM is a parameter-efficient, multimodal-aware framework for converting off-the-shelf vision-language models to Multi-Head Latent Attention, using a modality-adaptive partial-RoPE strategy and modality-decoupled low-rank approximation of visual and textual KV spaces. Experiments on three representative VLMs show it restores original model performance with minimal supervised data while significantly reducing KV-cache footprint and integrating with KV quantization.

AstroAgentBench: Evaluating Agentic Planning on Space Mission Planning Tasks
AstroAgentBench：评估空间任务规划中的智能体规划能力
agentic planning executable verification task-contract formulation memory accumulation space mission planning
AstroAgentBench tests executable planning for space missions in seven task families covering scheduling, observation planning, constellation design and relay support, using an external checker for schema, timing, geometry, resources and mission value. On 35 held-out cases with five LLM agent systems, the best systems come close to or beat solver references in several families, though even the strong ones lose ground on geometric, product-level or design-heavy work.

Auditing Frozen-Encoder Anomaly Detection Across Mechanical Systems: Representation Provenance, Calibration, and Protocol Effects
审计机械系统中的冻结编码器异常检测：表征来源、校准与协议效应
representation provenance reproducibility checkpoint integrity anomaly detection calibration
This reproducibility audit of frozen-encoder anomaly-detection experiments finds the numerical discrimination results reproducible but withdraws the original causal claim that they demonstrate a morphological prior transferred from gravitational-wave instrumentation. The released checkpoint contains a nested model state, and embeddings labelled as interferometric have norms of order 10^-12 matching freshly initialized EfficientNet-B0 networks, leading the author to reinterpret the results as an architecture-and-initialization effect coupled to Mahalanobis scoring.
