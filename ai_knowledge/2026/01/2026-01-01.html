ClinicalReTrial: Clinical Trial Redesign with Self-Evolving Agents
ClinicalReTrial：基于自演化智能体的临床试验方案重设计
self-evolving agents hierarchical memory closed-loop optimization knowledge transfer clinical trial redesign
ClinicalReTrial treats trial protocol redesign as an iterative, reward-driven optimization problem, combining failure diagnosis, safety-aware edits and candidate evaluation in a closed loop, with hierarchical memory that distills transferable redesign patterns across trials. Under its simulation environment it turns 56.7% of failed protocols into predicted successes, with a mean success-probability gain of 7.4% at about $0.156 per trial.

An Empirical Evaluation of LLM-Based Approaches for Code Vulnerability Detection: RAG, SFT, and Dual-Agent Systems
大模型代码漏洞检测方法的实证评估：RAG、SFT 与双智能体系统
retrieval-augmented generation dual-agent system supervised fine-tuning MITRE CWE knowledge vulnerability detection
This comparative study evaluates RAG, supervised fine-tuning with QLoRA adapters and a dual-agent audit architecture against a baseline LLM for detecting five CWE vulnerability categories. The RAG variant, augmented with internet and MITRE CWE knowledge, achieved the highest accuracy (0.86) and F1 (0.85), while the dual-agent setup improved reasoning transparency with lower resource overhead.

大模型被忽视的安全漏洞：恶意智能优化算法请求及其越狱

$α^3$-Bench: A Unified Benchmark of Safety, Robustness, and Efficiency for LLM-Based UAV Agents over 6G Networks
$α^3$-Bench：面向 6G 网络下大模型无人机智能体的安全、鲁棒性与效率统一基准
agent benchmark tool use consistency multi-agent coordination network robustness
$α^3$-Bench evaluates LLM-driven UAV agents as multi-turn control loops under simulated 6G conditions, using 113k conversational episodes and a composite metric spanning task outcome, safety policy, tool consistency, interaction quality, network robustness and communication cost. Across 17 LLMs, several achieve high mission success and safety compliance, but robustness and efficiency vary widely under degraded network conditions.

GRIT -- Geometry-Aware PEFT with K-FACPreconditioning, Fisher-Guided Reprojection, andDynamic Rank Adaptation
GRIT：几何感知的参数高效微调，结合 K-FAC 预条件与 Fisher 引导重投影
parameter-efficient fine-tuning catastrophic forgetting low-rank adaptation model drift Fisher information
GRIT keeps the LoRA parameterization but preconditions gradients in rank space with K-FAC, periodically reprojects the low-rank basis onto dominant Fisher eigendirections, and adapts rank from the spectrum. On LLaMA backbones it matches or beats LoRA and QLoRA while cutting trainable parameters by 46% on average, and reports lower drift and a better updates-versus-retention frontier.

Rule-Based Approaches to Atomic Sentence Extraction
基于规则的原子句抽取方法研究
atomic sentence extraction knowledge decomposition dependency parsing ROUGE
The study analyzes how relative clauses, adverbial clauses, coordination and passive constructions affect dependency-based atomic sentence extraction, implementing spaCy rules on WikiSplit and scoring them with ROUGE and BERTScore.
