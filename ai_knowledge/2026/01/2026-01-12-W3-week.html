将智能体记忆锚定于情境意图
contextual intent intent-aware retrieval long-horizon memory CAME-Bench retrieval noise
STITCH indexes each step of a long-horizon agent trajectory with a structured "contextual intent" cue—latent goal, action type, and salient entity types—and retrieves history by matching the current step's intent.
