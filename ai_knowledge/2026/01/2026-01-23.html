Implementing Tensor Logic: Unifying Datalog and Neural Reasoning via Tensor Contraction
实现 Tensor Logic：通过张量收缩统一 Datalog 与神经推理
tensor logic neuro-symbolic reasoning Datalog knowledge graph embeddings link prediction
The paper empirically validates Domingos's Tensor Logic by showing that recursive Datalog rules and iterative tensor contractions compute the same transitive closure on a 1,972-person genealogy graph, converging in 74 iterations. It also trains learnable transformation matrices for zero-shot compositional inference and reaches MRR 0.3068 on FB15k-237 link prediction and 0.3346 on a compositional reasoning benchmark.

arxiv:2601.16964 arXiv ✓
AgentDrive: An Open Benchmark Dataset for Agentic AI Reasoning with LLM-Generated Scenarios in Autonomous Systems
AgentDrive：面向自主系统智能体推理的开放基准数据集
agentic AI benchmark autonomous systems scenario generation reasoning evaluation
AgentDrive is an open benchmark of 300,000 LLM-generated driving scenarios organized along seven orthogonal axes, with simulation rollouts, safety metrics and rule-based labels. Its companion AgentDrive-MCQ adds 100,000 multiple-choice questions across five reasoning dimensions, and an evaluation of fifty LLMs finds proprietary models strongest in contextual and policy reasoning while open models are closing the gap in structured and physics-grounded reasoning.

arxiv:2601.17230 arXiv ✓
CaseFacts: A Benchmark for Legal Fact-Checking and Precedent Retrieval
CaseFacts：法律事实核查与判例检索基准
legal fact-checking precedent retrieval claim verification benchmark dataset
CaseFacts is a benchmark of 6,294 claims labeled Supported, Refuted or Overruled for verifying colloquial legal claims against U.S. Supreme Court precedents. Experiments show the task remains challenging, and unrestricted web search degraded performance relative to closed-book baselines because retrieved precedents were noisy and non-authoritative.

Interpreting and Controlling Model Behavior via Constitutions for Atomic Concept Edits
通过原子概念编辑的宪法框架解释与控制模型行为
model interpretability constitutional alignment prompt perturbation behavior control
The authors learn a natural-language constitution that maps atomic concept edits in prompts to predictable changes in model behavior, validated on mathematical reasoning and text-to-image alignment. They report an average 1.86x boost in success rate when using the learned constitutions for control, and find that GPT-Image emphasizes grammatical adherence while Imagen 4 prioritizes atmospheric coherence.

Authority Signals in AI Cited Health Sources: A Framework for Evaluating Source Credibility in ChatGPT Responses
AI 引用健康信息来源的权威信号：ChatGPT 回答来源可信度评估框架
source credibility AI search citations health information evaluation framework
The study introduces a four-domain Authority Signals Framework covering author credentials, institutional affiliation, quality assurance and digital authority, then codes 615 sources cited by ChatGPT 5.2 Pro across 100 HealthSearchQA questions. It finds that over 75% of cited sources came from established institutions such as Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, Wikipedia, the NHS and PubMed.

arxiv:2601.17173 arXiv ✓
Beyond Factual QA: Mentorship-Oriented Question Answering over Long-Form Multilingual Content
超越事实问答：面向长视频多语言内容的导师式问答
mentorship QA multilingual benchmark multi-agent QA long-form video
MentorQA is a multilingual benchmark of nearly 9,000 QA pairs drawn from 180 hours of long-form video in four languages, with evaluation dimensions for clarity, alignment and learning value beyond factual accuracy. Comparing single-agent, dual-agent, RAG and multi-agent architectures, the authors find multi-agent pipelines produce higher-quality mentorship responses, especially for complex topics and lower-resource languages.

arxiv:2601.16866 arXiv ✓
Boosting Deep Reinforcement Learning with Semantic Knowledge for Robotic Manipulators
利用语义知识增强机器人机械臂的深度强化学习
knowledge graph embeddings deep reinforcement learning robotic manipulation sample efficiency
The paper integrates knowledge graph embeddings with visual observations to give a deep RL agent contextual information during training. In robotic manipulation experiments with fixed and randomized target attributes, it reports up to 60% less learning time and about a 15 percentage-point accuracy gain without extra training time or computational complexity.

Frame-Guided Synthetic Claim Generation for Automatic Fact-Checking Using High-Volume Tabular Data
面向自动事实核查的框架引导合成声明生成：基于大规模表格数据
fact-checking synthetic data tabular knowledge evidence retrieval
The authors release a multilingual dataset of 78,503 synthetic claims grounded in 434 complex OECD tables, generated with a frame-guided method in English, Chinese, Spanish and Hindi.
