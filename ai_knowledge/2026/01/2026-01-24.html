Real-Time Trend Prediction via Continually-Aligned LLM Query Generation
通过持续对齐的 LLM 查询生成实现实时趋势预测
continual learning query generation trend detection catastrophic forgetting
RTTP generates search-style queries directly from news content with a continually learning LLM and scores them by engagement strength and creator authority, aiming to surface trends before search volume forms. Deployed at production scale on Facebook and Meta AI products, it reports a 91.4% improvement in tail-trend detection precision@500 and 19% higher query-generation accuracy, while Mix-Policy DPO mitigates catastrophic forgetting during updates.

arxiv:2601.17617 arXiv ✓
Agentic Search in the Wild: Intents and Trajectory Dynamics from 14M+ Real Search Requests
野外智能体搜索：来自 1400 万次真实搜索请求的意图与轨迹动态
agentic search query reformulation search logs context tracking
The authors analyze 14.44 million search requests across 3.97 million sessions from DeepResearchGym, labeling session intents and query reformulations and proposing Context-driven Term Adoption Rate (CTAR) to measure whether new query terms trace back to retrieved evidence. They find over 90% of multi-turn sessions contain at most ten steps, fact-seeking sessions grow more repetitive, and on average 54% of newly introduced query terms appear in the accumulated evidence context.

Human-Aligned Enhancement of Programming Answers with LLMs Guided by User Feedback
基于用户反馈、由 LLM 引导的编程回答人类对齐增强
feedback-driven refinement programming QA answer revision human evaluation
The authors introduce ReSOlve, a benchmark of 790 Stack Overflow answers with comment threads annotated for improvement-related and general feedback, and AUTOCOMBAT, a tool that revises answers using comments and question context. AUTOCOMBAT produces near-human-quality improvements while preserving original intent, and 84.5% of 58 surveyed practitioners said they would adopt or recommend it.

arxiv:2603.04405 arXiv ✓
Lost in Translation: How Language Re-Aligns Vision for Cross-Species Pathology
语言如何重新对齐视觉：跨物种病理学的语义锚定
vision-language alignment embedding collapse semantic anchoring cross-species transfer
The study fine-tunes CPath-CLIP for cross-cancer and cross-species pathology and finds that few-shot tuning improves same-cancer AUC from 64.9% to 72.6% and cross-cancer AUC from 56.84% to 66.31%, but cross-species transfer remains below the H-optimus-0 benchmark. Embedding analysis shows extremely high cosine similarity (above 0.99) between tumor and normal prototypes, and the proposed Semantic Anchoring uses language to provide a stable coordinate system for visual features.

arxiv:2601.17616 arXiv ✓
Sparse Subspace-to-Expert Sharing for Task-Agnostic Continual Learning
面向任务无关持续学习的稀疏子空间到专家共享
continual learning catastrophic forgetting mixture of experts sparse subspace decomposition
SETA separates task-specific knowledge into unique sparse experts and shared capabilities into shared experts, sharing parameters only when a new task passes subspace-agreement and sign tests, with adaptive elastic anchoring protecting shared knowledge. It improves over the strongest continual-learning baseline by 1.1–1.7 points on TRACE and up to 5.5 points on O-LoRA, with backward transfer between -2.2 and -1.3 on TRACE.

Cognitive Platform Engineering for Autonomous Cloud Operations
面向自主云运维的认知平台工程
autonomous operations AIOps policy orchestration anomaly detection
The paper proposes Cognitive Platform Engineering, a four-plane reference architecture that combines data collection, intelligent inference, policy-driven orchestration and human experience in a continuous feedback loop for cloud operations. A prototype built with Kubernetes, Terraform, Open Policy Agent and ML-based anomaly detection reports improvements in mean time to resolution, resource efficiency and compliance.
