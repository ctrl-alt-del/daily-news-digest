Knowledge Graphs are Implicit Reward Models: Path-Derived Signals Enable Compositional Reasoning
知识图谱即隐式奖励模型：路径派生信号实现组合推理
knowledge graph rewards compositional reasoning reinforcement learning multi-hop QA verifiable supervision
The authors use knowledge-graph paths as implicit reward models in a supervised fine-tuning plus reinforcement-learning pipeline, rewarding models for composing intermediate axioms rather than only matching final answers. A 14B model trained on one-to-three-hop medical paths reportedly outperforms much larger models and systems including GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3 Pro on four-to-five-hop zero-shot queries, and proves robust to option-shuffling perturbations.

arxiv:2601.15487 arXiv ✓
MiRAGE: A Multiagent Framework for Generating Multimodal Multihop Question-Answer Dataset for RAG Evaluation
MiRAGE：为 RAG 评估生成多模态多跳问答数据集的多智能体框架
multimodal RAG multi-hop QA multi-agent generation dataset synthesis
MiRAGE is a multi-agent framework that generates verified, domain-specific, multimodal, multi-hop question-answer datasets for RAG evaluation, using a recursive context-optimization loop, an adversarial verifier, and an expert-persona agent. Across regulations, finance, quantitative biology, and journalism it produces datasets with over 2.3 average hops and higher factual faithfulness, though visual grounding remains a frontier.

Reliability by design: quantifying and eliminating fabrication risk in LLMs. From generative to consultative AI: a comparative analysis in the legal domain and lessons for high-stakes knowledge bases
设计即可靠：量化并消除大语言模型的捏造风险——从生成式到咨询式 AI 的法律领域比较分析及高风险知识库启示
hallucination metrics legal RAG advanced RAG factuality verification embedding fine-tuning
The study compares standalone generative models, basic RAG, and an end-to-end optimized RAG system on 2,700 judicial-style answers from 12 LLMs across 75 legal tasks under expert double-blind review. Standalone models show false citation rates above 30%, basic RAG reduces but does not eliminate misgrounding, and advanced RAG with embedding fine-tuning, reranking, and self-correction lowers fabrication below 0.2%.

Benchmarking LLMs for Pairwise Causal Discovery in Biomedical and Multi-Domain Contexts
在生物医学与多领域语境中对大语言模型进行成对因果发现基准测试
causal discovery causal extraction prompting strategies biomedical benchmark
The benchmark tests 13 open-source LLMs on pairwise causal discovery from text across 12 datasets, separating causal detection from extraction of cause and effect phrases under zero-shot, chain-of-thought, and few-shot prompting. The best detection score was 49.57% (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B) and the best extraction score 47.12% (Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct), with performance dropping on implicit, cross-sentence, and multi-pair relations.

arxiv:2601.15457 arXiv ✓
Chunking, Retrieval, and Re-ranking: An Empirical Evaluation of RAG Architectures for Policy Document Question Answering
分块、检索与重排序：面向政策文档问答的 RAG 架构实证评估
document chunking cross-encoder reranking policy QA faithfulness
The study compares a vanilla Mistral-7B-Instruct baseline with basic RAG and advanced RAG using cross-encoder reranking over CDC policy documents, testing recursive character-based and token-based semantic chunking. Faithfulness rises from 0.347 for the vanilla model to 0.621 for basic RAG and 0.797 for advanced RAG, though document segmentation remains a bottleneck for multi-step reasoning.

arxiv:2602.15844 arXiv ✓
Language Model Representations for Efficient Few-Shot Tabular Classification
面向高效少样本表格分类的语言模型表示
tabular classification few-shot learning semantic embeddings temperature calibration
TaRL classifies table rows in few-shot settings by reusing semantic embeddings from deployed LLMs, after removing the common component shared by all embeddings and calibrating the softmax temperature with a meta-learner. It reaches performance comparable to specialized state-of-the-art models in low-data regimes of up to 32 examples per class on semantically rich tables.

AdversaRiskQA: An Adversarial Factuality Benchmark for High-Risk Domains
AdversaRiskQA：面向高风险领域的对抗事实性基准
adversarial factuality high-risk domains benchmark verification
AdversaRiskQA is a verified benchmark for adversarial factuality in health, finance, and law with two difficulty levels and two automated evaluation methods. Across six models from the Qwen, GPT-OSS, and GPT families, Qwen3 (80B) had the highest average accuracy while GPT-5 stayed consistently accurate.
