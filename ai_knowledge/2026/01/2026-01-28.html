DeepSearchQA: Bridging the Comprehensiveness Gap for Deep Research Agents
DeepSearchQA：弥合深度研究智能体的全面性差距
deep research agents multi-step retrieval benchmark design entity resolution long-horizon planning
DeepSearchQA is a 900-prompt benchmark for evaluating agents on difficult multi-step information-seeking tasks across 17 fields, requiring exhaustive answer lists, de-duplication and reasoning about when to stop. Evaluations of state-of-the-art agent architectures reportedly show struggles to balance recall with precision, with failure modes including premature stopping and hedging.

C3Box：基于 CLIP 的类增量学习工具箱
class-incremental learning continual learning CLIP embeddings reproducible benchmark
C3Box is a modular Python toolbox that unifies traditional, ViT-based and CLIP-based class-incremental learning methods in a single framework, with JSON-based configuration and a standardized execution pipeline. It is intended to make continual-learning experiments reproducible with low engineering overhead and to serve as a benchmark platform.

arxiv:2601.20861 arXiv ✓
Evolutionary Strategies lead to Catastrophic Forgetting in LLMs
进化策略导致大语言模型灾难性遗忘
evolutionary strategies catastrophic forgetting continual learning gradient-free optimization
The study analyzes evolutionary strategies (ES) as a gradient-free alternative for training LLMs and finds that ES can approach GRPO on math and reasoning tasks at comparable compute, but with significant forgetting of prior abilities. The authors attribute the difference to ES updates being much less sparse and having orders-of-magnitude larger L2 norm than GRPO updates.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.18448 arXiv ✓
INSURE-Dial: A Phase-Aware Conversational Dataset & Benchmark for Compliance Verification and Phase Detection
INSURE-Dial：面向合规验证与阶段检测的阶段感知对话数据集与基准
voice agents compliance verification conversation segmentation benchmark dataset
INSURE-Dial is described by the authors as the first public benchmark for compliance-aware voice agents, with 50 de-identified AI-initiated insurance calls and 1,000 synthetic calls annotated with a phase-structured schema. It defines phase boundary detection and span-based compliance verification tasks; per-phase scores are strong, but exact full-call segmentation on real calls remains low.
