Beyond Explicit Edges: Robust Reasoning over Noisy and Sparse Knowledge Graphs
超越显式边：在噪声与稀疏知识图谱上的鲁棒推理
GraphRAG knowledge graph similarity expansion query routing noisy graphs
INSES is a dynamic GraphRAG framework that couples LLM-guided navigation, which prunes noise and steers exploration, with embedding-based similarity expansion to recover hidden links in noisy or sparse knowledge graphs. A lightweight router sends simple queries to naive RAG and escalates complex ones to INSES, and the system performs favorably against RAG and GraphRAG baselines, notably on the MINE benchmark.

Agentic Critical Training uses reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards to teach models to judge actions, pairing an expert action with an alternative sampled from the initial policy and rewarding only selection of the expert action.
