arxiv:2603.14006 arXiv ✓
Beyond Explicit Edges: Robust Reasoning over Noisy and Sparse Knowledge Graphs
超越显式边：在噪声与稀疏知识图谱上的鲁棒推理
GraphRAG knowledge graph similarity expansion query routing noisy graphs
INSES is a dynamic GraphRAG framework that couples LLM-guided navigation, which prunes noise and steers exploration, with embedding-based similarity expansion to recover hidden links in noisy or sparse knowledge graphs. A lightweight router sends simple queries to naive RAG and escalates complex ones to INSES, and the system performs favorably against RAG and GraphRAG baselines, notably on the MINE benchmark.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.14045 arXiv ✓
The Reasoning Bottleneck in Graph-RAG: Structured Prompting and Context Compression for Multi-Hop QA
Graph-RAG 中的推理瓶颈：面向多跳问答的结构化提示与上下文压缩
GraphRAG multi-hop QA context compression SPARQL prompting reasoning bottleneck
Evaluating KET-RAG on three multi-hop QA benchmarks, the authors find that 77% to 91% of questions have the gold answer in the retrieved context yet accuracy is only 35% to 78%, with 73% to 84% of errors traced to reasoning failures. SPARQL chain-of-thought prompting and graph-walk compression improve accuracy, and with question-type routing an augmented Llama-8B matches or exceeds an unaugmented Llama-70B at roughly 12x lower cost.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.13950 arXiv ✓
ToolFlood: Beyond Selection -- Hiding Valid Tools from LLM Agents via Semantic Covering
ToolFlood：通过语义覆盖向 LLM agent 隐藏有效工具
tool retrieval embedding geometry adversarial attack retrieval saturation
ToolFlood attacks the tool-retrieval layer of LLM agents by injecting a few tools whose metadata is positioned to span many user queries in embedding space, pushing benign tools out of the agent's context. On the ToolBench catalog it reports up to 95% attack success at roughly a 1% injection rate.
→ arxiv.org

An Alternative Trajectory for Generative AI
生成式 AI 的另一条发展轨迹
domain-specific superintelligence symbolic abstractions ontology orchestration agents sustainable AI
The position paper argues that scaling monolithic models is colliding with energy, water and data constraints, and proposes domain-specific superintelligence built by first constructing explicit symbolic abstractions such as knowledge graphs, ontologies and formal logic. It envisions societies of small domain models routed by orchestration agents, moving capability from data centers to on-device experts.

arxiv:2603.14158 arXiv ✓
Clinician input steers AI toward accurate and harmful recommendations
临床医生的输入会引导 AI 给出准确但也有害的建议
clinical reasoning context influence adversarial context AI safety
Using 61 NEJM Case Records and 21 reasoning variants from eight models, the study finds that clinician context raises LLM-clinician concordance and improves correct final-diagnosis inclusion by a mean 20.4 points, while adversarial context degrades performance in 14 models. Inference-time scaling reduces harmful echoing of clinician-introduced recommendations across WHO harm-severity tiers.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.14130 arXiv ✓
The GELATO Dataset for Legislative NER
GELATO：面向立法文本命名实体识别的数据集
named entity recognition legislative text ontology dataset
GELATO is a dataset of U.S. House and Senate bills from the 118th Congress annotated with a two-level named-entity-recognition ontology. The authors fine-tune BERT and RoBERTa for first-level prediction and use prompted LLMs for the second level.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.14052 arXiv ✓
A Multi-Agent Perception-Action Alliance for Efficient Long Video Reasoning
面向高效长视频推理的多智能体感知-行动联盟
multi-agent system long-video reasoning cross-review VideoQA
A4VL runs a multi-round perception-action exploration loop with a team of VLM agents that extract query-specific clues from sampled frames, align them to relevant video blocks, and cross-review one another before deciding whether to continue or answer. It reports gains over 18 VLMs and 11 long-video methods on five VideoQA benchmarks with lower inference latency.

arxiv:2603.16934 arXiv ✓
AgriChat: A Multimodal Large Language Model for Agriculture Image Understanding
AgriChat：面向农业图像理解的多模态大语言模型
multimodal LLM scientific retrieval grounded annotation agriculture benchmark
AgriChat is a specialized multimodal LLM trained on AgriMM, a benchmark built by a Vision-to-Verified-Knowledge pipeline that combines visual captioning with web-augmented scientific retrieval to ground annotations in phytopathological literature. AgriMM contains more than 3,000 agricultural classes and over 607k visual question-answer pairs, and AgriChat is reported to outperform other open-source models on internal and external benchmarks.
