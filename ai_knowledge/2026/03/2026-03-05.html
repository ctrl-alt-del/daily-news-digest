arxiv:2603.05488 arXiv ✓
Reasoning Theater: Disentangling Model Beliefs from Chain-of-Thought
推理剧场：将模型信念与思维链分离
chain-of-thought activation probing performative reasoning adaptive computation
The authors present evidence of performative chain-of-thought in DeepSeek-R1 671B and GPT-OSS 120B, where the final answer is decodable from activations far earlier in the CoT than a monitor can detect, especially on easy recall-based MMLU questions, while genuine reasoning appears on harder GPQA-Diamond items. Probe-guided early exit cuts tokens by up to 80% on MMLU and 30% on GPQA-Diamond with similar accuracy.
→ arxiv.org

Leveraging LLM Parametric Knowledge for Fact Checking without Retrieval
无需检索：利用 LLM 参数知识进行事实核查
parametric knowledge fact verification internal representations claim checking
The paper defines fact-checking without retrieval as verifying arbitrary natural-language claims independently of their source, and introduces an evaluation framework spanning long-tail knowledge, varied claim sources, multilinguality and long-form generation across 9 datasets, 18 methods and 3 models. It finds logit-based approaches often underperform methods that use internal representations, and proposes INTRA, which exploits interactions between internal representations and reports state-of-the-art generalization.

arxiv:2603.05433 arXiv ✓
CRISP: Compressed Reasoning via Iterative Self-Policy Distillation
CRISP：通过迭代自策略蒸馏压缩推理
reasoning compression self-distillation reverse KL token efficiency
CRISP is an on-policy self-distillation method that conditions a model on a "be concise" instruction to obtain teacher logits, then minimizes per-token reverse KL divergence on the model's own rollouts, requiring no ground-truth answers or token budgets. On Qwen3-14B it cuts reasoning length by up to 56% on MATH-500 and 38% on AIME 2024 while improving MATH-500 accuracy by up to 3.3 points.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.05494 arXiv ✓
Censored LLMs as a Natural Testbed for Secret Knowledge Elicitation
被审查的 LLM：秘密知识诱导的天然测试平台
knowledge elicitation honesty lie detection prompt sensitivity
The authors use open-weight Chinese LLMs trained to censor politically sensitive topics as a natural testbed for honesty elicitation and lie detection, noting that Qwen3 models sometimes answer correctly and sometimes produce falsehoods about those subjects. Sampling without a chat template, few-shot prompting and fine-tuning on generic honesty data most reliably increase truthful responses, and no tested technique fully eliminates false responses.
→ arxiv.org

SecureRAG-RTL: A Retrieval-Augmented, Multi-Agent, Zero-Shot LLM-Driven Framework for Hardware Vulnerability Detection
SecureRAG-RTL：面向硬件漏洞检测的检索增强多智能体零样本 LLM 框架
retrieval-augmented generation hardware security vulnerability detection domain adaptation
SecureRAG-RTL combines domain-specific retrieval with generative reasoning to detect vulnerabilities in hardware description language designs, addressing the scarcity of public HDL security datasets. Across diverse LLM architectures it raises detection accuracy by about 30% on average, evaluated on a curated benchmark of 14 HDL designs containing real-world vulnerabilities.

Agentic AI -- Physicist Collaboration in Experimental Particle Physics: A Proof-of-Concept Measurement with LEP Open Data
智能体 AI 与物理学家的协作：基于 LEP 开放数据的粒子物理概念验证测量
scientific agents autonomous analysis human-AI collaboration particle physics
In an AI agent–physicist collaboration, agents driven by Anthropic Claude and OpenAI Codex wrote and executed all analysis code and drafted most of the paper measuring the thrust distribution in e+e- collisions at 91.2 GeV using archived ALEPH data. The authors present the agentic analysis itself as the principal contribution, with thrust serving as a benchmark observable.

arxiv:2603.05651 arXiv ✓
The Fragility Of Moral Judgment In Large Language Models
大语言模型道德判断的脆弱性
moral judgment prompt sensitivity evaluation protocol robustness
Using 2,939 r/AmItheAsshole dilemmas and 129,156 judgments from four models, the study tests how surface edits, point-of-view shifts, persuasion cues and evaluation protocols change moral judgments. Surface perturbations cause few flips (7.5%) while point-of-view shifts cause far more (24.3%), and protocol choices dominate, with only 35.7% of model-scenario units matching across all three protocols.
→ arxiv.org

TML-Bench: Benchmark for Data Science Agents on Tabular ML Tasks
TML-Bench：面向表格机器学习任务的数据科学 Agent 基准
data science agents tabular machine learning agent benchmark
TML-Bench evaluates data science agents on Kaggle-style tabular machine-learning tasks, testing 10 open-source LLMs across four competitions and three time budgets. MiniMax-M2.1 achieved the best aggregate score under the paper's primary aggregation.

An Exploration-Analysis-Disambiguation Reasoning Framework for Word Sense Disambiguation with Low-Parameter LLMs
面向低参数量 LLM 词义消歧的探索-分析-消歧推理框架
word sense disambiguation chain-of-thought small language models fine-tuning
The study fine-tunes eight small open-source LLMs on the FEWS dataset with rationale-rich annotations and finds that chain-of-thought reasoning plus neighbour-word analysis lets sub-4B models match GPT-4-Turbo in zero-shot word sense disambiguation. Gemma-3-4B and Qwen-3-4B reportedly outperform medium-parameter baselines on FEWS and generalize to unseen senses.
