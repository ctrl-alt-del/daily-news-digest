GraphRAG for Engineering Diagrams: ChatP&ID Enables LLM Interaction with P&IDs
面向工程图的 GraphRAG：ChatP&ID 让 LLM 与管道仪表图对话
GraphRAG knowledge graph construction agentic retrieval token efficiency
ChatP&ID is an agentic framework that converts DEXPI-standard smart P&IDs into knowledge graphs and uses GraphRAG to answer natural-language queries. Across commercial LLM APIs, graph-based representations improved accuracy by 18% over raw images and cut token costs by 85% versus ingesting smart P&ID files, while GPT-5-mini with ContextRAG reached 91% accuracy at $0.004 per task.

Lie to Me: How Faithful Is Chain-of-Thought Reasoning in Reasoning Models?
《Lie to Me》：推理模型的思维链有多忠实？
chain-of-thought faithfulness CoT monitoring sycophancy open-weight models
The study tests 12 open-weight reasoning models (7B-685B) on 498 MMLU and GPQA Diamond questions with six categories of injected hints across 41,832 runs. Faithfulness ranged from 39.7% to 89.9%; models acknowledged hint influence in thinking tokens about 87.5% of the time but in answer text only about 28.6%.

STRIATUM-CTF：面向自主 CTF 解题的协议驱动智能体框架
MCP tool grounding schema validation long-horizon state CTF benchmark

EviSearch：用可随使用改进的 Agent 可信地提取与综合临床试验证据
evidence extraction attribution verification multi-agent pipeline clinical trials

通过任务级自回归推理弥合知与行的差距

面向生物医学文献检索增强生成的图感知后分块
late chunking UMLS knowledge graph biomedical retrieval evidence coverage
