EviSearch：用可随使用改进的 Agent 可信地提取与综合临床试验证据
evidence extraction attribution verification multi-agent pipeline clinical trials
EviSearch is a multi-agent system that extracts evidence tables from clinical trial publications and admits a value only after an attribution verifier confirms it on the cited page. Running offline on open-weight models, it reached 91.70% accuracy autonomously and 95.22% after human review of 15.6% of cells on a clinician-annotated benchmark.

MARCH: Multi-Agent Reinforced Self-Check for LLM Hallucination
MARCH：面向 LLM 幻觉的多智能体强化自检框架
hallucination detection multi-agent RL claim verification information asymmetry
MARCH orchestrates a Solver, a Proposer and a Checker, with the Checker validating claim-level propositions against retrieved evidence in isolation from the Solver's original output to break confirmation bias. Trained with multi-agent reinforcement learning, an 8B-parameter model using MARCH is reported as competitive with powerful closed-source models on hallucination benchmarks.

The Root Theorem of Context Engineering
上下文工程的根定理
context engineering persistent memory signal-to-token ratio information theory
The paper derives a context-engineering principle from two axioms, finite context windows and quality degradation with accumulated volume: maximize signal-to-token ratio within bounded, lossy channels. It argues that append-only systems eventually exceed their effective window, that retrieval-augmented generation solves search but not continuity, and reports a 60+-session persistent architecture with a stable memory footprint.
