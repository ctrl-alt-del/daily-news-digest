Arbiter：检测 LLM 智能体系统提示中的干扰
system prompts prompt interference agent memory cross-model evaluation coding agents

MEMO：面向稳健多轮多智能体 LLM 游戏的记忆增强上下文优化

Quantifying the Accuracy and Cost Impact of Design Decisions in Budget-Constrained Agentic LLM Search
量化预算受限的智能体 LLM 搜索中设计决策对准确率与成本的影响
agentic RAG budget constraints hybrid retrieval reranking evaluation harness
This controlled measurement study examines how search depth, retrieval strategy, and completion budget affect accuracy and cost in agentic RAG under fixed constraints. Across six LLMs and three QA benchmarks, accuracy improved with additional searches up to a small cap, and hybrid lexical-dense retrieval with lightweight reranking produced the largest average gains.

arxiv:2603.08706 arXiv ✓
Agentic Critical Training
Agentic Critical Training：通过行动判别训练智能体
agent training reinforcement learning action comparison verifiable rewards
Agentic Critical Training uses reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards to teach models to judge actions, pairing an expert action with an alternative sampled from the initial policy and rewarding only selection of the expert action. On ALFWorld-ID, WebShop, and ScienceWorld it yields average gains of 5.85 points over imitation learning and 4.12 points over imitation learning followed by RL.
→ arxiv.org

Learning When to Sample: Confidence-Aware Selective Sampling for Efficient Chain-of-Thought Reasoning
学会何时采样：面向高效思维链推理的置信度感知选择性采样
chain-of-thought selective sampling inference efficiency confidence estimation
The framework analyzes a single chain-of-thought trajectory at inference time to decide whether to rely on it or trigger multi-path sampling. Trained on MedQA and transferred to MathQA, MedMCQA, and MMLU, it cut token usage by 71.7% versus full multi-path reasoning while accuracy changed by -0.41 percentage points.

The ARC of Progress towards AGI: A Living Survey of Abstraction and Reasoning
通往 AGI 的 ARC 之路：抽象与推理的持续更新综述
ARC-AGI abstraction reasoning benchmark survey program synthesis
This living survey presents the first cross-generation analysis of 82 approaches across three ARC-AGI benchmark versions, finding consistent 2-3x performance drops from ARC-AGI-1 to ARC-AGI-2 across program-synthesis, neuro-symbolic, and neural paradigms. It reports top scores of 93.0% on ARC-AGI-1, 68.8% on ARC-AGI-2, and 13% on ARC-AGI-3, while humans remain near-perfect.

Security Considerations for Multi-agent Systems
多智能体系统的安全考量
multi-agent security threat taxonomy OWASP data leakage
The study characterizes multi-agent system security risks as 193 main threat items across nine risk categories and scores 16 AI security frameworks against them. No framework achieved majority coverage of any single category, with non-determinism and data leakage the least addressed, while the OWASP Agentic Security Initiative led overall at 65.3% coverage.
