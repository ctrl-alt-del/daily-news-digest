Coding Agents are Effective Long-Context Processors
编程智能体是有效的长上下文处理器
long-context processing coding agents file-system navigation retrieval-augmented generation open-domain QA
The authors externalize long-context processing into file-system organization and executable tool use, evaluating frontier coding agents on long-context reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation and open-domain QA over corpora of up to three trillion tokens. Across benchmarks the agents outperform published state of the art by 17.3% on average, which the authors attribute to native tool proficiency and file-system familiarity.

arxiv:2603.20179 arXiv ✓
AI Agents Can Already Autonomously Perform Experimental High Energy Physics
AI 智能体已能自主完成实验高能物理分析
autonomous research agents literature retrieval multi-agent review scientific workflow analysis automation
Given a HEP dataset, an execution framework and prior literature, the authors report that Claude Code automates all stages of a typical analysis: event selection, background estimation, uncertainty quantification, statistical inference and paper drafting. Their Just Furnish Context framework produced a first Lund plane measurement on LEP data that they describe as the first genuinely novel result produced autonomously by an AI agent.

Measuring Faithfulness Depends on How You Measure: Classifier Sensitivity in LLM Chain-of-Thought Evaluation
测量方式决定忠实度结论：LLM 思维链评估中的分类器敏感性
chain-of-thought faithfulness classifier sensitivity model ranking reasoning traces
Applying three classifiers to 10,276 influenced reasoning traces from 12 open-weight models yields faithfulness rates of 74.4%, 82.6% and 69.7% on identical data, with per-model gaps of 2.6 to 30.6 percentage points. Classifier choice can reverse model rankings, leading the author to argue that published faithfulness numbers are not comparable across studies that use different classifiers.

Evaluating Evidence Grounding Under User Pressure in Instruction-Tuned Language Models
在用户压力下评估指令微调语言模型的证据依据能力
evidence grounding sycophancy epistemic conflict instruction-tuned models
The authors introduce a controlled epistemic-conflict framework based on the U.S. National Climate Assessment and test 19 instruction-tuned models from 0.27B to 32B parameters. They find richer in-context evidence improves accuracy under neutral prompts but does not reliably prevent user-aligned reversals under pressure, with non-monotonic robustness scaling and a negative partial-evidence interaction in Llama-3 and Gemma-3 families.

arxiv:2603.20562 arXiv ✓
Permutation-Consensus Listwise Judging for Robust Factuality Evaluation
面向稳健事实性评估的排列共识列表评判
listwise judging factuality evaluation permutation consensus judge aggregation
PCFJudge reruns a factuality-first listwise judging prompt over multiple candidate orderings and aggregates scores, ranks and uncertainty into a consensus decision. On RewardBench 2 Factuality, a seven-permutation aggregate raises top-1 accuracy from 86.00% to 91.33% with GPT-5.4 and from 86.33% to 89.67% with Claude Sonnet 4.6.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.20554 arXiv ✓
When Negation Is a Geometry Problem in Vision-Language Models
当否定成为视觉语言模型中的几何问题
negation understanding CLIP embedding space representation engineering retrieval evaluation
The paper examines why CLIP-style vision-language embedding models fail at negation, critiques retrieval-based evaluation and uses multimodal LLM judges to assess negation understanding. It finds evidence of a negation direction in CLIP embedding space that can be manipulated at test time through representation engineering without fine-tuning.

arxiv:2603.20441 arXiv ✓
A Training-Free Regeneration Paradigm: Contrastive Reflection Memory Guided Self-Verification and Self-Improvement
免训练再生成范式：对比反思记忆引导的自验证与自我改进
reflection memory self-verification training-free regeneration
The paper proposes a training-free regeneration paradigm that uses an offline-curated contrastive Reflection Memory to guide self-verification, followed by a single regeneration from scratch. Across nine benchmarks spanning algorithmic, reasoning, symbolic and domain-specific tasks, it reports better accuracy than prior methods at low computational cost.

arxiv:2603.20131 arXiv ✓
An Agentic Multi-Agent Architecture for Cybersecurity Risk Management
面向网络安全风险管理的智能体多智能体架构
multi-agent system shared context context capacity cybersecurity risk
The authors build a six-agent system in which profiling, asset mapping, threat analysis, control evaluation, risk scoring and recommendations share a persistent context that grows during the assessment. Tested on a 15-person HIPAA-covered healthcare company, it agreed with three CISSP practitioners 85% of the time on severity and covered 92% of identified risks in under 15 minutes, while the full pipeline failed all 30 runs on a 4,096-token context window.
