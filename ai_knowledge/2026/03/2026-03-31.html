LLM Agents Predict Social Media Reactions but Do Not Outperform Text Classifiers: Benchmarking Simulation Accuracy Using 120K+ Personas of 1511 Humans
LLM 智能体可预测社交媒体反应，但未能超越文本分类器：基于 1511 名人类、12 万+ 人设的模拟准确性基准
persona simulation social media reactions LLM benchmark human behavior prediction
The study benchmarks LLM agents' ability to predict individual social-media reactions across 120,000+ agent-persona combinations built from 1,511 Serbian participants and 27 LLMs. Agents reach 70.7% overall accuracy and an MCC of 0.29 on a binary like/dislike task, but TF-IDF text classifiers score higher (MCC 0.36), suggesting the signal comes from semantic access rather than uniquely agentic reasoning.

AlphaLab: Autonomous Multi-Agent Research Across Optimization Domains with Frontier LLMs
AlphaLab：面向优化领域的自主多智能体研究
autonomous research multi-agent experiment automation CUDA optimization
AlphaLab is an autonomous research harness that, given a dataset and a natural-language objective, explores the data, builds and adversarially validates its own evaluation framework, then runs GPU experiments through a Strategist/Worker loop with a persistent playbook. With GPT-5.2 and Claude Opus 4.6 it produces CUDA kernels averaging 4.4x faster than torch.compile (up to 91x) and cuts LLM pretraining validation loss by 22% versus a single-shot baseline.

A Study on the Impact of Fault localization Granularity for Repository-Scale Code Repair Tasks
故障定位粒度对仓库级代码修复任务的影响研究
fault localization code repair SWE-Bench localization granularity

arxiv:2604.00304 arXiv ✓
Asymmetric Actor-Critic for Multi-turn LLM Agents
多轮 LLM 智能体的非对称演员-评论家框架
actor-critic multi-turn agents runtime supervision small critics
The framework pairs a proprietary LLM actor with a smaller open-source critic that monitors actions and intervenes within the same multi-turn trajectory, exploiting a generation-verification asymmetry. On τ-bench and UserBench it improves reliability and task success over strong single-agent baselines, and lightweight critics rival or surpass larger proprietary models in the critic role.

arxiv:2604.00261 arXiv ✓
Can Large Language Models Self-Correct in Medical Question Answering? An Exploratory Study
大语言模型能在医学问答中自我纠错吗？一项探索性研究
self-reflection medical QA chain-of-thought error correction
The authors compare chain-of-thought prompting with an iterative self-reflection loop on MedQA, HeadQA and PubMedQA using GPT-4o and GPT-4o-mini. Self-reflection does not consistently improve accuracy, helping modestly on MedQA but giving limited or negative gains elsewhere, and increasing the number of reflection steps does not guarantee better performance.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.30025 arXiv ✓
ContextClaim: A Context-Driven Paradigm for Verifiable Claim Detection
ContextClaim：面向可验证声明检测的上下文驱动范式
claim detection retrieval augmentation fact-checking contextual summaries
ContextClaim moves retrieval into the verifiable-claim detection stage: it identifies entity mentions, queries Wikipedia as a structured background source and uses an LLM to compress retrieved material into contextual summaries for a classifier. The added context improves detection across several configurations on CheckThat! 2022 and PoliClaim, and the same summaries raise verification F1 on FEVER.
→ arxiv.org

面向网络遥测故障检测与原因分析的协作式 AI 智能体与评审器
