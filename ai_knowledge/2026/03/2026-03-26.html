MemoryCD: Benchmarking Long-Context User Memory of LLM Agents for Lifelong Cross-Domain Personalization
MemoryCD：面向终身跨域个性化的 LLM Agent 长上下文用户记忆基准
long-context memory cross-domain personalization memory benchmark user modeling
MemoryCD is described as the first large-scale, user-centric, cross-domain memory benchmark, built from lifelong real-world behaviors in the Amazon Review dataset. It evaluates 14 LLM base models and 6 memory methods on 4 personalization tasks across 12 domains and finds existing memory methods fall short of user satisfaction.

arxiv:2604.08571 arXiv ✓
Robust Reasoning Benchmark
Robust Reasoning Benchmark：文本扰动下的推理鲁棒性基准
reasoning robustness textual perturbation attention dilution AIME
The Robust Reasoning Benchmark applies 13 deterministic textual perturbations to AIME 2024 and AIME 2025 and evaluates eight state-of-the-art models. Frontier models are largely resilient except Claude, which often refuses transformed prompts, while open-weight reasoning models show failure modes with up to 54% average accuracy drops and up to 100% on some perturbations.

arxiv:2603.25960 arXiv ✓
When Chain-of-Thought Backfires: Evaluating Prompt Sensitivity in Medical Language Models
当思维链适得其反：评估医学语言模型的提示敏感性
chain-of-thought prompt sensitivity medical QA few-shot prompting
The authors evaluate MedGemma models on MedMCQA and PubMedQA and find chain-of-thought prompting lowers accuracy by 5.7% versus direct answering, while few-shot examples degrade performance by 11.9%. Shuffling answer options changed predictions 59.1% of the time, and cloze scoring outperformed all prompting strategies.

Runtime Burden Allocation for Structured LLM Routing in Agentic Expert Systems: A Full-Factorial Cross-Backend Methodology
智能体专家系统中结构化 LLM 路由的运行时负担分配：全因子跨后端方法
structured routing structured output cross-backend benchmark latency-cost tradeoff
The paper frames structured LLM routing as a systems-level burden-allocation problem and benchmarks 48 deployment configurations and 15,552 requests across OpenAI, Gemini and Llama backends. It finds no universal best routing mode: modes reliable on Gemini and OpenAI can degrade substantially on Llama, and gains from compressed output realization are strongly backend-dependent.

DeepSeek arxiv:2604.02367 arXiv ✓
Evaluating Small Language Models for Front-Door Routing: A Harmonized Benchmark and Synthetic-Traffic Experiment
评估用于前置路由的小语言模型：统一基准与合成流量实验
model routing small language models latency benchmark task classification
The paper tests whether 1-4B language models can handle front-door task routing, using a harmonized offline benchmark and a pre-registered four-arm synthetic-traffic experiment. Qwen-2.5-3B was Pareto-dominant among self-hosted models at 0.793 accuracy and 988 ms median latency with zero marginal cost, but no model met the pre-registered viability bar of 0.85 accuracy and 2,000 ms P95.

arxiv:2603.25944 arXiv ✓
Can Small Models Reason About Legal Documents? A Comparative Study
小模型能推理法律文书吗？一项比较研究
legal reasoning small language models RAG comparison prompting strategies
The study tests nine sub-10B models across ContractNLI, CaseHOLD and ECtHR with five prompting strategies, running 405 experiments. A mixture-of-experts model activating 3B parameters matched GPT-4o-mini in mean accuracy, while chain-of-thought helped contract entailment but hurt multiple-choice legal reasoning and BM25 and dense RAG gave near-identical results.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2603.25722 arXiv ✓
No Hard Negatives Required: Concept Centric Learning Leads to Compositionality without Degrading Zero-shot Capabilities of Contrastive Models
无需难负样本：以概念为中心的学习在不损害零样本能力的前提下实现组合性
compositionality contrastive learning vision-language retrieval attention pooling
The authors attribute weak compositionality in contrastive vision-language models to long training captions and global pooling, and propose short concept-centric caption parts plus parameter-free cross-modal attention pooling. The changes reach state-of-the-art compositionality results while maintaining or improving zero-shot and retrieval performance without extra inference cost.
