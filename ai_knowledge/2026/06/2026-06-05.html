MemDreamer: Decoupling Perception and Reasoning for Long Video Understanding via Hierarchical Graph Memory and Agentic Retrieval Mechanism
MemDreamer：通过分层图记忆与 Agent 检索机制解耦长视频理解的感知与推理
hierarchical graph memory agentic retrieval knowledge graph long video understanding multimodal reasoning
MemDreamer streams long videos into a three-tier Hierarchical Graph Memory anchored by a foundational graph of spatiotemporal and causal relations, then lets a reasoning model navigate it through an Observation-Reason-Action retrieval loop with tool augmentation. It reports state-of-the-art results on four benchmarks, narrowing the gap to human experts to 3.7 points while constraining the reasoning context to 2% of full-context ingestion and gaining 12.5 points absolute accuracy.

Beyond English benchmarks: clinical llm evaluation in Brazilian Portuguese
超越英语基准：巴西葡萄牙语临床 LLM 评估
clinical benchmark bilingual evaluation diagnosis retrieval medical reasoning
ClinicalBr is a bilingual Portuguese-English benchmark built from 2,892 Brazilian clinical case reports across 28 SciELO medical journals and 18 specialties, supporting diagnosis retrieval, differential diagnosis, exam recommendation and treatment planning. Evaluating MedGemma-27B, Sabiá-4, DeepSeek-R1 and o3-mini, the authors find the Portuguese-English gap is task-dependent: English leads by 7.5-12.1 accuracy points on diagnosis retrieval, but the advantage disappears on the other tasks.

Act As a Real Researcher: A Suite of Benchmarks Evaluating Frontier LLMs and Agentic Harnesses in Research Lifecycle
像真正的研究者一样行动：评估前沿 LLM 与 Agent 框架在研究生命周期中表现的基准套件
research agents research lifecycle agent benchmark scientific judgment
AARRI-Bench is the first benchmark in the AARR series, testing whether agents emulate the professionalism, thoroughness and nuanced reasoning of human researchers in granular research scenarios rather than only macro-level execution. Across frontier models and agentic harnesses the best configuration, Mini-SWE-Agent with Claude Opus 4.7, reached only 68.3% success, frequently overlooking details obvious to real human researchers.

arxiv:2606.07834 arXiv ✓
Cherry-pick Override: LLM Judges Under-use the Non-Directional Verdicts Their Contract Authorizes
Cherry-pick Override：LLM 评审未充分利用其契约授权的非方向性裁决
LLM judges fact verification verdict calibration evidence conflict
The paper defines directional overcommitment, where an LLM judge returns Supports or Refutes on a claim whose contract authorizes a non-directional verdict such as Conflicting or Not Enough Evidence. On AVeriTeC's gold-Conflicting subset a four-option typed panel committed directionally on 18.7% of claims and four frozen judges on 21.3-48.0%; asked in a fresh context, two judges identified the conflict on about two thirds of their own CPO claims, versus under 0.12 for claims they had committed correctly.

arxiv:2606.07515 arXiv ✓
How reliable are LLMs when it comes to playing dice?
LLM 玩骰子有多可靠？
probabilistic reasoning token bias chain-of-thought discrete probability
The authors benchmark eight state-of-the-art models on standard and counterintuitive discrete-probability problems, with and without chain-of-thought prompting. Average accuracy was 0.96 on standard problems but 0.59 on counterintuitive ones; performance dropped by over 20% when canonical formulations were disguised and by up to 34% with misleading suggestions embedded in the prompt, with no model immune.

Unsupervised Continual Clustering via Forward-Backward Knowledge Distillation
通过前向-后向知识蒸馏的无监督持续聚类
continual learning catastrophic forgetting knowledge distillation clustering
The authors introduce Unsupervised Continual Clustering and propose FBCC, which uses a continual teacher network with a clustering projector plus lightweight task-specific students in a dual-phase forward-backward distillation process. Across four benchmark datasets it improves clustering accuracy over continual-learning baselines while reducing catastrophic forgetting without storing past data.

VisualFLIP: Do Predictions Depend on Task-Critical Visual Evidence in Multimodal Reasoning?
VisualFLIP：多模态推理的预测是否依赖任务关键视觉证据？
multimodal reasoning visual evidence perturbation benchmark grounding
VisualFLIP is a paired benchmark of 1,374 images organized as same-question perturbation pairs across cardinality, attribute, spatial and logic tasks, used to evaluate 24 multimodal models on whether their correct answers actually depend on task-critical visual evidence.
