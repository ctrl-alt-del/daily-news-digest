Database Context Compression for Text-to-SQL on Real-World Large Databases
面向真实大型数据库 Text-to-SQL 的数据库上下文压缩
database context compression schema linking text-to-SQL Spider 2.0
The paper reframes Text-to-SQL performance on enterprise databases as a database representation problem and proposes DBCC, a model-agnostic middleware that applies the SGCF principle to offline compression of schemas, semantic descriptions and documentation, plus lightweight online evidence purification. On Spider 2.0-Snow and BIRD it cuts input context by up to two orders of magnitude (from 2.6M to 34.7K tokens on the largest subset), raises schema-linking strict recall from 0% to 56.5% under DeepSeek-V3.2, and improves execution accuracy by 1.8–1.9% across three Text-to-SQL systems.

labor-market evaluation economic exposure benchmark coverage occupational tasks
EconEvals is an open-source evaluation suite measuring language-model capabilities across tasks, work activities and occupations in the US labor economy, grounded where possible in real user queries and supplemented with synthetic data. The authors report improved coverage over OpenAI's GDPval benchmark at 500x lower cost and estimate that current models could save workers substantial time on at least half their tasks in 47% of occupations, while observed Claude usage is low for 79% of tasks with predicted savings.

OpenAI arxiv:2606.28235 arXiv ✓
For Coding Agents, the Repository Matters
对编程智能体而言，代码仓库才是关键
coding agents repository effects agent oversight pull requests
Using multilevel models on agent pull requests from the AIDev dataset and human pull requests in the same public GitHub repositories, the study finds that about two-fifths of the variation in how long agent pull requests take to be resolved lies between repositories. Across 280 repositories the between-repository variance is 1.76 times as large for agent as for human pull requests, leading the author to argue that oversight and evaluation of coding agents should treat the repository, not only the agent, as a unit.

arxiv:2606.28194 arXiv ✓
COCOLogic-V2: Identifying Logical Inconsistencies via Truly Hard-Negatives
COCOLogic-V2：通过真正困难的负样本识别逻辑不一致
visual inductive reasoning hard negatives first-order logic concept bottleneck models
COCOLogic-V2 is an object-centric dataset for visual inductive reasoning on real-world images that covers a broad subset of first-order logic, with samples split into positive variants plus near-boundary and far-from-boundary negatives for fine-grained diagnosis of model accountability. Evaluations show models separate positives from far-from-boundary samples well but fail on near-boundary ones, with perceptual noise and large rule-induced search spaces adding difficulty in few-shot settings.

arxiv:2606.28524 arXiv ✓
Developmental Trajectories of Situation Modeling and Mentalizing in Transformer Language Models
Transformer 语言模型中情境建模与心理理论的发展轨迹
theory of mind developmental evaluation situation modeling false belief task
Tracing mental-state reasoning across training stages in the Olmo2 and Pythia suites, the authors find above-chance false-belief-task performance depends on model size and sufficient training volume, emerges relatively late in pretraining, and is most improved by SFT and DPO in the False Belief, Implicit condition. Situation modeling generally precedes and exceeds false-belief accuracy, yet models remain fragile: non-factive verbs such as 'thinks' increase false-belief attributions even in the true-belief condition.

arxiv:2606.28570 arXiv ✓
Digitizing Coaching Intelligence: An Agentic Framework for Holistic Athlete Profiling using VLM and RAG
数字化教练智能：基于 VLM 与 RAG 的运动员整体画像智能体框架
multimodal RAG agentic workflow athlete profiling LLM-as-a-judge
The paper presents an LLM-based hybrid agentic framework, orchestrated with LangGraph, that combines MediaPipe kinematic tracking with Llama-4-scout vision-language reasoning to profile athletes against Sports Authority of India assessment protocols. It introduces a 3x3 Smart Grid temporal chunking strategy reported to cut computational overhead by over 88%, an LLM-as-a-Judge self-correction loop, and a ChromaDB-backed RAG pipeline for natural-language semantic queries over athlete records.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2606.28182 arXiv ✓
LLawCo: Learning Laws of Cooperation for Modeling Embodied Multi-Agent Behavior
LLawCo：学习协作法则以建模具身多智能体行为
embodied agents multi-agent cooperation behavioral laws chain-of-thought
LLawCo lets embodied LLM agents reflect on past failures to extract misaligned behavioral patterns, derive high-level behavioral laws such as 'Talk when necessary' and 'Wait for partner', and incorporate them into their chains of thought via supervised fine-tuning. The authors also introduce the PARTNR-Dialog benchmark and report average success-rate gains of 4.5% on PARTNR-Dialog and 6.8% on TDW-MAT across four backbone LLMs over state-of-the-art open-source communicative agent frameworks.
