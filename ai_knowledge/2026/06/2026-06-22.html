TRACE：面向企业LLM工具检索的业务规则驱动推理课程
tool retrieval enterprise LLM reasoning trace recall
Proposes a two-stage curriculum that trains an enterprise LLM to emit a reasoning trace before a JSON list of tool tokens; on a catalog of more than 8,300 tools it reports about 86% and 60% recall on two domains with single-beam greedy decoding, versus embedding baselines of about 27% and 52%.

arxiv:2606.23915 arXiv ✓
Do LLM Attribution Metrics Transfer? Auditing Retrieval-Augmented Generation Evaluation Across Datasets and Constructs
LLM归因指标可以迁移吗？跨数据集与构念审计RAG评估
attribution metrics RAG evaluation metric transfer LLM-as-judge
Audits eight automatic attribution scorers across three evaluation constructs and finds that none stays within the 95% confidence interval of the best scorer on every dataset; on generated-answer attribution, per-dataset rankings invert (Kendall tau = -0.64) and an NLI scorer that is best on short claims falls to chance (AUROC 0.53) on long-form answers, while a prompt-based LLM judge avoids collapse but is about 100x costlier.

RASC+：面向临床值集编写的检索约束型LLM裁决
