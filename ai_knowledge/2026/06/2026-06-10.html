Pythagoras-Prover: Advancing Efficient Formal Proving via Augmented Lean Formalisation
Pythagoras-Prover：通过增强 Lean 形式化推进高效形式证明
theorem proving Lean formalization diffusion prover self-distillation open-source models
Pythagoras-Prover is a compute-efficient open-source family of Lean theorem provers spanning 4B and 32B autoregressive models plus a 4B diffusion-based prover that iteratively refines proofs at inference. The 4B model reportedly surpasses DeepSeek-Prover-V2-671B at pass@32 on MiniF2F-Test (86.1% vs 82.4%) with about 167x fewer parameters, while the 32B model sets an open-source state of the art at 93.0% and solves 93 of 672 PutnamBench problems.

TrajGenAgent: A Hierarchical LLM Agent for Human Mobility Trajectory Generation
TrajGenAgent：面向人类移动轨迹生成的分层 LLM Agent
hierarchical agents POI retrieval synthetic trajectories plausibility evaluation
TrajGenAgent is a semantic-aware hierarchical LLM-agent framework that generates human mobility trajectories without model fine-tuning: an LLM synthesizes an activity chain via in-context learning, then a deterministic workflow grounds each activity using personalized POI retrieval, distance-aware location selection, and kinematics-aware travel-time propagation. It also introduces an anomaly-detection-based evaluation of behavioral and semantic plausibility.

GRIP: Feedback-Guided Prompt Retrieval for Large Multimodal Models
GRIP：面向大型多模态模型的反馈引导提示检索
in-context example retrieval multimodal models feedback-guided retrieval transfer
GRIP is a learnable vision-only retrieval framework that uses feedback from large multimodal models, rather than feature similarity alone, to choose in-context examples. It improves over similarity-based retrieval on Qwen2.5-VL-7B across classification, captioning, and VQA, and retrievers trained with one open model reportedly transfer to GPT-4o and Gemini without retraining.

PersonaDrive: Human-Style Retrieval-Augmented VLA Agents for Closed-Loop Driving Simulation
PersonaDrive：面向闭环驾驶仿真的人类风格检索增强 VLA Agent
retrieval-augmented agents vision-language-action demonstration retrieval driving simulation
PersonaDrive is a vision-language-action driving agent whose style comes from retrieved human demonstrations, collected from 15 participants driving CARLA under conservative, neutral, and aggressive instructions. It trains a lightweight retrieval head over per-style databases, so switching style means searching a different FAISS index without retraining; on Bench2Drive it improves Driving Score by 4.6% over SimLingo and 2.5% over HiP-AD.

通过波斯谚语条件故事生成研究 LLM 的受约束语义解压
semantic decompression cultural knowledge story generation evaluation
The paper frames proverb-conditioned story generation as constrained semantic decompression and introduces PAND, a Persian dataset pairing proverbs with human-written stories and explicit meanings. It finds a persistent decompression gap: models often produce fluent narratives that fail to faithfully instantiate the underlying moral and causal structure, though explicit reasoning and iterative refinement partially mitigate these failures.

AI 评审真的看清全貌了吗？攻击与防御多模态同行评审

PaperGuard is a benchmark for evaluating and defending AI-generated peer review against domain-specific cross-modal attacks, combining a multimodal peer-review dataset, black-box prompt injections and white-box text/figure perturbations, and a chunk-based embedding-search defense. EWAM: An Enhanced World Action Model for Closed-Loop Online Adaptation in Embodied Intelligence
EWAM：面向具身智能闭环在线适应的增强世界动作模型
world models experience memory online adaptation embodied AI
EWAM adds four lightweight neural layers to a frozen Cosmos3 world-action backbone for closed-loop online adaptation: an experience memory layer, an anomaly-detection layer, a policy-routing layer, and an action-correction layer, integrated differentiably into the forward path. Evaluated under a zero-shot protocol without task-specific demonstrations or backbone fine-tuning, it targets reducing the deployment data needed for new task layouts.

EvoFlux: Inference-Time Evolution of Executable Tool Workflows for Compact Agents
EvoFlux：面向小型 Agent 的可执行工具工作流推理时演化
tool use evolutionary search MCP small language models
EvoFlux repairs tool plans for small language models through an evolutionary loop over structured graphs of tool calls and dependencies, executing candidates against live tool servers and using errors to replace tools, fix parameters, insert steps, or remove redundancies. On held-out MCP-Bench tasks spanning 28 live servers and 250 tools, it raises execution success from roughly 3% to 17-24% across four small planners.

Keep Policy Gradient in Charge: Sibling-Guided Credit Distillation for Long-Horizon Tool-Use Agents
让策略梯度掌舵：面向长程工具使用 Agent 的兄弟引导信用蒸馏
credit assignment tool-use agents GRPO distillation
Sibling-Guided Credit Distillation uses distillation for bounded credit weighting rather than as a competing actor loss: mixed sibling rollouts are summarized by an external LLM into a training-only credit reference that reshapes GRPO token advantages. It reports higher held-out point estimates than GRPO-family comparators, with AppWorld TGC rising from 42.9 to 45.6 on test_normal and the tau^3-airline evaluator score from 0.583 to 0.602.

重新思考 LLM 的心理测量评估：自我报告何时以及为何能预测行为
