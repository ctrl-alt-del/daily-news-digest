Budgeted Act-or-Defer Multi-Agent LLM Deliberation with Local Reliability Bounds
带局部可靠性边界的预算化行动或延期多智能体 LLM 审议
multi-agent deliberation deferral policy reliability bounds calibration
The paper formulates multi-agent deliberation as budgeted act-or-defer decision making: at each round the system maps the debate prefix to a low-dimensional state, computes a k-nearest-neighbor lower confidence bound on correctness, and acts only when the bound exceeds a user-specified reliability threshold. On six benchmarks against nine baselines it uses 9–12% of the pre-declared budget on activated datasets, reaching up to 84% automation and 96% acted-on accuracy, and defers on stress-test datasets rather than forcing unreliable automation.

arxiv:2606.29580 arXiv ✓
MAM-AI: An On-Device Medical Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for Nurses and Midwives in Zanzibar
MAM-AI：面向桑给巴尔护士与助产士的端侧医疗检索增强生成系统
on-device RAG medical QA offline deployment evaluation harness
MAM-AI is a medical question-answering assistant for nurse-midwives in Zanzibar that runs entirely on a commodity Android device, embedding questions with a 300M EmbeddingGemma model and answering from 87 curated guideline documents (63,650 passages) with a 4B int4 Gemma generator, fully offline. Evaluation found on-device retrieval essentially solved — the embedder ranked third of seven retrievers — but the small generator did not improve with retrieved context; the deployed model was about twice as faithful to its sources, and a redesigned prompt cut deflection from 33% to 3%.

The Verbose Context Problem in Medical Records
医疗记录中的冗长上下文问题
context compression clinical records benchmark design population health
The paper defines the verbose context problem, where structured concepts have token-inefficient textual representations, and introduces PopMedQA, a benchmark of computational tasks over groups of longitudinal patient records built with the neopatient generation library. Ablations covering prompting strategies, prompt compression and agentic decomposition find that domain-independent methods fail to alleviate the problem, leaving room to exploit domain-specific structure for population-scale reasoning.

arxiv:2606.29502 arXiv ✓
UCOB: Learning to Utilize and Evolve Agentic Skills via Credit-Aware On-Policy Bidirectional Self-Distillation
UCOB：通过信用感知的在线策略双向自蒸馏学习利用与演化智能体技能
skill memory self-distillation on-policy learning utility-aware retrieval
UCOB treats skill-conditioned and no-skill prompts as two on-policy context views of the same model, compares their return-to-go at the same task and anchor state, and uses the higher-return view as a local teacher to internalize useful skills and correct misleading ones. It also guides skill-memory updates, utility-aware retrieval and reflection self-training; on ALFWorld, WebShop and Search-QA it outperforms skill-free RL, skill-memory and self-distillation baselines, with up to 23.5 and 18.0 point gains on ALFWorld and WebShop.

A Survey on Foundations and Frontiers of Multimodal Agentic Frameworks: Techniques and Applications
多模态智能体框架的基础与前沿综述：技术与应用
multimodal agents agent memory modality fusion taxonomy
This survey examines how multimodality shapes the core functional modules of agentic frameworks — perception, reasoning, planning, memory and action — tracing the evolution from text-centric agents to multimodal systems. It organizes work through a modality-centric taxonomy covering delegated, late-fusion and early-fusion architectures, reviews applications in robotics, GUI and web navigation, multimedia generation and long-form video understanding, and discusses efficiency-scalability trade-offs.

arxiv:2606.29545 arXiv ✓
AURORA: Asymmetry and Update-Induced Rotation for Robust Hallucination Detection in Large Language Models
AURORA：基于不对称性与更新诱发旋转的鲁棒幻觉检测
hallucination detection gradient dynamics model internals cross-dataset transfer
AURORA detects hallucinations by shifting from static hidden-state probes to weight-gradient dynamics, using two features: the skewness of cosine similarity between weight matrices and their gradient update directions, and a rotation ratio measuring how updates reorient singular-vector bases via SVD. It reports strong detection across four model families and four benchmark datasets, scales across model sizes, and transfers to out-of-domain tasks including mathematical reasoning and vision-language scenarios.

arxiv:2606.29571 arXiv ✓
Anisotropy Decides Cosine vs. Rank Metrics for Text Embeddings
各向异性决定文本嵌入的余弦相似度与秩度量之争
embedding anisotropy cosine similarity similarity metrics representation geometry
A study of nineteen parameter-free similarity metrics on nineteen encoders across seven datasets finds that cosine is best when an encoder spreads variance evenly, while rank-based and L1-type metrics beat cosine by a clear margin when variance concentrates in a few dominant directions, a gain of around 20% on average. The fraction of variance held by the single most dominant dimension predicts the benefit across all encoders with a rank correlation of 0.86 and linear correlation of 0.95; projecting out dominant directions restores cosine's advantage.
