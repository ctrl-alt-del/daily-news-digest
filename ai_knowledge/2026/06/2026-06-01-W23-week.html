PIXELRAG：网页截图在检索增强生成中胜过文本
visual RAG pixel retrieval visual embeddings screenshot datastore multimodal QA
PixelRAG represents websites in their native visual form and performs retrieval and reading entirely in pixel space, scaling to a 30-million-screenshot Wikipedia datastore built on a fine-tuned Qwen3-VL-Embedding. It outperforms text-based RAG baselines by up to 18.1% on text-centric, multimodal and agentic benchmarks, and image compression cuts token cost by up to 3x at lower resolutions while maintaining accuracy.

MLEvolve：自动机器学习算法发现的自进化框架
self-evolving agents experience memory MLE-Bench tree search algorithm discovery
MLEvolve extends tree search to Progressive MCGS so information flows across branches through graph-based reference edges, and adds Retrospective Memory combining a cold-start domain knowledge base with dynamic global memory for task-specific experience reuse. On MLE-Bench it reports state-of-the-art average medal rate and valid submission rate under a 12-hour budget, and also outperforms specialized methods including AlphaEvolve on mathematical algorithm optimization tasks.

Rotate2Think: Geometric Priming via Orthogonal Rotation to Improve Language Model Reasoning
Rotate2Think：通过正交旋转进行几何启动以提升语言模型推理
reasoning traces embedding geometry orthogonal Procrustes training-free
The authors show that input embeddings and thinking embeddings are highly conical around distinct mean directions, and cast the input-to-thinking transition as a rotation solvable in closed form via orthogonal Procrustes analysis. Rotate2Think estimates this rotation from a small set of correctly solved examples and injects a synthetic thinking vector at inference, improving accuracy in 30 of 32 model-benchmark configurations and generalizing zero-shot to multimodal reasoning on MATH-Vision.
