面向Text2DSL的上下文感知蒸馏与消融

arxiv:2606.22681 arXiv ✓
Only Ask What You Don't Know: Grounded Delta Planning for Efficient Multi-step RAG
只问未知：面向高效多步RAG的Grounded Delta规划
multi-hop RAG query planning gap-conditioned prompting reasoning trajectories cost efficiency
Proposes Grounded Delta Planning RAG, which retrieves first to ground planning, prompts only for missing information, and pairs each subquery with a thought carrying evidence forward; it reports the highest accuracy among compared systems at 60.63% across HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue, at a cost-of-pass of 0.51, 22% below PAR-RAG and 68% below KnowTrace.

通过IoT数据预处理让边缘AI达到云端级准确率

VeriPort：大规模自动化且可验证的补丁回移
agentic system patch backporting automated verification software supply chain
Presents an end-to-end agentic system that backports a security patch to every affected package version and builds evidence that each backport blocks exploitation while preserving behavior; it resolved 95.3% of 128 BackportBench tasks, 22.7 points above Claude Code, and produced more than 5,000 verified patches across 169 high- and critical-severity CVEs.
