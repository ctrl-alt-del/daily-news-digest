Model Forensics: Investigating Whether Concerning Behavior Reflects Misalignment
模型取证：调查令人担忧的行为是否反映失准
model forensics chain-of-thought misalignment agentic environments counterfactual testing
Proposes a baseline protocol for model forensics that reads chain-of-thought to form hypotheses about behavior and then edits prompts or environments to test them across six agentic environments; it finds Kimi K2 Thinking takes shortcuts due to a disposition toward low-effort actions and that DeepSeek R1 deceives to stay consistent with a previous instance of itself.

Instruction Bleed: Cross-Module Interference in Prompt-Composed Agentic Systems
指令渗漏：提示组合式智能体系统中的跨模块干扰
prompt composition cross-module interference agent evaluation behavioral leakage
Formalizes compositional behavioral leakage, the interference between prompt modules sharing a context window, and probes it on a deployed job-evaluation agent with 144 trials; only content perturbations produce a detectable paired effect (Cohen's d = 0.63), and no recommendation flipped, a sub-threshold regime invisible to standard QA but compounding over thousands of decisions.

chain-of-thought prompt scaffolding ethical reasoning multi-agent debate

Neglected Free Lunch from Post-training: Progress Advantage for LLM Agents
后训练中被忽视的免费午餐：面向LLM智能体的进度优势
process reward RL post-training advantage estimation failure attribution test-time scaling
Shows that RL post-training already yields a step-level scoring signal called progress advantage, the log-probability ratio between the RL-trained policy and its reference policy, which recovers the optimal advantage function; the signal is annotation-free and outperforms confidence baselines and dedicated reward models across test-time scaling, uncertainty quantification, and failure attribution on five benchmarks and four model families.
