Beyond Surface Forms: A Comprehensive, Mechanism-Oriented Taxonomy of Indirect Linguistic Encoding for LLM-Based Coded Language Detection
超越表面形式：面向LLM编码语言检测的间接语言编码机制分类法
prompt taxonomy coded language content moderation LLM prompting
Proposes a mechanism-oriented taxonomy of indirect linguistic encoding that abstracts away from communicative goals and categorizes how meaning is encoded and recovered; embedded in LLM prompts, it beats four existing taxonomies and a no-taxonomy baseline on 2,000 annotated TikTok and Bluesky posts, with document-level gains of +3.8 accuracy and +4.3 macro-F1 points for GPT-5.4, and also leads on 800 Chinese coded-language reviews.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2606.27275 arXiv ✓
How Surprising Is Historical Italian to Language Models? Tokenization Tax, Comprehension Tax, and a Simple Mitigation
历史意大利语对语言模型有多意外？分词税、理解税与一种简单缓解方法
historical text tokenization surprisal semantic retrieval temporal context
Decomposes historical-language difficulty into tokenization cost, predictive uncertainty, semantic robustness, and context sensitivity across 17th-century Italian, 19th-century Italian, and 18th-century Russian texts; tokenization penalties are comparable (25-30% inflation), but 17th-century Italian is on average 2.4 times more surprising than its modern equivalent while embedding similarity stays above 0.85, and a minimal temporal context prompt cuts surprisal by about 60%.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2606.27359 arXiv ✓
When are likely answers right? On Sequence Probability and Correctness in LLMs
可能的答案何时正确？论LLM中的序列概率与正确性
sequence probability decoding self-consistency correctness
Quantifies when sequence probability aligns with correctness across decoding methods, hyperparameters, prompt-answer pairs, and repeated responses; higher sequence probability is often predictive of correctness within a fixed dataset, but this does not generally transfer to decoding decisions, and it is not a good indicator of correctness for responses to the same prompt.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2606.27611 arXiv ✓
COOPA: A Modular LLM Agent Architecture for Operations Research Problems
COOPA：面向运筹学问题的模块化LLM智能体架构
provenance solver routing operations research agent architecture
A modular LLM-agent architecture for operations research that combines confidence-based iterative modeling, element-level provenance linking formulations to source text, and routing to specialized solver agents; across three OR benchmarks and eight LLM backbones it achieves the best macro-average accuracy on six backbones, improving over the strongest baseline by up to 6.7 percentage points.
