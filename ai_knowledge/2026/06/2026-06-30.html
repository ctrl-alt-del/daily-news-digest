Generative Skill Composition for LLM Agents
面向 LLM 智能体的生成式技能组合
skill composition skill library structured prediction SkillsBench
The paper formalizes structured skill composition — jointly predicting which skills, how many and in what order — and proposes SkillComposer, a constrained autoregressive decoder over skill identifiers that produces subset, count and order in a single pass. On SkillsBench with GPT-5.2-Codex and Gemini-3-Pro-Preview it raises pass rates by 23.1 and 18.2 points over the no-skill baseline, surpassing top-3 retrieval and matching gold-skill retrieval at lower prompt-token cost.

arxiv:2608.20389 arXiv ✓
Representation Affects Retrieval: A Case Study of Skill Discovery and Routing in a Multimodal Agent Harness
表示影响检索：多模态智能体框架中技能发现与路由的案例研究
skill routing in-context selection tool skills ablation study
This case study examines how Tinycloud, a production multimodal video agent harness, represents skills for its planner, distinguishing tool-skills that wrap single APIs from workflow-skills that orchestrate calls into a named deliverable, exposed either as inlined bodies or one-line listings. A six-task ablation across all-on, default and all-off exposure shows full autoload selects the gold skill on every task, while the production default misroutes one task because lexical overlap with an autoloaded tool-skill pulls planner attention away from a listed workflow-skill.

arxiv:2607.00272 arXiv ✓
ASPIRE: Agentic /Skills Discovery for Robotics
ASPIRE：面向机器人的智能体技能发现
skill library robotics code-as-policy continual learning
ASPIRE is a continual learning system that autonomously writes and refines robot control programs in a code-as-policy paradigm, compounding validated fixes into a reusable skill library through a closed-loop execution engine, an expanding skill library and evolutionary search. It reports gains of up to 77% on manipulation tasks under perturbation, 72% on Robosuite bimanual handover and 32% on long-horizon household tasks, with 31% zero-shot success on unseen long-horizon tasks versus 4% for prior methods.

arxiv:2607.00159 arXiv ✓
Identifying and Resolving Pitfalls of Knowledge-Based VQA Benchmarks: Auditing, Repairing, and Augmenting
识别与解决基于知识的 VQA 基准缺陷：审计、修复与增强
benchmark auditing knowledge-grounded reasoning visual question answering evaluation protocol
The authors audit knowledge-based VQA benchmarks and find systematic violations of the assumptions behind answer accuracy — missing or contradicted answers, underspecified questions and visually trivial single-entity scenes — that distort model rankings and overestimate reasoning. They propose an audit-and-repair protocol plus controlled multi-entity augmentation, and re-evaluation under corrected settings yields markedly different performance trends.

arxiv:2608.20391 arXiv ✓
ImmigrationReason: A Structured Dataset of U.S. Immigration Appeals for Legal Reasoning Research
ImmigrationReason：面向法律推理研究的美国移民上诉结构化数据集
legal dataset structured extraction reasoning benchmark high-stakes domains
ImmigrationReason is a structured dataset derived from 12,375 non-precedent USCIS Administrative Appeals Office decisions spanning 2005–2026, with each record capturing the applicable legal framework, per-criterion evidence-sufficiency findings, verbatim adjudicator criticism, citations and dispositions. Extraction was validated through a three-pass pipeline with Opus 4.7 adjudication and expert review of 500 records; the dataset documents nearly 9,000 AAO-identified legal errors and spans the 2016 Dhanasar rule change.

Ansari: A Retrieval-Grounded Islamic AI Assistant -- Architecture, Deployment, and Lessons from 140,000 Conversations
Ansari：基于检索的伊斯兰 AI 助手——架构、部署与 14 万次对话的经验
retrieval-grounded QA agentic retrieval religious knowledge deployment lessons
Ansari is a deployed, retrieval-grounded Islamic AI assistant that has handled more than 140,000 conversations across 25+ languages since June 2023, using an agentic retrieval loop over authenticated Qur'an, hadith, fiqh and tafsir corpora with citations. It tops the public IslamicMMLU leaderboard ahead of frontier models and is competitive on IslamicLegalBench while strongly resisting false premises, and the authors argue that grounding is necessary but not sufficient for faith- or values-sensitive deployments.
