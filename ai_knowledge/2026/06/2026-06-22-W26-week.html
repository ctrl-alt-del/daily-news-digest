各向异性决定文本嵌入的余弦相似度与秩度量之争

A study of nineteen parameter-free similarity metrics on nineteen encoders across seven datasets finds that cosine is best when an encoder spreads variance evenly, while rank-based and L1-type metrics beat cosine by a clear margin when variance concentrates in a few dominant directions, a gain of around 20% on average.
