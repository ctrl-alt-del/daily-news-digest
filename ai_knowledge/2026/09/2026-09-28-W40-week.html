ARCagent: An Adaptive Retrieval Calibration Agent for Clinical Question Answering
ARCagent：面向临床问答的自适应检索校准智能体
clinical QA retrieval calibration conflict registry LLM-as-judge
ARCagent pairs a 1,706-chunk, 10-source knowledge base and a structured inter-guideline conflict registry with a conflict-aware retrieval calibration pipeline that re-ranks evidence using query-specific focus and conflict signals. On a benchmark scored by LLM-as-Judge, which the authors say avoids a 10.1-percentage-point underestimation from keyword matching, it reaches 95.3% and outperforms all base LLMs.

Breaking Babel: A Self-Evolving Multi-Agent System for Long-Form Subtitle Translation
打破巴别塔：面向长篇字幕翻译的自演化多智能体系统
multi-agent translation episodic memory self-evolution MQM evaluation
SMART is a self-evolving multi-agent system for long-form subtitle translation: a test-time training stage builds persistent episode-level memory, dynamic routing and a Mixture-of-Agents layer translate partial sentences, and a judge-refine loop updates agent prompts and routing without retraining the base LLM. The authors release the Subtitle Arena and SubMQM framework and report the best MQM score across all 15 directions, a 6.9% lower average penalty than the strongest competing agent system, and 4.50/5 in human evaluation.

Ontology-Grounded, Reasoner-Verified Benchmarks for Evaluating LLM Reasoning in Scientific AI
以本体为据、经推理器验证的科学 AI 大模型推理评测基准
ontology grounding OWL reasoning benchmark generation scientific reasoning
The authors propose a pipeline that automatically generates ontology-grounded multiple-choice benchmarks from any sufficiently axiomatised OWL 2 ontology, with correct answers grounded in the ontology by design and distractors whose incorrectness is formally verified by an OWL reasoner. Applied to Pizza, PMDco and DOID, it produces 112, 2,491 and 15,216 questions; six LLMs score 41.1–76.8% zero-shot, well above the 25% random baseline.

LVMH: A Meta-Optimization Harness for Long-Video Agent Memory
LVMH：长视频智能体记忆的元优化框架
agent memory memory policy search long video meta-optimization
LVMH is an automated multi-agent harness that frames long-video memory-policy development as multi-objective code search around a fixed answer model, separating implementation from independent review and keeping persistent meta-memory of past proposals, traces and failures. It discovers SEEM, a text-first, vision-second policy that reaches 65.7% average accuracy across three benchmarks with GPT-5, 7.7 points above the prior state of the art, at roughly 32x lower evaluation token use and 91x shorter response time.

DeepRewind: Predicting and Repairing Premature Commitments in Deep Research Agents
DeepRewind：预测并修复深度研究智能体中的过早承诺
epistemic state graph deep research agents premature commitment rollback
DeepRewind adds a control layer that represents a deep-research agent's evolving epistemic state as a typed graph of sources, evidence, claims, hypotheses, assumptions and commitments. A prompt-based world model estimates the reversibility of each intermediate conclusion, a controller blocks risky commitments and a monitor rolls back invalidated ones; across DRBench and LiveDRBench it improves insight recall by 3.6 percentage points and cuts premature commitments by 59.1% versus Open Deep Research.

GNA: Granular Neighbor Assembly for Retrieval-Augmented Multivariate Time-Series Forecasting
GNA：面向检索增强多变量时间序列预测的细粒度邻居组装
retrieval-augmented forecasting per-variate neighbors time-series forecasting causal retrieval
GNA is a retrieval layer for forecasting backbones that assembles neighbors at two granularities: whole past windows that keep variates coherent, and per-variate neighbors that take each variate's future from its own best-matching past. With a learned gate deciding how much to trust retrieved futures, it improves two Transformer backbones in 85 of 96 dataset-horizon settings and gives the lowest MSE on 8 of 12 standard benchmarks.

Contrastive Covariance Subspaces for Activation Steering
用于激活引导的对比协方差子空间
activation steering representation subspaces deconfounding controllability
Contrastive Covariance Steering is a training-free method that projects activation-steering vectors into a low-dimensional subspace obtained in closed form from a contrastive variance objective, retaining directions along which the target concept varies while suppressing directions shared with nuisance classes. Across three LLMs and four steering domains it beats all baseline steering methods in all 12 settings, with the largest relative improvements of 48–70% on AxBench and 13–27% on Emotion.

NAQD Env: A benchmark for selective withdrawal in language agents
NAQD Env：语言智能体选择性撤回基准
agent reliability selective withdrawal policy compliance benchmark
NAQD-Env is a synthetic benchmark that evaluates language agents' selective withdrawal — suspending affected actions, preserving unaffected work and resuming after repair — against a deterministic reference policy over explicit evidence, authorization and constraint dependencies. Across 3,150 model-prompt episodes, withdrawal recall is at most 0.06, no valid resumption is observed, and only one episode matches the full reference policy; supervised fine-tuning probes raise Qwen2.5-3B decision accuracy from 0.45–0.54 to 0.83–0.92.

AnchorPrompt：面向稳健音频语言模型的自蒸馏软提示
soft prompts audio-language models self-distillation answer consistency
AnchorPrompt keeps a large audio-language model frozen and learns a single block of prompt vectors inserted at the decoder input between the audio and question embeddings, trained by self-distillation over diverse audio and text perturbations.
