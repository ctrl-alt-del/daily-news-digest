ARCagent: An Adaptive Retrieval Calibration Agent for Clinical Question Answering
ARCagent：面向临床问答的自适应检索校准智能体
clinical QA retrieval calibration conflict registry LLM-as-judge
ARCagent pairs a 1,706-chunk, 10-source knowledge base and a structured inter-guideline conflict registry with a conflict-aware retrieval calibration pipeline that re-ranks evidence using query-specific focus and conflict signals. On a benchmark scored by LLM-as-Judge, which the authors say avoids a 10.1-percentage-point underestimation from keyword matching, it reaches 95.3% and outperforms all base LLMs.

DeepRewind: Predicting and Repairing Premature Commitments in Deep Research Agents
DeepRewind：预测并修复深度研究智能体中的过早承诺
epistemic state graph deep research agents premature commitment rollback
DeepRewind adds a control layer that represents a deep-research agent's evolving epistemic state as a typed graph of sources, evidence, claims, hypotheses, assumptions and commitments. A prompt-based world model estimates the reversibility of each intermediate conclusion, a controller blocks risky commitments and a monitor rolls back invalidated ones; across DRBench and LiveDRBench it improves insight recall by 3.6 percentage points and cuts premature commitments by 59.1% versus Open Deep Research.

Better Nearest Neighbor Graph Indices via (Efficient) LLM-Guided Pruning
借助（高效）LLM 引导剪枝改进最近邻图索引
ANN search graph pruning semantic retrieval LLM-guided indexing
LGP uses LLM reasoning to refine an existing approximate nearest neighbor graph index, replacing structurally low-value neighbors with LLM-selected alternatives while retaining the graph's sparsity and navigability. Experiments on semantic retrieval benchmarks show consistent end-to-end gains over both greedy graph search and LLM-based reranking on indices such as DiskANN and HNSW.

Cognitive Expert Language Models Better Align with the Corresponding Brain Systems
认知专家语言模型与对应大脑系统更契合
cognitive specialization model-brain alignment expert models fMRI evaluation
The authors build expert LLM variants for six cognitive domains through prompting and fine-tuning, then test whether each expert better predicts activity in the brain system associated with that domain. Across three base models and three fMRI datasets, each expert's representations align more closely with its matching brain system, while aggregate prediction accuracy stays largely unchanged.

In-Context Learning Amplifies a Latent Symbolic Circuit
上下文学习会放大潜在的符号电路
in-context learning symbolic circuits activation patching function vectors
Tracing a three-stage abstraction, induction and retrieval circuit across shot counts in three model families, the author finds the circuit is detectable and functional well before models achieve high accuracy. Per-head causal contribution grows up to 8x from 1-shot to 10-shot, and cross-shot activation patching raises accuracy from 1% to 56% at 0-shot and from 17% to 88% at 1-shot.

ATLAS: Aligned Transport of Latent Structure for Reliable World Model Planning
ATLAS：面向可靠世界模型规划的对齐潜在结构传输
latent geometry world models representation alignment planning
ATLAS is a training objective that preserves relational geometry from an informative encoder representation in the latent used for world-model planning, while calibrating its marginal distribution through Wasserstein embedding matching. Instantiated in LeWM, it improves goal-reaching success on PushT, TwoRoom and OGBench-Cube, with the largest gain on higher-novelty TwoRoom episodes.

arxiv:2609.36365 arXiv ✓
Engineering Simplicity: Simple Mechanism Interfaces Steer LLM Agents
工程化的简洁：简单的机制接口引导 LLM 智能体
agent interfaces mechanism design reasoning scaffolds behavioral evaluation
Across auctions and matching tasks, the authors compare interfaces that elicit a complete bid or ranking with sequential interfaces and vary reasoning scaffolds across four model families. The ascending auction interface substantially reduces bid deviations and explaining why truth-telling is safe improves choices, while prompts to plan through matching rounds or form beliefs about opponents worsen play; these behavioral gains do not appear in the agents' stated plans.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.36281 arXiv ✓
GNA: Granular Neighbor Assembly for Retrieval-Augmented Multivariate Time-Series Forecasting
GNA：面向检索增强多变量时间序列预测的细粒度邻居组装
retrieval-augmented forecasting per-variate neighbors time-series forecasting causal retrieval
GNA is a retrieval layer for forecasting backbones that assembles neighbors at two granularities: whole past windows that keep variates coherent, and per-variate neighbors that take each variate's future from its own best-matching past. With a learned gate deciding how much to trust retrieved futures, it improves two Transformer backbones in 85 of 96 dataset-horizon settings and gives the lowest MSE on 8 of 12 standard benchmarks.
→ arxiv.org

AdaKerNet: Neural Kernel Decoding for Task-Adaptive Prediction with Multimodal Large Models
AdaKerNet：面向多模态大模型任务自适应预测的神经核解码
multimodal embeddings kernel decoding frozen representations scarce labels
AdaKerNet is a task-adaptive neural kernel decoder that learns from a frozen multimodal model's representations using Lipschitz-controlled features and a reference kernel as a structural prior. Across BLIP-2, LLaVA-1.5, Qwen2.5-VL and Gemini Embedding 2 with text, audio, image and tabular inputs, it reports average error reductions of up to 41% over MLP, attention, autoencoder and kernel decoders in scarce-label regimes.
