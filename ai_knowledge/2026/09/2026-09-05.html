OpenDiscoveryTrace: Process Traces for Evaluating AI Scientist Workflows
OpenDiscoveryTrace：用于评估 AI 科学家工作流的过程轨迹
process traces AI scientist trajectory evaluation error analysis open dataset
OpenDiscoveryTrace releases 558 complete AI scientific agent trajectories with a structured nine-field-per-step trace covering thoughts, tool calls, observations, errors, revision triggers and confidence across 124 scientific tasks and seven models. Pilot analysis of 363 LLM-judged trajectories shows frontier models reach comparable 84–89% success rates but differ sharply in process, with Claude Opus 4.6 producing 30x more errors per trajectory than GPT-5.4.

Evaluating Deep-Search Agents under Hierarchical Web Evidence Poisoning
分层网络证据投毒下的深度搜索智能体评估
evidence poisoning deep-search agents source verification generative engine optimization agent evaluation
HAE-GEO is a benchmark that tracks deep-search agents from exposure to recovery under progressively persuasive web poisoning across three attack levels, using 72,039 clean pages and 770 poisoned pages per level over eight product categories. Evaluating 10 agents, the authors find evidence recognition degrades under apparent corroboration, agentic search improves final resistance without improving evidence recognition, and defense prompting increases verification but rarely converts it into recovery.

STQA: A Benchmark for Stock-Focused Tabular Question Answering over Historical and Forecasted Data
STQA：面向历史与预测数据的股票表格问答基准
tabular question answering financial agents tool coordination forecasting benchmark
STQA is an end-to-end benchmark for natural-language question answering over historical stock data, numerical forecasts and forecast-based reasoning, covering 4,417 stocks and 31,400 expert-templated question-answer pairs with intent and slot annotations. The accompanying SQFRS agent orchestrates SQL retrieval and time-series forecasting tools, and experiments show forecast-based reasoning remains a substantial challenge involving tool coordination under uncertainty.

CWF: A Collaborative Writing Framework for Personalized and Reliable Popular Science Writing
CWF：面向个性化且可靠科普写作的协作写作框架
fact-checking multi-agent debate confidence propagation personalization factual accuracy
The paper defines personalized and reliable popular science writing as a task requiring audience adaptation without factual distortion, and introduces a 39,134-entry dataset plus the PSCB benchmark that jointly scores adaptation and factual accuracy. Its DA-MoE model decouples audience adaptation from domain knowledge, and a multi-agent fact-checking mechanism uses role-specific debate and confidence propagation over a graph to verify claims when evidence is scarce.

arxiv:2609.06263 arXiv ✓
Beyond the Flag: Clinical Framing Closes the Moderation Gap in Suicide Risk Measurement
超越标记：临床框架弥合自杀风险测量中的审核差距
clinical framing severity measurement moderation APIs ordinal evaluation safety alignment
The authors release a benchmark of 516 r/SuicideWatch posts rated by a licensed psychiatrist on a four-level severity schema and compare moderation APIs, prompted LLMs and supervised baselines. Vendor APIs separate low- from high-risk posts well (0.860 high-risk F1) but measure severity poorly (0.395 macro F1), while clinically grounded zero-shot prompting raises severity macro F1 to 0.562, with expert-authored framing the effective lever rather than fine-tuning or multi-agent aggregation.

A Tutorial on Prompt Engineering: From Messy Thoughts to AI Workflows
提示工程教程：从混乱想法到 AI 工作流
prompt engineering prompt design agentic workflows structured critique verification
The tutorial frames prompt engineering as a discipline for turning informal human intent into structured AI work specifications, built from reusable moves such as defining the work, constructing only the context an answer depends on, choosing a role as an attention lens and stating affirmative quality targets. It adapts Occam's razor and Chekhov's gun to keep prompts lean and adds structured critique, verification and agentic operating loops with explicit boundaries for consequential tasks.

arxiv:2609.05993 arXiv ✓
Alignment by Stereotyping: How LLMs Sacrifice Individual Distinctiveness for Cultural Adaptation
以刻板印象对齐：LLM 如何为文化适配牺牲个体独特性
cultural alignment prototype retrieval demographic conditioning individual distinctiveness value alignment
Studying seven models including GPT-5.1 on the World Values Survey, the authors find that demographic profiles improve value alignment accuracy for most models but pull responses toward group centroids rather than preserving individual differences, a pattern they call alignment by stereotyping. Permutation tests certify that top-performing models compress individuals above the human baseline, and distributing demographic signals across conversational turns partially suppresses prototype retrieval.

arxiv:2609.06212 arXiv ✓
SLATE: Are AI-Generated Slides Educationally Effective? A Benchmark for Language Teaching Quality and Learner Knowledge Acquisition
SLATE：AI 生成的幻灯片是否具有教学效果？面向语言教学质量与学习者知识获得的基准
knowledge acquisition instructional design educational evaluation multimodal generation learning transfer
SLATE is described as the first benchmark evaluating AI-generated language-teaching slides through instructional effectiveness and learner knowledge acquisition, converting linguistics-olympiad puzzles from low-resource languages into 90 standardized teaching units and 1,133 assessable questions with pre-test and post-test near- and far-transfer sets. Using a VLM as a learner proxy, it finds content validity only weakly associated with learning gains while instructional design is robustly associated, and even frontier models can produce negative learning gains.
