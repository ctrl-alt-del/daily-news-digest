PARSER: Read in Parallel, Reason in Depth for Long-Context LLM Agents
PARSER：面向长上下文 LLM 智能体的并行阅读与深度推理
long-context agents parallel reading iterative retrieval reinforcement learning agent memory
PARSER decouples reading from reasoning by assigning lightweight subagents to individual document chunks that read in parallel while a lead agent runs iterative scatter–gather rounds, broadcasting queries, aggregating evidence and forming deeper follow-ups; only the lead agent is trained with reinforcement learning. On multi-hop QA with contexts from 7K to 896K tokens, a 4B-backbone PARSER beats the strongest sequential memory baseline by 5.7 points on average and 12.0 points at 896K tokens, and a 9B version surpasses DeepSeek-V4-Pro by 6.3 points while cutting latency up to 11x.

Which Part of the Context Layer Does the Work? Separating Semantic Content from Retrieval Scaffolding in Text-to-SQL Agents
上下文层的哪一部分在起作用？区分 text-to-SQL 智能体中的语义内容与检索脚手架
context layer semantic content retrieval scaffolding text-to-SQL ablation study
A four-arm ablation on DABStep across four models separates the semantic content of a curated context layer from the retrieval scaffolding that delivers it and from accompanying precomputed views. Content dominates, raising hard-task accuracy from 13.9% to 55.1%, 22.6% to 56.6%, 22.9% to 68.4% and 37.0% to 77.4%, while scaffolding without content is worth 0 to 5 points on two flash models and 14 to 15 on two frontier models; the contract also beats pasting the same knowledge into the prompt.

arxiv:2609.06530 arXiv ✓
Selective Knowledge Control for Continual GUI Agent Learning over Application Streams
面向应用流上持续 GUI 智能体学习的选择性知识控制
continual learning catastrophic forgetting knowledge retention GUI agents gradient surgery
The paper proposes activation-conditioned selective knowledge control for GUI agents that learn over streams of applications, using neuron-level gradient manipulation to protect highly activated MLP neurons that hold prior knowledge. When a new application arrives, gradients for unactivated neurons carrying specific knowledge are truncated while those for activated neurons carrying shared knowledge are orthogonally projected, and newly identified critical neurons are merged into a compact historical state; experiments on a multi-app sequential benchmark show reduced catastrophic forgetting with continued adaptation.

DrugReason: Dynamic Multi-View Reasoning over Knowledge Graph and Language Evidence for Drug Repurposing
DrugReason：面向药物重定位的知识图谱与语言证据动态多视角推理
knowledge graph reasoning drug repurposing multi-hop reasoning expert routing knowledge distillation
DrugReason combines knowledge-graph-based grounded multi-hop reasoning with LLM-generated mechanistic inference for drug repurposing, adaptively routing reasoning paths to specialized experts based on query context. A cross-expert distillation objective shares knowledge without sacrificing specialization, and experiments on PharmaDB, DDInter and DrugBank show gains over strong single-view baselines with interpretable routing-based predictions.

Companion-style QA Assistance in Ego-Vision
自我视角中的伴侣式问答辅助
streaming video QA multi-level memory multimodal reasoning egocentric video benchmark
BuddyVQA is a benchmark for companion-style question answering on egocentric streaming video, containing 21.6K questions linked to 6K highlight moments across 1,012 long first-person videos, with ego-deictic expressions and interactively chained questions. The MyBuddy assistant adds multimodal chain-of-thought reasoning, a question filter and multi-level memory for efficient retrieval under streaming QA, improving foundation-model performance on BuddyVQA and generalizing to other streaming and standard video QA benchmarks.

arxiv:2609.06831 arXiv ✓
NormViz: A Benchmark and Framework for Grounding Multimodal Reasoning in Global Cultures
NormViz：将多模态推理锚定于全球文化的基准与框架
cultural norms multimodal reasoning visual benchmark fine-tuning cross-cultural grounding
NormViz-Bench is a human-validated benchmark of 3,268 contrastive image pairs from 16 countries that asks whether a visually observable behavior conforms to, violates or is irrelevant to local social norms, requiring both images in a pair to be judged correctly. The authors also release a 64K-image training set with explanations; the strongest VLM solves only 26.6% and 21.6% of pairs, while fine-tuning on NormViz-Train improves Qwen3-VL 4B and 8B pair accuracy by up to 125% and 36% relative.

arxiv:2609.06771 arXiv ✓
AuthBench: A Large-Scale Multilingual Benchmark for Authorship Representation across Genres and Lengths
AuthBench：跨体裁与篇幅的大规模多语言作者身份表示基准
authorship representation same-author retrieval multilingual benchmark zero-shot evaluation representation learning
AuthBench contains 428,150 documents by 153,825 individuals across ten languages, nine primary genres, 66 fine-grained genres and four length buckets, supporting authorship attribution as same-author retrieval and verification as a binary decision. Benchmarking 47 neural models and three non-neural baselines under a unified zero-shot protocol, the best retrieval model reaches only 0.258 Success@5 and the best verification model 0.076 EER, showing authorship representation remains far from solved.

arxiv:2609.06783 arXiv ✓
AURA-Eval: Evaluation Framework for Acting Under Risk Awareness in LLM Agent Trajectories
AURA-Eval：LLM 智能体轨迹中的风险意识行动评估框架
risk awareness agent safety trajectory evaluation tool-use safety oversight
AURA-Eval truncates tool-use trajectories at safety-critical decision points and asks models to generate the next thought and action, varying six risk mechanisms and pairing scenarios by whether a safe fulfillment path exists. From 157 seed trajectories it builds 1,249 evaluation items and tests 20 models, finding unsafe-action rates higher when no safe path exists (27–40% for frontier models versus 5–7%) and that removing pre-execution oversight flips 52.3% of previously safe cases to unsafe.

SRD-GUARD: A Defense Framework of LLMs via Semantic Rewriting and Joint Multi-Model Scoring for Latent Intent Exposure
SRD-GUARD：通过语义重写与多模型联合评分防御潜在意图暴露的 LLM 框架
jailbreak defense semantic rewriting intent detection multi-model scoring safety evaluation
SRD-GUARD is a parameter-free black-box jailbreak defense that generates five semantically related rewrites of a prompt to strip contextual packaging, then has multiple LLM safety scorers rate the original and rewrites on a continuous risk scale. A decision module combines absolute thresholds with relative risk changes to intercept, preserve or warn, achieving average defense success rates of 91.44% and 100% on Llama-3-8B-Uncensored and DeepSeek-V4-Flash with over-refusal rates of 8.00% and 12.00%.
