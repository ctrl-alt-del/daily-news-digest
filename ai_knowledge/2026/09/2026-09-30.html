Ontology-Grounded, Reasoner-Verified Benchmarks for Evaluating LLM Reasoning in Scientific AI
以本体为据、经推理器验证的科学 AI 大模型推理评测基准
ontology grounding OWL reasoning benchmark generation scientific reasoning
The authors propose a pipeline that automatically generates ontology-grounded multiple-choice benchmarks from any sufficiently axiomatised OWL 2 ontology, with correct answers grounded in the ontology by design and distractors whose incorrectness is formally verified by an OWL reasoner. Applied to Pizza, PMDco and DOID, it produces 112, 2,491 and 15,216 questions; six LLMs score 41.1–76.8% zero-shot, well above the 25% random baseline.

arxiv:2610.00706 arXiv ✓
AnchorPrompt: Self-Distilled Soft Prompts for Robust Audio-Language Models
AnchorPrompt：面向稳健音频语言模型的自蒸馏软提示
soft prompts audio-language models self-distillation answer consistency
AnchorPrompt keeps a large audio-language model frozen and learns a single block of prompt vectors inserted at the decoder input between the audio and question embeddings, trained by self-distillation over diverse audio and text perturbations. Across three models and three benchmarks it improves answer consistency, raises clean accuracy in six of nine model-benchmark pairs with at most a 1.2% impact on the rest, and reduces hallucinations under severe audio corruption while keeping false refusals on clean audio rare.
→ arxiv.org

PLDiv: Measuring Dataset Diversity and Semantic Collapse via Persistence Landscapes
PLDiv：通过持久景观衡量数据集多样性与语义坍缩
dataset diversity persistence landscapes semantic collapse synthetic data
PLDiv is a diversity metric derived from persistence landscapes that summarizes how data neighborhoods evolve as nearby samples progressively connect, giving an interpretable scalar for structural diversity without task-specific supervision. It performs competitively on text generations and image embeddings and detects semantic collapse in instruction datasets and reasoning trajectories, supporting synthetic-data monitoring, dataset construction and augmentation.

arxiv:2610.00689 arXiv ✓
Towards Robust Numerical Claim Verification
迈向稳健的数值断言验证
claim verification numerical reasoning adversarial fine-tuning robustness
The authors show that brittleness to small numerical changes persists in frontier LLMs but can be mitigated through adversarial fine-tuning on numerically perturbed examples. Parameter-efficient tuning lets small Qwen3 models (0.6B–8B) reach 98.7% accuracy on label-flipping perturbations, above GPT-5.4 Pro (74.0%) and Gemini 2.5 Flash (73.9%), and the gains generalise to unseen perturbation types and cross-lingual transfer.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2610.14029 arXiv ✓
Can We Trust This Evaluation? Instance-Level Reliability of LLM-as-a-Judge
我们能信任这项评估吗？LLM 作为评判者的实例级可靠性
LLM-as-a-judge judge reliability risk scoring uncertainty
SCUR is a label-free method that estimates the instance-level reliability of LLM-as-a-judge evaluations from judge-panel behavior, combining panel uncertainty, measurement cross-entropy and perturbation sensitivity into a risk score under a latent-variable measurement model. On five real benchmarks it is reported to be more effective than other methods on high-agreement risky items, a blind spot for many approaches.

arxiv:2610.00795 arXiv ✓
Can large language models unlock discrete data in ophthalmic diagnostic reports?
大语言模型能否解锁眼科诊断报告中的离散数据？
structured extraction prompting strategies schema-constrained output clinical reports
A small proof-of-concept study ran two GPT-4o-assisted pipelines over 20 de-identified ophthalmic diagnostic PDF reports to extract structured data against a reconciled manual ground truth. Prompt-only extraction reached 100% value accuracy across all four report types, schema-constrained output reached 100% formatting accuracy, and mean extraction time fell from 56.51 seconds per report manually to about 5 seconds.
