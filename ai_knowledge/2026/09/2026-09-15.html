DeepSeek arxiv:2609.17890 arXiv ✓
OBC-Prune: Outcome-Based Calibration for Large Reasoning Model Pruning
OBC-Prune：面向大型推理模型剪枝的结果导向校准
outcome-based calibration reasoning circuits model pruning benchmark
OBC-Prune addresses reasoning-model pruning by constructing difficulty-matched pairs of correct and incorrect rollouts and estimating each reasoning sentence's causal importance through intervention-based analysis. The resulting per-token weights rescale calibration activations for one-shot pruning methods such as SparseGPT, Wanda and ALPS, and improve accuracy over calibration baselines on MATH500, LiveCodeBench and AIME 2025 for DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5B, 7B and 14B models at 40% and 50% sparsity.

arxiv:2609.17888 arXiv ✓
Long-Context Demonstration Selection Using State Space Models
基于状态空间模型的长上下文示例选择
demonstration selection state space models in-context learning inference efficiency
The paper distills a trained transformer into a small set of state space models by partitioning layers into consecutive groups and estimating an SSM for each group, then maps the distilled outputs to token embeddings for demonstration selection. The distilled SSMs incur under 0.7% approximation error relative to the true output, and on text classification and reasoning tasks the approach reduces FLOPs by 14.2x and improves accuracy by 6.48% relative to baseline demonstration selection.

arxiv:2609.17855 arXiv ✓
SNOMED CT Concept Recommendation from Masked Clinical Context
从掩码临床上下文推荐 SNOMED CT 概念
clinical ontology concept recommendation sparse retrieval low-resource benchmark
The paper presents a masked-concept recommendation benchmark built from SNOMED CT Entity Linking Challenge data derived from MIMIC-IV-Note, with 75,491 annotations across 272 discharge summaries, and ranks concepts observed during training after masking the target mention from local clinical context. Sparse TF-IDF performs best with Recall@10 of 33.43%, retrieval augmentation does not improve that baseline, and Recall@10 falls to 7.74% for concepts appearing in only one or two training notes.

arxiv:2609.17853 arXiv ✓
AfriSyCo: Measuring Assertive Framing, Verification, and Wording Sensitivity Around African-Language Content
AfriSyCo：衡量非洲语言内容中的断言式表述、验证与措辞敏感性
assertive framing factual knowledge prompt sensitivity multilingual evaluation
AfriSyCo studies answer switching around African-language factual content using native-language follow-ups and a controlled cross-language factorial across 1,415 turn-1-correct observations from seven open-weight checkpoints and six languages. Under native prompts, assertive endorsement produces 29.3 percentage points more any-turn false-target selection than mention-plus-verification, and in the precommitted 2x2 factorial assertive framing raises target selection by 30.4 points while verification lowers it by 17.4 points.

arxiv:2609.17786 arXiv ✓
FairCompressAgent: An Agentic Framework for Fairness-Aware Model Compression for FPGA Deployment
FairCompressAgent：面向 FPGA 部署的公平性感知模型压缩智能体框架
agentic framework model compression measured feedback constraint-based selection
FairCompressAgent composes fairness-aware pruning, incremental quantization and sparse low-rank factorization behind a common operator interface, with a language-model planner that selects configurations from model profiles and measured outcomes while an execution layer performs compression, fine-tuning, evaluation and constraint-based selection. On Fitzpatrick-17k with VGG-11, one accuracy-constrained request yields 59.54% less inference tensor storage while validation average precision rises from 0.5141 to 0.5233, and it reaches the same final selection as one-shot planning with 7.33 versus 12 candidate evaluations.

NanoForecast v0.5: Competitive Time Series Forecasting Through Training Pipeline Optimization
NanoForecast v0.5：通过训练流程优化实现有竞争力的时间序列预测
time series forecasting training pipeline small models open-source release
NanoForecast v0.5 is a 6.5M-parameter time-series forecaster that matches models 31x its size after training-pipeline fixes with no architecture change, cutting overall Mean Absolute Scaled Error by 43.8% (3.030 to 1.704) under one fixed protocol on the same data and compute budget. It beats TimesFM on all three ETT datasets and on exchange rate while TimesFM retains a clear lead on high-cardinality electricity and traffic sets, and code, pretrained checkpoints and the evaluation framework are released under Apache 2.0.
