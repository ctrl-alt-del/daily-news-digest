Euston: Training Away Mathematical Sycophancy Without Losing the Mathematics
Euston：在不牺牲数学能力的前提下训练模型摆脱数学谄媚
mathematical verification sycophancy GRPO claim verification
Euston is an 8B mathematical claim-verification model trained to resist proving corrupted theorems, using 3,026 matched true/corrupted statement pairs generated with GraphSynth and GRPO fine-tuning of DeepSeek-R1-8B. On a balanced held-out split, balanced accuracy rises from 29.50% to 63.75% while AIME 2026 accuracy falls by a non-significant 4.17 points, and the authors note confounds including the all-false composition of official evaluation sets.

Test-Time Unlearning via Sparse Autoencoder
基于稀疏自编码器的测试时遗忘
test-time unlearning sparse autoencoder knowledge removal adversarial robustness
ARIA is a test-time unlearning method that leaves model weights intact and uses sparse-autoencoder latents to train a lightweight linear detector that gates access to unwanted knowledge when generation enters a forget-related state. On TOFU, R-TOFU and WMDP it improves the forget-retain trade-off over weight-based baselines, keeping MMLU within 1% of the pre-unlearning model, and forgetting changes by less than 1% under three post-unlearning adversarial attacks.

RAFT: A Stateful Retrieval-Augmented Framework for Troubleshooting Agents
RAFT：面向故障排查智能体的有状态检索增强框架
stateful RAG case-based retrieval graph retrieval troubleshooting agents retrieval evaluation
RAFT abstracts each closed support case into a directed chain of timeline entries and retrieves at the entry level, returning the parent-case trajectory anchored at the matched state, with an optional case-level similarity graph. It improves Case Hit over vanilla RAG and GraphRAG at every stage of case progress on a synthetic Microsoft Learn benchmark, with statistically significant gains over the strongest baseline and directional evidence of transfer on Apache Jira duplicate labels.

XYEval: Agents say yes to bad advice
XYEval：智能体对糟糕建议说“是”
evaluation robustness misleading suggestions agent behavior benchmark mutation
XYEval is a meta-evaluation framework that converts existing benchmarks into XY-problem tests of whether agents resist plausible but misleading user suggestions and explain their reasoning, across five models and six benchmark suites. It reports relative performance drops of up to 46.7% under XY mutation, further declines with a pedantic user, and only partial mitigation from a simple system-instruction baseline.

虚假工具使用：当强化学习智能体学会错误的行动理由
tool use reinforcement learning shortcut learning LLM judge
The paper studies RL-trained LLM agents that learn shortcut tool-selection policies driven by superficial prompt cues rather than genuine task requirements, finding spurious tool invocation rates increase by up to 39% in counterfactual evaluations.
