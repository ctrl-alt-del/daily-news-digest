Looks the Same, Answers Differently: Flip-Direction Steering for Robust Vision-Language Reasoning
看起来相同却答案不同：面向鲁棒视觉语言推理的翻转方向引导
activation steering vision-language reasoning robustness benchmark
FlipDir is a training-free inference-time method that estimates a low-rank flip-inducing activation subspace from contrastive input pairs and steers hidden states during decoding, with a margin-based gate that limits intervention to uncertain steps so stable predictions are preserved; on the new VisFlip benchmark it outperforms existing methods across 18 settings on combined recovery and preservation.

The Fellowship of the Query: Learning Retrieval Actions
查询远征：学习检索动作
retrieval actions trajectory fine-tuning small language models question answering
The paper fine-tunes small language models as next-action controllers for retrieval-augmented QA over seven structured actions; Granite 4.1 3B reaches macro-F1 0.6536 on 1,646 held-out examples versus 0.1736 zero-shot, and using the tuned model as both controller and generator lifts Exact Match from 0.7530 to 0.7946.

智能体编辑世界模型：重新思考 LLM 智能体的世界建模
world model state revision task-state contamination agent benchmark
AEWM models how reasoning and actions shape future task progress instead of predicting tool responses, combining an Action Judge that labels decisions as critical, exploratory or noisy with State Revision that edits contaminated history; EditAct improves average scores by 3.2-6.7 points across six benchmarks and three backbones.
