GTA: Graph Theory Agent and Benchmark for Algorithmic Graph Reasoning with LLMs
GTA：面向 LLM 算法图推理的图论智能体与基准
graph reasoning representation selection agent scaffolding benchmark
The authors introduce GT Bench, a benchmark of 24 classical graph problems in 44 task-structure settings with over 100,000 examples across four input representations, and find that LLM accuracy is strongly tied to representation and shifts with graph density, size and topology. They also propose GTA, which pairs a preference-trained representation selector with plan-and-decompose scaffolding around a frozen executor LLM, lifting Phi-4 from 53.5% to 69.1% on the easy split and from 33.0% to 41.5% on the hard split.

arxiv:2609.12230 arXiv ✓
Repair Before Reinforce: Context-Augmented Knowledge Graph Reasoning for Multi-Hop Question Answering
先修复再强化：面向多跳问答的上下文增强知识图谱推理
knowledge graph multi-hop QA context augmentation reinforcement learning graph repair
The paper proposes a context-augmented training framework that attaches supporting triples from the same source chunk to each knowledge-graph triple, improving multi-hop question answering over KG-only supervision on Gastroparesis and Diabetes knowledge graphs. An LLM-judged adaptive repair pipeline brings Qwen3-14B to 100% accuracy on cleaned retained one-hop validation sets, and RL from repaired checkpoints yields larger and more stable gains on 3- to 5-hop tasks.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.12162 arXiv ✓
Can LLMs in Draft-Verify-Revise Pipelines Resolve Deictic Ambiguity?
草拟-验证-修订流水线中的 LLM 能解决指示性歧义吗？
draft-verify-revise context engineering deictic shift reasoning effort
The study tests draft-verify-revise pipelines on a synthetic dataset of 10 base examples rendered in three conditions, finding that models at different stages can resolve context-dependent expressions differently and produce deictic shifts. Across six models and 21 reasoning-effort configurations, balanced accuracy ranged from 0.156, below chance, to near-perfect; GPT-5.2 rose from 0.156 without reasoning to 0.942 at its highest effort, while Gemini 3 Pro stayed above 0.94 at every level.

arxiv:2609.13324 arXiv ✓
Decoupling Error Attribution in Cloud-Native Graph-RAG: A Data Integrity Diagnostic Framework
解耦云原生 Graph-RAG 的错误归因：数据完整性诊断框架
Graph-RAG error attribution data integrity parametric knowledge Cypher generation
The paper proposes a three-layer decoupled diagnostic framework that attributes Graph-RAG errors to reasoning loss, knowledge-graph defects and Cypher generation errors, evaluated on a spatio-temporal ecological KG with eight defect types. Structural defects degrade accuracy from 0.93 to 0.39, and the observed Parametric Knowledge Masking Effect lets LLMs compensate for broken retrieval paths, shrinking apparent query-generation errors by over 70% while obscuring storage deterioration.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.31636 arXiv ✓
Grounding Vision-Language Models in Driving Semantics: A Multi-Dataset Predicate Framework for Explainable Reasoning
将视觉语言模型锚定在驾驶语义中：面向可解释推理的多数据集谓词框架
predicate knowledge graph visual grounding explainable reasoning cross-dataset evaluation
The paper introduces a deterministic multi-dataset predicate framework that derives driving-scene semantics from geometric, kinematic, temporal, map and traffic-control evidence and materializes them in a shared Predicate Knowledge Graph. It reports macro F1 of 0.94 on nuPlan and 0.93 on nuScenes, and predicate grounding achieves the highest accuracy among evaluated visual-input conditions in seven of nine NuPlanQA subtasks with a frozen LLaVA-OneVision-7B.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.11900 arXiv ✓
MindTopo: Can Foundation Models Reason in Topological Space?
MindTopo：基础模型能在拓扑空间中推理吗？
topological reasoning spatial benchmark multimodal LLM agent planning
MindTopo is a benchmark of topological intuition across continuity, separation, order, enclosure and knots, with 11,030 instances in 13 procedurally generated task types evaluated at reasoning and planning levels. Across 14 MLLMs, every model performs better on reasoning than planning and the best remains far below human performance, while supervised fine-tuning and RL on Qwen3-VL-2B improve reasoning more than planning.
→ arxiv.org

EAR: Entity-Aware Partitioning Approach for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Development
EAR：面向检索增强生成开发的实体感知划分方法
entity-aware retrieval chunking multiple-choice QA retrieval efficiency
EAR extracts normalized surface anchors from questions, answer options and corpora, then retrieves local windows around matching anchors and can attach a larger parent passage through an extractive summary. On a cleaned 153-question MMLU-style subset with Mistral, Gemma and DeepSeek, entity-window retrieval cut retrieved words by 37.5-40.2% relative to chunks, while accuracy changes were mixed and not statistically significant.
