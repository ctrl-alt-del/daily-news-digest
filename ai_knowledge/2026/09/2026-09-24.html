Where Does Retrieval-Based Open-Ended Evaluation Fail? Automatic Taxonomy Induction from Long-Form Medical Answer Factuality Verification

信或不信：语音中的检索增强事实核查
spoken fact checking audio language models modality gap retrieval reasoning
VeriSpeak is a 3,879-claim spoken fact-verification benchmark for large audio language models that exposes a consistent text-speech modality gap; retrieval alone gives limited gains, while retrieval plus explicit reasoning lets a thinking-tuned model reach 86.1% accuracy.

arxiv:2610.08820 arXiv ✓
Task-Oriented Key-Layer KV Communication for Efficient Latent Multi-Agent Collaboration
面向任务的关键层 KV 通信：高效潜在多智能体协作
latent communication KV cache multi-agent systems inference efficiency
KITE reframes latent multi-agent communication around receiver-side task sufficiency rather than sender-state fidelity, transmitting only the key layer's latent working memory; across seven benchmarks it cuts communication volume by 28-36x, speeds inference up to 3x and improves accuracy by up to 23.3 points.

arxiv:2609.30614 arXiv ✓
Subjects, Not Authors: The Authorship Hazard in Agentic Dataspaces
是主体，而非作者：智能体数据空间中的作者身份风险
agent governance policy authorization dataspaces
The paper argues that agents in agentic dataspaces should be subjects of the governance plane rather than authors of it; across 90 preregistered edits evaluated on 344,512 requests, six field-reclassification edits alone changed authorization and narrowed a duty without touching policy text, while compiled tool-call constraints blocked protected fields in 105 of 105 cases.
