An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents
编码智能体运行框架设计的实证研究
coding harness context management context window planning agent benchmark
The study fixes a coding-agent execution loop and varies planning, action space and context management across 176 matched settings, four models, SWE-Bench Verified and Terminal-Bench 2.1. It finds context management becomes increasingly valuable as budgets tighten, rule-based elision before LLM summarization gives the strongest overall efficiency, and bash-only interfaces lower cost for bash-capable models.

OpenAI arxiv:2609.21081 arXiv ✓
Loopjacking: Hijacking Human-in-the-Loop Approval
循环劫持：劫持人在回路审批
human-in-the-loop approval binding agent security state substitution
The paper defines Loopjacking as a failure where a human approves operation A but the implementation uses that approval for a materially different operation B, via representation mismatch or post-approval state substitution. It reproduces the attacks in seven Agno AgentOS releases and 12 LangGraph Agent Server versions, while OpenAI Agents SDK 0.22.0 and 0.22.2 reject them.

arxiv:2609.20754 arXiv ✓
RAFT: A Stateful Retrieval-Augmented Framework for Troubleshooting Agents
RAFT：面向故障排查智能体的有状态检索增强框架
stateful RAG case-based retrieval graph retrieval troubleshooting agents retrieval evaluation
RAFT abstracts each closed support case into a directed chain of timeline entries and retrieves at the entry level, returning the parent-case trajectory anchored at the matched state, with an optional case-level similarity graph. It improves Case Hit over vanilla RAG and GraphRAG at every stage of case progress on a synthetic Microsoft Learn benchmark, with statistically significant gains over the strongest baseline and directional evidence of transfer on Apache Jira duplicate labels.

A Simulation Platform for AUV Fault Recovery: Exploring LLM-Based Diagnostic Strategies
面向 AUV 故障恢复的仿真平台：探索基于 LLM 的诊断策略
fault diagnosis diagnostic reasoning simulation platform LLM-as-judge
SPAR is a closed-loop simulation platform that couples autonomous underwater vehicle software with physics-based fault injection, structured prompting, mission generation, validation and LLM-judge scoring. Across 480 mass-shift fault trials, a frontier model placed the CG-shift mechanism in its top three hypotheses in 85-90% of trials, versus 60-78% for the best local model.

arxiv:2609.20822 arXiv ✓
Coding Agents with Harness for Safe Robot Control
面向安全机器人控制的编码智能体及其运行框架
robot control safety constraints route planning coding agents
The paper evaluates coding agents on robot manipulation tasks that pair a goal with an obstacle and finds agents collide in most cases because the safety constraint never becomes a planning priority. SafeHarness adds obstacle-aware route planning and contact execution, reaching 81.2% task success and 91.9% collision avoidance with GPT-6-Astra, 13.7 and 23.0 points above the previous state of the art.

arxiv:2609.20973 arXiv ✓
Complex Problem Solving in Large Language Models: A Statistical Control Survey and Diagnostic Framework
大语言模型的复杂问题求解：统计控制综述与诊断框架
complex problem solving process control uncertainty management survey
The survey frames complex problem solving with LLMs as sequential estimation and decision-making over a latent solution state, organized around explicit state representation, transition structuring, validation, search and rollback, and uncertainty management. It frames alignment between failure type and intervention as control fit and hypothesizes that interventions should target the error or uncertainty component implicated by an observed failure.

Clinician-Grounded Quality Assurance for AI-Assisted Psychiatric Intake
面向 AI 辅助精神科问诊的临床医生锚定质量保证
clinical evaluation memory-augmented simulator grounding AI interviewing
The paper presents InterviewPlayground, a clinician-grounded evaluation platform built around a memory-augmented patient simulator for open-ended AI interviewing. In a pilot with six clinicians, a GPT-based interviewer recovered more clinically relevant items than clinicians (88.0% vs. 38.9%) but made more inferences not grounded in the interview and characterized identified safety concerns less often.
