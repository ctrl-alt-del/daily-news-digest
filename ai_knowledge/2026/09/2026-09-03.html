GRACE: Graph-Grounded Reflective Agent Copilot Engine for Expert-in-the-Loop Knowledge Expansion
GRACE：面向专家在环知识扩展的图锚定反思型智能体副驾驶引擎
claim grounding knowledge graph expert-in-the-loop hallucination mitigation uncertainty routing
GRACE deconstructs LLM responses into atomic claims and grounds them against trusted knowledge priors in a weighted bipartite graph, classifying claims as Grounded, Refuted or Boundary. A Return on Attention objective defers only high-priority uncertain claims to expert review, and expert-verified claims become new evidence anchors in an iterative knowledge-base expansion loop.

LentEx: Generalizable Latent Entity Extraction via Synthetic Data and Instruction-Tuned LLMs
LentEx：通过合成数据与指令微调实现可泛化的潜在实体抽取
latent entity extraction synthetic data instruction tuning RAG MTEB
LentEx is a framework for latent entity extraction that uses template-based synthetic data and instruction fine-tuning to adapt smaller LLMs to implicit, contextually inferred entities. The authors report improvements across multiple tasks, including surpassing state-of-the-art models on the MTEB Clustering Benchmark, with generalization to unseen domains relevant to RAG and knowledge-graph enrichment.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.04570 arXiv ✓
Dynamic Adaptation of the LLM Context for Generating Routines with Coupled Semantics
动态适配 LLM 上下文：生成具有耦合语义的程序例程
context adaptation knowledge-graph constraints execution feedback simulated annealing
The paper targets code generation where correctness depends on execution-dependent coupling between routines, a limitation it calls static binding. Its validation-generation loop extracts structured diagnostics from execution traces, supplies knowledge-graph constraints from the problem description and uses simulated annealing, outperforming zero-shot, Reflexion and OpenEvolve on seven of eight problems at 300 and 600 evaluations.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.04444 arXiv ✓
HarvestBench: Measuring Whether LLM Agents Will Pay to Avoid Killing Animals
HarvestBench：衡量 LLM 智能体是否愿意为避免伤害动物而付出代价
agent alignment side-effect pricing instruction fragility LLM agents
HarvestBench is a programmatically scored benchmark in which nine LLMs each drive a crew of two tractors through a corn harvest and decide whether to run over an animal for free or swerve at a fuel cost. Kill rates range from 0.4% to 98.8% and are not ordered by capability, and removing a morality briefing raised the kill rate above 84% in all six reasoning models.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.05559 arXiv ✓
LLM-Aided Design for Manufacturing: A Multi-Agent System for Intent-Preserving Redesign of CAD for Improved Manufacturability
面向制造的大模型辅助设计：保持设计意图的 CAD 多智能体重构系统
multi-agent system CAD redesign verification loop design intent
DFM-Redesign is a multi-agent pipeline in which a reviewer proposes one intent-preserving manufacturability improvement at a time and a CAD modifier executes each proposal as an edit, compiles it and visually checks it against the intended change. On a 46-part benchmark the modifier reproduces target parts more accurately on average than chain-of-thought single agents, and a 32-transition centrifugal pump casing illustrates compounding verified edits.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2609.04336 arXiv ✓
MedProb: Probing Internal Representations of Vision-Language Models for Medical Question Answering
MedProb：探测视觉语言模型内部表示以回答医学问题
representation probing medical VQA vision-language models positional bias
MedProb is a lightweight probing framework that predicts multiple-choice medical VQA answers from frozen vision-language model representations without free-text generation. Across PATH-VQA, SLAKE and VQA-RAD it recovers more answer-relevant signal than prompting and outperforms medical VLMs and agentic systems, while free-text generation shows an answer-position bias of up to 10 percentage points.
→ arxiv.org
