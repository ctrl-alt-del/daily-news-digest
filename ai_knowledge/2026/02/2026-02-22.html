AgenticRAGTracer: A Hop-Aware Benchmark for Diagnosing Multi-Step Retrieval Reasoning in Agentic RAG
AgenticRAGTracer：诊断 Agentic RAG 多步检索推理的跳感知基准
agentic RAG multi-hop reasoning hop-aware evaluation benchmark generation
AgenticRAGTracer is an automatically constructed Agentic RAG benchmark of 1,305 data points that supplies intermediate hop-level questions so failures can be traced step by step. Even the best models do poorly, with GPT-5 reaching only 22.6% EM on the hardest portion, and hop-aware diagnosis attributes failures to reasoning chains that collapse early or over-extend.

OpenAI arxiv:2603.13247 arXiv ✓
ILION: Deterministic Pre-Execution Safety Gates for Agentic AI Systems
ILION：面向智能体 AI 系统的确定性执行前安全门
agent safety execution gating adversarial robustness interpretable verdicts
ILION is a deterministic pre-execution gate that classifies proposed agent actions as BLOCK or ALLOW through a five-component cascade, requiring no statistical training or labeled data and producing interpretable verdicts at sub-millisecond latency. On the 380-scenario ILION-Bench v2 it reports F1 0.8515 at a mean latency of 143 microseconds, outperforming Lakera Guard, the OpenAI Moderation API and Llama Guard 3.

Active perception and disentangled representations allow continual, episodic zero and few-shot learning
主动感知与解耦表征支撑持续的片段式零样本和少样本学习
continual learning episodic memory disentangled representations active perception complementary learning systems
The authors describe a Complementary Learning System in which a fast learner gives up generalization in exchange for continual zero-shot and few-shot learning and operates as a parallel reasoning system rather than only as a replay buffer. A slow statistical learner, biased by context from the fast learner through active perception, encodes novel stimuli in familiar generalized terms.

arxiv:2602.19133 arXiv ✓
A Dataset for Named Entity Recognition and Relation Extraction from Art-historical Image Descriptions
艺术史图像描述的命名实体识别与关系抽取数据集
named entity recognition relation extraction knowledge graph construction cultural heritage dataset
FRAME is a manually annotated dataset of art-historical image descriptions collected from museum catalogs, auction listings, open-access platforms and scholarly databases, with stand-off annotations in metadata, content and co-reference layers. Its 37 entity types are aligned with Wikidata to support entity linking and downstream knowledge-graph construction, and it is released as UIMA XMI CAS files for benchmarking NER and relation extraction.

Scaling Inference-Time Computation via Opponent Simulation: Enabling Online Strategic Adaptation in Repeated Negotiation
通过对手模拟扩展推理时计算：在重复谈判中实现在线策略适应
inference-time scaling opponent modeling strategic reasoning negotiation
The authors scale inference-time computation for repeated strategic interactions by embedding smooth Fictitious Play into LLM inference, using an auxiliary opponent model that learns the opponent's time-averaged behavior in context and best-of-N sampling that simulates against it. Across two repeated negotiation games the method improves online performance without any parameter updates.

arxiv:2602.19357 arXiv ✓
MentalBlackboard: Evaluating Spatial Visualization via Mathematical Transformations
MentalBlackboard：用数学变换评估空间可视化能力
spatial reasoning vision-language models benchmark design
MentalBlackboard is an open-ended benchmark for paper-folding and hole-punching tests that probes spatial visualization in vision-language models through prediction and planning tasks. Models struggled with symmetric transformations and rotations; Claude Opus 4.1 scored 10% on planning, while o3 reached 71.6% on a generalization task but only 25% on text-based prediction.
