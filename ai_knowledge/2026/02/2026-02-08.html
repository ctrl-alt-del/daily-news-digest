DeepSeek arxiv:2602.08030 arXiv ✓
Free(): Learning to Forget in Malloc-Only Reasoning Models
Free()：在仅分配式推理模型中学习遗忘
self-forgetting reasoning models context pruning LoRA adapter long-horizon tasks
The authors argue that standard LLMs behave like "malloc-only" engines that accumulate redundant reasoning steps, and introduce Free()LM, a plug-and-play LoRA Free-Module that alternates between reasoning and cleaning modes to prune useless context chunks. It improves average accuracy by 3.3% across models from 8B to 685B, sets a new state of the art on IMOanswerBench with DeepSeek V3.2-Speciale, and restores 50% accuracy on long-horizon tasks where Qwen3-235B-A22B collapses to 0%.

arxiv:2602.07962 arXiv ✓
LOCA-bench: Benchmarking Language Agents Under Controllable and Extreme Context Growth
LOCA-bench：在可控且极端上下文增长下评测语言智能体
long-context agents context management agent benchmark context rot scaffolds
LOCA-bench evaluates language agents under controllable, potentially unbounded context growth by automatically regulating environment states while keeping task semantics fixed. It scores model-plus-scaffold combinations, including context-management strategies, and finds that performance generally degrades as environment states grow more complex while advanced context management substantially improves success rates; the benchmark is open-sourced.

Alibaba arxiv:2603.29888 arXiv ✓
Generative AI in Action: Field Experimental Evidence from Alibaba's Customer Service Operations
生成式 AI 实践：来自阿里巴巴客服运营的现场实验证据
generative AI assistant human-AI collaboration field experiment service quality
In a large-scale field experiment with Alibaba after-sales chat agents, a generative AI assistant drafted issue diagnoses and solution proposals in the opening stage while agents could adopt, modify or disregard them. On average it improved service speed and subjective ratings without a significant effect on objective quality; lower-performing agents benefited most, while top performers saw declines consistent with workflow disruption.

arxiv:2602.08036 arXiv ✓
TAAM:Inductive Graph-Class Incremental Learning with Task-Aware Adaptive Modulation
TAAM：面向归纳式图类增量学习的任务感知自适应调制
continual learning catastrophic forgetting graph neural networks task-specific modules benchmark leakage
TAAM freezes a shared GNN backbone and trains a lightweight Neural Synapse Modulator per task as an expert module, keeping new knowledge in compact task-specific modules to avoid catastrophic forgetting without data replay. It also introduces Anchored Multi-hop Propagation for unknown task IDs, and reports gains over state-of-the-art methods on eight datasets under a stricter inductive protocol that avoids leakage found in existing benchmarks.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.16316 arXiv ✓
CrossTraffic: An Open-Source Framework for Reproducible and Executable Transportation Analysis and Knowledge Management
CrossTraffic：可复现可执行的交通分析与知识管理开源框架
knowledge graph ontology tool invocation reproducible analysis methodological fidelity
CrossTraffic treats transportation methodologies and regulatory knowledge as continuously deployable, verifiable software infrastructure, combining an executable computational core, an ontology-driven knowledge graph that encodes engineering rules and provenance, and a conversational LLM interface that uses structured tool invocation. Knowledge-graph-constrained execution achieved near-zero numerical error (MAE below 0.50) across multiple LLMs and perfect detection of invalid analytical inputs (F1 approximately 1.0).

arxiv:2602.08013 arXiv ✓
Small Agent Group is the Future of Digital Health
小型智能体团队是数字健康的未来
multi-agent collaboration clinical reasoning retrieval-augmented generation deployment cost
The paper challenges a scaling-first approach to clinical AI and proposes a Small Agent Group that distributes reasoning, evidence-based analysis and critical audit across a collaborative deliberation process. Evaluations spanning effectiveness, reliability and deployment cost show the group outperforms a single giant model both with and without additional optimization or retrieval-augmented generation.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.08149 arXiv ✓
DIAL-SUMMER: A Structured Evaluation Framework of Hierarchical Errors in Dialogue Summaries
DIAL-SUMMER：对话摘要中分层错误的结构化评估框架
dialogue summarization error taxonomy hallucination detection LLM-as-judge
DIAL-SUMMER introduces a taxonomy and annotated dataset for evaluating dialogue-summary errors at the dialogue level and within individual turns. Its analyses find that middle turns are most often missed and extrinsic hallucinations tend to occur at the end of summaries, and experiments show LLM judges struggle to detect these errors.
