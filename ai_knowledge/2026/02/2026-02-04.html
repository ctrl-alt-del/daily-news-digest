Locas: Your Models are Principled Initializers of Locally-Supported Parametric Memories
Locas：你的模型是局部支持参数化记忆的原则性初始化器
parametric memory continual learning catastrophic forgetting memory consolidation long context
Locas is a locally supported parametric memory shaped like transformer FFN blocks that can be offloaded from or merged into model parameters, supporting efficient continual learning. Initialized from model parameters, activations or gradients, Locas-GLU stores past context with as little as 0.02% additional parameters on PG-19 and LoCoMo while limiting catastrophic forgetting in MMLU tests.

arxiv:2602.04837 arXiv ✓
Group-Evolving Agents: Open-Ended Self-Improvement via Experience Sharing
群体演化智能体：通过经验共享实现开放式自我改进
self-evolving agents experience sharing open-ended evolution coding benchmarks multi-agent systems
Group-Evolving Agents treats a group of agents as the fundamental evolutionary unit, enabling explicit experience sharing and reuse across branches instead of tree-structured isolated evolution. On coding benchmarks it reports 71.0% versus 56.7% on SWE-bench Verified and 88.3% versus 68.3% on Polyglot, fixes framework-level bugs in 1.4 iterations on average versus 5, and shows transferability across coding models.

LABBench2: An Improved Benchmark for AI Systems Performing Biology Research
LABBench2：评估 AI 系统开展生物学研究的改进基准
scientific agents benchmark biology research frontier models

Frontier models show substantial improvement over the original benchmark but a meaningful jump in difficulty, with model-specific accuracy drops of 26% to 46% across subtasks; the dataset and public eval harness are released.

arxiv:2602.04813 arXiv ✓
Agentic AI in Healthcare & Medicine: A Seven-Dimensional Taxonomy for Empirical Evaluation of LLM-based Agents
医疗与医学中的智能体 AI：评估 LLM 智能体的七维分类法
healthcare agents taxonomy knowledge management drift detection multi-agent design
The review maps 49 studies of LLM-based healthcare agents onto a seven-dimensional taxonomy with 29 operational sub-dimensions and a three-level implementation rubric. It finds external knowledge integration fully implemented in about 76% of studies, event-triggered activation absent in about 92% and drift detection absent in about 98%, with multi-agent designs dominant but orchestration mostly partial.

Bypassing AI Control Protocols via Agent-as-a-Proxy Attacks
通过“智能体即代理”攻击绕过 AI 控制协议
prompt injection AI control monitoring agent security AgentDojo
The paper introduces an Agent-as-a-Proxy attack in which prompt injection treats the agent as a delivery mechanism, bypassing the agent and its monitor at the same time. On AgentDojo it reports high attack success against AlignmentCheck and Extract-and-Evaluate monitors across monitoring LLMs, with GPT-4o mini and Llama-3.1-70B bypassing a Qwen2.5-72B monitor.

arxiv:2602.04881 arXiv ✓
Contrastive Continual Learning for Model Adaptability in Internet of Things
物联网中模型适应性的对比持续学习
continual learning contrastive learning concept drift edge AI
This review examines contrastive continual learning for IoT, connecting replay, regularization, distillation and prompt-based designs with TinyML constraints, intermittent connectivity and privacy requirements. It presents a unifying formulation, an on-device/edge/cloud reference architecture, and open challenges including concept drift, federated settings and energy-aware training.

arxiv:2602.04853 arXiv ✓
Decomposed Prompting Does Not Fix Knowledge Gaps, But Helps Models Say "I Don't Know"
分解式提示无法修复知识缺口，但能帮助模型说“我不知道”
decomposed prompting abstention hallucination uncertainty estimation closed-book QA
The authors compare Direct, Assistive and Incremental prompting across model scales and multi-hop QA benchmarks, finding that accuracy gains from decomposition diminish in frontier models. Disagreement between regimes remains a precise uncertainty signal, and a training-free abstention policy based on it improves F1 and AUROC over standard uncertainty baselines without retrieval or fine-tuning.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.05110 arXiv ✓
Understanding LLM Evaluator Behavior: A Structured Multi-Evaluator Framework for Merchant Risk Assessment
理解 LLM 评估器行为：面向商户风险评估的结构化多评估器框架
LLM-as-judge self-evaluation bias rubric scoring risk assessment
The paper pairs a five-criterion rubric with Monte-Carlo scoring to assess how LLMs judge merchant-category risk rationales across five frontier models. It finds substantial self-evaluation bias, negative for GPT-5.1 and Claude 4.5 Sonnet and positive for Gemini-2.5 Pro and Grok 4, and reports that 26 payment-industry experts scored LLM judges on average 0.46 points above human consensus.

GAMMS: Graph based Adversarial Multiagent Modeling Simulator
GAMMS：基于图的多智能体对抗建模模拟器
multi-agent simulation graph environments LLM agents open-source simulator
GAMMS is a lightweight, extensible open-source simulator for multi-agent behavior in graph-representable environments such as urban road networks and communication systems. It supports heuristic, optimization-based and learning-based agents, including LLM-based ones, and integrates with machine learning libraries and planning solvers.
