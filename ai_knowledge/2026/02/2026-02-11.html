arxiv:2602.11151 arXiv ✓
Diffusion-Pretrained Dense and Contextual Embeddings
扩散预训练的稠密与上下文嵌入
embedding models diffusion pretraining contextual embeddings late chunking multilingual retrieval
The report introduces pplx-embed, a family of multilingual embedding models that apply multi-stage contrastive learning on a diffusion-pretrained backbone for web-scale retrieval, using mean pooling and late chunking to preserve global context. pplx-embed-v1 is competitive on MTEB, MTEB(Code), MIRACL, BERGEN and ToolRet, while pplx-embed-context-v1 sets new records on ConTEB, and the models are tested on an internal suite built from 1B production web pages.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.15898 arXiv ✓
MultiCube-RAG for Multi-hop Question Answering
面向多跳问答的 MultiCube-RAG
multi-hop QA ontology cube structure training-free RAG
MultiCube-RAG is a training-free method that builds an ontology-based cube structure with multiple orthogonal dimensions to model subjects, attributes and relations, then decomposes complex multi-hop queries into subqueries along cube dimensions for sequential retrieval. Across four multi-hop QA datasets it improves response accuracy by 8.9% over the average of various baselines while reporting greater efficiency and inherent explainability.
→ arxiv.org

Asymmetric Prompt Weighting for Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards
面向可验证奖励强化学习的不对称提示加权
reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards prompt weighting reasoning post-training policy optimization
The paper studies asymmetric prompt weighting for reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards, assigning higher weights to prompts with low or even zero empirical success probability. It reports that this particularly benefits from-scratch RL such as R1-Zero and less so post-SFT RL, and provides theory on weights that minimize the time needed to reach a target accuracy under a fixed update budget.
→ arxiv.org

Bi-Level Prompt Optimization for Multimodal LLM-as-a-Judge
面向多模态 LLM-as-a-Judge 的双层提示优化
prompt optimization multimodal evaluation LLM-as-a-judge visual tokenization
BLPO is a bi-level prompt optimization framework for multimodal LLM-as-a-judge that converts images into textual representations preserving evaluation-relevant visual cues, jointly refining the judge prompt and the image-to-text prompt under limited context budgets. Experiments on four datasets and three LLM judges demonstrate its effectiveness.

arxiv:2602.11149 arXiv ✓
Data Repetition Beats Data Scaling in Long-CoT Supervised Fine-Tuning
数据重复胜过数据扩展：长思维链监督微调中的发现
data repetition chain-of-thought supervised fine-tuning catastrophic forgetting
The authors show that under a fixed update budget, supervised fine-tuning with more epochs on smaller chain-of-thought datasets outperforms single-epoch training on larger ones. Olmo3-7B trained for 128 epochs on 400 samples beats one epoch on 51,200 samples by 12-26 percentage points on AIME'24/25 and GPQA without additional catastrophic forgetting, and token accuracy signals when repetition saturates.

arxiv:2602.11416 arXiv ✓
Optimizing Agent Planning for Security and Autonomy
为安全与自主性优化智能体规划
prompt injection agent security autonomy metrics human-in-the-loop
The authors introduce autonomy metrics measuring how many consequential actions an agent can execute without human-in-the-loop approval while preserving security, and design a security-aware agent with richer HITL interactions and explicit planning for both task progress and policy compliance. Implemented atop an information-flow control defense against prompt injection and evaluated on AgentDojo and WASP, the approach yields higher autonomy without sacrificing utility.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.11081 arXiv ✓
SteuerLLM: Local specialized large language model for German tax law analysis
SteuerLLM：用于德国税法分析的本地专用大语言模型
domain adaptation legal reasoning retrieval-augmented generation benchmark
The authors release SteuerEx, an open benchmark of 115 expert-validated German tax law examination questions with statement-level partial-credit evaluation, and present SteuerLLM, a 28B domain-adapted model trained on synthetic data generated through a controlled retrieval-augmented pipeline. SteuerLLM consistently outperforms general-purpose instruction-tuned models of comparable size and, in several cases, substantially larger systems.
→ arxiv.org
