Nemotron ColEmbed V2: Top-Performing Late Interaction Embedding Models for Visual Document Retrieval
Nemotron ColEmbed V2：面向视觉文档检索的顶级晚交互嵌入模型
visual document retrieval late interaction embedding models hard-negative mining ViDoRe
Nemotron ColEmbed V2 is a family of vision-language embedding models at 3B, 4B and 8B parameters, built on Eagle 2 with a Llama 3.2 3B backbone and Qwen3-VL backbones, for visual document retrieval. The 8B model ranks first on the ViDoRe V3 leaderboard as of February 3, 2026 with average NDCG@10 of 63.42, and the work details cluster-based sampling, hard-negative mining, late interaction and model merging.

arxiv:2602.03798 arXiv ✓
FullStack-Agent: Enhancing Agentic Full-Stack Web Coding via Development-Oriented Testing and Repository Back-Translation
FullStack-Agent：通过面向开发的测试与仓库反向翻译增强智能体全栈 Web 编程
full-stack coding multi-agent system coding benchmark self-improvement repository back-translation
FullStack-Agent combines a multi-agent developer with planning, code editing, codebase navigation and bug localization, a self-improving method that back-translates website repositories into training data, and a benchmark testing frontend, backend and database behavior. It reports gains of 8.7%, 38.2% and 15.9% over the previous state of the art on those three test sets, and self-improvement lifts a 30B model by 9.7%, 9.5% and 2.8%.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.03979 arXiv ✓
Likelihood-Based Reward Designs for General LLM Reasoning
面向通用 LLM 推理的似然奖励设计
reward design log-probability chain-of-thought RL fine-tuning reasoning benchmarks
The authors systematically compare rewards derived from the probability or log-probability of the reference answer against binary and other baselines for chain-of-thought fine-tuning. They find log-probability rewards are the only variant that performs well across verifiable and non-verifiable settings, matching or beating binary rewards on success rate with better perplexity, while probability-based VeriFree flatlines without a verifier.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.03955 arXiv ✓
AgentArk: Distilling Multi-Agent Intelligence into a Single LLM Agent
AgentArk：将多智能体智能蒸馏进单一 LLM 智能体
multi-agent distillation knowledge distillation self-correction inference efficiency
AgentArk distills multi-agent debate dynamics into the weights of a single model through three hierarchical strategies: reasoning-enhanced fine-tuning, trajectory-based augmentation and process-aware distillation. The distilled agents keep single-agent inference cost while retaining multi-agent reasoning and self-correction, with reported robustness and generalization gains across reasoning tasks.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.03814 arXiv ✓
Conformal Thinking: Risk Control for Reasoning on a Compute Budget
保形思考：计算预算下推理的风险控制
adaptive reasoning risk control token budget early stopping
The paper reframes token-budget selection as risk control, adding an upper confidence threshold that stops reasoning when the model is confident and a parametric lower threshold that preemptively stops unsolvable instances. Given a target risk and validation set, distribution-free risk control sets both thresholds, and experiments show compute savings while adhering to the user-specified risk target.
→ arxiv.org
