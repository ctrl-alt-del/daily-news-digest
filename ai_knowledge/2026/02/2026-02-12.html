arxiv:2602.12268 arXiv ✓
CM2: Reinforcement Learning with Checklist Rewards for Multi-Turn and Multi-Step Agentic Tool Use
CM2：面向多轮多步智能体工具使用的清单奖励强化学习
checklist rewards agentic tool use reinforcement learning simulated environments
CM2 is an RL framework that replaces verifiable outcome rewards with checklist rewards, decomposing each turn's intended behavior into fine-grained binary criteria with evidence grounding and structured metadata, and trains in a scalable LLM-simulated tool environment. Starting from an 8B base model and an 8k-example RL dataset, it improves over the SFT counterpart by 8 points on tau^-Bench, 10 on BFCL-V4 and 12 on ToolSandbox.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2602.12275 arXiv ✓
On-Policy Context Distillation for Language Models
面向语言模型的同策略上下文蒸馏
context distillation experiential knowledge system prompt distillation on-policy distillation
On-Policy Context Distillation trains a student model on its own generated trajectories while minimizing reverse KL divergence against a context-conditioned teacher. It is demonstrated on experiential knowledge distillation from historical solution traces and on system prompt distillation, outperforming baselines on mathematical reasoning, text-based games and domain-specific tasks while better preserving out-of-distribution capabilities, and it enables cross-size distillation.

GT-HarmBench: Benchmarking AI Safety Risks Through the Lens of Game Theory
GT-HarmBench：从博弈论视角评测 AI 安全风险
multi-agent safety game theory AI risk benchmark
GT-HarmBench is a benchmark of 1,535 high-stakes scenarios spanning game-theoretic structures such as the Prisoner's Dilemma, Stag Hunt and Chicken, drawn from realistic AI risk contexts in the MIT AI Risk Repository. Across 15 frontier models, agents fail to choose socially beneficial actions in 39% of high-stakes cases, and game-theoretic interventions improve socially beneficial outcomes by up to 31%.
→ arxiv.org

Intent-Driven Smart Manufacturing Integrating Knowledge Graphs and Large Language Models
融合知识图谱与大语言模型的意图驱动智能制造
knowledge graph LLM fine-tuning intent-driven interaction smart manufacturing
The paper presents a framework that integrates instruction-tuned LLMs with ontology-aligned knowledge graphs for intent-driven interaction in Manufacturing-as-a-Service ecosystems. It fine-tunes Mistral-7B-Instruct on a domain-specific dataset to translate natural language intents into structured JSON requirement models mapped to a Neo4j knowledge graph grounded in ISA-95, reporting 89.33% exact match and 97.27% overall accuracy.

arxiv:2602.13345 arXiv ✓
BLUEPRINT Rebuilding a Legacy: Multimodal Retrieval for Complex Engineering Drawings and Documents
BLUEPRINT：面向复杂工程图纸与文档的多模态检索
multimodal retrieval layout-aware OCR hybrid retrieval document archives
Blueprint is a layout-aware multimodal retrieval system for legacy engineering archives that detects canonical drawing regions, applies region-restricted VLM-based OCR, normalizes identifiers and fuses lexical and dense retrieval with a lightweight region-level reranker. Deployed on about 770k unlabeled files, it reports a 10.1% absolute gain in Success@3 and an 18.9% relative improvement in nDCG@3 over the strongest vision-language baseline on a 5k-file benchmark.
