Adaptive Collaboration of Arena-Based Argumentative LLMs for Explainable and Contestable Legal Reasoning
基于竞技场论辩 LLM 的自适应协作：可解释且可质疑的法律推理
legal reasoning multi-agent collaboration argumentation graph human-in-the-loop neuro-symbolic reasoning
ACAL is a neuro-symbolic framework that combines adaptive multi-agent collaboration with an Arena-based Quantitative Bipolar Argumentation Framework, deploying expert agent teams, resolving clashes between claims and escalating borderline cases under uncertainty. It adds a human-in-the-loop workflow that lets users audit and edit the reasoning graph, and reports gains over strong baselines on LegalBench with Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite and Gemini-2.5-Flash.

Why Agent Caching Fails and How to Fix It: Structured Intent Canonicalization with Few-Shot Learning
智能体缓存为何失效及如何修复：基于少样本学习的结构化意图规范化
semantic caching intent canonicalization few-shot learning cost reduction selective prediction
The authors show that existing agent caches such as GPTCache and APC fail because they optimize classification accuracy instead of cache-key consistency and precision, then recast cache-key evaluation as clustering evaluation over 8,682 points. Their W5H2 intent decomposition with SetFit reaches 91.1% on MASSIVE in about 2 ms, and a five-tier cascade is projected to handle 85% of interactions locally with a 97.5% cost reduction.

LAMMI-Pathology: A Tool-Centric Bottom-Up LVLM-Agent Framework for Molecularly Informed Medical Intelligence in Pathology
LAMMI-Pathology：工具中心的自底向上 LVLM 智能体框架，面向病理学分子知情医疗智能
tool-calling agents hierarchical planning pathology AI reasoning trajectories
LAMMI-Pathology is a tool-centric, bottom-up LVLM agent framework for pathology in which domain-adaptive tools are clustered into component agents and coordinated by a top-level planner to avoid excessively long contexts and task drift. It builds semi-simulated reasoning trajectories from Atomic Execution Nodes and uses trajectory-aware fine-tuning to align the planner with multi-step tool use.

UniRank: A Multi-Agent Calibration Pipeline for Estimating University Rankings from Anonymized Bibliometric Signals
UniRank：从匿名文献计量信号估算大学排名的多智能体校准流水线
multi-agent pipeline bibliometric reasoning model memorization calibration
UniRank is a three-stage multi-agent LLM pipeline that estimates university positions from anonymized OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar bibliometrics, using zero-shot estimation, per-system tool-augmented calibration and final synthesis. On 352 THE-ranked universities it reports a Spearman correlation of 0.769 and a memorization index of exactly zero, with accuracy declining from the elite tier to the tail tier.

When Do LLM Preferences Predict Downstream Behavior?
LLM 的偏好何时能预测下游行为？
LLM preferences behavioral alignment agentic tasks
The study tests whether stated entity preferences predict downstream behavior in five frontier LLMs across donation advice, refusal behavior and task performance. It finds consistent preference-aligned advice and refusal patterns even without instructions to act on preferences, but no consistent effect on complex agentic task performance.

INDUCTION: Finite-Structure Concept Synthesis in First-Order Logic
INDUCTION：一阶逻辑中的有限结构概念合成
concept synthesis first-order logic symbolic reasoning benchmark design
INDUCTION is a benchmark for finite-structure concept synthesis in first-order logic, where a model must output one formula that explains a target predicate across small relational worlds and is checked by exact model checking. The authors report sharp difficulty gradients and find that low-bloat formulas generalize better to held-out worlds.
