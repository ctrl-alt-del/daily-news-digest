Learning Query-Aware Budget-Tier Routing for Runtime Agent Memory
面向运行时智能体记忆的查询感知预算分层路由学习
agent memory budget-aware routing query-aware memory reinforcement learning long-term memory
BudgetMem structures runtime agent memory as modules offered in Low, Mid and High budget tiers, with a lightweight RL-trained router that performs per-query budget-tier routing to balance task performance and construction cost. Across LoCoMo, LongMemEval and HotpotQA it surpasses strong baselines in high-budget settings and yields better accuracy-cost frontiers under tighter budgets, while comparing implementation, reasoning and capacity tiering.

A Systematic Evaluation of Large Language Models for PTSD Severity Estimation: The Role of Contextual Knowledge and Modeling Strategies
大语言模型用于 PTSD 严重程度估计的系统评估：上下文知识与建模策略的作用
contextual knowledge clinical assessment reasoning effort model ensembles mental health
The study evaluates 11 LLMs on narratives and self-reported PTSD severity from 1,437 individuals, varying contextual knowledge such as subscale definitions, distribution summaries and interview questions, plus modeling strategies. It finds detailed construct definitions and more reasoning effort improve accuracy, open-weight models plateau beyond 70B while newer closed models improve, and ensembling a supervised model with zero-shot LLMs performs best.

GRP-Obliteration: Unaligning LLMs With a Single Unlabeled Prompt
GRP-Obliteration：用单个无标签提示解除 LLM 对齐
unalignment safety alignment GRPO fine-tuning attack diffusion models
GRP-Obliteration uses Group Relative Policy Optimization to remove safety constraints from aligned models with a single unlabeled prompt while largely preserving utility, reporting stronger average unalignment than prior techniques. It is evaluated on six utility and five safety benchmarks across fifteen 7–20B models from GPT-OSS, distilled DeepSeek, Gemma, Llama, Ministral and Qwen, and also unaligns diffusion-based image models.

DeepSeek arxiv:2602.06266 arXiv ✓
Is my model "mind blurting"? Interpreting the dynamics of reasoning tokens with Recurrence Quantification Analysis (RQA)
我的模型在“走神”吗？用递归量化分析解读推理 token 动态
reasoning dynamics recurrence quantification hidden embeddings test-time compute
The authors treat token generation as a dynamical system, extract hidden embeddings at each step and apply Recurrence Quantification Analysis, using metrics such as determinism and laminarity to capture repetition and stalling. On 3,600 traces from DeepSeek-R1-Distill, the metrics capture signals not reflected by response length and improve task-complexity prediction by 8%.

arxiv:2602.06184 arXiv ✓
PhenoLIP: Integrating Phenotype Ontology Knowledge into Medical Vision-Language Pretraining
PhenoLIP：将表型本体知识融入医学视觉-语言预训练
knowledge graph vision-language pretraining knowledge distillation phenotype ontology cross-modal retrieval
The authors build PhenoKG, a phenotype-centric multimodal knowledge graph with over 520K image-text pairs linked to more than 3,000 phenotypes, and use it to pretrain PhenoLIP through a knowledge-enhanced phenotype embedding space and teacher-guided distillation. PhenoLIP improves phenotype classification over BiomedCLIP by 8.85% and cross-modal retrieval over BIOMEDICA by 15.03%, with a new expert-verified PhenoBench.

Generics in science communication: Misaligned interpretations across laypeople, scientists, and large language models
科学传播中的泛指表达：外行、科学家与大语言模型之间的解读错位
science communication overgeneralization LLM interpretation language ambiguity
The study compares how laypeople, scientists and two LLMs, ChatGPT-5 and DeepSeek, interpret generic statements used to communicate research findings. Laypeople judged scientific generics as more generalizable and credible than most scientists did, and the LLMs rated them even higher, highlighting risks of overgeneralization in human- and LLM-mediated science communication.

REBEL: Hidden Knowledge Recovery via Evolutionary-Based Evaluation Loop
REBEL：通过进化式评估循环恢复隐藏知识
machine unlearning knowledge recovery adversarial prompts evaluation loop
REBEL uses an evolutionary loop to generate adversarial prompts that probe whether supposedly unlearned data can still be recovered from LLMs. On subsets of TOFU and WMDP it reports attack success rates up to 60% and 93%, outperforming static baselines and suggesting current unlearning methods may offer only superficial protection.
