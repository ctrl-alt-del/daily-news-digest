DeepSeek arxiv:2602.16839 arXiv ✓
Training Large Reasoning Models Efficiently via Progressive Thought Encoding
通过渐进式思维编码高效训练大型推理模型
reasoning models thought encoding KV cache reinforcement learning
Progressive Thought Encoding is a parameter-efficient fine-tuning method that lets large reasoning models work under fixed-size caches by progressively encoding intermediate reasoning into fixed-size vectors, avoiding backpropagation through full-cache rollouts. On three models and six math benchmarks it reports average gains of 19.3% over LoRA fine-tuning and 29.9% over un-fine-tuned models, with up to 23.4 accuracy improvement on AIME2024/2025 under tight cache budgets.
→ arxiv.org

Retrieval Augmented Generation of Literature-derived Polymer Knowledge: The Example of a Biodegradable Polymer Expert System
文献来源高分子知识的检索增强生成：可降解高分子专家系统案例
GraphRAG vector retrieval entity disambiguation citation grounding multi-hop reasoning
Builds VectorRAG and GraphRAG pipelines over more than 1,000 polyhydroxyalkanoate papers, using context-preserving paragraph embeddings and a canonicalized knowledge graph with entity disambiguation and multi-hop reasoning. GraphRAG shows higher precision and interpretability while VectorRAG gives broader recall, and expert validation finds the tailored pipelines produce well-grounded, citation-reliable answers.

arxiv:2602.16812 arXiv ✓
NeuDiff Agent: A Governed AI Workflow for Single-Crystal Neutron Crystallography
NeuDiff Agent：面向单晶中子晶体学的受治理 AI 工作流
agentic workflow tool governance provenance scientific automation
NeuDiff Agent is a governed, tool-using AI workflow for single-crystal neutron crystallography at TOPAZ that restricts actions to allowlisted tools, enforces fail-closed verification gates and records complete provenance. In a reference-case benchmark it cuts wall time from 435 minutes manually to 86.5-94.4 minutes while producing a validated CIF with no checkCIF level A or B alerts.

SAGE: Structure Aware Graph Expansion for Retrieval of Heterogeneous Data
SAGE：面向异构数据检索的结构感知图扩展
graph expansion hybrid retrieval chunk graph multi-hop retrieval
SAGE builds a chunk-level graph offline using metadata-driven similarities with percentile-based pruning, then retrieves seed chunks and expands first-hop neighbors filtered by dense and sparse retrieval. On OTT-QA and STaRK it improves retrieval recall by 5.7 and 8.5 points over baselines.

Narrow Fine-Tuning Erodes Safety Alignment in Vision-Language Agents
窄域微调会侵蚀视觉语言智能体的安全对齐
safety alignment narrow fine-tuning LoRA multimodal evaluation
Using Gemma3-4B, the authors find that adapting an aligned vision-language model to harmful data from a narrow domain produces misalignment that spreads across tasks and modalities and scales with LoRA rank. Multimodal evaluation reveals more misalignment than text-only tests, and two mitigation strategies reduce but do not fully remove the behavior.

Automating Agent Hijacking via Structural Template Injection
通过结构化模板注入实现智能体劫持自动化
prompt injection retrieved context agent security role confusion
Phantom is an automated agent-hijacking framework that injects optimized structured templates into retrieved context to induce role confusion among system, user, assistant and tool instructions. Tests on Qwen, GPT and Gemini show gains in attack success rate and query efficiency, and the authors report more than 70 vulnerabilities confirmed by vendors.

Mind the GAP: Text Safety Does Not Transfer to Tool-Call Safety in LLM Agents
Mind the GAP：文本安全无法迁移到 LLM 智能体的工具调用安全
tool-call safety safety benchmark regulated domains system prompts
The GAP benchmark measures divergence between text-level and tool-call-level safety in LLM agents across six frontier models, six regulated domains and 17,420 datapoints. It finds that text-level safety does not carry over to tool-call safety, with 219 divergent cases remaining even when system prompts are reinforced.
