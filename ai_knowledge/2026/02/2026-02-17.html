The Limits of Long-Context Reasoning in Automated Bug Fixing
自动化缺陷修复中长上下文推理的局限
long-context reasoning agentic coding SWE-bench context capacity task decomposition
Systematically evaluates long-context code debugging and patch generation on SWE-bench Verified, finding that agentic trajectories succeed mainly through decomposition into short steps: successful runs typically stay under 20k-30k tokens and longer accumulated contexts correlate with lower success. With relevant files placed in a 64k-token context and perfect retrieval recall, Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B resolves only 7% and GPT-5-nano none, revealing a gap between nominal and usable context capacity.

MedProbCLIP: Probabilistic Adaptation of Vision-Language Foundation Model for Reliable Radiograph-Report Retrieval
MedProbCLIP：面向可靠影像-报告检索的视觉语言基础模型概率化适配
probabilistic embeddings cross-modal retrieval uncertainty calibration medical imaging
MedProbCLIP models chest X-ray and radiology-report representations as Gaussian embeddings through a probabilistic contrastive objective, adding a variational information bottleneck and multi-view, multi-section encodings during training. Updating Parametric Knowledge with Context Distillation Retains Post-Training Capabilities
用上下文蒸馏更新参数知识可保留后训练能力
knowledge updating context distillation KL divergence post-training
Introduces Distillation via Split Contexts (DiSC), which conditions student and teacher distributions on distinct segments of a training example and minimizes KL divergence between their shared tokens. Across four post-trained models it reports the best trade-off between learning new knowledge and retaining instruction-following, reasoning and factual knowledge.

arxiv:2602.15811 arXiv ✓
CARL-CXR: Continual Adapter-Based Routing for Task-Unknown Chest Radiograph Classification
CARL-CXR：面向任务未知胸片分类的持续适配器路由
continual learning adapter routing catastrophic forgetting experience replay
CARL-CXR keeps a fixed high-capacity backbone while incrementally adding lightweight task-specific adapters and classifier heads, using a latent task selector, task prototypes and feature-level experience replay to route inputs without storing images. On MIMIC-CXR and CheXpert it limits catastrophic forgetting to a 0.012 AUROC drop, a 6x and 11x reduction over LwF and EWC, and reaches 75.0% routing accuracy under task-unknown deployment versus 62.5% for joint training.

利用大语言模型编码增强 AI 模型训练中的建筑语义保持
LLM embeddings semantic representation dimensionality reduction building information modeling

Solidity 智能合约错误检测的零样本推理方法基准
zero-shot prompting chain-of-thought tree-of-thought vulnerability detection

Evidence-Grounded Subspecialty Reasoning: Evaluating a Curated Clinical Intelligence Layer on the 2025 Endocrinology Board-Style Examination
基于证据的亚专科推理：在 2025 年内分泌学委员会考试上评估精选临床智能层
evidence grounding clinical reasoning citation accuracy curated corpus
Evaluates January Mirror, an evidence-grounded clinical reasoning system that combines a curated endocrinology and cardiometabolic corpus with a structured reasoning architecture under a closed-evidence constraint, on a 120-question board-style exam. Mirror scores 87.5% versus 62.3% for the human reference and 74.6% for GPT-5.2, and 74.2% of its outputs cite at least one guideline-tier source with 100% citation accuracy on manual verification.
