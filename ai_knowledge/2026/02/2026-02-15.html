CORPGEN: Simulating Corporate Environments with Autonomous Digital Employees in Multi-Horizon Task Environments
CORPGEN：在多时间跨度任务环境中用自主数字员工模拟企业
multi-horizon tasks tiered memory context saturation digital employees
The paper introduces Multi-Horizon Task Environments, a problem class requiring coherent execution across 45+ interleaved tasks and 500-1,500+ steps in persistent contexts, and identifies context saturation, memory interference, dependency complexity and reprioritization overhead as failure modes. CorpGen addresses them with hierarchical planning, sub-agent isolation, tiered memory and adaptive summarization, achieving up to 3.5x improvement over baselines (15.2% vs 4.3%) across three computer-use backends on OSWorld Office.

Text Before Vision: Staged Knowledge Injection Matters for Agentic RLVR in Ultra-High-Resolution Remote Sensing Understanding
先文本后视觉：分阶段知识注入对超高分辨率遥感智能体 RLVR 至关重要
knowledge injection agentic RLVR remote sensing multimodal reasoning
The paper compares cold-start SFT, RLVR and agentic RLVR for ultra-high-resolution remote sensing and finds that high-quality Earth-science text-only QA is a primary driver of visual reasoning gains. Its staged recipe first cold-starts on knowledge-graph-verified Earth-science text QA and then pre-warms on hard image-text examples during SFT, achieving 60.40% Pass@1 on XLRS-Bench and outperforming larger general-purpose models such as GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3.0 Pro.

A Multi-Agent Framework for Medical AI: Leveraging Fine-Tuned GPT, LLaMA, and DeepSeek R1 for Evidence-Based and Bias-Aware Clinical Query Processing
医疗 AI 多智能体框架：利用微调的 GPT、LLaMA 与 DeepSeek R1 进行循证且抗偏见的临床查询处理
multi-agent systems evidence retrieval medical QA uncertainty estimation
The paper proposes a two-phase multi-agent medical QA framework: fine-tuning GPT, LLaMA and DeepSeek R1 on MedQuAD-derived data with 20k+ question-answer pairs, then composing a Clinical Reasoning agent, an Evidence Retrieval agent that queries PubMed and a Refinement agent, with uncertainty scoring, bias checks and optional human validation. The full system reports 87% accuracy with relevance around 0.80, reduced uncertainty after evidence augmentation and mean end-to-end latency of 36.5 seconds.

arxiv:2602.18497 arXiv ✓
PIPE-RDF: An LLM-Assisted Pipeline for Enterprise RDF Benchmarking
PIPE-RDF：面向企业 RDF 基准构建的 LLM 辅助流水线
RDF knowledge graphs SPARQL benchmark generation enterprise data
PIPE-RDF is a three-phase pipeline that constructs schema-specific natural-language-to-SPARQL benchmarks for enterprise RDF knowledge graphs using reverse querying, category-balanced template generation, retrieval-augmented prompting, deduplication and execution-based validation with repair. Instantiated on a fixed-schema 5,000-company location slice, it generates a balanced benchmark of 450 question-SPARQL pairs across nine categories and achieves 100% parse and execution validity after repair.

arxiv:2602.14200 arXiv ✓
TS-Haystack: A Multi-Task Retrieval Benchmark for Long-Context Time-Series Reasoning
TS-Haystack：面向长上下文时间序列推理的多任务检索基准
long-context retrieval time series agentic retrieval benchmark
TS-Haystack is a multi-domain retrieval benchmark with ten event-grounded QA tasks over contexts from 100 seconds to 24 hours, covering direct retrieval, temporal reasoning, multi-step reasoning and contextual anomaly detection. Existing time-series language models show severe long-context degradation, while an agentic retrieval framework using specialized time-series classifier tools matches or outperforms state-of-the-art TSLMs on 9 of 10 tasks.

AXE: Grey-Box Exploitability Confirmation for Localized Vulnerability Reports
AXE：面向定位漏洞报告的灰盒可利用性确认
multi-agent security vulnerability confirmation grey-box testing
The paper introduces Agentic eXploit Engine (AXE), a multi-agent framework that uses minimal vulnerability metadata, a CWE classification and a vulnerable code location, to confirm the exploitability of reported web application vulnerabilities. On CVE-Bench it reports a 30% exploitation success rate, a 3x improvement over black-box baselines, with grey-box metadata yielding a 1.75x gain even in a single-agent setup.
