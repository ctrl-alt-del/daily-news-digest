FlyAOC: Evaluating Agentic Ontology Curation of Drosophila Scientific Knowledge Bases
FlyAOC：评估果蝇科学知识库的智能体本体整理
ontology curation scientific knowledge base retrieval-augmented curation agent benchmark tool use
FlyAOC evaluates AI agents on end-to-end ontology curation from scientific literature: given a gene symbol, a short FlyBase description, a 16,898-paper corpus and ontology resources, agents must search for evidence and recover curator-relevant annotations such as function terms, expression patterns and historical synonyms. The benchmark contains 7,397 expert-curated annotations across 100 genes, and results vary with harness design, model family and tool-use reliability.

When Does Embedding Magnitude Matter? A Cross-Task Functional-Symmetry Framework
嵌入模长何时重要？一个跨任务的功能对称性框架
embedding magnitude normalization dense retrieval functional symmetry
The paper proposes a 2x2 framework that independently controls query-side and document-side normalization, exposing QNorm and DNorm variants between cosine similarity and dot product. On MS MARCO, BEIR, BRIGHT and multi-hop QA the unilateral variants beat both, with relative gains up to +72% out-of-domain and +24% on downstream RAG, and the coarse prediction extends to five additional task families.

STaR: Scalable Task-Conditioned Retrieval for Long-Horizon Multimodal Robot Memory
STaR：面向长时程多模态机器人记忆的可扩展任务条件检索
long-term memory task-conditioned retrieval information bottleneck robot navigation multimodal memory
STaR builds a task-agnostic multimodal long-term memory for mobile robots that preserves object attributes, spatial relations and dynamic events, and adds a Scalable Task-Conditioned Retrieval algorithm based on the Information Bottleneck principle to select compact, non-redundant memories. It outperforms strong baselines on NaVQA and a warehouse benchmark with markedly lower spatial error, and was deployed on a real Husky wheeled robot indoors and outdoors.

DeepSeek arxiv:2602.09000 arXiv ✓
iGRPO: Self-Feedback-Driven LLM Reasoning
iGRPO：自反馈驱动的 LLM 推理
reinforcement learning self-feedback GRPO mathematical reasoning self-conditioning
iGRPO is a two-stage extension of GRPO that first samples multiple exploratory drafts and selects the highest-reward one, then appends that draft to the prompt and applies a GRPO-style update on draft-conditioned refinements. Under matched rollout budgets it outperforms GRPO across base models and reaches 85.62% on AIME24 and 79.64% on AIME25 with OpenReasoning-Nemotron-7B trained on AceReason-Math.
→ arxiv.org

CoMMa: Contribution-Aware Medical Multi-Agents for Decentralized Oncology Decision Support
CoMMa：面向去中心化肿瘤决策支持的贡献感知医疗多智能体
multi-agent systems data decentralization contribution attribution embedding projection clinical decision support
CoMMa is a decentralized LLM-agent framework for oncology in which specialists operate on partitioned clinical data streams and are fine-tuned for their roles. It replaces stochastic narrative coordination with deterministic embedding projections that approximate each agent's marginal utility, giving explicit credit assignment, and is evaluated on tumor-board datasets from hospitals in North America and East Asia plus public benchmarks.
