Unified Vision-Language Modeling via Concept Space Alignment
通过概念空间对齐实现统一视觉-语言建模
multilingual embeddings vision-language alignment concept space video captioning low-resource languages
V-SONAR extends the text-only SONAR embedding space to vision through a post-hoc alignment pipeline that maps an existing vision encoder into it, achieving competitive text-to-video retrieval. With the OMNISONAR decoder it surpasses state-of-the-art vision-language models on video captioning, and the resulting V-LCM matches them on captioning and QA while outperforming them in 61 of 62 tested languages.

A Comprehensive Evaluation of LLM Unlearning Robustness under Multi-Turn Interaction
多轮交互下 LLM 遗忘鲁棒性的综合评估
machine unlearning knowledge erasure multi-turn interaction evaluation robustness
The paper examines whether LLM unlearning remains stable in interactive settings through self-correction and dialogue-conditioned querying, and finds that knowledge appearing forgotten in static evaluation can often be recovered through interaction. Stronger unlearning improves apparent robustness but often produces behavioral rigidity rather than genuine knowledge erasure, suggesting static evaluation may overestimate real-world effectiveness.

Efficient Continual Learning in Language Models via Thalamically Routed Cortical Columns
通过丘脑路由皮层柱实现语言模型的高效持续学习
continual learning catastrophic forgetting memory consolidation architecture design
TRC² is a decoder-only architecture that makes continual learning a property of the backbone by combining stacked cortical columns, a thalamic pathway for selective inter-column communication and a hippocampal pathway for event-selective retrieval, delayed surprise-based writing and replay-driven consolidation. Across task-sequential language modeling on C4, WikiText-103 and GSM8K it reduces cumulative forgetting versus Transformer, Mamba, MoE, DeepSeek and continual-learning baselines, while remaining competitive on throughput and training cost.
