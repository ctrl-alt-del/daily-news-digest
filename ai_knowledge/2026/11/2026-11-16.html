Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights
语言模型能够重写自身权重
self-rewriting weight editing recursive self-improvement gradient-free adaptation
The authors report that an open 27B language model can rewrite its own weights through reasoning alone, with no gradients and no training, after diagnosing its failures from its weights, graded answers and ability to run itself. The self-edit makes it 6.6 points more accurate on problems it never saw, improves over eight consecutive sessions, and restores a damaged copy to over 80% of the original accuracy on unseen image tasks.

八成 LLM 泄露系统提示秘密：Nevan 智能体夺旗研究

arxiv:2611.21946 arXiv ✓
Conserved Verdicts, Drifting Commitments: Identifying Harmful Overthinking
结论守恒、承诺漂移：识别有害的过度思考
overthinking reasoning budgets stopping rules chain-of-thought
The paper separates a reasoning model's commitment—the probability that the answer it would give if stopped now is correct—from its verdict, the probability that the answer it will finish with is correct, and shows the commitment-verdict gap equals the expected remaining change in answer quality. On MATH-500, at all 379 points where DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B holds a correct answer, continuing loses 3.6 points on average, and an oracle stopping rule gains at most 1.8 and 2.5 points at 1.5B and 7B.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2611.21891 arXiv ✓
Call-Efficient ICD-10 Code Prediction via Supervised Candidate Generation and Selective LLM Invocation
通过监督式候选生成与选择性 LLM 调用实现低调用量的 ICD-10 编码预测
clinical coding candidate generation selective LLM invocation hierarchical labels
The pipeline combines transformer embeddings, tree-based supervised candidate generation, candidate-level reranking and gated LLM assistance for assigning ICD-10 codes from short clinical diagnosis text. Its deployable Pre-LLM fusion system reaches 83.19% locked-test accuracy while invoking the LLM for 31.97% of cases, a 34.5% relative error reduction over the supervised parent-code baseline.

arxiv:2611.21598 arXiv ✓
Mitigating Catastrophic Forgetting with Physics-Indexed Specialist Memory: the Evolutionary Neuroplastic Agent
以物理索引专家记忆缓解灾难性遗忘：进化神经可塑性 Agent
specialist memory catastrophic forgetting policy reuse continual reinforcement learning
The Evolutionary Neuroplastic Agent evolves small neural networks without gradients, archives selected policies by detected dynamic regime and retrieves them through Bayesian policy reuse as a specialist memory. In a 30-replicate CartPole ablation the complete architecture reaches a mean success rate of 83.2% across five test environments, though it adapts more slowly than PPO on CartPole and achieves lower returns than SAC on HalfCheetah.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2611.21828 arXiv ✓
Coordination of Heterogeneous Teams with Uncertain Task Requirements
任务需求不确定下的异构团队协同
heterogeneous teams multi-agent planning task discovery graph neural planner
The paper introduces a hierarchical planner that uses a graph neural network policy to assign actions to heterogeneous agents and a shortest-path algorithm to route them, with the CAAT graph representing agents, tasks, capabilities and actions. The learned planner discovers task requirements and completes tasks faster than baselines that optimize discovery and engagement separately, and transfers zero-shot from an outdoor map to a larger indoor map.
→ arxiv.org
