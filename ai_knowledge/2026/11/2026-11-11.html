DISCERN: Preserve at Write, Resolve at Read for Persistent LLM-Agent Memory
DISCERN：写入时保留、读取时消解的持久化 LLM 智能体记忆
persistent agent memory bi-temporal provenance graph entity resolution abstention calibration
DISCERN is an agent-memory framework that keeps a strictly additive bi-temporal provenance graph at write time and resolves entities, time-states and abstention through an agentic read loop with a zero-LLM tool surface. On the 310-query ENT-310 benchmark it reaches abstention F1 0.924 versus at most 0.802 for eight baselines and macro answer correctness 0.861 versus 0.806, with entity resolution at 0.800 where no baseline exceeds 0.40.

arxiv:2611.15925 arXiv ✓
ShadowBench: Exposing Lexical Anchoring and the Illusion of Forgetting in Large Language Models
ShadowBench：揭示大语言模型的词汇锚定与“遗忘幻觉”
lexical anchoring latent entity association machine unlearning benchmark design
ShadowBench evaluates latent entity association with a dual-trait association task that removes explicit entity names, and finds a "Shadow Gap" of over 20% performance loss across model scales up to GPT-5.4 and Claude-Sonnet-4.6. Applied to machine unlearning, its Latent Entity Leakage Rate shows that popular "forgotten" entities are reconstructed in internal reasoning traces in over 74% of cases, indicating superficial rather than parametric erasure.
→ arxiv.org

ADAPT: Agentic Diff-Aware Patch Testing for Pull Requests
ADAPT：面向拉取请求的智能体差异感知补丁测试
agentic test generation diff-aware testing fail-to-pass verification coding agents
ADAPT uses an autonomous coding agent to analyze a pull request's diff, generate change-focused unit tests, and refine them with a fail-to-pass verification tool that reports uncovered changed lines. On 426 real-world PRs it reaches an F2P rate of 62.0% with GPT-4o and 89.9% with GPT-5-mini, versus 35.4% reported for BLAST, and full patch coverage on 83.9% of 137 shared PRs versus 79.6% for ChaCo.

arxiv:2611.16039 arXiv ✓
Improved Elastic Weight Consolidation as an Optimization Constraint for Continual Learning
改进的弹性权重巩固：作为持续学习的优化约束
continual learning catastrophic forgetting elastic weight consolidation
The paper derives Elastic Weight Consolidation as a first-order form of a constraint that preserves chosen functionals of old behavior, and shows that EWC's empirical-Fisher weighting under-penalizes samples the model already fits well. Improved EWC penalizes movement off each old output's loss level set instead, raising ImageNet-R accuracy over EWC by 2.00 points (95% CI 0.93-3.07) in a held-out evaluation across classification, segmentation, language, forecasting and diffusion.

The Noise Floor of a Quantization Comparison: Engine, restart, batch and kernel drift, measured against 8-bit and 4-bit effects in two decision models
量化比较的噪声底线：引擎、重启、批处理与内核漂移相对 8 位和 4 位效应的测量
quantization evaluation measurement noise model serving
The study measures non-quantization sources of decision drift for two Cloudflare decision models on an NVIDIA GB10 and states quantization effects as excess over them. Non-quantization sources move 0-0.92% of decisions while 4-bit quantization moves 3.50-5.79%, and a Q4_K_M build changes net accuracy by only six questions out of 31,275 even as 436 decisions get worse.
