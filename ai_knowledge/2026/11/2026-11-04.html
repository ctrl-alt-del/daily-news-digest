Micius-Flash: Lightweight Agentic Intelligence for Engineering
Micius-Flash：面向工程领域的轻量级智能体智能
engineering model knowledge-tree data flywheel multi-teacher distillation multimodal reasoning semiconductor benchmark
Micius-Flash is a 27-billion-parameter engineering model with native multimodal input, deep reasoning and a 256K-token context window, trained through a knowledge-tree-driven data flywheel and multi-teacher on-policy distillation. The authors report 64.2% average on hardware code benchmarks, 15.0 points above a same-size baseline, and 62.4% on a semiconductor agent benchmark, 17.2 points higher, and release the model as open source.

arxiv:2611.06858 arXiv ✓
CompressionBench: How can your coding agents compress LLMs?
CompressionBench：你的编码智能体如何压缩 LLM？
model compression coding agents recursive self-improvement agent benchmark
CompressionBench frames LLM compression as a terminal task in which a coding agent receives a checkpoint, storage limit, inference environment and time budget, then produces compressed weights and executable inference code. Across six agents and six model-size settings, 29 of 36 submissions pass verification, and the lowest-perplexity designs average a 3.4% perplexity increase over BF16 with 77.33% downstream accuracy versus 79.56% for BF16.

arxiv:2611.07050 arXiv ✓
Authorization Propagation in Agent2Agent Systems: An Experimental Security Evaluation of Multi-Hop Delegation
Agent2Agent 系统中的授权传播：多跳委托的实验性安全评估
agent delegation authorization propagation multi-agent security adversarial evaluation
The paper evaluates a layered downstream-verification design for delegated Agent2Agent workflows in a synthetic four-party healthcare reimbursement scenario, separating caller context, transitive authorization evidence, per-hop Ed25519 authentication and replay state. It reports 14 of 15 adversarial conditions rejected, but an isolated delegation-path test exposes the absence of semantic authority attenuation, and a holder of the shared HMAC key can alter the represented authority.

arxiv:2611.10016 arXiv ✓
Pixie: A Context-Aware Multi-Agent Multi-Modal Large Language Models (LLM) Architecture for Digital Marketing
Pixie：面向数字营销的上下文感知多智能体多模态 LLM 架构
multi-agent orchestration context-aware prompting expert guidelines digital marketing
Pixie is a context-aware multi-agent LLM architecture for digital marketing that pairs a primary orchestration agent with task-specific and reconciliation/reviewer agents, combining zero-shot and few-shot prompting with marketing data and expert guidelines. The authors evaluate single-agent through multi-agent workflows with trained marketing experts and output-variance measurements, reporting improved decision quality and operational efficiency.

arxiv:2611.07126 arXiv ✓
Adapting LLM Judges to Observed Human Annotation Policy through Soft Prompt Tuning
通过软提示调优使 LLM 评审器适配观察到的人工标注策略
LLM-as-judge soft prompt tuning prompt embeddings annotation alignment
The paper studies whether soft prompt tuning can align lightweight open-source LLM judges more closely with human annotation policy, training prompts on FairBotBench and evaluating bias, toxicity and personalization. It reports that hybrid textual-plus-learned prompts consistently improve agreement over untuned baselines, with Qwen 3.5 9B surpassing both hosted reference judges on all three dimensions.

arxiv:2611.07048 arXiv ✓
Adapting the Process of Adaptation: Darwinian, Lamarckian, and Agent-Directed Evolution in AI
适配的进程：AI 中的达尔文式、拉马克式与智能体主导演化
agent-directed evolution recursive self-improvement evaluation misspecification AI safety
The paper develops a theoretical framework comparing Darwinian, Lamarckian and agent-directed evolution after environmental change, where agents generate and evaluate behavioral variants and choose how extensively to search. It argues that more search accelerates adaptation, but that misspecified evaluation targets can make additional search converge faster toward the wrong behavior, a risk for recursive self-improvement.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2611.07063 arXiv ✓
SD-LoRA: Training-Time Structural Distillation into LoRA for Few-Shot Vision-Language Adaptation
SD-LoRA：面向少样本视觉语言适配的训练时结构蒸馏到 LoRA
few-shot adaptation LoRA vision-language embeddings structural distillation
SD-LoRA uses a heterogeneous graph teacher over high-resolution ViT tokens during training only, distilling patch-patch, global-local and visual-text structure into a lightweight CLIP-LoRA backbone. Across 11 few-shot benchmarks it improves over a strong CLIP-LoRA baseline by 2.0-3.0 percentage points in the 4/8/16-shot settings without extra graph computation at inference.
