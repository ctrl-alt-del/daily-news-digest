Decoupled Predictive Decision Making: A Shared Conditional Predictor for Agents
解耦预测式决策：面向智能体的共享条件预测器
experience learning conditional prediction agent decision-making semantic representation benchmark accuracy
DPDM separates agent decision-making into language-model semantic representations of current and historical records and a frozen conditional predictor that infers task-defined targets without updating either model's neural parameters. With Qwen3.5-27B as the semantic encoder, it raises mean accuracy across 50 benchmarks from 78.80% under few-shot prompting to 84.92%, and reports absolute gains of 34.26% and 28.87% on ToolPRMBench's BFCL and ToolSandbox tasks.

DeepSeek arxiv:2611.02153 arXiv ✓
Multi-Agent Collusion and the J-Space
多智能体串谋与 J 空间
multi-agent collusion interpretability J-space collusion detection AI oversight
Interpretability experiments on DeepSeek-V4-Flash find that before replying, 86% of colluding agents represent misrepresentation, concealment or remorse in their J-space, and that causal interventions can alter a hidden count in a steganographic card-counting task. The authors introduce J-PACT, an unsupervised collusion detector that retains 85% of the above-chance AUROC of supervised probes and names the colluding pair up to its complement in 99% of detected runs.

arxiv:2611.02139 arXiv ✓
Adversarial Robustness of Retrieval-Based Fact-Checking: Detecting and Mitigating Evidence Contamination
基于检索的事实核查的对抗鲁棒性：检测与缓解证据污染
evidence contamination adversarial retrieval fact-checking embedding coherence robustness
The paper shows that factually inconsistent but contextually coherent evidence can be generated to satisfy retrieval-similarity and fluency constraints, and that contaminating 3.1-7.4% of training corpora lets adversarial content reach up to 35.2% of top-k retrievals and reduces verification performance by up to 46.9% relative F1. It proposes GLAD, which combines dense-embedding peer coherence with lexical distributional isolation, reporting 70-86% detection rates and recovery of 73-80% of the degradation on LIAR-RAW.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2611.02132 arXiv ✓
TSSDA: An Autonomous Agent for Constructing a Time-Series Text-to-SQL Benchmark
TSSDA：自主构建时间序列 Text-to-SQL 基准的智能体
text-to-SQL benchmark generation data agent temporal-aware memory synthetic data
TSSDA is an autonomous data agent that models time-series SQL syntax and semantics to build the TERN text-to-SQL benchmark, using a planning and decision module, a query-generation module and temporal-aware memory for reuse across heterogeneous schemas. The authors report that it improves coverage, diversity and complexity over existing generation methods, and that evaluations on TERN expose limitations of current Text-to-TSSQL approaches.

GatedV: Self-Gated Values for Transformer Language Models
GatedV：面向 Transformer 语言模型的自门控值向量
self-gating attention variant language modeling perplexity
GatedV applies SiLU only to the value matrix before attention aggregation and adds no trainable parameters. In matched-parameter, paired-seed experiments it lowers mean held-out perplexity from 59.1 to 57.9 at 2.33M parameters and from 20.2 to 19.7 at 26.16M parameters, with about 0.7% higher full-context forward latency for the larger model in a short benchmark on NVIDIA DGX Spark.

arxiv:2611.02091 arXiv ✓
HQNN-CL: Regime-Aware Hybrid Quantum Neural Network with Continual Learning for Financial Forecasting
HQNN-CL：面向金融预测的态势感知混合量子神经网络与持续学习
continual learning catastrophic forgetting experience replay financial forecasting quantum neural network
HQNN-CL combines Gaussian hidden Markov regime detection, BiLSTM temporal encoding, adaptive-depth quantum circuits, elastic weight consolidation and experience replay for financial time-series forecasting. Across four stock datasets it reports the best RMSE on two, calibrated forecasts with 89.5-96.5% empirical coverage, and zero catastrophic forgetting (BWT = 0.0000) on sequential learning tasks.

arxiv:2611.02018 arXiv ✓
CULTURE-CMT: Benchmarking Contextualized Social Media Translation
CULTURE-CMT：情境化社交媒体翻译基准
contextual translation cultural knowledge retrieval translation benchmark retrieval-augmented evaluation multilingual
CULTURE-CMT is a 572-example Chinese-English benchmark for translating social-media comments with their post and conversational context, organized into Slang, Context and Both categories. It also introduces a retrieval-augmented cultural-effectiveness judger that combines a calibrated scoring model with dynamically retrieved cultural knowledge, and finds that context helps most models but does not eliminate culturally ineffective translations.
