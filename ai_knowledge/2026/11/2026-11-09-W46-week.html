WeightBench：评估语言模型作为权重空间程序员
weight-space programming benchmark model internals agentic evaluation
WeightBench evaluates LLMs on 42 weight-space programming tasks across reading, fixing and writing weights, recognizing impossible edits, and data-free compression, with networks from 2K-parameter control policies to 7B language models.
