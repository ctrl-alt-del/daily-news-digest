追踪大语言模型中的知识与推理
knowledge vs reasoning internal representations benchmark characterization model internals
The paper proposes a task-centric framework that combines attention-, activation- and representation-based signals to characterize whether a benchmark is retrieval-dominant or reasoning-dominant. Uncertainty-Aware Continual Learning for 2D XRD Interpretation Using Simulated and Experimental Data
面向二维 XRD 解读的不确定性感知持续学习：结合模拟与实验数据
continual learning catastrophic forgetting uncertainty estimation domain adaptation
The paper presents a proof-of-concept framework for continual interpretation of 2D X-ray diffraction data, combining maximum-mean-discrepancy domain adaptation, a CNN with a spectral-normalized neural Gaussian process for uncertainty, and replay with knowledge distillation. It reports accurate classification across simulated and experimental data, better domain alignment and less forgetting than naive fine-tuning, while leaving closed-loop deployment to future work.

arxiv:2611.17585 arXiv ✓
Rethinking Visual Reasoning in Text-to-Image Reward Modeling
重新思考文生图奖励模型中的视觉推理
reward models visual reasoning preference learning text-to-image
The authors find that widely used text-to-image reward models perform poorly on targeted visual reasoning tests and that preference fine-tuning erodes these abilities even from strong vision-language backbones. They inject visual reasoning supervision by converting reasoning datasets into preference pairs that share an image but differ in prompt, improving GenEval by 4.0% in best-of-n selection and 5.5% in RL post-training while outperforming HPSv3 trained on six times more preference pairs.

可扩展的对话式评估：基于双智能体 LLM 流水线的聊天考试
conversational assessment multi-agent architecture rubric tracking prompt injection
The paper presents a two-agent LLM system for scalable conversational assessment in which an interviewer talks to the student while a hidden evaluator tracks rubric progress and guides follow-up questions. It tests the design with simulated student personas including answer-extractors, prompt-injectors and confident fabricators, then deploys it in a small AI literacy course and reviews transcripts for grading fairness.

arxiv:2611.17398 arXiv ✓
Alignment with Reflect-Reasoning: Learning How To Follow Principles
以反思推理实现对齐：学习如何遵循原则
constitutional alignment principle following reasoning traces jailbreak robustness
Reflect-Reasoning trains models to reason about how to align with governing principles rather than only which principles to follow, and is evaluated across five models against system prompting and in-context reflective prompting. The authors report consistent gains that generalize to out-of-distribution medical and financial principles, fewer rare serious violations, and robustness to adversarial-constitution jailbreaks without eroding safety engineering.

arxiv:2611.17495 arXiv ✓
An Operationalization of Opaque Serial Depth
不透明串行深度的操作化
chain-of-thought monitorability opaque serial depth model architecture safety evaluation
The paper operationalizes opaque serial depth as a proxy for unverbalized serial cognition, defining natural-language-rooted nodes and an NLS depth measure that can be adjudicated from architecture, training recipe and deployment configuration before training. Across open-weight models NLS depth correlates strongly with layer count and has grown slowly, while latent-reasoning architectures could increase it substantially; the authors argue developers should report it alongside chain-of-thought monitorability.
→ arxiv.org
