FinEvolveKG: Controlled and Attributable Prompt Evolution for Financial Knowledge Graph Construction
FinEvolveKG：面向金融知识图谱构建的可控且可归因的提示演化
financial knowledge graph prompt evolution attributable optimization temporal knowledge graph SEC 10-K
FinEvolveKG is a knowledge-graph construction harness that separates reusable general and persona-policy prompt blocks and confines a reflective optimizer to the persona block, keeping the approved general block byte-stable and every edit attributable. On ticker-disjoint SEC 10-K splits, the complete two-stage block-constrained workflow improves over a frozen prompt baseline by 19.4-30.9% and over an unconstrained full-prompt workflow by 2.2-6.4%.

Unpacking the Compute Efficiency of Strong-to-Weak OPD against GRPO
解析强模型到弱模型的 OPD 相对 GRPO 的计算效率
on-policy distillation compute efficiency multi-teacher distillation GRPO
The paper compares on-policy distillation (OPD) with GRPO by measuring held-out accuracy against training FLOPs across task difficulty, model size and domain. It finds that OPD generally loses to GRPO in accuracy per training FLOP with an external post-trained teacher, but when the teacher is the student's own GRPO checkpoint, OPD recovers that checkpoint's math accuracy with 50 times fewer distillation FLOPs, and multi-teacher OPD reaches joint GRPO performance on a two-domain mix with 42.0% fewer total FLOPs.

arxiv:2611.08487 arXiv ✓
User Embeddings are Superpositions of Interpretable Behavioral Modes
用户嵌入是可解释行为模式的叠加
user embeddings sparse autoencoders behavioral modes interpretability zero-shot retrieval
The paper argues that user embeddings can be understood as sparse superpositions of interpretable behavioral modes, recoverable with sparse dictionary methods such as sparse autoencoders and nameable in natural language. It validates mode recovery on synthetic ground truth and applies the post-hoc, model-agnostic framework to classical and transformer-based embeddings from two real-world datasets, enabling zero-shot audience retrieval.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2611.08755 arXiv ✓
AtariBench: Evaluating Spatiotemporal Reasoning of Vision-Language Models in Atari Gameplay
AtariBench：评估视觉语言模型在 Atari 游戏中的时空推理
spatiotemporal reasoning VLM benchmark sequential decision-making memory design prompting strategies
AtariBench evaluates vision-language models across 20 Atari 2600 games that require sequential decisions from high-temporal-resolution gameplay, decoupling in-game dynamics from wall-clock inference speed. The authors report that state-of-the-art VLMs often fail to infer object motion and direction, make brittle decisions and over-rely on textual interaction history, and use the benchmark to study memory design and prompting strategies.
→ arxiv.org

Turkish Multimodal Video Fact-Checking: A New Dataset and a Comparison of Zero-Shot Agentic and Fine-Tuned Systems
土耳其多模态视频事实核查：新数据集与零样本智能体及微调系统对比
multimodal fact-checking agentic verification low-resource languages evidence retrieval benchmark dataset
TeViD is presented as the first multimodal video fact-checking dataset for Turkish, compiled from Teyit.org articles and enriched with translated Snopes articles and synthetic examples. The authors compare an agentic 3MFact-TR pipeline using Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen2-VL-7B with a LoRA fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B baseline and report accuracy, macro F1 and ablation results.
