Generating Biomedical Fact-Checking Reports with RL-Enhanced Agentic Search
用 RL 增强的智能体搜索生成生物医学事实核查报告
agentic search biomedical fact-checking evidence grounding GRPO hallucination reduction
BioCheck Agent generates structured biomedical fact-checking reports rather than isolated labels, searching only PubMed with Boolean operators and synthesizing conclusions with retrieved evidence. Trained with Evidence-Grounded GRPO, it improves label accuracy on SciFact by 9.95% over its Qwen3.5-4B base, with a 3.7% higher evidence quality score and a 19.63% lower evidence hallucination rate.

arxiv:2608.23897 arXiv ✓
Names Can Hurt: Spotting Slopsquatting Risks Caused by Package Name Hallucinations in Local Coding LLMs
名称也能伤人：识别本地编码 LLM 中包名幻觉引发的 slopsquatting 风险
slopsquatting package hallucination supply chain security hallucination detection fallback models
The paper proposes a two-layer detector for slopsquatting that combines a deterministic PyPI existence check with a Random Forest classifier over ten package-name and metadata features, bridged by an import-name reconciler and embedded in a LangGraph state machine that retries at escalating temperatures before falling back to another model family. Across 300 curated prompts the pipeline produced hallucination-free code on 76% of runs, and the authors report that hallucination rates scale almost linearly with prompt adversariality, from 0-10% on routine coding to 40-73% on slopsquat baits.

Retrieval-augmented generation vs. deterministic tax computation in multi-agent financial advisory: A 2x2 factorial experiment
检索增强生成与确定性税务计算在多智能体财务顾问中的对比：一项 2x2 析因实验
RAG evaluation multi-agent system vector store factorial experiment financial advisory
The study builds a capital-gains calculation engine and a RAG vector store of market advisory reports as context providers for a multi-agent trade recommendation system, then tests their effects in a 2x2 repeated-measures experiment. Enabling the tax engine significantly reduced tax savings by roughly 55 percentage points relative to no-engine conditions, while the RAG main effect and the interaction were not significant and the RAG-only condition had the highest descriptive mean savings at 47.7%.

arxiv:2608.23719 arXiv ✓
ADE: Agentic Data Evolution Framework for Human-Centered Objectives
ADE：面向以人为本目标的智能体数据演化框架
synthetic data data evolution closed-loop refinement quality gating post-training
ADE is a data-centric framework that organizes synthetic supervision as evolving data snapshots improved through a closed-loop Observation-Variation-Selection procedure, with a steady-state admission mechanism that conservatively gates updates. On DEV300 it raises the intrinsic win rate from 50% to 75.81% and the extrinsic win rate from 55.20% to 68.86%, and blind expert evaluation shows a 66.11% preference for evolved answers.

arxiv:2608.23903 arXiv ✓
Continual Visual Learning under Evolving Semantic Concept Shift
演化语义概念漂移下的持续视觉学习
semantic drift knowledge revision semantic memory concept shift benchmark design
SemReWrite addresses evolving semantic concept shift by representing changes between old and revised semantic specifications, localizing affected visual regions with sparse revised supervision, and using input-dependent low-rank rewriting together with structured semantic memory and obsolete-decision suppression. The authors introduce EvoShift-Bench across four image datasets plus Rewrite Accuracy, Preservation Accuracy, Obsolete Retention and Selective Revision Score metrics, and report a stronger balance between learning revised semantics and retaining unaffected knowledge than prompt replacement, fine-tuning and continual-learning baselines.

arxiv:2608.23782 arXiv ✓
GAP-Prompt: Gated Adaptive Prompting for Efficient Continual Learning
GAP-Prompt：面向高效持续学习的门控自适应提示
continual learning catastrophic forgetting prompt tuning knowledge fusion prompt distillation
GAP-Prompt adds instance-level adaptability to prompt-based continual learning through instance-conditioned gating that selects prompt injection layers per image, dynamic knowledge fusion that aggregates current and historical prompts, and shared prompt distillation that anchors foundational knowledge in early shared layers. It reaches state-of-the-art results on CIFAR-100, ImageNet-R and CUB-200, including 87.29% on CUB-200 against an 88.00% joint-training upper bound.

BenchBench-Protocol: Evaluating Real-World Wet-Lab Protocol Reasoning and Modification
BenchBench-Protocol：评估真实湿实验室实验方案推理与修改能力
protocol reasoning benchmark scientific reasoning rubric scoring
The benchmark contains 149 protocol-modification tasks reconstructed from changes scientists made to published wet-lab protocols, drawn from 96 source protocols across nine domains of wet-lab biology. Across nine closed and open models, Claude Opus 5 scores highest at 59.2% normalized rubric score while the others fall between 34.1% and 47.1%, and the benchmark remains unsaturated when taking the best of ten attempts.
