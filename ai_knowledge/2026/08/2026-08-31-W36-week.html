Which Part of the Context Layer Does the Work? Separating Semantic Content from Retrieval Scaffolding in Text-to-SQL Agents
上下文层的哪一部分在起作用？区分 text-to-SQL 智能体中的语义内容与检索脚手架
context layer semantic content retrieval scaffolding text-to-SQL ablation study
A four-arm ablation on DABStep across four models separates the semantic content of a curated context layer from the retrieval scaffolding that delivers it and from accompanying precomputed views. Content dominates, raising hard-task accuracy from 13.9% to 55.1%, 22.6% to 56.6%, 22.9% to 68.4% and 37.0% to 77.4%, while scaffolding without content is worth 0 to 5 points on two flash models and 14 to 15 on two frontier models; the contract also beats pasting the same knowledge into the prompt.

Evaluating Deep-Search Agents under Hierarchical Web Evidence Poisoning
分层网络证据投毒下的深度搜索智能体评估
evidence poisoning deep-search agents source verification generative engine optimization agent evaluation
HAE-GEO is a benchmark that tracks deep-search agents from exposure to recovery under progressively persuasive web poisoning across three attack levels, using 72,039 clean pages and 770 poisoned pages per level over eight product categories. Evaluating 10 agents, the authors find evidence recognition degrades under apparent corroboration, agentic search improves final resistance without improving evidence recognition, and defense prompting increases verification but rarely converts it into recovery.

弥合词汇差异：LLM 辅助、低成本、零样本的科学实体链接

Sci-ZSEL is a zero-human-annotation framework for scientific entity linking that selectively generates entity aliases with an LLM to control cost, applies an ontology-aware filter to remove aliases drifting toward ontology neighbours, and uses the filtered aliases to construct pseudo-labelled mention-entity pairs for fine-tuning. Across five benchmarks it outperforms the non-fine-tuned baseline and is most useful on non-overlapping mentions, and the authors release a new animal-science benchmark linked to three livestock trait ontologies.

How Fast Do Agents Rot? An Empirical Study of Long-Horizon Degradation in LLM Agents for Production Decision-Making
Agent 衰减有多快？生产决策中 LLM Agent 长程退化的实证研究
long-horizon agents agent reliability horizon-aware evaluation agent orchestration
Across nine models, four task families, five horizons and three context regimes, the study finds task success follows a geometric law governed by a per-step reliability parameter that rises with model scale but saturates well below 1. On the agentic tool-use task every model tested falls from near-perfect success to near zero within sixteen steps, and bounding the context window steepens decay, indicating degradation is driven by step count rather than context length.

Selective Knowledge Control for Continual GUI Agent Learning over Application Streams
面向应用流上持续 GUI 智能体学习的选择性知识控制
continual learning catastrophic forgetting knowledge retention GUI agents gradient surgery
The paper proposes activation-conditioned selective knowledge control for GUI agents that learn over streams of applications, using neuron-level gradient manipulation to protect highly activated MLP neurons that hold prior knowledge. When a new application arrives, gradients for unactivated neurons carrying specific knowledge are truncated while those for activated neurons carrying shared knowledge are orthogonally projected, and newly identified critical neurons are merged into a compact historical state; experiments on a multi-app sequential benchmark show reduced catastrophic forgetting with continued adaptation.
