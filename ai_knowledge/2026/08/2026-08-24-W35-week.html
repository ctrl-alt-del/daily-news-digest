REIGN: Refurbished Embeddings with Integrated Guidance Networks for Efficient Context-Length Scaling
REIGN：面向高效上下文长度扩展的重构嵌入与集成引导网络
long-context retrieval document embeddings bi-encoder retrieval benchmark
REIGN is a contrastively trained bi-encoder that operates on contextualised chunk embeddings from a frozen Guidance Network rather than on raw tokens, targeting multi-chunk document-to-document retrieval and cutting per-document training cost by roughly four orders of magnitude relative to chunked Transformer fine-tuning. It also releases a synthetic long-document retrieval benchmark, matches dense long-context retrievers at smaller parameter budgets on Wikipedia, LoCo and a patent retrieval case study, and stays within 0.65 nDCG@10 of a 20x-larger model on LoCo.

采集偏移下冻结血液学基础模型可信吗？

The authors audit 15 frozen hematology, pathology and general vision encoders across four public single-cell acquisition domains for accuracy robustness and calibration, finding in-domain macro-F1 saturated at 0.98–0.997 while cross-dataset macro-F1 drops 34–72% and model rankings reorder.
