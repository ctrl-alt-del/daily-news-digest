Self-evolving Agentic Customer Support System at LinkedIn
LinkedIn 的自演化智能体客服系统
retrieval-augmented generation self-evolving agents auto-prompting production evaluation enterprise agents
LinkedIn describes a production support system that combines retrieval-augmented generation with evolutionary auto-prompting and a modular evaluation framework, treating prompts, retrieval and evaluation as a closed-loop versioned workflow without retraining foundation models. In a two-week user-randomized A/B test it raised QA self-serve by 9.0 percentage points, cancellation self-serve by 4.8 points and routing accuracy by 30.6 points.

arxiv:2608.09888 arXiv ✓
BDH-CQ: In-Context Learning with Recurrent Latent Reasoning
BDH-CQ：结合上下文学习与循环潜在推理
in-context learning recurrent memory latent reasoning ARC-AGI cost efficiency
BDH-CQ combines in-context learning with recurrent latent reasoning, letting inputs presented at inference time continuously update a recurrent memory and solving queries through iterative computation in a high-dimensional latent space without verbalizing intermediate steps. A 150M-parameter configuration reaches 29.5% pass@2 on ARC-AGI-1 at a computed cost of $0.0007 per task, which the authors say sets a new state of the art on the benchmark's cost-accuracy Pareto frontier.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.10296 arXiv ✓
Cracks in the Foundation: Seemingly Minor Architectural Choices Impact Long Context Extension
地基上的裂缝：看似微小的架构选择会影响长上下文扩展
long-context extension architectural ablation attention sinks open model suite
The authors show that four minor architectural choices used by at least one of the Olmo, Llama and Qwen dense families compound to hurt long-context extensibility, with three or more combined dropping downstream performance by up to 47% in ways short-context loss does not reveal. After more than 170,000 GPU hours of controlled ablations they release OlmPool, 26 comparable 7B models with checkpoints before and after long-context extension.

Not a Monolith: Lab-Level Divergence in the Cooperative Equilibria of Chinese Frontier LLM Agents
并非铁板一块：中国前沿 LLM 智能体合作均衡中的实验室级差异
agent behavior cooperative equilibrium iterated prisoner's dilemma cross-lab comparison
The study compares four Chinese frontier models — DeepSeek V4 Pro, Qwen3-Max, Kimi K2.5 and GLM-5.1 — in an evolutionary Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma while holding the strategy-to-code converter fixed across labs. It reports that the labs differ significantly in aggressive-equilibrium proportion, from 1% for Qwen3-Max to 9% for DeepSeek V4 Pro, and that within-ecosystem variation exceeds the average gap between the Chinese and Western ecosystems.

arxiv:2608.10237 arXiv ✓
Beyond Decision Boundaries: Relational Geometry Attacks on Contrastive Embedding Manifolds
超越决策边界：对比嵌入流形上的关系几何攻击
contrastive embeddings embedding manifold adversarial attack similarity structure verification systems
The paper introduces a geometry-aware adversarial attack that corrupts relational structure in contrastive embedding manifolds by pushing positive pairs apart and pulling negative pairs closer, using an offline-trained generator to produce perturbations in a single forward pass. On the Markmatch verification system it reduces accuracy from 95.4% to 38.6% and completely reverses the positive-negative similarity structure.
→ arxiv.org
