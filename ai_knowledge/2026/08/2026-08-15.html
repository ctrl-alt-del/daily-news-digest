LongRCA Bench: Root-Cause Localization in Long-Horizon Agent Trajectories
LongRCA Bench：长周期智能体轨迹中的根因定位基准
root-cause localization long-horizon trajectories trajectory attribution agent benchmark
LongRCA Bench provides 1,140 complete failed agent trajectories from five sources, human-annotated for responsible roles and earliest decisive root-cause steps, with reference roots preceding completion by a median of 48 recorded steps. The strongest of five baselines reaches only 13.2% exact root-step accuracy with DeepSeek-V4-Flash, while the training-free Root-Cause Trajectory Attribution method raises it to 24.1% and responsible-role accuracy to 51.1%.

Valhalla: A Layered Knowledge-State and Service-Governance Framework for Long-Term Scientific Knowledge Work
Valhalla：面向长期科学知识工作的分层知识状态与服务治理框架
knowledge lifecycle layered knowledge graph service governance provenance
Valhalla is a layered knowledge-state and service-governance framework for long-term scientific knowledge work that replaces flat graphs with a five-layer File-Resource-Entity-Relationship-Graph model, preserving provenance while letting knowledge states be exchanged and reorganized across researchers. A Router-Contract-Workflow architecture constrains how language models access, modify and extend knowledge, validated on an antibody-design review task with 26 paper resources, 80 knowledge entities and 92 semantic relations.

Divergent-Convergent Reasoning: Scaling Test-Time Compute through Structured Solution Synthesis
发散—收敛推理：通过结构化解答合成扩展测试时计算
test-time compute solution synthesis minority reports compute allocation
Divergent-Convergent Reasoning is a two-phase test-time primitive that explores multiple candidate solutions and then reconciles them, which the authors show can amplify correct minority reports where majority voting fails. Recursive DCR reaches 93.3% on AIME 2024 and 92.0% on AIME 2025 while using about 27% less compute on average than fixed-compute baselines.

Agentic-SQL Revisited: Autonomy-Based Taxonomy and Empirical Benchmark Analysis for LLM Text-to-SQL
Agentic-SQL 再审视：面向 LLM Text-to-SQL 的自主性分类体系与实证基准分析
Text-to-SQL autonomy taxonomy benchmark aggregation chain-of-thought
The authors reframe Text-to-SQL progress as a leaderboard aggregation organized along an inference-autonomy axis from constrained and in-context generation through iterative, agentic and reasoning-internalized generation, with traceable provenance for each reported metric. A Spider case study comparing 8B open-source backbones with and without chain-of-thought supervision against few-shot DeepSeek V3 and GLM-4 finds that Spider gains transfer unevenly to BIRD and Spider 2.0 and that autonomy buys robustness at non-trivial cost.

arxiv:2608.15175 arXiv ✓
LAPF: LLM-Agent-Based Path Finder Using the UAVScenes Dataset
LAPF：基于 LLM 智能体的无人机路径规划框架
LLM agent UAV navigation agent memory chain-of-thought
LAPF is an LLM-agent path-finding framework for town-scale UAV navigation that closes a loop over perception, memory, planning and action, reusing prior navigation experience and coupling each detected hazard to a bounded corrective action. Across three trials per method it reaches mean path lengths of 512.83 m and 506.37 m against a 497.33 m straight-line optimum, 17.2% and 15.6% shorter than chain-of-thought prompting, with zero clamp events in both scenarios.

arxiv:2608.15432 arXiv ✓
Does the Proof Prove It That Way? Faithful Formalization of Elements Proofs
证明是否以那种方式成立？《几何原本》证明的忠实形式化
faithful formalization agentic proof search citation dependencies proof verification
The paper defines five necessary conditions for a faithful formal proof and introduces Pistis, an agentic oracle-guided proof search whose OrderDecompose strategy tracks citation dependencies and blocks unfaithful shortcuts, paired with a refutation search. Applied to the first three books of Euclid's Elements, Pistis produces compiling artifacts favored over prior work in a blinded human study and an LLM-judge protocol, and uncovers gaps in Euclid's proofs and their translation.

arxiv:2608.15428 arXiv ✓
Gated Against One Model, Open to the Next: Option-Only Solvability in Legal Multiple-Choice Benchmarks
对一种模型设防，却对下一种开放：法律单选题基准中的仅选项可解性
benchmark contamination multiple-choice QA option-only solvability model gating
The paper measures how far multiple-choice benchmark scores depend on answer options alone, using 11,990 four-option items from a Ukrainian judicial exam: shown only the options, Claude Haiku 4.5 scores 0.383 against chance, with 11.8% of items answered blind under all eight option orders. Gating out items one model solves blind does not transfer to other models, distractor rewriting overshoots below chance, while the same probe on LEXam returns chance.
