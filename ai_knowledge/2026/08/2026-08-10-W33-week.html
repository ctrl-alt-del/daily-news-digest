in-context learning recurrent memory latent reasoning ARC-AGI cost efficiency
BDH-CQ combines in-context learning with recurrent latent reasoning, letting inputs presented at inference time continuously update a recurrent memory and solving queries through iterative computation in a high-dimensional latent space without verbalizing intermediate steps.
