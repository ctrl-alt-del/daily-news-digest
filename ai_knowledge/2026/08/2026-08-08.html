Defending Retrieval-Augmented Intrusion Detection Against Knowledge Poisoning and Prompt Injection
防御知识投毒与提示注入的检索增强入侵检测
RAG security knowledge poisoning prompt injection embeddings
RAG-IDS is a three-tier multi-agent intrusion detection framework with retrieval-boundary defenses combining soft trust scoring, label-embedding consistency checking and prompt sanitization. On CIC-UNSW-NB15 it recovered clean performance from R=1.0 at 1% poisoning to R=0.57 at 30%, and multi-document retrieval limited label-flip success to 0.6-2.4% under prompt injection.

面向黑盒 LLM 所有权验证的定向反事实指纹

Thinking vs. NoThinking: Towards Interpreting Reasoning Mechanisms of Large Language Models via Sparse Autoencoders
思考与非思考：用稀疏自编码器解析大语言模型的推理机制
sparse autoencoders chain-of-thought interpretability reasoning modes
Applying Top-K sparse autoencoders to DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, the authors find Thinking mode relies on sparse, high-intensity feature activations while NoThinking mode shows an adaptive, diffuse pattern centered on symbolic manipulation. Suppressing the three most active features degraded formatting, triggered compensatory over-generation and impaired output structure.
