Teaching Nemotron Greek: Mining a Corpus, Adapting Retrieval, and Grounding Generation for Modern Greek across Specialist Domains
让 Nemotron 掌握现代希腊语：语料挖掘、检索适配与生成溯源
multilingual RAG Greek retrieval benchmark embedding adaptation reranking grounded generation
The authors adapt NVIDIA's Nemotron retrieval stack to Modern Greek end to end, covering corpus mining, synthetic supervision, embedder and reranker training, reader fine-tuning and a new benchmark called HERA. After fine-tuning on 65,773 Greek retrieval pairs, a Nemotron 1B embedder lifts nDCG@10 from 0.362 to 0.835, and a LoRA-tuned 30B-A3B reader raises judged answer correctness from 29.4% to 66.9%.

arxiv:2608.05144 arXiv ✓
Argus: A General-Purpose Agentic Reasoning Runtime for Long-Horizon Tasks
Argus：面向长程任务的通用智能体推理运行时
agent runtime self-evolution persistent state verification-gated learning long-horizon tasks
Argus is a persistent, self-evolving agentic runtime in which Manager, Planner, Engineer and Reviewer roles execute bounded missions over durable project state, admitting memories, skills, procedures and verifiers only after review and, where available, task-native verification. With model weights fixed, it reaches about 78% on SWE-Bench Pro versus 59% for Direct Copilot and 76.8% on AARRI-Bench, and after verification-gated self-evolution mature SWE-Bench waves use 21% fewer solve-input tokens per task.

Cross-platform epistemic verification for improving factual reliability in AI-generated news summarization
跨平台认识论验证：提升 AI 生成新闻摘要的事实可靠性
hallucination correction multi-source verification consensus scoring multi-LLM jury
MECV is a post-hoc hallucination-correction framework that aggregates evidence from the source document, Wikipedia and open-web retrieval, then uses a multi-LLM jury with contradiction-aware consensus scoring and iterative minimal-edit refinement. Evaluated on SummEdits with GPT-4o-mini and DeepSeek-Chat as the verifier jury and Qwen-Plus as orchestrator, it improves factual consistency while preserving the semantic structure of the summaries.

DeepSeek arxiv:2608.05466 arXiv ✓
Recursive Synthesis for Long-Horizon Terminal Tasks
面向长程终端任务的递归合成
synthetic data long-horizon agents verifier-guided synthesis agent training
RST is a recursive verified synthesis framework that extends verified seed tasks, realigns verifiers and instructions, validates results in fresh sandboxes and reuses accepted tasks as seeds. Across fifteen rounds it produces 37,484 terminal-agent tasks at roughly $0.05 each, with median reference solutions growing from 67 to 374 lines and DeepSeek-V4-Pro pass@4 falling from 90% to 2.5%; fine-tuning on the resulting trajectories improves Qwen3.5 models by up to 10 points on terminal benchmarks.
→ arxiv.org
DeepSeek arxiv:2608.05097 arXiv ✓
Same Formulas, Different Semantics: Do Language Models Follow Modal Logic Specifications?
相同公式，不同语义：语言模型是否遵循模态逻辑规范？
modal logic logical reasoning reasoning mode benchmark design
The authors build paired modal problems with identical premises and conjectures but different frame or domain conditions, verified by automated reasoning to carry opposite labels, and test whether models follow the stipulated semantics. Four of five recent models fall below a condition-only baseline under direct prompting, yet enabling reasoning mode raises DeepSeek V4 Flash from 4.4% to 88.1% on unchanged prompts.

Can Open-Weight LLMs Produce Kernel-Verified Coq Proofs? A Pilot Study
开源权重 LLM 能生成经内核验证的 Coq 证明吗？一项初步研究
formal verification theorem proving LLM evaluation Coq
This pilot study gave six open-weight LLMs one attempt per theorem on 100 CoqStoq theorems and counted only proofs accepted by the Coq kernel, finding Gemma 4 at 12/100 and Llama 3.3 at 8/100 while three models verified none. Across 600 attempts the overall success rate was 3.5%, and no model verified a theorem with a long reference proof.

arxiv:2608.05124 arXiv ✓
Chained Recursive Language Models for Multi-Iteration Reasoning
链式递归语言模型：面向多轮迭代推理
context management recursive reasoning artifact workspace inference-time architecture
Chained RLM is an inference-time architecture that calls the same underlying model repeatedly as fresh reasoning roots, each receiving the original problem and context plus a compact plain-text summary, a blackboard and durable artifacts written by predecessor roots. The paper describes the handoff mechanism, artifact workspace and evaluation protocol, and studies when fresh-context artifact continuation yields measurable accuracy gains over direct answering even with recursive tool calling.
