PCFBench: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Product Carbon Footprint Estimation
PCFBench：产品碳足迹估算的诊断性基准
diagnostic benchmark multi-step reasoning ontology matching retrieval
PCFBench is a diagnostic benchmark that carves product carbon footprint modeling into six independently evaluable tasks requiring decomposition, retrieval, ontology matching and numerical extraction, comprising 614 expert-labelled items. Across eight frontier LLMs from four providers no single model dominates: the strongest models estimate total product emissions within 2× of declared totals on 77% of products, but that rate drops to 37–58% when the PCF is generated step by step, with only 45–75% obeying mass conservation.

Knowing Before Answering: Decoding Language Models for Reliable RAG
答前先知：解码语言模型以实现可靠 RAG
RAG triage internal representations evidence sufficiency probing
The paper frames RAG reliability as three-way classification of whether retrieved evidence is sufficient, insufficient or conflicting, training lightweight linear probes on hidden activations and attention-derived features from 16 language models. The feature-based router consistently outperforms prompting baselines and specialized RAG models, with the most informative signals in middle layers and hidden activations generally more effective than attention or MLP features.

Topic Matching in the Wild: Benchmark and Lessons from Real-World ASR Transcripts
真实场景中的主题匹配：来自真实 ASR 转录的基准与经验
topic matching sentence embeddings ASR transcripts LLM matching
The authors curate a human-annotated topic-utterance judgment dataset from real call-center ASR transcripts and compare regex, zero-shot sentence-embedding and Gemini-based LLM matchers under two topic representations, keyphrases and natural-language descriptions. Lightweight LLM matchers equipped with natural-language descriptions outperform both embedding and regex models.

Beyond the Vacuum: Combinatorial Strategy Selection for Competitor-Aware Generative Engine Optimization
超越真空：面向竞品感知的生成引擎优化的组合策略选择
generative engine optimization content rewriting Bayesian optimization strategy selection
The paper formalizes generative engine optimization as a competitor-aware strategy-selection problem whose optimal rewriting strategies change as adoption grows, and proposes a two-phase pipeline using Bayesian optimization of combinatorial structures followed by fine-tuning a language model on preference pairs and grounded reasoning traces. The method reports state-of-the-art impression metrics over agentic and single-heuristic baselines on geo-bench and a synthetically augmented competitive dataset, with transfer to out-of-distribution datasets.

arxiv:2608.27746 arXiv ✓
NormasTCU --- A Brazilian Portuguese IR Dataset and an Evaluation of LLM-as-a-Judge for Relevance Assessment
NormasTCU：巴西葡萄牙语 IR 数据集与 LLM-as-a-Judge 相关性评估
LLM-as-a-judge relevance assessment IR dataset multilingual retrieval
NormasTCU is a Brazilian Portuguese IR dataset with 14,469 legal documents, 46 queries and 3,048 human judgments over 812 query-document pairs, used to evaluate LLM-as-a-judge relevance assessment with three models and two prompt techniques. LLM judges show positive scoring bias (MAE 0.46–0.66 on a 0–2 scale) and only fair-to-moderate agreement with humans (Cohen's kappa 0.32–0.53), but their system rankings for nDCG@10 and MRR often match human rankings (observed Kendall's tau ≥ 0.90, though bootstrap confidence intervals did not always stay above that threshold) while being less reliable for P@10 and R@10.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.27663 arXiv ✓
A Versioned Unified Graph Index for Dynamic Timestamp-Aware Nearest Neighbor Search
面向动态时间戳感知最近邻搜索的版本化统一图索引
approximate nearest neighbor temporal indexing vector search index versioning
TiGER is a time-integrated graph index for approximate nearest neighbor search on dynamic vector datasets that maintains one unified graph with versioned connectivity, so arbitrary time intervals can be queried directly without traversing invalid vectors or post-search filtering. Empirical evaluations report up to 5× improvement in queries per second without compromising accuracy over filtering-based or per-time-segment sub-graph baselines.
