MemToC: Benchmarking Memory-Tool Conflict Resolution in Large Language Models
MemToC：大语言模型记忆—工具冲突消解基准
memory-tool conflict parametric memory tool use arbitration benchmark
MemToC is a controlled benchmark for post-tool-return arbitration built from 6,504 evaluation episodes, 542 quality-controlled factual questions, model-specific closed-book answers and tool returns of known correctness, instantiating four source-correctness cases. Across five open-weight 7–9B models tool returns strongly dominate parametric memory: instruction-tuned models retain a verified-correct answer against an incorrect tool in only 6.5–17.1% of eligible cases, follow a correct tool in 86.0–93.1%, and no cross-model ordering is stable across three instruction-wording variants.

Co-Evolving Structured Knowledge and Reasoning in Language Models
语言模型中结构化知识与推理的协同演化
structured knowledge base co-evolution knowledge construction compositional reasoning
KBevo is a co-evolving framework that jointly learns to construct a structured knowledge base and reason over it for knowledge-intensive question answering, optimizing both components end-to-end with QA outcome rewards so that reasoning success directly improves the constructed knowledge base. The authors report larger, better-connected knowledge structures with higher answer reachability, plus improved compositional factual reasoning and controllability compared with standard retrieval baselines.

Don't Overthink, Don't Underthink: Toward Adaptive Reasoning in Agentic AI
别过度思考，也别思考不足：迈向智能体 AI 的自适应推理
adaptive reasoning over-reasoning compute allocation GAIA benchmark
The paper argues that fixed or pre-allocated reasoning controls suit agentic systems poorly because reasoning demands evolve through planning, tool use, memory retrieval and agent-to-agent interaction, characterizing over-reasoning and under-reasoning as recurring failure modes of misallocated reasoning. On MATH-500 and the GAIA public validation set, over-reasoning cases incur higher computational cost without proportional accuracy gains, while under-reasoning cases are consistently associated with incorrect or incomplete solutions.

Assessing mentalization in humans and large language models
评估人类与大语言模型的心理化能力
theory of mind mentalization cognitive modeling strategic prompting
The study uses two economic games with cognitive computational modeling to uncover latent mentalization strategies in LLM agents from four model families (DeepSeek, GPT-4.1, GPT-5 and Gemini 2.0 Flash; N = 2,099), benchmarked against 251 human participants. LLMs showed behavioural and computational signatures of mentalizing that differed by provider and size, strategic prompting generally improved performance, and GPT-5 agents adapted their recursive depth of reasoning to opponent sophistication, outperforming human participants.

arxiv:2608.26344 arXiv ✓
MoganColBERT-TR: A Late-Interaction Multi-Vector Retrieval Model for Turkish
MoganColBERT-TR：面向土耳其语的晚期交互多向量检索模型
late interaction multilingual retrieval knowledge distillation zero-shot retrieval
MoganColBERT-TR is a Turkish multi-vector retrieval model that represents queries and documents at the token level through a 768→128 projection and MaxSim late interaction, initialized from the MoganBERT-TR embedding encoder and adapted with single-epoch distillation from a cross-encoder teacher. With 148.9M parameters it scores 37.36 overall (35.53 nDCG@100, 31.81 nDCG@10) across five zero-shot Turkish BEIR datasets, beating the twice-as-large ColmmBERT-base-TR on four of five datasets and finishing second among five compared models.

arxiv:2608.26004 arXiv ✓
AsymSpec: Context-Asymmetric Speculative Decoding for Agentic LLMs
AsymSpec：面向智能体 LLM 的上下文非对称推测解码
speculative decoding context compression inference efficiency agent benchmarks
AsymSpec is an asymmetric speculative decoding framework in which a lightweight drafter reads the full input while a large verifier operates on a compressed context, with contrastive δ-fusion of logits and a divergence-aware acceptance gate to keep verification stable. Across four agentic capabilities and two end-to-end agent benchmarks it retains about 90% of full-context accuracy on average while delivering 1.3–1.7× throughput speedups at 0.2–0.3× the compute cost on isolated text capabilities.

arxiv:2609.22162 arXiv ✓
Beyond Raw Context Transfer: Representation-based Federated Retrieval-Augmented Generation
超越原始上下文传输：基于表征的联邦检索增强生成
federated RAG representation exchange privacy-preserving retrieval context compression
FedRepRAG is a decentralized RAG framework that keeps raw documents at their owning clients and exchanges only compact latent representations during cross-client retrieval, using a collaboratively trained projector to turn retrieval embeddings into generator-compatible representation tokens for a frozen LLM or VLM backbone. Across decentralized visual QA and QA benchmarks it consistently beats direct-inference and local-retrieval baselines while substantially reducing retrieval-context length and inference overhead compared with raw-context transfer, though residual representation-level leakage remains.

FaithSieve: Fine-Grained Evaluation of Math Proofs with Faithful Formal Evidence
FaithSieve：基于忠实形式化证据的数学证明细粒度评估
proof evaluation Lean verification error localization formal evidence
FaithSieve is a Lean-assisted framework for fine-grained evaluation of natural-language mathematical proofs that decomposes coarse proof steps into local reasoning units, extracts typed proof obligations, and verifies them through a formal evaluation agent gated by semantic alignment scoring. On the 350-problem ProofLoc-Olympiad dataset it reaches 81.43% exact first-error accuracy with a GPT-5.4 backbone versus 72.29% for direct judging, and 84.5% versus 75.0% on the 200-problem ProofLoc-University benchmark.
