arxiv:2606.00062 arXiv ✓
Graph-Augmented Retrieval for Cross-Entity Financial Sentiment Analysis: A Comparative Study
面向跨实体金融情感分析的图增强检索：比较研究
GraphRAG knowledge graph financial sentiment entity recall hybrid retrieval
The study builds a sentiment-weighted knowledge graph of 59 equity entities from 255 news articles and compares two-hop Graph-RAG with vector-only retrieval on 100 grounded queries. Graph-RAG improved entity recall by 6.4% and answer relevancy by 11.7%, with gains concentrated in relational questions, at a 22.6% latency cost.

OSCToM: RL-Guided Adversarial Generation for High-Order Theory of Mind
OSCToM：面向高阶心理理论的强化学习引导对抗生成
theory of mind synthetic data reinforcement learning benchmark
OSCToM generates observer-self belief conflicts using reinforcement learning, an extended domain-specific language and compositional surrogate models. OSCToM-8B reaches 76% accuracy on the information-asymmetric FANToM benchmark, compared with 0.2% reported by ExploreToM, and its data-synthesis procedure is 6x more efficient.

arxiv:2605.20485 arXiv ✓
ZEBRA: Zero-shot Budgeted Resource Allocation for LLM Orchestration
ZEBRA：面向 LLM 编排的零样本预算资源分配
multi-agent orchestration budget allocation inference-time control evaluation
ZEBRA treats multi-phase budget allocation as a continuous nonlinear knapsack problem, with an LLM controller estimating per-phase utility curves and a water-filling search returning the split. At half the unconstrained budget it recovered 94.4% of unconstrained quality on APPS, versus 88.1% for LLM-direct allocation.
