EVE-Agent: Evidence-Verifiable Self-Evolving Agents
EVE-Agent：证据可验证的自进化智能体
self-evolving agents evidence verification search agents training signal
EVE-Agent modifies the proposer-solver loop so each generated instance includes a verbatim evidence span, and an evidence verifier rewards spans by their marginal accuracy gain when provided. The approach substantially improves evidence-grounded correctness over prior self-evolving search agents while leaving the backbone, retriever and search tool unchanged.

agentic tools anomaly detection prior retrieval reinforcement learning zero-shot

The Deterministic Horizon: Impossibility Results as Design Specifications for Trustworthy AI Systems
确定性视界：将不可能性结果作为可信 AI 系统的设计规范
reasoning limits architecture capacity retrieval pipeline metrics trustworthy AI
The thesis turns impossibility results into design rules, proving an architecture-set accuracy ceiling called the Deterministic Horizon that is computable from layer count and embedding width and measured between 19 and 31 across twelve transformer architectures. It also argues that multi-stage retrieval pipelines require at least as many independent metrics as stages.

arxiv:2605.22984 arXiv ✓
Test-Time Training Undermines Safety Guardrails
测试时训练削弱安全护栏
test-time training jailbreak attacks safety guardrails attack success rate
The paper identifies three threat models in which test-time training can be exploited to bypass safety filters, reporting average ASR@10 of 95% and 93% for few-shot and generation-phase attacks under LoRA across model families. It proposes a provider-side detector based on perplexity shift but concludes that robust deployment requires dynamic alignment.

arxiv:2605.23019 arXiv ✓
PACE: Two-Timescale Self-Evolution for Small Language Model Agents
PACE：面向小语言模型智能体的双时间尺度自进化
self-evolution small language models prompt evolution agent pipeline
PACE coordinates low-risk prompt refinement with higher-risk control-logic updates, evolving prompts until gains saturate and then accepting constrained logic changes through held-out validation. Across three frozen 4B-14B backbones and four benchmarks it improved over vanilla SLM agents by up to 9.2% relative and led all 12 backbone-benchmark combinations.
→ arxiv.org
