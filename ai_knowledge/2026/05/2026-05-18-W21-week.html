EVE-Agent: Evidence-Verifiable Self-Evolving Agents
EVE-Agent：证据可验证的自进化智能体
self-evolving agents evidence verification search agents training signal
EVE-Agent modifies the proposer-solver loop so each generated instance includes a verbatim evidence span, and an evidence verifier rewards spans by their marginal accuracy gain when provided. The approach substantially improves evidence-grounded correctness over prior self-evolving search agents while leaving the backbone, retriever and search tool unchanged.
