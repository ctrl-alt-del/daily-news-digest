MDIA: A Multi-Agent Diagnostic Intelligence Pipeline on HealthBench Professional
MDIA：基于 HealthBench Professional 的多智能体诊断智能流水线
clinical reasoning multi-agent pipeline benchmark evaluation grader variability
MDIA is a seven-node specialty-routed clinical reasoning graph built on a non-fine-tuned LLM that scores 0.6272 on the full HealthBench Professional benchmark under GPT-5.4, 3.72 percentage points above ChatGPT for Clinicians. The authors attribute the lift to specialty routing, multi-turn context preservation, drug-state safety gating, site-filtered search and length-aware synthesis, and note that grader choice changes the score.

Llama-3.2 models in the 1B-3B range go from near-zero accuracy to outperforming DeepSeek-V3.2, with A*-informed signals trading some accuracy for efficiency.

OpenAI arxiv:2608.02616 arXiv ✓
OpenAI Privacy Filter: A Cross-Lingual, Cross-Domain PII Evaluation Across 32 Benchmarks
OpenAI Privacy Filter：跨语言、跨领域的 PII 评估覆盖 32 个基准
PII detection cross-lingual evaluation privacy filter fine-tuning
The paper evaluates OpenAI's 1.5B-parameter Privacy Filter across 32 benchmarks in 14 languages and five domains, finding zero-shot F1 of 0.855 on AI4Privacy and 0.464 on the SPY medical benchmark but sharp degradation outside its PII training distribution. Fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa surpasses it with roughly 500 labeled English examples, and performance collapses for non-Latin scripts.

arxiv:2607.20471 arXiv ✓
Benchmarking the Personalization Capabilities of Large Language Models
大语言模型个性化能力基准评测
personalization retrieval bottleneck sales outreach benchmark
The authors introduce SDR-Arena and SDR-Bench, a corpus of 50,000 customer success stories across 22 industries and 3,500 enterprises, to test two-party personalization where an agent reconstructs the arguments that won a deal. The best model, Claude Sonnet 4.6, reaches 55.8% weighted nugget recall, and an ablation shows the bottleneck is retrieval rather than reasoning.

arxiv:2605.24693 arXiv ✓
CP-Agent: A Calibrated Risk-Controlled Agent for Feedback-Driven Competitive Programming
CP-Agent：面向反馈驱动竞赛编程的校准风险受控智能体
competitive programming execution feedback self-evolution risk calibration
CP-Agent models feedback-driven competitive programming as a calibrated stopped process and instantiates dual-granularity verification, test augmentation and experience-driven self-evolution. Without updating model parameters it raises Pass@1 from 25.8% to 48.5% on LiveCodeBench Pro and improves Refine@5 by 11.0% on ICPC-Eval across three backbones.

OpenAI arxiv:2606.26118 arXiv ✓
The Open Source Economic Index of AI Adoption and Capability
AI 采用与能力的开源经济指数
AI adoption labor tasks MCP servers agent evaluation
The authors build an open-source economic index from public user-LLM chat data and O*NET tasks, finding the highest adoption in finance, computer science and arts occupations. They also generate O*NET- and MCP-grounded benchmark scenarios and find that Kimi-k2.5 with an OpenAI agents SDK harness executes high-level workflows but often errs on granular tool calls.

arxiv:2605.24775 arXiv ✓
PRIMA: Operational Patterns for Resilient Multi-Agent Research with Verifiable Identity and Convergent Feedback
PRIMA：具备可验证身份与收敛反馈的弹性多智能体研究运行模式
multi-agent systems failure recovery research agents context compaction
PRIMA presents three operational patterns for multi-hour multi-agent research runs: a resilience layer that persists typed pause records and resumes without redoing converged work, a sub-agent discipline for task fidelity and tool use, and a multi-phase drafting pattern with cross-document harmonization. Agent identities derive from prime powers, giving collision-free identifiers and O(k) verification.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2605.27445 arXiv ✓
RAGe: A Retrieval-Augmented Generation Evaluation Framework
RAGe：检索增强生成评估框架
RAG evaluation vector databases component selection efficiency
RAGe is a modular framework for benchmarking RAG applications that correlates retrieval and generation quality with hardware constraints while recommending pipeline components for a domain-specific dataset. It evaluates document chunking, vector databases, embedding models and retrievers across accuracy, efficiency and scalability trade-offs, including on consumer-grade hardware.
