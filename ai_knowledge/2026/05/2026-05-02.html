SciResearcher: Scaling Deep Research Agents for Frontier Scientific Reasoning
SciResearcher：为前沿科学推理扩展深度研究智能体
deep research agents scientific reasoning data construction agentic reinforcement learning
SciResearcher is a fully automated agentic framework that synthesizes conceptual and computational tasks grounded in academic evidence to train deep research agents. EPIC：通过基于嵌入的上下文提示训练增强 LLM 文本编码器

面向健康视频的交互式多轮检索
multi-turn retrieval video retrieval query refinement retrieval benchmark

Led to Mislead: Adversarial Content Injection for Attacks on Neural Ranking Models
被引向误导：针对神经排序模型的内容注入攻击
adversarial retrieval neural ranking prompt injection retrieval robustness
CRAFT is a supervised framework for black-box adversarial rank attacks that uses LLM-based adversarial data generation, self-refinement, supervised fine-tuning and preference-guided optimization. On MS MARCO and TREC Deep Learning 2019/2020 it achieved higher promotion rates and rank boosts than prior baselines while transferring across cross-encoder, embedding-based and LLM-based rankers.

VisInject: Disruption != Injection -- A Dual-Dimension Evaluation of Universal Adversarial Attacks on Vision-Language Models
VisInject：扰动不等于注入——视觉语言模型通用对抗攻击的双维评估
adversarial attacks vision-language models prompt injection evaluation methodology
VisInject separates whether a model's output was perturbed from whether an attacker's target concept was actually emitted, evaluating 6,615 image-text pairs with a deterministic drift score and a four-tier injection judge. It found 66.4% of pairs were programmatically disturbed but only 0.756% reached any non-none injection tier, a roughly 90x divergence between the two axes.
