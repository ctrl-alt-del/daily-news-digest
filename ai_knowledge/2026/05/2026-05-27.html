Better Later Than Sooner：基于本体约束后置修正的神经符号知识图谱构建
knowledge graph construction ontology grounding neuro-symbolic reasoning entity canonicalization symbolic querying

Real-rootedness of the Poincaré polynomials of $\overline{\mathcal M}_{0,n}$: an AI-assisted proof
模空间 Poincaré 多项式的实根性：一个 AI 辅助证明
AI-assisted proof agentic mathematics human-AI collaboration algebraic geometry
The authors prove real-rootedness of the Poincaré polynomial of the Deligne-Mumford moduli space of stable n-pointed rational curves, settling a conjecture of Aluffi-Chen-Marcolli through a bivariate deformation that reveals a hidden interlacing structure. They report that the proof was obtained through an iterative AI-assisted workflow with Co-Mathematician, an agentic frontier-model system developed by Google DeepMind, while the authors formulated the problem, evaluated proof attempts and requested corrections.

FormInv: A Measurement Protocol for Semantic Invariance in Mathematical Reasoning Benchmarks
FormInv：数学推理基准中语义不变性的测量协议
semantic invariance paraphrase robustness mathematical reasoning benchmark auditing
FormInv audits paraphrase quality in mathematical reasoning benchmarks and introduces the Conditional Inconsistency Rate, which measures how often a semantically equivalent rewording flips a model's known-correct answer. A MathCheck audit found 4 incorrect paraphrases in 129 groups, enough to reorder model rankings, and 47% of auto-generated connective-variation paraphrases in the authors' own dataset were judged semantically incorrect.

Beyond Recall: Behavioral Specification as an Interpretive Layer for AI Personalization
超越召回：将行为规范作为 AI 个性化的解释层
behavioral specification personalization representational accuracy context compression commercial memory systems
The paper introduces representational accuracy to measure how faithfully an AI system captures a person's interpretation and operationalizes it as a Behavioral Specification that compresses personal data into interpretive patterns. Across 14 autobiographical corpora it recovers most of what a raw corpus provides at roughly 25x less context cost, and it is tested alongside Mem0, Letta, Supermemory and Zep.

arxiv:2605.29027 arXiv ✓
Mind Your Tone: Does Tone Alter LLM Performance?
注意语气：语气会改变 LLM 的表现吗？
prompt tone prompt sensitivity multiple-choice evaluation accuracy variance
The study tests whether prompt tone changes LLM accuracy on objective multiple-choice questions using a 50-question set with five tone variants and a 570-question MMLU subset spanning 57 subjects with seven tone variants. Across ChatGPT-4o, ChatGPT-5-nano, Gemini 2.5 Flash and Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite, tonal effects are systematic but highly model-dependent, and the authors caution against assuming tone-robust reliability.

ReasonBreak: Probing Vulnerabilities in Reasoning-Enabled Vision-Language-Action Models for Autonomous Driving
ReasonBreak：探测面向自动驾驶的推理增强视觉-语言-动作模型漏洞
adversarial robustness vision-language-action reasoning evaluation autonomous driving
The paper presents the first systematic black-box study of reasoning-enabled vision-language-action models under realistic textual input corruptions, using NVIDIA's Alpamayo models as representative industry systems. It reports up to 89% attack success rate on reasoning and up to 72% on trajectory manipulation in closed-loop simulation, along with a reasoning-aware evaluation framework and a benchmark for attacks and defenses.
