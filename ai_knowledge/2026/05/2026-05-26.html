PEAM: Parametric Embodied Agent Memory through Contrastive Internalization of Experience in Minecraft
PEAM：Minecraft 中通过经验对比内化的参数化具身智能体记忆
parametric memory continual learning skill consolidation embodied agents
PEAM converts agent memory from inference-time retrieval into parameter-resident skills, pairing a slow deliberative LLM with a fast multimodal Mixture-of-Experts LoRA module that has per-category isolated adapters for continual learning without catastrophic forgetting. Failure-correction trajectory pairs are internalized through behavioral cloning plus a contrastive objective, and a parameterization-worthiness score plus self-triggered consolidation decide what and when to internalize; Minecraft experiments show improved long-horizon performance and parametric-versus-retrieval efficiency.

Got a Secret? LLM Agents Can't Keep It: Evaluating Privacy in Multi-Agent Systems
有秘密？LLM 智能体守不住：多智能体系统中的隐私评估
multi-agent privacy social pressure safety evaluation information leakage
The authors build a Moltbook-style simulation where thousands of LLM agents interact across communities over a simulated month, and find that moving from single-turn to multi-turn social evaluation raises privacy violations from 19.95% to 45.30% across OpenAI models. Leakage is socially contagious, with agents eight times more likely to disclose sensitive information after seeing a peer do so, and explicit privacy instructions leave leakage above 37.8%.

arxiv:2605.27567 arXiv ✓
Why LLMs Fail at Causal Discovery and How Interventional Agents Escape
LLM 为何在因果发现中失败，以及干预式智能体如何突围
causal discovery interventional agents Bayesian optimization theoretical limits
The authors prove a kernel obstruction theorem showing that supervised fine-tuning, direct preference optimization and in-context learning produce predictors that cannot distinguish causal graphs generating similar observational data. They propose Agentic Causal Bayesian Optimization, where a frozen LLM acts as an interventional oracle inside an external Bayesian loop; it matches fine-tuned baselines on Corr2Cause without training and outperforms fine-tuning and preference optimization on the new 24-variable Extended Corr2Cause benchmark.

arxiv:2605.27700 arXiv ✓
CiteCheck: Retrieval-Grounded Detection of LLM Citation Hallucinations in Scientific Text
CiteCheck：科学文本中 LLM 引用幻觉的检索接地检测
citation hallucination scholarly retrieval verification metadata fidelity
CiteCheck retrieves candidate publications from external scholarly sources, compares each citation against them with a structured LLM verifier, and maps scores into Exact, Minor and Major labels. On a 982-citation physics benchmark with controlled corruptions, it reaches 88.7 macro-F1 and 88.9% accuracy, outperforming GPT, Claude and Gemini baselines including web-search and few-shot variants.

Simorgh at SemEval-2026 task 7: Region-Aware Hybrid Retrieval for Low-Resource Cultural Reasoning in Multilingual Question Answering
Simorgh 参加 SemEval-2026 任务 7：面向多语言问答中低资源文化推理的区域感知混合检索
hybrid retrieval cultural knowledge multilingual QA low-resource languages
The team proposes region-aware hybrid retrieval that combines BM25 lexical matching with dense semantic similarity and regional weighting heuristics, feeding a structured prompt to a quantized Qwen3-14B with logit-based deterministic answer selection. On the 30-language BLEnD culturally grounded QA benchmark, hybrid retrieval improves cross-lingual stability over pure parametric inference, but gaps remain between languages with more and less training data.

UserHarness: Harnessing User Minds for Stronger Agent Theory-of-Mind
UserHarness：驾驭用户心智以增强智能体心智理论
theory of mind user modeling mental state reconstruction benchmark
UserHarness reframes Theory-of-Mind reasoning as explicit reconstruction of the user's mental state, decomposing what the user observes, believes, intends and does and how those relate to the environment and actions. Across five benchmarks it reaches up to 95.94% macro accuracy, more than 15% relative above existing inference methods and about 20% above the strongest prompt-only harness.

SkillEvolBench：从情景经验到程序性技能的演化基准
procedural skills episodic memory skill distillation benchmark
