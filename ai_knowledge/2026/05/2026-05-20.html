CASTLE: Contrastive and Seed-Guided Training for Cold-Start Natural Language Search
CASTLE：面向冷启动自然语言搜索的对比与种子引导训练
synthetic queries relevance labeling embedding retrieval cold start
CASTLE generates synthetic queries and relevance labels from structured catalog data for Airbnb's natural language search, combining structure-guided prompting with seed queries and contrastive listing pairs derived from booking sessions. It reports a query-distribution KL of 1.01 versus real users, a 9.2x improvement over the best baseline, and 91-93% human agreement with its labels.

Energy per Successful Goal: Goal-Level Energy Accounting for Agentic AI Systems
以成功目标为单位的智能体 AI 能耗核算
agentic systems energy accounting orchestration overhead benchmarking
The paper proposes Energy per Successful Goal, dividing total workflow energy including failures and retries by successfully completed goals, plus an Orchestration Overhead Index comparing agentic with linear execution. Across nine task families, including GPU dynamic energy widened the measured overhead range to 0.4x-26.48x, showing that CPU-only measurement understates agentic costs.

MindLoom: Composing Thought Modes for Frontier-Level Reasoning Data Synthesis
MindLoom：为前沿级推理数据合成组合思维模式
reasoning data synthesis thought modes retrieval model fine-tuning data
MindLoom decomposes verified solutions into chains of atomic knowledge-reasoning transformations called thought modes, trains a retrieval model to match problem states to compatible modes, and composes new problems by iteratively applying them. Models fine-tuned on its synthesized data outperformed base, distillation and external-data baselines across nine STEM and math benchmarks.
