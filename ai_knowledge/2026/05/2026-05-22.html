How Well Do Models Follow Their Constitutions?
模型对其行为宪章的遵循程度如何？
constitutional adherence adversarial auditing behavioral specification agentic deployment model governance
The authors present a multi-method audit that decomposes Anthropic's constitution into 205 testable tenets and OpenAI's Model Spec into 197, then probes seven models per specification with multi-turn adversarial scenarios. Violation rates fell from 15.0% for Claude Sonnet 4 to 2.0% for Sonnet 4.6 and from 11.7% for GPT-4o to 3.6% for GPT-5.2, though the authors cannot isolate whether the gains come from specification-specific training, broader post-training or evaluation awareness.

Paraphrase Brittleness in Production Retrieval-Augmented Commercial Recommendation: Reproducibility Below the Rerun-Stability Baseline
生产级检索增强商业推荐中的改写脆弱性：可复现性低于同提示重跑基线
paraphrase robustness recommendation evaluation prompt sensitivity measurement stability
Across roughly 6,000 paraphrase runs and 6,000 same-prompt rerun controls on OpenAI and Anthropic models, recommendation-set similarity between two paraphrases of the same buying intent was 0.288 for cosmetic rewording and 0.135 for constraint-adding rewording, versus a 0.50-0.61 rerun baseline. The authors argue that tracking brand mentions over fixed prompts is structurally unstable as a measurement unit because the prompt string, not buyer intent, dominates which brands surface.

Benchmarking Patent Embeddings: A Multi-Task Evaluation of 22 Models Across Retrieval, Classification, and Clustering
专利嵌入基准：22 个模型在检索、分类与聚类上的多任务评估
patent embeddings information retrieval cross-domain transfer multi-task benchmark
The study evaluates 22 pretrained embedding models from 22M to 12B parameters on retrieval, classification and clustering over 113,148 WIPO assistive-technology patents plus the external DAPFAM dataset. It finds that the best fine-tuning recipe depends on the downstream task, and that single-landscape fine-tuning degrades cross-domain retrieval for five of eight model-recipe combinations.

Beyond Final Answers: Auditing Trajectory-Level Hallucinations in Multi-Agent Industrial Workflows
超越最终答案：审计多智能体工业工作流中的轨迹级幻觉
trajectory hallucination multi-agent workflows agent traces failure taxonomy
Trajel is a dataset and evaluation framework with a five-type hallucination taxonomy over expert-annotated agent traces from AssetOpsBench, covering factual, referential, logical, procedural and scope-based failures. The authors report that nearly half of hallucinated trajectories involve multiple types at once and that trajectory-aware detection significantly outperforms standard post-hoc verification.

Divergent Recommendations, Convergent Diagnoses: Cross-Provider Failure-Mode Convergence in AI Commercial Recommendation
推荐分歧、诊断趋同：AI 商业推荐中的跨提供商故障模式收敛
cross-provider comparison failure diagnosis brand recommendation long-tail brands
Across 215 commercially framed prompts, ChatGPT and Claude disagreed on which brands they recommend roughly two-thirds of the time, with a cross-provider recommendation Jaccard of 0.35. On 7,763 joint failures the two providers diagnosed the same failure mode 95.1% of the time, with agreement rising to 99.6% for long-tail regional brands.

EPPC-OASIS: Ontology-Aware Adaptation and Structured Inference Refinement for Electronic Patient-Provider Communication Mining in Secure Messages
EPPC-OASIS：面向安全消息中医患沟通挖掘的本体感知适配与结构化推理精炼
ontology alignment structured extraction inference refinement knowledge representation
EPPC-OASIS augments supervised fine-tuning with a Wasserstein alignment objective that aligns model representation neighborhoods with EPPC ontology-derived neighborhoods, plus inference refinement using verification, self-consistency, hybrid correction and ensembling. The best deployable pipeline reached 77.13% Code+Sub-code F1 and 63.83% Triplet F1, modest but consistent absolute gains over the strongest supervised baseline.

全注意力反击：百步训练内将全注意力转为稀疏注意力
sparse attention long context KV cache retrieval heads

Baidu arxiv:2605.24233 arXiv ✓
Bayesian Rational Search Engine User
贝叶斯理性的搜索引擎用户
search behavior learning-to-rank user modeling click logs
The paper models a user who decides whether to inspect another search result or stop, where each inspection both yields a candidate and updates beliefs about the remaining page. It shows the optimal policy is a depth-dependent standout rule, derives a closed-form distribution of inspection depth, and applies the resulting likelihood to public Baidu search logs.

重新思考边缘端持续异常检测：真实工业条件下的基准评测
