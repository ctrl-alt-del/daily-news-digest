ZAYA1-8B Technical Report
ZAYA1-8B 技术报告
reasoning model mixture-of-experts test-time compute Markovian RSA AMD training
ZAYA1-8B is a reasoning-focused mixture-of-experts model with 8B total and 700M active parameters whose core pretraining, midtraining and supervised fine-tuning ran on a full-stack AMD platform; it matches or exceeds DeepSeek-R1-0528 on several math and coding benchmarks. A test-time method called Markovian RSA lifts it to 91.9% on AIME'25 and 89.6% on HMMT'25 while carrying forward only a 4K-token reasoning tail.

arxiv:2605.05386 arXiv ✓
BALAR : A Bayesian Agentic Loop for Active Reasoning
BALAR：用于主动推理的贝叶斯智能体循环
active reasoning Bayesian belief clarifying questions agent loop
BALAR is a task-agnostic outer-loop algorithm that maintains a structured belief over latent states and selects clarifying questions by maximizing expected mutual information, without fine-tuning. It beats baselines by 14.6%, 38.5% and 30.5% on detective-case, puzzle and clinical-diagnosis benchmarks, and can be distilled into a Llama-8B questioning policy.
→ arxiv.org

面向大语言模型的归因引导持续学习
continual learning catastrophic forgetting parameter attribution knowledge retention
The framework uses Layer-wise Relevance Propagation to estimate which parameters hold knowledge from earlier tasks, constraining updates to those weights during continual fine-tuning. Experiments show reduced catastrophic forgetting while retaining adaptability to new tasks.
