Agent Island: A Saturation- and Contamination-Resistant Benchmark from Multiagent Games
Agent Island：源自多智能体博弈、抗饱和与抗污染的基准
agent benchmark multi-agent games model behavior contamination resistance
Agent Island is a multiplayer simulation where language-model agents compete in a game of cooperation, conflict and persuasion, designed to resist saturation and contamination and ranked with a Bayesian Plackett-Luce model that quantifies uncertainty in player skill. In 999 games involving 49 models, openai/gpt-5.5 had a posterior mean skill of 5.64 versus 3.10 for the second-ranked model, and models were 8.3 percentage points more likely to support a same-provider finalist.

EnterpriseRAG-Bench: A RAG Benchmark for Company Internal Knowledge
EnterpriseRAG-Bench：面向公司内部知识的 RAG 基准
enterprise RAG internal knowledge RAG benchmark corpus generation
EnterpriseRAG-Bench provides a synthetic enterprise corpus of about 500,000 documents across nine source types and 500 questions in ten categories, with cross-document coherence and realistic noise such as near-duplicates and conflicting information. It includes a generation framework, evaluation harness and leaderboard so teams can create variants for their own industry and source mix.

Physics-Grounded Multi-Agent Architecture for Traceable, Risk-Aware Human-AI Decision Support in Manufacturing
基于物理约束的多智能体架构：面向制造业可溯源、风险感知的人机决策支持
knowledge graph retrieval multi-agent architecture human-in-the-loop provenance

Safety and accuracy diverge across deployment conditions in clinical large language models
临床大模型的安全性在不同部署条件下与准确性背离
clinical LLMs RAG safety deployment conditions evidence grounding
The study introduces SaFE-Scale and RadSaFE-200, a 200-question radiology benchmark with clinician-defined clean evidence, conflict evidence and option-level safety labels, and evaluates 34 local LLMs across six deployment conditions. Clean clinician-written evidence raised mean accuracy from 73.5% to 94.1% and cut high-risk errors from 12.0% to 2.6%, while standard and agentic RAG did not reproduce that safety profile.

arxiv:2605.04005 arXiv ✓
Domain-Adaptive Dense Retrieval for Brazilian Legal Search
面向巴西法律检索的领域自适应稠密检索
dense retrieval domain adaptation legal search NDCG
The paper compares three Qwen3-Embedding-4B training setups for Brazilian legal retrieval: no fine-tuning, legal-only data and a mix of legal data with SQuAD-pt. The mixed setup improved average NDCG@10 from 0.414 to 0.447, MRR@10 from 0.586 to 0.595 and MAP@10 from 0.270 to 0.308 across six datasets, while the legal-only model remained best on the most specialized tasks.

Evaluating Advanced Prompting on Gemini Flash for Multi-Hop Biomedical QA
评估高级提示策略在 Gemini Flash 多跳生物医学问答中的表现
prompt engineering multi-hop QA biomedical reasoning evaluation
The authors evaluated Gemini Flash models on the MedHopQA challenge using a multi-component prompt combining role-playing, multi-shot chain-of-thought examples and detailed formatting rules. Their best prompt reached a Concept Level Score of 0.720 versus 0.565 for a baseline prompt, nearly matching the result from Gemini 2.5 Flash.

BlenderRAG：基于检索增强代码合成的高保真三维物体生成
