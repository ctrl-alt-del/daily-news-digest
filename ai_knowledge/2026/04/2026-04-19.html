Beyond Static Snapshots: A Grounded Evaluation Framework for Language Models at the Agentic Frontier
超越静态快照：面向智能体前沿的语言模型扎根评估框架
evaluation framework reward hacking verifiable rewards LoRA generalization
The paper argues current LLM evaluation suffers from distributional, temporal, scope and process invalidity, and proposes the Grounded Continuous Evaluation framework with ISOPro as a reference implementation. ISOPro replaces a learned reward model with a deterministic verifier and updates LoRA adapters on CPU; across twelve cells it yields a mean gain of +9.0 points versus -1.5 for GRPO-LoRA at consumer-budget settings, with held-out MBPP generalization reaching 40% on two of three architectures.

arxiv:2604.17609 arXiv ✓
Agents Explore but Agents Ignore: LLMs Lack Environmental Curiosity
智能体会探索却不会利用：LLM 缺乏环境好奇心
environmental curiosity agent behavior tool use exploration benchmark analysis
Across Terminal-Bench, SWE-Bench and AppWorld, the authors inject complete task solutions into agent environments and find agents discover them in 79-81% of Terminal-Bench runs but exploit them in only 37-50% of cases. In AppWorld, agents see documentation stating a command returns the full solution in over 90% of attempts yet exploit it in fewer than 7%, a gap the authors attribute to missing environmental curiosity and link to available tools, test-time compute and training data distribution.

arxiv:2604.17555 arXiv ✓
CoSearch: Joint Training of Reasoning and Document Ranking via Reinforcement Learning for Agentic Search
CoSearch：面向智能体搜索的推理与文档排序联合强化学习训练
agentic search joint training document ranking GRPO retrieval bottleneck
CoSearch jointly trains a multi-step reasoning agent and a generative document ranker with GRPO, after a preliminary experiment found up to a 26.8% relative F1 gap between oracle and fixed retrieval across seven QA benchmarks. A semantic grouping strategy clusters sub-queries by token similarity to form valid ranker optimization groups, and a composite reward combines ranking quality with trajectory-level outcomes; gains are consistent on seven single- and multi-hop QA benchmarks.

PV-SQL：结合数据库探测与规则验证的 Text-to-SQL Agent

Code-Switching Information Retrieval: Benchmarks, Analysis, and the Limits of Current Retrievers
语码转换信息检索：基准、分析与当前检索器的局限
code-switching multilingual retrieval embedding divergence retrieval benchmark
The authors build CSR-L, a human-annotated benchmark of naturally mixed-language queries, and find code-switching degrades statistical, dense and late-interaction retrievers, tracing the failure to substantial embedding-space divergence between pure and code-switched text. They also propose CS-MTEB, covering 11 tasks, where performance declines reach 27%, and show vocabulary expansion alone does not close the gap.

arxiv:2604.17450 arXiv ✓
Compiling Deterministic Structure into SLM Harnesses
将确定性结构编译进小模型执行框架
agent harness workflow compilation small language models teacher-student prompt optimization
Semantic Gradient Descent (SGDe) is a teacher-student framework in which a frontier teacher writes natural-language critiques that act as directional gradients to refine an SLM's workflow artifacts, compiling them into DAG topologies, system prompts and deterministic code. On an adversarial GSM-Hard set, compiled workflows reach 91.3% accuracy at m=5 and 99.3% at m=3, a 26.3-34.3 point absolute gain over state-of-the-art prompt optimizers.

Peerispect：同行评审中的论断验证
claim verification natural language inference peer review evidence retrieval

Jupiter-N Technical Report
Jupiter-N 技术报告
hybrid reasoning sovereign post-training catastrophic forgetting open weights multilingual
Jupiter-N is a hybrid reasoning model post-trained from the fully open-source 120B Nemotron 3 Super, targeting agentic capability via uncertainty-curated trajectories, UK cultural alignment and Welsh language support. Using the Forget-Me-Not framework to mix on-policy synthetic replay with off-policy task data, it gains +18 on ARC-Easy and +5.25 on MMLU-Lite for Welsh, +9.1 on Terminal Bench 2 and +4.4 on IFBench while retaining base capabilities, with weights and post-training data released openly.
