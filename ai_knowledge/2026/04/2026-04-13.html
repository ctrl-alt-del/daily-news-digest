从计划到行动：智能体在多大程度上遵循计划？
plan compliance coding agents SWE-bench workflow internalization prompting
The first extensive systematic analysis of plan compliance examines 21,120 SWE-agent trajectories across four LLMs on SWE-bench Verified and SWE-bench Pro under eight plan variations, finding that without an explicit plan agents fall back on incomplete or overfit internalized workflows. A standard plan improves issue resolution, periodic reminders reduce violations, and a subpar plan hurts performance more than no plan at all.

Towards Platonic Representation for Table Reasoning: A Foundation for Permutation-Invariant Retrieval
面向表格推理的柏拉图表示：排列不变检索的基础
table retrieval permutation invariance representation geometry layout robustness CKA
The paper argues that linearizing tables into sequences discards geometric and relational structure, leaving representations brittle to layout permutations, and proposes permutation invariance as a requirement for robust table latents. Using CKA-based metrics it shows minor layout changes cause disproportionate shifts in LLM table embeddings, and presents a structure-aware encoder enforcing cell-header alignment with greater geometric stability.

ViLL-E: Video LLM Embeddings for Retrieval
ViLL-E：面向检索的视频 LLM 嵌入
video embeddings video retrieval temporal localization contrastive learning adaptive computation
ViLL-E is a unified VideoLLM architecture whose embedding mechanism can spend more computation on complex videos and stop early on easy ones, trained in three stages that combine generative and contrastive learning. The authors report average gains of 7% in temporal localization and up to 4% in video retrieval, plus zero-shot improvements of 5% in composed video retrieval and 2% in retrieval from long text.

Narrative over Numbers: The Identifiable Victim Effect and its Amplification Under Alignment and Reasoning in Large Language Models
叙事胜过数字：大语言模型中的可识别受害者效应及其在对齐与推理下的放大
moral reasoning identifiable victim effect alignment training chain-of-thought model evaluation
Across 51,955 API trials with 16 frontier models, the study finds the identifiable victim effect is prevalent but modulated by alignment: instruction-tuned models show extreme effects (Cohen's d up to 1.56) while reasoning-specialized models invert it (down to d=-0.85). Standard chain-of-thought prompting nearly triples the effect size, and only utilitarian chain-of-thought reliably eliminates it.

RegReAct：面向结构化监管信息抽取的自校正多智能体流水线

Beyond Perception Errors: Semantic Fixation in Large Vision-Language Models
超越感知错误：大型视觉语言模型中的语义固着
semantic fixation vision-language models prompt aliases activation steering benchmark
The paper introduces semantic fixation, the tendency of vision-language models to retain a default interpretation even when a prompt specifies an alternative equally valid mapping, and builds VLM-Fix, a benchmark over four abstract strategy games with paired standard and inverse rules. Across 14 open and closed VLMs accuracy favored standard rules, neutral alias prompts narrowed the inverse-rule gap, semantically loaded aliases reopened it, and late-layer activation steering partially recovered performance.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation Must Move Beyond Factual Grounding to Represent Diverse Opinions
检索增强生成必须超越事实依据，以呈现多元观点
opinion-aware RAG factual bias metadata indexing sentiment distribution human evaluation
This position paper argues that RAG's assumption that queries have correct answers creates a factual bias that ignores aleatoric uncertainty in opinion-rich content and risks erasing minority voices or enabling opinion manipulation. It proposes Opinion-Aware RAG, which adds LLM-extracted entity-linked opinion metadata before indexing and reduced Wasserstein distance to corpus sentiment distributions by 18-48% on two datasets, with human evaluators preferring its responses 79.2% of the time.
