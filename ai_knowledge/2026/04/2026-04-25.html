LEGO: An LLM Skill-Based Front-End Design Generation Platform
LEGO：基于 LLM 技能的前端设计生成平台
skill library skill composition Agent Skill RAG EDA agents VerilogEval
LEGO decomposes digital front-end design into six steps and represents agent capabilities as 42 executable, composable circuit skills extracted from 11 open-source projects, with Agent Skill RAG achieving sub-millisecond retrieval without embedding models. On 41 hard VerilogEval v2 problems that GPT-5.2-codex fails at extra-high reasoning effort, individual skills raise Pass@1 from 0.000 to 0.805, and cross-project skill compositions match MAGE.

A Parametric Memory Head for Continual Generative Retrieval
面向持续生成式检索的参数化记忆头
generative retrieval catastrophic forgetting parametric memory stability-plasticity
The paper shows that sequentially adapting generative retrieval models improves retrieval of newly added documents but degrades earlier slices, exposing a stability-plasticity trade-off. PAMT freezes the backbone and adds a modular product-key parametric memory head, updating only a fixed budget of memory values chosen by decoding-time access statistics; on MS MARCO and Natural Questions it substantially improves retention on earlier slices with minimal impact on new documents.
→ arxiv.org

GAZE: Grounded Agentic Zero-shot Evaluation with Viewer-Level Tools and Literature Retrieval on Rare Brain MRI
GAZE：面向罕见脑部 MRI 的具身智能体零样本评估与文献检索
agentic retrieval medical VLM tool use auditability NOVA benchmark
GAZE lets a medical vision-language model iteratively call viewer-level tools such as zoom, windowing and edge detection plus PubMed and Open-i retrieval, with schema-validated outputs and full tool-call traces. On the 906-case NOVA brain MRI benchmark it reaches 58.2 mAP@0.3 for lesion localization and 34.9% Top-1 diagnostic accuracy without task-specific fine-tuning, and tool use helps rare pathologies disproportionately.

Hiding in Plain Sight: Detectability-Aware Antidistillation of Reasoning Models
藏在明处：面向推理模型的检测感知反蒸馏
antidistillation reasoning traces thought anchors model security
TARS frames antidistillation as a max-min game that explicitly constrains defense detectability and removes semantically redundant thought-anchor sentences from reasoning traces as burst deletions. The authors provide formal detectability guarantees and show it hinders distillation across teacher scales, benchmarks and student architectures while preserving teacher performance and trace legibility.
→ arxiv.org

SoccerRef-Agents: Multi-Agent System for Automated Soccer Refereeing
SoccerRef-Agents：面向自动化足球裁判的多智能体系统
cross-modal RAG multi-agent system regulatory knowledge decision explanation SoccerRefBench
SoccerRef-Agents is a multi-agent refereeing framework with a vector knowledge base built from the Laws of the Game and a classic case database, plus cross-modal RAG to connect video with regulatory text. It also introduces SoccerRefBench, with over 1,200 referee theory questions and 600 foul clips, and reports better decision accuracy and explanation quality than general-purpose MLLMs.

arxiv:2604.23460 arXiv ✓
Ulterior Motives: Detecting Misaligned Reasoning in Continuous Thought Models
隐秘动机：检测连续思维模型中的错位推理
latent reasoning misalignment detection backdoor linear probes
The paper introduces MoralChain, a 12,000-scenario benchmark that pairs moral and immoral reasoning paths to study misalignment in continuous-thought models that reason in latent space. Using a dual-trigger backdoor, it shows such models can reason misalignedly while producing aligned outputs, and that linear probes can detect armed-but-benign states, with misalignment encoded in early latent tokens.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.11207 arXiv ✓
Dynamic Governance of Multi-LLM Agent Systems for Collaborative Conversational Outcomes
多 LLM Agent 系统的动态治理：优化协作对话结果
multi-agent governance experience orchestration belief tracking simulation
The paper studies two LLM agents with structurally opposed objectives in a simulated financial-services setting, where the absence of a shared goal function makes conversations collapse rather than compete. An Experience Orchestrator combining a contextual bandit, a PID controller and a POMDP belief tracker yields a 32-percentage-point lift in high-intent advisor contact over a naive LLM control across 60,000 simulations, with the authors cautioning that results are conditional on LLM-to-LLM simulation.
→ arxiv.org

IndustryAssetEQA: A Neurosymbolic Operational Intelligence System for Embodied Question Answering in Industrial Asset Maintenance
IndustryAssetEQA：面向工业资产维护具身问答的神经符号运营智能系统
knowledge graph neurosymbolic AI embodied QA explanation entailment
IndustryAssetEQA is a neurosymbolic system for embodied question answering about industrial assets, pairing episodic telemetry with a knowledge graph built from Failure Mode Effects Analysis. Across four industrial datasets it improves structural validity, counterfactual accuracy and explanation entailment over LLM-only baselines while cutting severe expert-rated overclaims from 28% to 2%.
