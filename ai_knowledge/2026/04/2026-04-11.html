DeepSeek arxiv:2604.10135 arXiv ✓
Think in Sentences: Explicit Sentence Boundaries Enhance Language Model's Capabilities
以句子思考：显式句子边界增强语言模型能力
sentence boundaries dummy tokens reasoning supervised fine-tuning internal representations
The paper inserts delimiters at sentence boundaries in LLM inputs, combining dummy-token insertion with sentence-by-sentence processing during reasoning, and tests in-context learning and supervised fine-tuning from 7B models up to 600B DeepSeek-V3. It reports consistent gains, including up to 7.7% on GSM8K and 12.5% on DROP, and finds sentence awareness reflected in the fine-tuned models' internal representations.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.10297 arXiv ✓
FashionMV: Product-Level Composed Image Retrieval with Multi-View Fashion Data
FashionMV：面向多视角时尚数据的产品级组合图像检索
composed image retrieval multi-view data multimodal LLM retrieval benchmark contrastive training
FashionMV defines a multi-view composed image retrieval task and releases a dataset with 127K products, 472K multi-view images and over 220K CIR triplets built through an automated multimodal pipeline. Its ProCIR framework combines two-stage dialogue, caption-based alignment and chain-of-thought guidance, and a 0.8B-parameter model outperforms baselines including general-purpose embedding models ten times larger.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.10114 arXiv ✓
CircuitSynth: Reliable Synthetic Data Generation
CircuitSynth：可靠的合成数据生成
synthetic data neuro-symbolic semantic prior schema validity teacher distillation
CircuitSynth is a neuro-symbolic framework that decouples semantic reasoning from surface realization by distilling a teacher LLM's reasoning into a Probabilistic Sentential Decision Diagram that enforces hard logical constraints, with convex optimization for soft distributional goals. It reports 100% schema validity on complex logic puzzles where unconstrained baselines reach 12.4%, and better rare-combination coverage than prior methods.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.10189 arXiv ✓
FAITH: Factuality Alignment through Integrating Trustworthiness and Honestness
FAITH：融合可信度与诚实度的事实性对齐
factuality alignment uncertainty signals retrieval augmentation PPO knowledge-intensive benchmarks
FAITH maps confidence scores and semantic entropy from LLM outputs into a natural-language knowledge-state quadrant describing trustworthiness and honestness, then fine-tunes with PPO using a reward that combines correctness and uncertainty signals. It adds a retrieval module for external passages and reports improved factual accuracy and truthfulness on four knowledge-intensive benchmarks.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2604.20878 arXiv ✓
AITP: Traffic Accident Responsibility Allocation via Multimodal Large Language Models
AITP：基于多模态大语言模型的交通事故责任划分
legal knowledge retrieval-augmented generation multimodal reasoning traffic benchmark chain-of-thought
AITP is a multimodal LLM for traffic accident responsibility allocation that combines multimodal chain-of-thought reasoning with retrieval-augmented generation over traffic regulations, and the authors release DecaTARA, a ten-task benchmark with 67,941 annotated videos and 195,821 question-answer pairs. It reports state-of-the-art results on responsibility allocation, accident detection and accident understanding.
