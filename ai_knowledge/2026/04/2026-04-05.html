Schema-Aware Planning and Hybrid Knowledge Toolset for Reliable Knowledge Graph Triple Verification
面向可靠知识图谱三元组验证的模式感知规划与混合知识工具集
triple verification knowledge graph auditing schema-aware planning evidential reasoning retrieval trajectories
SHARP is a training-free agent that reformulates knowledge-graph triple verification as a dynamic process of strategic planning, active investigation and evidential reasoning, using retrieved historical verification trajectories and entity-relation schema information to build task-specific investigation plans. On balanced test sets from FB15K-237 and Wikidata5M-Ind it achieves average accuracies of 87.1% and 93.6%, with gains attributed to schema-aware planning, memory augmentation and iterative hybrid tool use rather than retrieval alone.

基于嵌入与生成方法的 LLM 文档分类评估：机遇与挑战
document classification embeddings vision-language models chain-of-thought zero-shot evaluation
The study compares embedding-based and generative models for classifying geoscience technical documents, evaluating accuracy, stability and computational cost on a multi-disciplinary benchmark. Generative vision-language models such as Qwen2.5-VL with chain-of-thought prompting reach 82% zero-shot accuracy, versus 63% for a state-of-the-art multimodal embedding model, while supervised fine-tuning is sensitive to training-data imbalance.

Belief-Aware VLM Model for Human-like Reasoning
面向类人推理的信念感知视觉语言模型
retrieval memory belief modeling vision-language models reinforcement learning multimodal context
The paper proposes a belief-aware vision-language model framework that approximates belief with a vector-based memory retrieving relevant multimodal context, rather than learning an explicit belief model, and refines decisions with a reinforcement learning policy over the VLM latent space. Evaluated on public VQA datasets such as HD-EPIC, it shows consistent improvements over zero-shot baselines.

Beyond Fluency: Toward Reliable Trajectories in Agentic IR
超越流畅性：迈向智能体信息检索中的可靠轨迹
agentic retrieval trajectory integrity failure modes verification gates calibrated abstention
This position paper synthesizes failure modes from industrial agentic retrieval systems, categorizing errors across planning, retrieval, reasoning and execution, and argues that safe deployment requires moving beyond endpoint accuracy toward trajectory integrity and causal attribution. Adaptive Cost-Efficient Evaluation for Reliable Patent Claim Generation
面向可靠专利权利要求生成的自适应低成本评估
adaptive evaluation patent claims uncertainty routing cost efficiency chain-of-thought
ACE is a two-stage framework for patent claim validation that routes claims by predictive entropy from a fine-tuned encoder to an expert LLM using a schema-constrained Chain-of-Patent-Thought protocol. On a new 40,000-claim dataset it surpasses baselines including a supervised 70B-parameter model while cutting costs by 78%.

法律 AI 的神经符号框架 TRISM：可信、可靠、可解释、安全
neurosymbolic AI legal knowledge retrieval-augmented generation citation attribution precedent verification
