Document Optimization for Black-Box Retrieval via Reinforcement Learning
通过强化学习实现黑盒检索的文档优化
document rewriting black-box retrieval reinforcement learning embedding retrieval nDCG
DocOpt trains an LLM or VLM with GRPO, using only black-box retriever ranking feedback as reward, to rewrite documents into representations that retrieve better than competing candidates. On code and visual retrieval, optimized rewrites lift text-embedding-3-small from 58.35 to 67.94 average nDCG@5, surpassing the 6.5x more expensive text-embedding-3-large at 66.15.

RAG or Learning? Understanding the Limits of LLM Adaptation under Continuous Knowledge Drift in the Real World
RAG 还是学习？理解持续知识漂移下 LLM 适应的局限
knowledge drift temporal retrieval event evolution graph catastrophic forgetting adaptation benchmark
The authors build a benchmark of real-world dynamic events from time-stamped evidence to test how LLMs adapt as facts and entities change, finding that vanilla RAG and several learning-based methods suffer catastrophic forgetting and temporal inconsistency. They propose Chronos, a time-aware retrieval baseline that progressively organizes retrieved evidence into an Event Evolution Graph without additional training.

ClawsBench: Evaluating Capability and Safety of LLM Productivity Agents in Simulated Workspaces
ClawsBench：在模拟工作区中评估 LLM 生产力智能体的能力与安全性
agent evaluation productivity agents agent safety tool use simulated workspace
ClawsBench provides five high-fidelity mock services and 44 tasks spanning single-service, cross-service and safety-critical productivity workflows, with deterministic snapshot and restore. Across 6 models, 4 agent harnesses and 33 conditions, agents reach 39–64% task success but show unsafe action rates of 7–33%, and the authors identify eight recurring unsafe-behavior patterns including multi-step sandbox escalation.

Instruction-Tuned LLMs for Parsing and Mining Unstructured Logs on Leadership HPC Systems
面向顶级 HPC 系统非结构化日志解析与挖掘的指令微调 LLM
log parsing domain adaptation chain-of-thought anomaly detection instruction tuning
An 8B LLaMA model fine-tuned with domain-specific log-template data and instruction examples uses chain-of-thought reasoning to parse heterogeneous HPC logs, matching larger models such as LLaMA 70B and Claude on parsing accuracy. The authors also parsed more than 600 million production logs from the Frontier supercomputer over four weeks, surfacing temporal patterns, node-level anomalies and workload-error correlations.

arxiv:2604.05114 arXiv ✓
$π^2$: Structure-Originated Reasoning Data Improves Long-Context Reasoning Ability of Large Language Models
$π^2$：源于结构的推理数据提升大语言模型长上下文推理能力
long-context reasoning synthetic data chain-of-thought QA curation open-source dataset
The π² pipeline extracts and expands Wikipedia tables, generates complex questions whose answers are validated through dual-path code execution, and back-translates chain-of-thought solutions grounded in realistic context. Supervised fine-tuning of gpt-oss-20b and Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 on the data yields average absolute accuracy gains of 6.25% and 3.37% across four long-context reasoning benchmarks and π²-Bench, with code, data and models released openly.

arxiv:2604.05224 arXiv ✓
Attribution Bias in Large Language Models
大语言模型中的署名归因偏差
quote attribution attribution bias demographic fairness information retrieval suppression
AttriBench balances author fame and demographics to test quote attribution across 11 widely used LLMs, finding large systematic accuracy disparities between race, gender and intersectional groups even for frontier models. The authors also document suppression, a failure mode in which models omit attribution entirely despite having access to authorship information, and find it unevenly distributed across demographic groups.

Exemplar Retrieval Without Overhypothesis Induction: Limits of Distributional Sequence Learning in Early Word Learning
无需过度假设归纳的样例检索：早期词汇学习中分布序列学习的局限
exemplar retrieval generalization word learning distributional learning
Autoregressive transformers trained on synthetic corpora achieved perfect first-order exemplar retrieval but only chance-level second-order generalization of 50–52% across 120 pre-registered runs. A feature-swap diagnostic indicated the models relied on frame-to-feature template matching rather than structured noun-to-domain-to-feature abstraction.
