State-of-the-Art Arabic Language Modeling with Sparse MoE Fine-Tuning and Chain-of-Thought Distillation
Arabic-DeepSeek-R1：稀疏 MoE 微调与思维链蒸馏驱动的阿拉伯语 SOTA 建模
Arabic LLM chain-of-thought distillation multilingual benchmark retrieval-augmented evaluation
Arabic-DeepSeek-R1 fine-tunes a sparse MoE backbone with a four-phase chain-of-thought distillation scheme and a 372M-token, contamination-controlled 80/20 Arabic-English mixture. The authors report the highest average score across the seven-benchmark Open Arabic LLM Leaderboard and state-of-the-art or near-state-of-the-art results on MadinahQA, AraTrust, AlGhafa and ALRAGE, outperforming GPT-5.1 on the majority of evaluated benchmarks.

arxiv:2604.06474 arXiv ✓
DataSTORM: Deep Research on Large-Scale Databases using Exploratory Data Analysis and Data Storytelling
DataSTORM：基于探索性数据分析与数据叙事的超大规模数据库深度研究
deep research structured databases hypothesis validation data storytelling InsightBench
DataSTORM reframes deep research over structured databases as a thesis-driven process of discovering candidate theses, validating them through iterative cross-source investigation and developing coherent analytical narratives. It reaches a new state of the art on InsightBench with a 19.4% relative improvement in insight-level recall and 7.2% in summary-level score, and outperforms ChatGPT Deep Research on a new ACLED-based dataset in automated and human evaluations.

arxiv:2604.06170 arXiv ✓
Paper Circle: An Open-source Multi-agent Research Discovery and Analysis Framework
Paper Circle：开源多智能体科研发现与分析框架
multi-agent orchestration literature retrieval research knowledge graph research discovery reproducibility
Paper Circle combines a discovery pipeline that merges offline and online retrieval, multi-criteria scoring and diversity-aware ranking with an analysis pipeline that converts papers into typed knowledge graphs of concepts, methods, experiments and figures. Both pipelines run in a coder-LLM multi-agent framework and emit reproducible JSON, CSV, BibTeX, Markdown and HTML at each agent step, and the authors report consistent retrieval and review-generation gains with stronger agent models.

arxiv:2604.06391 arXiv ✓
Toward a universal foundation model for graph-structured data
迈向图结构数据的通用基础模型
graph foundation model structural prompts transfer learning zero-shot generalization
The authors pretrain a feature-agnostic graph foundation model on heterogeneous graphs by encoding degree, centrality, community and diffusion signatures as structural prompts for a message-passing backbone. The pretrained model matches or exceeds strong supervised baselines and transfers zero-shot or few-shot to held-out graphs, reaching 95.5% mean ROC-AUC on SagePPI after supervised fine-tuning, a 21.8% gain over the best supervised message-passing baseline.

arxiv:2604.06385 arXiv ✓
Application-Driven Pedagogical Knowledge Optimization of Open-Source LLMs via Reinforcement Learning and Supervised Fine-Tuning
通过强化学习与监督微调实现开源 LLM 的教学知识优化
domain specialization reinforcement learning supervised fine-tuning pedagogy benchmark
EduQwen 32B models combine progressive-difficulty reinforcement learning with supervised fine-tuning on difficulty-weighted synthetic data to strengthen pedagogical knowledge in a dense Qwen3-32B backbone. The authors report state-of-the-art accuracy on the Cross-Domain Pedagogical Knowledge benchmark and the interactive Pedagogy Benchmark Leaderboard, surpassing larger proprietary systems including the previous leader Gemini-3 Pro.

MedConclusion: A Benchmark for Biomedical Conclusion Generation from Structured Abstracts
MedConclusion：从结构化摘要生成生物医学结论的基准
conclusion generation biomedical NLP benchmark LLM-as-judge scientific writing
MedConclusion pairs non-conclusion sections of 5.7 million PubMed structured abstracts with author-written conclusions to benchmark biomedical conclusion generation. Initial evaluations find that conclusion writing is behaviorally distinct from summarizing and that judge identity can substantially shift scores.
