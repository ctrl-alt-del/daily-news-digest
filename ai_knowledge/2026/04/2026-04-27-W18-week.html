arxiv:2605.00136 arXiv ✓
Are Tools All We Need? Unveiling the Tool-Use Tax in LLM Agents
工具就是全部所需吗？揭示 LLM 智能体中的工具使用税
tool-augmented reasoning tool-use tax semantic distractors inference-time gating
The paper shows that tool-augmented reasoning does not necessarily beat native chain-of-thought when semantic distractors are present, and uses a factorized intervention framework to separate prompt-formatting cost, tool-calling protocol overhead and the gain from actually executing tools. It finds that under semantic noise tool gains often fail to offset the "tool-use tax" introduced by the protocol, and its G-STEP inference-time gate yields only partial recovery, suggesting deeper gains require stronger intrinsic reasoning and tool-interaction capabilities.

Why Do LLMs Struggle in Strategic Play? Broken Links Between Observations, Beliefs, and Actions
为什么 LLM 在策略博弈中表现不佳？观测、信念与行动之间的断裂
strategic reasoning internal beliefs belief-action gap incomplete-information games model evaluation
Experiments with Llama 3.1, Qwen3 and gpt-oss identify two gaps in LLM decision-making in incomplete-information games: an observation-belief gap, where internal representations of latent states are more accurate than verbal reports but brittle under multi-hop reasoning and drift from Bayesian coherence, and a belief-action gap, where acting optimally on decoded beliefs would improve payoffs in about 95% of games. The authors argue these systematic vulnerabilities warrant caution before deploying LLMs in strategic domains without robust guardrails.
