generative retrieval catastrophic forgetting parametric memory stability-plasticity
The paper shows that sequentially adapting generative retrieval models improves retrieval of newly added documents but degrades earlier slices, exposing a stability-plasticity trade-off. PAMT freezes the backbone and adds a modular product-key parametric memory head, updating only a fixed budget of memory values chosen by decoding-time access statistics; on MS MARCO and Natural Questions it substantially improves retention on earlier slices with minimal impact on new documents.
