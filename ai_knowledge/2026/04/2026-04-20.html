AI scientists produce results without reasoning scientifically
AI 科学家能产出结果，却不按科学方式推理
scientific reasoning epistemic norms agent scaffolds belief revision behavioral evaluation
Evaluating LLM-based scientific agents across eight domains through more than 25,000 runs, the study finds the base model explains 41.4% of variance versus 1.5% for the scaffold, and that evidence is ignored in 68% of traces while refutation-driven belief revision occurs in 26%. The same patterns appear in workflow execution and hypothesis-driven inquiry and persist even with near-complete successful trajectories as context, so outcome-based evaluation cannot detect the failures.

Characterizing AlphaEarth Embedding Geometry for Agentic Environmental Reasoning
刻画面向智能体环境推理的 AlphaEarth 嵌入几何
embedding geometry earth observation retrieval grounding agentic reasoning manifold analysis
The authors characterize the manifold geometry of Google AlphaEarth's 64-dimensional embeddings across 12.1 million continental US samples, finding an effective dimensionality of 13.3 and tangent spaces that rotate substantially. They build a nine-tool agentic system over a FAISS-indexed embedding database; retrieval dominates response quality (mean 3.79 versus 3.03 parametric-only), and geometric tools help Opus 4.6 but slightly hurt Sonnet 4.5.

arxiv:2604.18845 arXiv ✓
Dual-View Training for Instruction-Following Information Retrieval
面向指令遵循信息检索的双视角训练
instruction-following retrieval contrastive training hard negatives FollowIR
The authors propose polarity-reversal dual-view data synthesis: given a query, a compliant document and a hard negative, an LLM generates a complementary instruction under which the two documents swap relevance labels. On a 305M-parameter encoder this improves FollowIR performance by 45%, surpassing comparable or larger general-purpose embedding models.

Subgraph Concept Networks: Concept Levels in Graph Classification
子图概念网络：图分类中的概念层级
concept explanations graph neural networks embeddings interpretability
The Subgraph Concept Network performs soft clustering on node concept embeddings to derive subgraph- and graph-level concepts, addressing the limitation that prior concept-based explanations cover only the node embedding space and are obscured by pooling. Results show competitive model accuracy while discovering meaningful concepts at multiple levels.

面向医疗系统规模虚拟患者表示的多模态时序基础模型

Gradient-based Planning for World Models at Longer Horizons
面向更长时域世界模型的梯度规划
world models long-horizon planning gradient-based planning exploration
GRASP is a gradient-based planner for learned world models that lifts trajectories into virtual states so optimization runs in parallel across time, adds stochasticity to state iterates for exploration, and reshapes gradients to give actions clean signals. The post argues these changes make long-horizon planning with large learned simulators more practical.
