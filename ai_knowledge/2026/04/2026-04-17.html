Audio2Tool: Speak, Call, Act -- A Dataset for Benchmarking Speech Tool Use
Audio2Tool：评测语音工具调用的数据集
speech tool use tool calling speech language models benchmark dataset compositional reasoning
Audio2Tool is a dataset of roughly 30,000 queries for evaluating tool calling by speech language models across smart car, smart home and wearables domains. It spans direct commands through multi-intent and needle-in-a-haystack tasks, uses zero-shot voice cloning and varied noise profiles, and shows strong simple-command performance but significant degradation under compositional and acoustic challenges.

Agentic Frameworks for Reasoning Tasks: An Empirical Study
推理任务中的智能体框架：一项实证研究
agentic frameworks orchestration context growth cost management reasoning benchmark
The study evaluates 22 widely used agentic frameworks on BBH, GSM8K and ARC under a unified setting, finding 19 completed all three and 12 showed stable accuracy of 74.6-75.9% at 4-6 seconds and 0.14-0.18 cents per task. Failures traced mainly to orchestration, including uncontrolled context growth, costly retry loops and exhausted API quotas, while GSM8K accuracy fell to 44.35%.

Authority Signals in Claude AI Health Citations: A Descriptive Analysis Using the Authority Signals Framework
Claude 健康类引用中的权威信号：基于权威信号框架的描述性分析
citation integrity source authority health information grounding evaluation framework
Analyzing 3,075 HealthSearchQA consumer health questions that yielded 10,038 citations, the study finds established institutional sources account for 97.8% of Claude's citations, with medical institutions, government resources and professional associations the most frequent organization types. The top 10 organizations supply 57.8% of citations, and the authors propose the Authority Signals Framework as a cross-platform evaluation tool.

Debate as Reward: A Multi-Agent Reward System for Scientific Ideation via RL Post-Training
以辩论为奖励：面向科学构思的 RL 后训练多智能体奖励系统
multi-agent debate reward hacking scientific ideation RL post-training expert evaluation
The authors propose a multi-agent reward function for RL post-training of scientific idea generation that is designed to resist reward hacking, and train with an unbiased Group Relative Policy Optimization variant on the ICLR-320 dataset. They report gains over state-of-the-art baselines on expert-evaluated novelty, feasibility and effectiveness.

MARCH: Multi-Agent Radiology Clinical Hierarchy for CT Report Generation
MARCH：面向 CT 报告生成的多智能体放射科临床层级
multi-agent collaboration retrieval-augmented revision consensus reasoning clinical report generation medical benchmark
MARCH emulates a radiology department's hierarchy, using a Resident Agent for initial drafting with multi-scale CT feature extraction, Fellow Agents for retrieval-augmented revision, and an Attending Agent that runs stance-based consensus discourse to resolve diagnostic discrepancies. On RadGenome-ChestCT it outperforms state-of-the-art baselines in clinical fidelity and linguistic accuracy.

Complete Cyclic Subtask Graphs for Tool-Using LLM Agents: Flexibility, Cost, and Bottlenecks in Long-Horizon Workflows
工具使用型 LLM 智能体的完全循环子任务图：长程工作流中的灵活性、成本与瓶颈
workflow control long-horizon tasks backtracking token cost tool use
The paper studies fully connected cyclic subtask graphs in which a state-analysis-and-routing agent selects transitions from natural-language criteria, comparing them with ReAct and dependency-directed workflows on TextCraft, ALFWorld and Finance-Agent. It concludes cyclic graphs are best treated as a diagnostic workflow-control tool: revisitation helps in partially observable recovery settings but adds overhead in prerequisite chains and cannot replace stronger retrieval and verification in open-ended synthesis.

不要开始你无法完成的事：LLM 智能体支持状态分诊的反事实审计
preflight triage abstention context prompting agent audit
The Support-State Triage Audit tests whether agents can diagnose why a task is blocked before acting. Default execution overcommitted on 41.7% of non-complete tasks, while typed preflight or action-only prompting reached 91.7% typed deferral accuracy in a single-context-window evaluation.
