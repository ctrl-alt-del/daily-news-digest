SemiFA: An Agentic Multi-Modal Framework for Autonomous Semiconductor Failure Analysis Report Generation
SemiFA：面向半导体失效分析报告自动生成的智能体多模态框架
agentic pipeline vector retrieval multimodal fusion failure analysis report generation
SemiFA is a four-agent LangGraph pipeline that generates structured semiconductor failure-analysis reports from inspection images in 48 seconds, combining DINOv2 and LLaVA-1.6 defect description with SECS/GEM telemetry fused with historically similar defects retrieved from a Qdrant vector database. Its DINOv2 classifier reaches 92.1% accuracy on 140 validation images, and a GPT-4o judge ablation found multimodal fusion improves root-cause reasoning by 0.86 composite points over an image-only baseline.

从手写到结构化数据：基准测试 AI 对手写表单的数字化

Concrete Jungle: Towards Concreteness Paved Contrastive Negative Mining for Compositional Understanding
Concrete Jungle：以具体性铺就面向组合理解的对比负样本挖掘
contrastive learning hard negatives compositional reasoning cross-modal retrieval loss design
The paper establishes lexical concreteness as a determinant of negative-sample efficacy in contrastive pretraining, since modifying highly concrete terms yields stronger structural and visual discrepancies, and proposes ConcretePlant to isolate and manipulate perceptually grounded concepts. Indexing Multimodal Language Models for Large-scale Image Retrieval
面向大规模图像检索的多模态语言模型索引
image retrieval multimodal LLM zero-shot re-ranking similarity estimation robustness
The work investigates multimodal LLMs as training-free similarity estimators for instance-level image-to-image retrieval, prompting the model with paired images and converting next-token probabilities into similarity scores for zero-shot re-ranking. Combined with memory-efficient indexing and top-k candidate re-ranking, MLLMs outperform task-specific re-rankers outside their native domains and are more robust to clutter, occlusion and small objects, though they fail under severe appearance changes.

One Token Away from Collapse: The Fragility of Instruction-Tuned Helpfulness
距崩溃仅一步之遥：指令微调有用性的脆弱性
instruction tuning lexical constraints planning failure prompt robustness evaluation methods
The study shows simple lexical constraints such as banning one punctuation character or common word cause instruction-tuned LLMs to lose 14-48% of comprehensiveness across seven models, with information criteria degrading 1.5-2.3 times more than surface criteria in blinded human evaluation. Diagnostics identify a planning failure: two-pass generation recovers 59-96% of response length, linear probes predict length with R^2 of 0.51-0.94, and pairwise evaluation reveals a 23% quality drop where independent LLM-as-judge sees only 3.5%.

Peer-Predictive Self-Training for Language Model Reasoning
面向语言模型推理的同伴预测自训练
self-training cross-model collaboration mutual information math reasoning label-free learning
PST is a label-free fine-tuning framework in which multiple language models generate responses sequentially and the cross-model aggregate, often more reliable than individual answers, serves as an internal training signal weighted by pointwise mutual information. On math reasoning benchmarks it improves exact-match accuracy by 2.2-4.3 points across three small models and reduces the generator-verifier gap by 26-40% without external supervision.
