Agentic Harness Engineering: Observability-Driven Automatic Evolution of Coding-Agent Harnesses
智能体脚手架工程：可观测性驱动的编码智能体脚手架自动演化
coding-agent harness observability experience corpus self-evolution Terminal-Bench
Agentic Harness Engineering (AHE) is a closed loop that evolves coding-agent harnesses through three observability pillars: file-level representations of every editable component, a layered evidence corpus distilled from millions of trajectory tokens, and per-edit predictions verified against later task outcomes. Ten iterations lift pass@1 on Terminal-Bench 2 from 69.7% to 77.0%, above the human-designed Codex-CLI (71.9%), and the frozen harness transfers to SWE-bench-verified with 12% fewer tokens and to three other model families with +5.1 to +10.1 pp gains.

AMMA: A Multi-Chiplet Memory-Centric Architecture for Low-Latency 1M Context Attention Serving
AMMA：面向低延迟百万上下文注意力服务的多芯粒内存中心架构
long-context serving attention latency memory-centric architecture agentic workloads
AMMA is a multi-chiplet, memory-centric architecture for low-latency long-context attention that replaces GPU compute dies with HBM-PNM cubes, roughly doubling available memory bandwidth for memory-bound decode-phase attention. Against an NVIDIA H100, it reports 15.5x lower attention latency and 6.9x lower energy consumption, motivated by reasoning and agentic workloads pushing context toward one million tokens.

CacheRAG: A Semantic Caching System for Retrieval-Augmented Generation in Knowledge Graph Question Answering
CacheRAG：面向知识图谱问答检索增强生成的语义缓存系统
semantic caching knowledge-graph QA retrieval planning schema grounding maximal marginal relevance
CacheRAG turns stateless LLM planners for knowledge-graph question answering into continual learners by caching retrieval plans, using a two-stage semantic parsing interface, a Domain-to-Aspect hierarchical index with maximal marginal relevance, and bounded heuristic subgraph expansion. It reports +13.2% accuracy and +17.5% truthfulness on the CRAG dataset over state-of-the-art baselines, arguing that generating retrieval plans in isolation leads to schema hallucinations and limited retrieval coverage.

arxiv:2604.26102 arXiv ✓
SWE-Edit: Rethinking Code Editing for Efficient SWE-Agent
SWE-Edit：重新思考 SWE 智能体的代码编辑方式
context coupling code editing subagents SWE-bench edit-format policy
SWE-Edit decomposes the code-editing interface into a Viewer subagent that extracts task-relevant code on demand and an Editor subagent that executes high-level natural-language plans, addressing a context-coupling problem in which exploration and strict edit generation share one window. On SWE-Bench Verified it raises resolve rate by 2.1 pp and cuts inference cost by 17.9% across Kimi-K2, MiniMax-M2.1 and GLM-4.7, and GRPO training on Qwen3-8B improves edit success by 12.5 pp.

BioGraphletQA: Knowledge-Anchored Generation of Complex QA Datasets
BioGraphletQA：知识锚定的复杂问答数据集生成
knowledge graph QA generation graphlet prompting biomedical QA dataset
BioGraphletQA is a biomedical KGQA dataset of 119,856 QA pairs generated by grounding structured prompts in graphlets of up to five nodes from the OREGANO knowledge graph, with most pairs enriched by PubMed snippets. Expert review of 106 pairs confirmed scientific validity and complexity, and augmenting downstream benchmarks raised PubMedQA accuracy from 49.2% to 68.5% in a low-resource setting.

RAG 增强的核启发式合成（RKHS）：利用大语言模型进行硬件设计的结构化方法
retrieval-augmented generation heuristic synthesis hardware design iterative refinement
RKHS combines retrieval-augmented generation, compact kernel heuristic templates and an LLM-driven refinement loop to synthesize reusable optimization heuristics.
