DeepSearch-World: Self-Distillation for Deep Search Agents in a Verifiable Environment
DeepSearch-World：在可验证环境中通过自我蒸馏训练深度搜索智能体
deep search self-distillation web agents multi-hop QA verifiable environment
DeepSearch-World is a deterministic, verifiable environment with 420K multi-hop QA tasks built from entity-level random walks, paired with DeepSearch-Evolve, a self-distillation loop of trajectory generation, filtering and fine-tuning. The resulting DeepSearch-World-9B reaches 31.2% on BrowseComp, 61.5% on GAIA and 93.4% on HotpotQA without distilling from stronger models, and the environment, data, model and code are to be released.

DeepSWE: Measuring Frontier Coding Agents on Original, Long-Horizon Engineering Tasks
DeepSWE：在原创长时程工程任务上评测前沿编码智能体
coding agents benchmark long-horizon tasks verifier data contamination
DeepSWE is a benchmark of 113 original, long-horizon software engineering tasks written from scratch across 91 open-source repositories and five languages, with hand-written verifiers instead of tests inherited from merged fixes. An independent LLM judge disagreed with DeepSWE's verifier 1.4% of the time versus 32.4% for SWE-Bench Pro's inherited tests, and the benchmark, verifiers and trajectories are released.

Infinity-Parser2 Technical Report
Infinity-Parser2 技术报告：开源 500 万样本文档解析语料与多任务强化学习模型
document parsing multimodal model OCR benchmark reinforcement learning data synthesis
Infinity-Parser2 couples a controllable data-synthesis pipeline with multi-task reinforcement learning across eight objectives for end-to-end document parsing, and releases Infinity-Doc2-5M, a five-million-sample bilingual corpus with layout, reading-order and structured-content annotations. Infinity-Parser2-Pro reports 87.6% on olmOCR-Bench and 74.3% on ParseBench, while the Flash variant claims a 3.68x throughput gain over Infinity-Parser-7B.

arxiv:2607.07847 arXiv ✓
When Does Continual Learning Require Learning
持续学习何时真正需要学习
continual learning knowledge update context compression catastrophic forgetting sequential benchmarks
The authors argue that continual learning should be framed as increasing model competence as the world changes, along two axes: new domains over space and drifting data over time. Recasting standard LLM benchmarks as sequential problems, they compare prompt-based methods, supervised and reinforcement learning, and context compression, finding that prompt methods adapt quickly but degrade later while online RL handles knowledge updates best but is sensitive to noisy rewards.
→ arxiv.org

Agentic AI and Retrieval-Augmented Models in Straight-Through Underwriting
智能体 AI 与检索增强模型在直通式核保中的应用
Agentic RAG multi-step reasoning human-in-the-loop retrieval decision support
The paper compares a single-LLM baseline, a naive RAG pipeline and a multi-agent Agentic RAG pipeline for straight-through underwriting of small commercial Business Owner Policies in a synthetic environment. The agentic system performs best overall, with the largest gains in multi-step and missing-information scenarios where structured retrieval and reflection help avoid unsupported decisions.

A Multi-cluster Boundary Learning Method for Out-of-Scope Intent Detection via MiniLM Embedding
基于 MiniLM 嵌入的多簇边界学习域外意图检测方法
embeddings intent detection out-of-scope detection MiniLM
The paper proposes a one-class classification method that learns the boundaries of multiple embedding clusters from all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and rejects out-of-domain utterances as out-of-scope intents. It reports state-of-the-art out-of-scope intent detection on CLINC150, StackOverflow and Banking77 relative to the compared baselines.
