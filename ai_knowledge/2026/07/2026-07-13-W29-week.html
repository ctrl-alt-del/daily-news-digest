Do Agent Optimizers Compound? A Continual-Learning Evaluation on Terminal-Bench 2.0
智能体优化器会复利吗？Terminal-Bench 2.0 上的持续学习评估
continual learning agent optimization regression control Terminal-Bench lifelong learning
The paper runs a two-phase continual-learning evaluation on hard Terminal-Bench 2.0 tasks comparing GEPA, Meta Harness and RELAI-VCL under identical optimization budgets. All three beat the baseline in the static single-phase setting, but only RELAI-VCL both transferred positively to new tasks and kept improving after those tasks were added, reaching a lifelong average pass rate of 76.4% versus 66.0% for GEPA, 64.6% for Meta Harness and 58.7% for the baseline.

AutoTrace：通过智能体跨过程探索从补丁定位触发点
