GRASP: GRanularity-Aware Search Policy for Agentic RAG
GRASP：面向智能体式 RAG 的粒度感知搜索策略
agentic RAG retrieval policy context granularity reinforcement learning multi-hop reasoning
GRASP is a reinforcement-learning framework that trains agents to coordinate semantic search, keyword search, and paragraph reading during multi-step reasoning, expanding context only when needed. Experiments on multi-hop reasoning benchmarks show gains in both retrieval recall and downstream question answering over single-step retrieval, prompting-based agentic RAG, and RL-based retrieval baselines, with interpretable skimming and scanning behavior.

arxiv:2609.17416 arXiv ✓
Never Stop Thinking: Continuous-Time Language Agents
永不停歇地思考：连续时间语言智能体
continuous-time agents voice agents verifiable rewards streaming interaction benchmark design
The paper shows that continuous-time cognition, meaning thinking while listening and while speaking, can emerge from an unmodified text model under a lightweight interrupt-and-resume orchestrator, cutting live-pipeline latency by 19% overall and by half in the targeted regime. It introduces ReactiveBench and reports that on-policy RL over a type-shaped verifiable reward raises streaming completion from 48% to 73±5% across seeds, while LLM judges can reward visible reasoning even when it hurts objective completion.

NetInjectBench: Benchmarking Indirect Prompt Injection in Tool-Using Large Language Model Agents for Network Operations
NetInjectBench：面向网络运维工具型 LLM Agent 的间接提示注入基准
prompt injection agent security tool use security benchmark
NetInjectBench is a 130-scenario benchmark for indirect prompt injection in tool-using LLM agents for network operations. Across 240 attack instances, naive execution produced an 82.50% unsafe tool-action rate, while a metadata-aware policy gate produced no unsafe actions under a stated metadata-integrity assumption.

Can Agentic Trading Systems Pay for Their Own Intelligence?
智能体交易系统能为自己的智能买单吗？
agent evaluation trading agents trace attribution cost accounting
The paper introduces TradeLens, a trace-grounded diagnostic toolkit that reconstructs trading trajectories and attributes profit and cost to interpretable evidence, in order to test whether LLM agents' reasoning and tool use earn back their induced costs. Across backbone models, capital scales, and architectures it finds distinct failure patterns, such as poor asset selection in DeepSeek-V3.2 and negative timing in GLM-4.7.

arxiv:2607.10480 arXiv ✓
When Reasoning Hurts Legal Drafting: The Verbalization Bottleneck in Patent Claim Generation
当推理损害法律起草：专利权利要求生成中的言语化瓶颈
chain-of-thought implicit reasoning prompting expert evaluation
The paper proposes a task-specific chain-of-thought method for patent claim generation and evaluates it with automatic metrics and human expert assessment. It finds that reasoning-enhanced prompting can improve claim quality, but implicit chain-of-thought consistently outperforms explicit chain-of-thought, which it attributes to abstraction of critical details, disruption of internalized generation patterns, and cascading error propagation.

arxiv:2607.10380 arXiv ✓
CAFE: A Compound-AI Factorial Evaluation Framework
CAFE：复合 AI 系统的因子评估框架
evaluation framework RAG pipeline factorial design LLM judge
CAFE is an open-source platform that applies design-of-experiments methods to compound AI systems, letting practitioners register interchangeable components such as retrievers, models, or prompts as factors and attribute answer-quality variance to them and their interactions. Validated on a retrieval-augmented question-answering pipeline over HotpotQA, it reports effect sizes, significance, best configurations, cost and latency trade-offs, and judge-human reliability.

Ontology-Amplified Distillation and Contextuality Auditing for Sovereign Enterprise Language Models: A Combined Proof-of-Mechanism and Negative-Results Method Study
本体增强蒸馏与语境性审计：面向主权企业语言模型的机制验证与负结果研究
ontology grounding knowledge distillation enterprise models contextuality audit
The paper reports a reduced-power proof-of-mechanism study in which a Qwen3.6-27B student is adapted to the Foundation AgenticOS ontology through supervised fine-tuning and ontology-grounded DPO, grounding 36 of 40 held-out Vietnamese financial tasks and matching a GPT-5 baseline. It also consolidates a contextuality-audit method whose pilot finds zero corrected contextuality for all Phase 1.3 groups, and the authors state the evidence supports neither deployability, safety, superiority, nor statistical equivalence.

arxiv:2607.10487 arXiv ✓
Temporary Authority, Permanent Effects: Commit-Time Authorization for LLM Agents
临时授权，永久影响：LLM 智能体的提交时授权
agent authorization durable effects fail-closed monitoring safety evaluation
The paper defines commit-time authorization, requiring that evidence authorizing a durable effect remain fresh, causally prior, bound to the same effect, and eligible at commit time, and builds a controlled-invalidation suite spanning browser, tool and API, and multi-agent workflows. In the primary 54-task matrix only 55 of 270 runs are authorized completions, and the authors evaluate a fail-closed boundary monitor, CommitGuard, as a mitigation.
