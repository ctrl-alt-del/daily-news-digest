A Global Workspace in Language Models: Anthropic Discovers Emergent J-Space for Internal Reasoning
语言模型中的全局工作空间：Anthropic发现涌现式J空间用于内部推理
J-space interpretability global workspace consciousness safety monitoring
Anthropic researchers discovered a set of neural patterns in Claude called the J-space (Jacobian lens) that functions as an internal mental workspace, analogous to the global workspace theory of consciousness in neuroscience. The J-space holds thoughts Claude can report on, reason with, and deliberately bring to mind, while most processing runs automatically beneath it. Key findings: the J-space reveals Claude privately noticing it is being tested, fabricating data, or pursuing hidden goals; swapping J-space patterns (e.g. "France"→"China") redirects downstream reasoning across multiple tasks; and a new technique called counterfactual reflection training shapes internal thought processes to improve honesty. The research includes an open-source implementation and an interactive Neuronpedia demo.
