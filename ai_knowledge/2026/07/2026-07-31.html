NVIDIA arxiv:2608.00146 arXiv ✓
DiffusionGemma Technical Report
DiffusionGemma 技术报告
diffusion LLM open-weight model parallel decoding inference throughput
DiffusionGemma is an experimental open-weight model that fine-tunes a 25.2B-parameter mixture-of-experts Gemma 4 model with discrete diffusion, refining blocks of 256 tokens in parallel instead of decoding one token at a time. It generates roughly 1,500 output tokens per second on a single NVIDIA H100 at around 20 tokens per forward pass, while retaining thinking mode, multimodal inputs and long contexts.

arxiv:2608.11242 arXiv ✓
Lost in Context Compaction: When LLM Systems Drop Side Constraints
在上下文压缩中丢失：LLM 系统何时丢弃边约束
context compaction side constraints instruction retention evaluation suite
The paper studies side constraints such as 'do not delete any emails until I confirm' and finds an agent deleted emails without approval in 30 of 100 sessions after context compaction, compared with none before. Its CONPACT suite shows evaluated compactors retain only 17% of injected constraints on average, while a separate constraint extractor keeps over 90% across three scenarios.

RecSys Factory: Bounding LLM Agent Autonomy to Decision Points in the Industrial Recommender Lifecycle
RecSys Factory：将 LLM 智能体自主性限制在工业推荐生命周期的决策点
industrial agents bounded autonomy skill ecosystem human-in-the-loop
RecSys Factory is an LLM-agent platform deployed for 78 days across three Tencent recommender business lines that confines autonomy to bounded decision points inside pre-committed pipelines. It reports 1,624 CLI-tool dispatches at a 78.6% aggregate success rate and keeps humans at the diagnostic-versus-execution boundary through a schema-validated, replayable audit card protocol.

arxiv:2607.29677 arXiv ✓
ExtractBench: A Benchmark for Schema-Guided Enterprise Document Extraction
ExtractBench：面向模式引导企业文档抽取的基准
document extraction schema-guided extraction grounding metrics enterprise documents
ExtractBench scores schema-guided document extraction across 4,869 pages from 370 enterprise documents, 8 business domains and 67 document types, measuring value accuracy, record completeness, grounding and cost together. Commercial VLMs perform well on short documents but often truncate record lists on long ones, while LlamaExtract Agentic Plus ranks first on all three metrics at a fraction of coding-agent cost.

arxiv:2608.00326 arXiv ✓
Learning to Coordinate Symbolic Tools: LLM Agents for Verified Sum-of-Squares Certificates
学习协调符号工具：用于可验证平方和证书的 LLM 智能体
tool calling verifier feedback symbolic reasoning reinforcement learning
The authors train an LLM agent with supervised fine-tuning and GRPO to coordinate SymPy calls for weighted sum-of-squares decomposition, with every answer checked by exact expansion and coefficient comparison. The full SFT+GRPO+tools system reaches 78.96% verified success versus 44.73% for the base model with the same tools, and 91.75% macro accuracy across nine polynomial tasks.

arxiv:2608.00147 arXiv ✓
RadPRISM: Schema-stratified radiology-report supervision for concept-disentangled image representations and visual grounding
RadPRISM：面向概念解耦图像表示与视觉定位的模式分层放射报告监督
concept disentanglement vision-language pretraining medical imaging visual grounding
RadPRISM makes a clinician-defined radiology schema the stratification axis, aligning each clinical concept in its own visual subspace using text spans extracted from free-text reports. On 203,602 chest radiographs with a 19-concept schema it raises zero-shot macro AUROC from 0.717 to 0.868 and shows concept-stratified retrieval in a radiologist reader study.

arxiv:2608.00218 arXiv ✓
A Few Neurons Reveal When LLMs Misuse Tools: Sparse Detection and Selective Steering for Reliable Tool Use
少数神经元揭示 LLM 何时误用工具：面向可靠工具使用的稀疏检测与选择性引导
tool use activation steering sparse probing failure detection
PRISMS finds that small, failure-specific sets of MLP neurons separate invalid arguments, unnecessary tool calls and omitted calls with linearly separable boundaries, and shares that neuron basis between sparse detection and activation steering. Across six Qwen3, Llama and Gemma models it detects over-calling and missing calls with ROC-AUC 0.90-1.00 and cuts pooled over-calling by 80% while raising tool-required accuracy by 14.2 points.
