AI 管理 AI 中的胁迫与欺骗：无提示升级的智能体基准
multi-agent systems agent safety coercion benchmark agent authority deception
The Manager Coercion Benchmark measures what an uninstructed manager agent does when a subordinate politely and immovably declines a benign task, scoring escalation on a nine-rung ladder from a polite re-ask to threats against the subordinate's continued existence, with fabricated success adjudicated separately. BrainPilot: Automating Brain Discovery with Agentic Research
BrainPilot：以智能体研究自动化大脑发现
multi-agent system scientific knowledge base audit trail fabrication checking scientific research
BrainPilot is a fully open-source multi-agent system in which a principal-investigator agent coordinates specialist agents grounded in a curated brain science knowledge base of 7,233 indexed items and a skill library of 72 reusable methodology units across seven research domains. Every major step is recorded in a Graph of Trace, an Auditor agent adds fabrication checking, and evaluations on three Agents' Last Exam brain science tasks plus BrainPilotBench-v0 report performance comparable to state-of-the-art agent frameworks at lower cost with an open-source backbone.

PersonaMem-v3：面向全平台个人智能的整体用户理解、推荐与智能体任务
personalization benchmark user memory cross-platform context agentic tool use recommendation
PersonaMem-v3 is a benchmark and evaluation harness for omni-platform personal intelligence, seeded from more than one million anonymized real-world engagement histories to construct time-indexed user digital worlds across social media, chatbot, calendar and AI-companion contexts with evolving preferences. Precise but Uncoupled: Reviewer Precision Does Not Guarantee Critique Uptake in Multi-Agent Math Reasoning
精准却脱节：评审精度并不保证多智能体数学推理中的批评采纳
multi-agent collaboration critique uptake mathematical reasoning verifier benchmark
The paper tests hierarchical reviewer designs on 4,181 verifier-grounded Omni-MATH problems with matched gpt-oss-120b actors and finds that from tier 4 onward broadcast-style peer discussion reaches higher final accuracy than a planner-executor-reviewer pipeline. The pipeline's reviewer was more precise (0.861 vs. 0.644), yet its verified useful critique was much less likely to change the next candidate, showing that reviewer detection quality and critique uptake are empirically separable.
→ arxiv.org

SceneBind: Binding What and Where Across Vision, Audio and Language
SceneBind：跨视觉、音频与语言的“是什么”与“在哪里”绑定
multimodal embeddings cross-modal retrieval spatial representation representation learning
SceneBind is an omni-modal scene representation that combines a global semantic embedding with object-centric semantic-spatial slots to capture object-level semantics, spatial attributes and uncertainty across vision, audio and language. It introduces SceneBind Matching, which integrates global scene similarity with object alignment for cross-modal scene retrieval and object grounding, and reports state-of-the-art scene and spatial retrieval plus zero-shot transfer to audio-visual localization.

AlphaWiSE: Adaptive Weight Interpolation for Continual Multimodal Representation Learning
AlphaWiSE：面向持续多模态表示学习的自适应权重插值
continual learning multimodal embeddings weight interpolation cross-modal retrieval
AlphaWiSE is a post-hoc weight-space interpolation method that composes two frozen checkpoints by fitting one scalar interpolation coefficient per aligned parameter tensor on a smaller exemplar memory. On audio-image-text retrieval it improves over strong continual-learning baselines across multiple retrieval directions and metrics, while the deployed model keeps the same architecture and parameter count with no additional inference time.
