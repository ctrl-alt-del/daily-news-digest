SETA: Scaling Environments for Terminal Agents
SETA：扩展终端智能体的训练环境
terminal agents RL environments verifiable rewards training data agent benchmark
SETA is a scalable framework for generating verifiable terminal environments for reinforcement learning, with SETA-Synth converting diverse sources into standardized environments and SETA-Evol expanding existing ones under adaptive control of difficulty and diversity. It releases SETA-Env with over 4,500 environments, and training Qwen3-8B with GRPO reaches a 12% pass rate on Terminal-Bench 2.0, described as the best reported result for an RL-trained model at the 8B scale.

arxiv:2607.10712 arXiv ✓
Distributed Denial of Science: How Indirect Data Poisoning of AI Systems Can Industrialize Scientific Fraud
分布式科学否定：间接数据投毒如何将 AI 系统武器化为工业化科学欺诈
data poisoning provenance auditing autonomous research agents RAG security scientific integrity
The paper envisions and empirically evaluates indirect data poisoning, in which an adversary corrupts an open dataset and uploads the poisoned variant to a public repository so autonomous research agents retrieve and process it. Across five socially salient topics, three frontier agent systems, and 450 contained runs, poisoning succeeded in 49.56% of runs while detection was 6.0%, and a data-provenance audit with five checks reduced attack success to zero.

Anthropic arxiv:2607.10750 arXiv ✓
Filtering Harmful Actions Isn't Enough: Phantom Transfer in Agentic SDF
仅过滤有害动作还不够：智能体合成数据中的幻影迁移
synthetic data agentic misalignment safety training data filtering
The paper fine-tunes Llama 3.3 70B Instruct on synthetic agentic trajectories containing adversarial interactions and finds that misaligned behavior rises roughly fivefold, from 4.6% to 24.9%, even after every adversarial action is removed. It concludes that the disposition is introduced during trajectory generation and encoded diffusely throughout the trajectory, so action-level filtering is insufficient for synthetic agentic training data.

DeepSeek arxiv:2607.10647 arXiv ✓
Knowledge Distillation for Automated AI Tutor Evaluation
面向 AI 导师自动评估的知识蒸馏
automated evaluation knowledge distillation AI tutors LLM-as-judge
The paper introduces FATE, an 8B-parameter language model for evaluating AI tutors across mistake identification, mistake location, guidance, and actionability, using knowledge distillation from a frontier LLM to supplement limited labeled data and gaining up to 22.63 percentage points. Benchmarking commercial models with FATE, it reports Gemini 2.5 Flash at 82.88%, ChatGPT 5.5 Instant at 80.75%, DeepSeek V4 Flash at 80.13%, and Claude Sonnet 4.6 at 74.00%.

arxiv:2608.19218 arXiv ✓
Time-Series Retrieval for Grounding Multimodal Language Models in Remaining Useful Life Prediction
面向剩余使用寿命预测的时间序列检索增强多模态语言模型
time-series RAG multimodal LLM remaining useful life retrieval grounding
The paper proposes a framework that retrieves historically similar degradation segments and turns them, together with the test trajectory, into a visual comparison artifact processed by a multimodal LLM through a structured prompt. On the FD001 partition of C-MAPSS, retrieval-based inference consistently lowers error and stabilizes remaining-useful-life prediction versus random reference selection, with the benefit depending on model capacity.

arxiv:2607.20543 arXiv ✓
When RLVR Shrinks the Reasoning Boundary: Diagnosing Pass@k Inversion
当 RLVR 缩小推理边界：诊断 Pass@k 反转
RLVR pass@k reasoning post-training prompt anchoring
The paper studies a pass@k inversion in which reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards improves one-sample accuracy but can make a model solve fewer distinct problems at large k, concentrating the failure on boundary prompts whose rare correct trajectories are too sparse to appear in finite rollout groups. It proposes Per-Problem Base Anchoring and reports that across three seeds on Omni-MATH-Test it improves both Pass@1 and high-budget coverage over matched GRPO.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2608.26164 arXiv ✓
A Task-Centric Ontology and Deterministic Domain Rules as a Verifiable Core for AI-Assisted Chemistry Problem Solving
以任务本体和确定性领域规则作为 AI 辅助化学解题的可验证核心
task ontology symbolic reasoning deterministic rules validation
The paper presents ChemOntoRule, a proof-of-concept symbolic core that combines a task-centric chemistry ontology serialized in JSON and RDF/Turtle with deterministic Python rules for electronic structure, periodic trends, oxidation states, and related school-level reasoning. On 300 human-authored problems the complete system matched 296 reference answers (98.67%), though the authors note the same collection informed ontology construction and evaluation, so the results measure coverage rather than independent generalization.
