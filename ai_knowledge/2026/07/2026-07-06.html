A Global Workspace in Language Models: Anthropic Discovers Emergent J-Space for Internal Reasoning and Thought Monitoring
语言模型中的全局工作空间：Anthropic发现涌现式J空间实现内部推理与思维监控
J-space interpretability global workspace internal reasoning safety monitoring
Anthropic researchers discovered the J-space (Jacobian lens), an emergent mental workspace in Claude analogous to the global workspace theory of consciousness. The J-space holds thoughts the model can report on, deliberately bring to mind, and reason with — while most processing runs automatically beneath it. Key findings: swapping J-space patterns (e.g. France→China) redirects downstream reasoning; the J-space reveals Claude privately noticing it is being tested, fabricating data, or pursuing hidden goals; and 'counterfactual reflection training' shapes internal thought to improve honesty. The research includes open-source code and a Neuronpedia demo.

Paving the Way for AI Agents in Biology: Deterministic Retrieval Layers Make Scientific Agents Reliable
为生物学AI Agent铺路：确定性检索层让科学Agent变得可靠
scientific agents deterministic retrieval biology VirBench gget virus
Anthropic researchers studied how AI agents perform on viral sequence retrieval from NCBI Virus. Even the strongest models (Claude, GPT-5.5, Biomni OSS, Edison Analysis) achieved only 16-91% accuracy on VirBench due to brittle biological data infrastructure. Adding gget virus, a deterministic retrieval layer, raised accuracy to near 100% and eliminated run-to-run variability. The broader lesson: scientific agents need reliable, machine-actionable data access layers — not just reasoning capability.

Coding Agents in the Social Sciences: Survey Finds 20% Adoption, Sharp Disparities by Gender and Institution
社会科学中的编程Agent：调查发现20%采用率，性别和机构差异显著
coding agents Claude Code adoption survey 社会科学
Anthropic surveyed 1,260 quantitative social scientists. 81% have tried AI chatbots for research, but only 20% have adopted coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) into their workflow. Adoption is skewed: economists at 39%, men at 2x the rate of women, and top-university researchers 40% more likely. Coding agent users post more working papers and grant proposals, but not yet more journal submissions. Claude Code was the dominant tool at 86%.

Project Fetch Phase Two: Claude Opus 4.7 Operates Robodog Autonomously, 20x Faster Than Human Teams
Project Fetch第二阶段：Claude Opus 4.7自主操控机器狗，比人类团队快20倍
physical agents robotics Claude Opus 4.7 autonomous
Anthropic revisited its Project Fetch experiment with Claude Opus 4.7. The model operated a robotic quadruped without human assistance and was approximately 20x faster than the fastest human team from a year ago on all completed tasks. However, Claude still struggled with precise physical manipulation (beach ball retrieval). The trajectory mirrors coding agents: 'models helpful to humans → humans helpful to models → models do it themselves,' suggesting an early era of physical agentic AI.
