MANTA: Multi-Agent Network Topology Adaptation for Self-Evolving Multi-Agent Systems
MANTA：面向自演化多智能体系统的网络拓扑自适应
multi-agent systems topology adaptation inference-time self-improvement task decomposition
MANTA lets multi-agent communication structures self-evolve at inference time, starting from a task-conditioned topology and applying bounded updates to roles, links, execution order and validation pathways when collaboration becomes insufficient. OSReward: Instituting Standardized Evaluation for Cross-Platform Computer-Use Reward Models
OSReward：跨平台计算机使用奖励模型的标准化评估
reward models computer-use agents VLM judges open models
OSReward evaluates vision-language judges on computer-use agent trajectories and finds a systematic leniency bias that labels failed runs as successes, while the most reliable judges are too expensive to run at scale. The authors release OS-Shepherd-100K and train 9B and 35B open reward models that match commercial judges at 30-60x lower cost.

Benchmarks Are Not Monolithic: Sample-Level Auditing and Orchestration for LLM Evaluation
基准并非铁板一块：面向 LLM 评估的样本级审计与编排
benchmark auditing sample-level annotation dataset heterogeneity LLM evaluation

The annotations expose internal heterogeneity that aggregate accuracy hides and enable criterion-driven composition of benchmark subsets.

arxiv:2608.00099 arXiv ✓
LLMBDC: Language Model for Biological Domains Oriented Clustering of Gene Ontology
LLMBDC：面向基因本体生物学域聚类的大语言模型框架
gene ontology ontology clustering zero-shot reasoning concept summarization
LLMBDC is a training-free framework that uses zero-shot LLM reasoning with confidence scoring to cluster Gene Ontology terms into interpretable BioDomains. On Alzheimer's disease and Fragile X syndrome data it raises ARI from 9.7% to 73.3% and from 15.7% to 66.6%, respectively, over REVIGO.

MA-RAG: Multi-Agent Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Query-Driven Summarization of Longitudinal Parkinson's Disease Assessments
MA-RAG：面向帕金森病纵向评估查询式摘要的多智能体检索增强生成
multi-agent RAG clinical summarization fact verification hallucination reduction
MA-RAG splits clinical reasoning across domain-specialized agents, combines structured fact extraction and adds a final verification stage to summarize single-session and longitudinal Parkinson's disease assessments. It reports up to a 122% relative gain in Fact Precision (0.436 to 0.990) and up to a 98% reduction in hallucination rate (0.564 to 0.010) over RAG baselines.

ReToken: Improving Long-Context VLMs with Visual Retrieval Token
ReToken：以视觉检索令牌改进长上下文视觉语言模型
visual retrieval KV cache token selection long-context VLM
