Prune the Summary, Not the History: Source-Judged Compaction for Long-Horizon Agents
修剪摘要而非历史：面向长程智能体的来源判定压缩
context compaction long-horizon agents agent memory summary faithfulness cross-run reliability
Source-Judged Compaction is a training-free post-processing step that scores each entry in a context-compaction summary for support in environment outputs or the previous summary and removes unsupported entries. In an analysis of a widely used structured compressor, 83% of failed runs traced to an action carrying out a specific summary entry, most of which no environment output supported; SJC improved cross-run reliability by 2.1%-8.9% on AppWorld and OfficeBench without increasing token usage.

arxiv:2610.20964 arXiv ✓
VELD-QA: A Large-Scale Dataset for Visual Evidence Grounding and Question Answering in Long Documents
VELD-QA：面向长文档视觉证据定位与问答的大规模数据集
visual evidence grounding long-document QA multimodal RAG evidence localization benchmark dataset
VELD-QA is a benchmark of 15,000 human-authored question-answer pairs across 5,859 real-world PDFs totaling 372,445 pages, with page-level and visual-element evidence annotations that allow retrieval and interpretation to be scored separately. Evaluating nine vision-language models and seven retrieval-augmented or agentic systems found that even with ground-truth supporting pages, accuracy peaks at 81.7%, and enriching inputs with visual-element annotations narrows the performance gap by 17-24%.

arxiv:2610.20776 arXiv ✓
INFUSE: A Unified Surface Benchmark for Indirect Prompt Injection in Agentic Systems
INFUSE：智能体系统中间接提示注入的统一表面基准
indirect prompt injection agent security attack surfaces prompt hardening benchmark
INFUSE is an end-to-end benchmark for indirect prompt injection across long-horizon agent workflows, with 40 realistic tasks and 56 attack scenarios injected through interaction surfaces encountered during execution. Evaluating 19 LLMs produced 41,824 adversarial trajectories, with mean attack success of 19.6% under LLM-based evaluation; prompt-level instructional hardening reduced mean attack success from 58.2% to 14.3%.

arxiv:2610.21018 arXiv ✓
Failure Is the Mother of Success: Recovering Supervision with Grounding Hindsight
失败乃成功之母：利用有依据的事后回放恢复监督信号
reinforcement learning hindsight distillation failed rollouts verifiable rewards
Replay-Selected Hindsight Distillation turns failed GRPO rollouts into extra supervision: the policy writes a corrective note without seeing a reference answer, re-solves the problem with the note, and a note is admitted only if, after screening for answer-stating notes, its replays succeed more often than the original group. The method consistently improves over GRPO across model families and sizes and carries gains to out-of-domain benchmarks.

arxiv:2610.20814 arXiv ✓
Gibbs-Gap Policy Optimization for Off-Policy RL with Verifiable Rewards
用于可验证奖励离策略 RL 的 Gibbs-Gap 策略优化
off-policy RL verifiable rewards policy optimization KL regularization
Gibbs-Gap Policy Optimization minimizes a log-mean-exp contrast of KL-adjusted rewards among responses to the same prompt, avoiding learner-to-behavior importance weights in off-policy RL with verifiable rewards. It outperforms GRPO and AGRO under training-inference mismatch and on fixed offline data, and its default reference-refresh schedule scored 46.58 versus 32.96 for a fixed reference on a five-benchmark average.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2610.20960 arXiv ✓
StepSafe: Learning Timely Intervention with Context-Aware Safety Reasoning
StepSafe：通过上下文感知安全推理学习及时干预
agent safety guardrails step-level intervention safety benchmark
StepSafe is a step-level reasoning guardrail built on ASPO, a training framework for timely intervention that credits each decision at its own prefix and self-distills the reasoning behind intervention choices. It reports state-of-the-art performance among open-source guardrails across five agent-safety benchmarks, with a safety-utility Youden's J of 53.0 at 4B parameters versus 46.2 for the prior best.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2610.20993 arXiv ✓
One Bias Score, Two Factors: How Mitigations Affect Option Bias in Multiple-Choice Evaluation
一个偏差分数，两个因素：缓解措施如何影响多项选择评估中的选项偏差
multiple-choice evaluation selection bias order sensitivity benchmarking methodology
The paper links selection bias and order sensitivity in multiple-choice evaluation through a pooled standard deviation of option selection rates, which factors into a flip rate and a positional skew. Across 41 checkpoints and 12 benchmarks, prior correction mainly reduces positional skew while aggregation mainly reduces flip rate, and the authors recommend reporting both alongside accuracy.
