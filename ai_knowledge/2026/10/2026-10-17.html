Neuro-Inspired Inhibitory Message Passing for Accurate and Token-Efficient Multi-Agent Reasoning
受神经科学启发的抑制性消息传递：更准确、更省 token 的多智能体推理
multi-agent communication context management evidence routing peer-group benchmarks
The paper adapts cortical inhibition into inhibitory message passing, a training-free communication router that cancels redundant evidence separately for each sentence and receiver and forwards each restated evidence sentence at most once. Against standard message passing it raised peer-group accuracy by 39-77 percentage points and cut tokens per run by 10-46% across three benchmarks, seven open models and groups of up to 64 agents.

agentic retrieval secure code generation token efficiency categorizer agent

A Cross-Domain Evaluation of Prompting Techniques for Large Language Models
大语言模型提示技术的跨领域评估
retrieval-augmented generation prompt optimization cross-domain evaluation reasoning benchmarks
One LLM is compared across zero-shot prompting, few-shot prompting, RAG and a PE2-inspired automatic prompt-optimization pipeline on six benchmarks covering arithmetic, logical and commonsense reasoning plus factual verification. No strategy consistently won, pairwise differences among the best configurations were not significant, and RAG had the highest average accuracy at 78.33% with 300 retrieval examples.

Measuring Data Diversity with Embeddings: A Taxonomy and Benchmark for Diversity Measures
用嵌入衡量数据多样性：多样性度量的分类与基准
embedding diversity diversity metrics data curation text corpora
The authors present a taxonomy of 21 embedding-based diversity measures grouped into four families and introduce EmbDivBench, which evaluates them on variety, balance and disparity using simulated and natural text. Mean Pairwise Distance, the most commonly used measure, was outperformed on those components, with Graph Entropy the best overall choice.

An Impossibility Result for ETF Target Geometry, and an Equinorm Remedy for Imbalanced Analytic Continual Learning
ETF 目标几何的不可能性结果，以及面向不平衡解析持续学习的等范数补救
continual learning catastrophic forgetting classifier geometry long-tailed benchmark
The paper shows that fusing analytic continual learning with progressively expanded Simplex ETF targets is identical to plain one-hot ACIL, and that the literal expansion introduces about four times the catastrophic forgetting of a classic ACIL head. Applying equinorm post-processing to the fitted classifier weights instead recovers tail and overall accuracy under long-tailed imbalance, including +32.0 tail and +16.0 average points at 100:1 on CIFAR-100/ViT.

A Symmetry-Matching Approach to Blind-Spot Reduction in Sparse Autoencoders
用对称匹配方法减少稀疏自编码器的盲点
sparse autoencoders interpretability safety auditing representation blind spots
The paper frames representation blind spots as a feature-basis orientation problem and adds a differentiable V_Gini regularisation term to sparse-autoencoder training that penalises uneven perturbation response within a specified family. Across GPT-2, Gemma 2 and Qwen 2.5, it raises relative and absolute lower-tail responses and reduces response-profile imbalance without material general-text reconstruction degradation, with a zero-shot clinical radiology minimal-pair audit finding weak-tail lift.

LLM-Based Evaluation Planning for Heterogeneous Optimization
面向异构优化的基于 LLM 的评估规划
LLM planning evaluation planning adaptive allocation heterogeneous optimization
REP-HO replaces the rule-based mixed-fidelity selection step in heterogeneous optimization with an LLM planner that receives surrogate predictions, uncertainties, evaluation costs and budget status. Across five regimes one of its variants achieved the best mean ranking score in every regime, REP-HO(DeepSeek) placed first or second throughout, and the planner adapted allocation such as prioritizing constraint evaluations under tighter budgets.
