CM-DPO: Constraint-Margin Direct Preference Optimization for LLM Planning
CM-DPO：面向 LLM 规划的约束边际直接偏好优化
constraint-aware planning preference optimization symbolic verification LLM planning
CM-DPO replaces DPO's binary preference signal with a continuous margin derived from a deterministic symbolic verifier and scaled by violation severity, separating hard and soft constraints through a lexicographic objective. An 8B model fine-tuned with CM-DPO reports an 89.2% pass rate and 93.4% solve rate on TravelPlanner, NaturalPlan and out-of-distribution PlanBench, matching multi-agent systems at 13x lower latency while outperforming GPT-4o on unseen Blocksworld by 9.2 points.

Frozen Models, Evolving Expertise: Model-Agnostic Learning from Deployment Experience for Multimodal Medical AI
冻结的模型，演进的专长：面向多模态医疗 AI 的模型无关部署经验学习
continual learning knowledge memory medical AI deployment adaptation
The framework lets frozen LLMs and VLMs learn from deployment experience through three forms of external expertise: a Skill guiding reasoning and tool use, a Knowledge Memory of reliable facts, and a Multimodal Knowledge Base of visual examples. Across six benchmarks and four open-weight and closed-source base models, it improves online deployment performance by up to 34.2% on medical tasks without updating model weights.

Bookkeeping, Composition, or Unreachable Gold? Reading MemoryAgentBench's Conflict-Resolution Scores Against a Frozen Last-Write Resolver
记账、组合，还是不可达的标注？以冻结的最后写入解析器解读 MemoryAgentBench 的冲突解决分数
agent memory conflict resolution benchmark validity last-write semantics
The authors execute MemoryAgentBench's own newest-statement-wins rule as a zero-learning resolver frozen on one of four fact lists, finding it answers 80.25% of Conflict Resolution questions under the official metric (74.5% on the three held-out lists). They identify 67 items whose released gold answers cannot be reached by the last-write graph, and two long-context models score 84.7% and 82.6% on rule-solvable items but only 10.4% and 11.9% on those 67.

GeoNatureAgent (GNA): A Framework and Benchmark for Pre-Production Evaluation of Tool-Using Agents on Geospatial and Environmental Tasks
GeoNatureAgent（GNA）：面向地理空间与环境任务工具型智能体的预生产评估框架与基准
tool-using agents MCP agent evaluation geospatial AI
GeoNatureAgent is a pre-production evaluation framework for tool-using agents built on a fixed sixteen-tool geospatial interface published as an MCP server, plus a 103-task benchmark. Across nine LLMs, Claude Sonnet 4 reaches the highest capability at 61.7%, DeepSeek V3.2 follows at 57.9% with 11.3x lower list-price cost, and strict all-checks scoring leaves the best model 24-36 points below figures reported on general-purpose GIS benchmarks.

BEACON-SP: Ontology-Grounded GraphRAG Framework for Clinical Suicide Risk Assessment
BEACON-SP：面向临床自杀风险评估的本体驱动 GraphRAG 框架
GraphRAG clinical knowledge ontology evidence grounding
BEACON-SP combines patient knowledge graphs with ontology-guided retrieval to support multi-hop reasoning across diagnoses, medications, risk and protective factors, life events and temporal relationships in clinician-facing suicide-prevention support. On a 1,500-query benchmark spanning 15 clinical categories and 100 patients, it improves completeness, clinical relevance and evidence grounding over a vector RAG baseline, with GraphRAG preferred in 76.4% of paired criterion-level comparisons.

arxiv:2610.09003 arXiv ✓
Algorithmic Scratchpads and Curriculum Staging for Arithmetic Reasoning in Tiny Transformers
面向微型 Transformer 算术推理的算法草稿纸与课程分级
scratchpads arithmetic reasoning curriculum learning tiny transformers
The study trains Tiny Transformers of about 10.6M non-embedding parameters on step-by-step arithmetic scratchpads and finds that sequence padding creates a severe gradient-starvation artifact, while linguistic pretraining is an essential prerequisite. A digit-by-digit long-division scratchpad within a four-stage curriculum raises single-digit division accuracy from 4.0% to 86.7%, but performance collapses to 0.00% on unseen four-digit operands.

What Transfers from a VLM Teacher? Comparing Supervision Signals for Visual Document Retrieval
VLM 教师能迁移什么？比较视觉文档检索中的监督信号
visual document retrieval knowledge distillation hard negatives ViDoRe
The study asks whether a vision-language teacher is better spent judging the candidates a visual document retriever mines as negatives rather than enriching its labelled positives. With student, data, optimizer and evaluation fixed, teacher-judged hard negatives and score distillation raise ViDoRe v2 nDCG@5 from 55.2 to 62.6 and 63.0, while description alignment gains 2.6 points and attention grounding shows nothing measurable.
