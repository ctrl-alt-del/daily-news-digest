RoleFlow: Self-Evolving Multi-Role Agentic Collaboration via Structural Adaptation
RoleFlow：通过结构自适应实现自演化多角色智能体协作
multi-agent systems role synthesis structural adaptation credit assignment benchmark
RoleFlow refines multi-agent collaboration structures through feedback-driven atomic edits and on-demand role synthesis, trained with Single-Edit Counterfactual Policy Optimization that credits each structural edit against its no-edit counterpart at the same chain state. 为什么又来烦我？面向交互偏好的个人智能体记忆基准
agent memory interaction preferences personal agents on-policy evaluation memory benchmark
MemPABench is an interactive environment for on-policy evaluation of personal-agent memory, with four simulated users holding 14 preferences across 99 sessions over four months and five event-driven preference changes per user. Evaluating four memory systems on four LLM backbones, the authors find that retrieval-based memory does not outperform No Memory on preference selection, while file-based memory improves accuracy but applies preferences beyond relevant contexts.

SWE-VERIFIER: Selective Verification for Cost-Efficient Coding Agents
SWE-VERIFIER：面向高性价比编码智能体的选择性验证
coding agents action verification inference cost selective sampling
SWE-Verifier is a lightweight model trained from Qwen3.5-4B that scores a coding agent's proposed action to decide when candidate sampling is needed, then ranks the resulting actions before execution. It raises MiniMax-M2.7 resolution from 75.47% to 77.20% at 1.09x cost, generalizes to DeepSeek-V4-Flash without training on its actions, and verifies fewer than 4% of steps.

工单之前：基于文档与已解决工单的 HPC 用户支持智能体

Breaking the high-degree barrier in Lehmer's problem with a human-AI discovery loop
以人机发现闭环突破 Lehmer 问题的高次障碍
human-AI collaboration mathematical discovery distributed search symbolic verification
A human-AI discovery loop extends conversational prompting with a fast numerical pipeline, an exact symbolic verifier and large-scale distributed search to derive a five-parameter Generalized Boyd algorithm. Evaluated across all even degrees up to 2000, the construction eliminates degree-skipping, improves six previously published values and holds 99.6% of all best known records.

arxiv:2610.30435 arXiv ✓
Self-Assessment Reinforcement Learning for Search-State Awareness in Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models
面向检索增强大模型搜索状态感知的自评估强化学习
search agents reinforcement learning search-state awareness multi-hop reasoning deep research
SARL trains search agents to recognize what information they have obtained and what remains missing, using a dual reward that credits the corrective effect of each self-assessment while penalizing excessive reliance on it, with no additional assessment at test time. It achieves a 24.3% relative improvement over the strongest baseline, with average gains of 35.9% on multi-hop reasoning and 27.9% on deep research benchmarks.
