Auditing Pairwise Equivalence Judgments: Self-Critique Effects and Diversity Measurement in Multi-Agent Hypothesis Generation
审计成对等价判断：多智能体假设生成中的自我批评效应与多样性度量
multi-agent systems hypothesis generation diversity measurement LLM-as-a-judge
The study measures how self-critique rounds change delivered hypotheses and how the rule used to group equivalent hypotheses affects measured diversity in multi-agent hypothesis generation. Moving from zero to one critique round produced 34.5 percentage points of additional mechanism-level divergence, and an LLM judge found 83% of pairs with a replaced initiating event to be different mechanisms while TF-IDF caught none.

arxiv:2610.04047 arXiv ✓
Periscope: Extending Frozen Language Models Beyond Their Context Window
Periscope：将冻结语言模型扩展到上下文窗口之外
context extension evidence mapping frozen models LongBench
Periscope is a training-free inference method that arranges a long text's chunks on a grid and asks a frozen model the same question over local and strided spans, producing an evidence map that identifies the chunk behind an answer. On LongBench v2, reading only the chunks the map ranks highest, about 9k tokens, matches the same model's best window read across windows from 32k to 1M tokens, and the method lets a 27B model read 4.5M-token contexts on one 80GB GPU.

Teaching Agents to Code Reliably
教智能体可靠地写代码
coding agents verification SWE-bench reinforcement learning
The paper identifies narrow location diversity, unexploited edit diversity and misleading self-written tests as three behaviors that decide whether extra inference-time compute helps coding agents. Directing search by execution feedback and scoring each patch against its own reverted tree resolves 52.8% of SWE-bench Verified using 48.1% of the agent steps of an eight-sample baseline, and training these behaviors into the policy raises pass@8 from 46.7% to 60.7%.

arxiv:2610.04137 arXiv ✓
Dynamic Harness Search: Building Multi-Agent Systems Per-Query via Prediction
Dynamic Harness Search：通过预测为每个查询构建多智能体系统
multi-agent orchestration Monte Carlo tree search harness design benchmark
SHIFT learns a policy over harness-building actions and a value function from measured executions, then uses Monte Carlo tree search to construct a per-query multi-agent harness without running alternatives at inference time. Across 9,193 tasks in six benchmarks with a Gemini 3.5 Flash executor, it reached about 80% mean accuracy, outperforming 17 baselines and exceeding the strongest by 7.2 points, while a cheaper mode used 32% fewer execution tokens than the strongest baseline.

Evolving LLM-Generated Features for Interpretable Classification
演化 LLM 生成的特征以实现可解释分类
interpretable classification feature engineering rubrics auditing
An evolutionary framework has an LLM generate and refine natural-language binary feature rubrics whose activation vectors train a transparent classifier such as logistic regression. Evolved features beat single-shot rubrics by 2.9 points on average and, on a credit-risk task where zero-shot LLM classification sat at chance, produced balanced and auditable predictions.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2610.04011 arXiv ✓
Exploration-Preserving Policy Optimization
保留探索的策略优化
reinforcement learning exploration verifiable rewards response diversity
Exploration-Preserving Policy Optimization reshapes advantage assignment using prompt-relative, length-normalized response surprisal and prompt pass rate, aiming to keep credit from concentrating on frequently sampled modes. Experiments report improved in-domain and out-of-domain reasoning coverage, higher aggregate response accuracy, and greater diversity among verified-correct responses at large sampling budgets.

arxiv:2610.04129 arXiv ✓
InvestigationWorlds: An Agentic Environment for Legal Investigation
InvestigationWorlds：面向法律调查的 Agent 环境
agentic environments legal reasoning evidence evaluation benchmark
InvestigationWorlds builds agentic environments from real U.S. federal court cases retrieved from PACER, focused on summary judgment motions and augmented with an attorney-validated generator that adds role-tagged documents. Across 100 cases, agents often settled on the wrong hypothesis even after finding the relevant evidence and struggled to tell the court-adopted account apart from the alternatives.
→ arxiv.org

LongSocialBench: Do Long-Context LLMs Understand Online Discussion Threads?
LongSocialBench：长上下文 LLM 能理解在线讨论串吗？
long-context understanding discourse understanding benchmark human comparison
LongSocialBench is a 1,462-item human-written multiple-choice benchmark drawn from 94 full discussions on Hacker News, Stack Exchange and Reddit's r/ChangeMyView, with a median length of roughly 73K tokens. Across 18 models and 29 settings, Claude-Opus-4.7 scored highest at 63.0% versus 72.4% for human readers, while the full-context baseline averaged 43.9%.
