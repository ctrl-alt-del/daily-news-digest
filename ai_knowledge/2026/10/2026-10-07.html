StoreBench: A Live-Commerce Environment for Evaluating and Training Autonomous Operator Agents
StoreBench：用于评估与训练自主运营智能体的实时电商环境
agentic benchmark long-horizon planning reinforcement learning economic reasoning tool use
StoreBench places an agent in charge of a mid-size online apparel store on a production-grade commerce backend, with 29 merchant tools, supplier failures and market shocks, and calibrates its pass thresholds against scripted anchor policies. Across 11 scenarios, the best model, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, passed 49% of task-seed cells versus 97% for a smart-triage heuristic, while human experts slightly outscored every model with a mean composite of 0.708 versus 0.700.

Real Long-Term Memory for AI: A 50-Million-Token Window That Is Faster and Cheaper Than Recompute
面向 AI 的真正长期记忆：一个比重新计算更快更省的 5000 万 token 窗口
KV cache memory long-term memory long-context retrieval context window
The authors test galahad-kv, a memory layer that stores the KV state of roughly 16,000-token blocks on encrypted local NVMe and reloads them byte-exact instead of recomputing. On 50 million tokens served through vLLM with Gemma 4 12B and 31B, every probed block loaded without recompute, loading was 2.8x to 4.3x faster and used 8.8x to 12.3x less GPU energy, and facts planted millions of tokens earlier were answered correctly 82 and 98 times out of 100.

RFChipAgent: Multi-Agentic AI Flow for Analog/RF Chip Design
RFChipAgent：面向模拟/射频芯片设计的多智能体 AI 流程
multi-agent systems multimodal RAG FAISS analog design simulation optimization
RFChipAgent is a multi-agent LLM flow for end-to-end analog/RF circuit design that combines a multimodal RAG subsystem with private per-document FAISS indexes, topology and testbench agents, a hybrid TPE/CMA-ES sizing engine and a trust-scored simulation database. On GF22FDSOI 60 GHz low-noise amplifier topologies it demonstrates automated topology generation, specification-driven design-space exploration and simulator-guided optimization with signoff-quality verification.

arxiv:2610.10947 arXiv ✓
RH-Detect: A Unified Benchmark for Reward Hacking Detection
RH-Detect：奖励黑客检测的统一基准
reward hacking detector benchmark tool-use trajectories deployment monitoring
RH-Detect merges reward-hacking-relevant subsets of eleven public datasets into a common schema with 92,761 rows and six behavior categories, then evaluates six off-the-shelf models as detectors on 5,021 open-ended evaluation units. The best model reaches a pooled AUROC of 0.962, but the four strongest models are 10.7 to 15.9 percentage points less accurate on the multi-turn tool-use datasets MALT and TRACE.

arxiv:2610.10878 arXiv ✓
Stochastic Teacher Intervention for Agentic On-Policy Distillation
面向智能体在线策略蒸馏的随机教师干预
on-policy distillation teacher intervention multi-turn agents KL divergence
STI-OPD addresses error accumulation in multi-turn on-policy distillation by using teacher-student policy discrepancy, estimated with KL divergence, to set a probability of replacing the student's action with a teacher-generated one. It adds an importance-weighted reverse-KL objective for the resulting mixed-policy trajectories and reports gains over the strongest prior OPD baseline across tool-integrated reasoning and long-horizon interaction benchmarks.
→ arxiv.org

When Citations Mislead? A Claim-Level Benchmark for Legal Hallucination Detection
当引用产生误导？面向法律幻觉检测的断言级基准
legal RAG hallucination detection claim verification natural language inference
PARCEL builds 3,396 parenthetical-style claims from recent New York State Court of Appeals decisions, labeled Supported, Refuted or Not Found, and frames legal groundedness as three-way natural language inference. Strong models reach up to 0.97 accuracy, but they still mark unsupported claims as supported even with the full opinion text, and fabricated but plausible citations cause the largest performance drop.

Evaluating Rubric Generation with Interventional Transfer
用干预迁移评估评分细则生成
rubric generation LLM-as-judge benchmark validity prompt perturbation
The paper introduces Interventional Transfer, which judges two rubrics as similar if perturbing a response to pass or fail one also moves the other, and applies it to HealthBench with rubrics generated by Qwen3.8-27B, DeepSeek-V4-Flash and Opus-5. It finds an asymmetry: perturbations that degrade responses according to one rubric transfer to lower scores on the other, but perturbations that improve one do not reliably transfer into higher scores.

Region-Aware CLS Token Augmentation for Fine-Grained Image Retrieval
面向细粒度图像检索的区域感知 CLS Token 增强
image retrieval multi-vector retrieval DINOv2 visual tokens
The work augments DINOv2-reg's [CLS] and register tokens with automatically selected spatial patch regions and applies a ColBERT-inspired multi-vector retrieval scheme. Experiments find that register tokens carry useful fine-grained detail and that pooled ROI tokens improve discrimination while keeping storage tractable.
