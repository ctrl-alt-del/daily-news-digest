Continual Test-Time Learning with Evolution Strategies
基于进化策略的持续测试时学习
continual learning test-time adaptation evolution strategies catastrophic forgetting
Evolution Strategy Reinforcement Learning draws self-generated supervision from a population of perturbed policies rather than the single policy being updated, refreshing the anchor policy at block boundaries to limit drift. In single-task test-time adaptation it improves average accuracy by up to 31.7% over the base model and 16.6% over the strongest test-time baseline, while substantially reducing catastrophic forgetting.

Self-Improving Video-to-Action Models
自我改进的视频到动作模型
self-improvement video-to-action embodied agents learning from observation
Mirror is a self-supervised approach that learns video-to-action mappings by attempting to follow visual plans in an environment and training on the resulting video-action pairs, without prior action data or hand-crafted rewards. It outperforms leading learning-from-observation methods with learned video plans across Atari, OGBench, and DeepMind Control, and the paper reports it as the first method to acquire an iron pickaxe in Minecraft survival through self-improvement alone.

Who Plans, Who Edits? Verified, Cost-Aware Division of Labor for LLM-Driven Analog Layout Repair
谁规划，谁编辑？LLM 驱动模拟版图修复中经验证、成本感知的分工
division of labor model routing tool-grounded agents cost-aware planning
A tool-grounded analog-layout repair agent has models choose bounded layout operators while sign-off tools recheck every edit, and 182 blind runs show cheap models execute plans but rarely succeed alone. A learning controller that assigns planner and editor roles fixed 7 of 8 episodes on four sealed held-out tasks, matching the OpenAI flagship alone at 15-40% of its spend per fix on three tasks, though with 8% higher spend overall.

Training-Free Prompt Selection for Efficient Reinforcement Learning With Partial Rollouts
面向部分推演的高效强化学习：免训练提示选择
prompt selection partial rollouts RLVR data efficiency
LIFT is a training-free data selector for reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards that scores prompts from only a short reasoning-rollout prefix using Partial Rollout Context Lift. Prefixes retaining at least 90% and 95% of full-rollout scores required only 26% and 4.7% of generated tokens on two 1.5B models, reducing selection cost 3.7-9.1x versus SHIFT.

arxiv:2610.24304 arXiv ✓
CME-SpectrumBench: Can LLMs Analyze Condensed Matter Spectral Data?
CME-SpectrumBench：LLM 能分析凝聚态光谱数据吗？
scientific data analysis dense context reasoning tool use benchmark design
CME-SpectrumBench is an expert-designed 190-question benchmark that tests whether frontier LLMs can analyze simulated condensed-matter spectral data, scored by how closely numerical answers approach ground truth. Under direct query models find simple features but struggle to trace patterns or compute derived quantities, while tool access to a Python interpreter brings performance close to handwritten code.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2610.26902 arXiv ✓
MOSS: A Production Framework for Monitoring and Diagnosing Semantic Failures in Search and Recommendation Systems
MOSS：用于监控和诊断搜索与推荐系统语义故障的生产框架
semantic failure monitoring LLM-as-judge production monitoring search relevance
MOSS embeds LLM-based relevance scoring into a recurring production monitoring loop with targeted sampling, strategy-aware prompting, structured alerting, and diagnostic follow-up that categorizes failures into root causes. Running for over six months at a large U.S. retailer, it surfaced two confirmed serving defects, and its LLM judges achieved higher F1 and AUC point estimates than non-LLM baselines on a human-annotated recommendation benchmark, with the same judge transferring to a search benchmark after only a surface-specific prompt adaptation.
→ arxiv.org

Continuous Ontology Assurance through External Resources -- An Ontology Engineering Method with a Case Study in Software Language Engineering
通过外部资源实现持续本体保证：面向软件语言工程的案例研究
ontology engineering ontology assurance external resources LLM-assisted linkage
COATER is an ontology engineering method that manages executable review components called arrows to check ontology axioms against evolving external resources. In an FSL ontology case study, an LLM-assisted linkage exercise raised Wikipedia-link compliance from 405 to 600 of 600 entities.

Who Speaks, Who Authorizes, Who Acts? Understanding Trust Boundaries in Multi-Agent LLMs
谁说话、谁授权、谁行动？理解多智能体 LLM 中的信任边界
multi-agent systems trust boundaries authorization safety evaluation
The paper separates communication, authorization, and execution trust boundaries in multi-agent LLM systems, finding that request-level and commitment-level safety readouts diverge. It also shows that a policy-valid compromised-signer proof can pass an authorization gate even when the claim is false.
