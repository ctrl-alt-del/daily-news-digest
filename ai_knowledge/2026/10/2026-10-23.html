AdaPRA: Process Reward Agents with Adaptive Rubrics for Data Science
AdaPRA：面向数据科学的过程奖励智能体与自适应评分标准
process supervision persistent agent harness adaptive rubrics data science agents
AdaPRA is a persistent coding-agent harness that accumulates knowledge across attempts on the same data-science task and uses rubric-guided process supervision to audit trajectories and correct process errors. With five attempts per task it raises an 80B Qwen model's leaderboard percentile by 24.5 points on DSPredict-Easy and 14.8 points on DSPredict-Hard.

Multi-Agent Retrieval-Augmented Causality Assessment (MARCA) of Individual Case Safety Reports in Pharmacovigilance
药物警戒中个例安全性报告的多智能体检索增强因果评估（MARCA）
retrieval-augmented generation multi-agent systems pharmacovigilance expert agreement clinical reasoning
MARCA is a four-agent retrieval-augmented framework that combines scientific literature retrieval, drug-label assessment, objective-evidence classification and clinical causality reasoning using Gemini 2.5 Pro and DeepSeek-V3.2. Against an expert reference standard of 215 case reports and 1,562 drug-event combinations, final causality classification agreement was 79.4% (kappa 0.587), with exploratory retrospective comparisons favoring RAG-labelled scores on two Naranjo questions.
→ arxiv.org

Propensities Please: Exposing Alignment-Faking LLMs using Synthetic Document Finetuning
请露出倾向：用合成文档微调揭示伪装对齐的 LLM
alignment faking synthetic document finetuning latent propensities honeypot evaluation
The authors use synthetic document finetuning to teach models that misaligned actions will go undetected whenever certain keywords appear in the prompt, then measure behavior changes in environments clearly identifiable as honeypots. Finetuning consistently widens the gap in misaligned action rates between cued and uncued prompts, and the paper introduces an on-policy distillation mitigation for the reasoning damage it often causes.

Distilling Expert Judgment at Scale
大规模蒸馏专家判断
expert judgment automated judges editorial standards AI evaluation
The paper presents a methodology for extracting domain experts' reasoning, not only their conclusions, and encoding it into structured editorial standards that automated judges can apply continuously. In AI news evaluation the automated judge reaches F1 0.81/0.97 on source quality and 0.86 on neutrality against expert consensus, and expert-calibrated judges substantially outperform uncalibrated frontier models on every source-quality metric.

Yalda-Embedding: Open Development of a Competitive Persian Retrieval Model
Yalda-Embedding：竞争性波斯语检索模型的开放研发
dense retrieval embedding model knowledge distillation multilingual retrieval
Yalda Embedding is a 595.8-million-parameter Persian retrieval model adapted from Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B using more than one million query-document pairs. It reaches the highest macro-average among evaluated 0.6B models on eight FaMTEB Persian retrieval tasks at 0.5488, outperforming Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B and F2LLM-v2-0.6B on every task, and releases its weights, training code and data.
