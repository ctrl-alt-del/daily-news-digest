RATED：通过执行动态进行可靠性感知的智能体工具检索
tool retrieval contextual bandit execution feedback semantic matching retrieval benchmark
RATED combines a frozen dual-encoder with contrastive fine-tuning for semantic tool matching and a lightweight contextual bandit that re-ranks candidates from real-time execution feedback, adapting to runtime tool failures without retraining. On the EcoAgent benchmark of 5,172 tools and over 210,000 query-tool pairs it improves relative MRR by 52.2% over the ToolLLM API retriever and cuts fault selection from 86.2% to 2.9% under a 30% API failure rate.
