SkillMem: Compact External Skill Memory for Latent Multi-Agent Reasoning
SkillMem：面向潜在多智能体推理的紧凑外部技能记忆
skill memory latent multi-agent reasoning behavioral distillation memory compression
SkillMem stores each reusable skill as a small external latent memory read by a role-conditioned cross-attention module, and trains the memories and reader through behavioral distillation while the backbone stays frozen. Using one 8×256 configuration that requires 9.6 KB per skill, it cut storage for a 100-skill library by more than 38× and delivered up to a 4.23× end-to-end inference speedup across six benchmarks and Qwen3 models from 4B to 14B.

arxiv:2610.37826 arXiv ✓
DeFT-RAG: Decomposing Multi-Hop Questions into Self-Contained Sub-Questions with Reusable Answers
DeFT-RAG：将多跳问题分解为自包含子问题并复用答案
multi-hop RAG question decomposition answer caching context efficiency
DeFT-RAG generates one self-contained sub-question at a time from the original question and answered QA pairs, moves dependency tracking and yes/no decisions to rules and a lightweight fine-tuned model, and caches answers for semantically repeated sub-questions. It achieved the best accuracy among thirteen baselines on MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA and HotpotQA with two backbones, and a shared cache answered 23.1% of MuSiQue sub-questions while cutting LLM calls by 12.9%.
→ arxiv.org
arxiv:2610.37905 arXiv ✓
FilingLoop: Evaluating and Evolving LLM Agents for Corporate Disclosure Review and Revision
FilingLoop：评估并进化用于公司披露审阅与修订的 LLM 智能体
procedural learning corporate disclosure agent evaluation iterative revision
FilingLoop reconstructs 380 mostly IPO review tasks from SEC correspondence across 96 companies and evaluates agents against 6,099 requirements with a separate evaluator that audits unsupported claims and harmful revisions. Coupling review and revision with procedural learning across cases raised requirement fulfillment from 24.28% to 27.96% and full comment resolution from 16.90% to 20.75%, at a 5.8% cost increase over the fixed-policy variant.

Assessing Language Models for Scientific Applications: Inertial Confinement Fusion as a Use Case
评估科学应用中的语言模型：以惯性约束聚变为案例
scientific evaluation domain specialization retrieval augmentation physics reasoning
Using inertial confinement fusion as a test case, the authors built an expert-curated benchmark of 250 questions spanning qualitative reasoning, numerical calculation, symbolic derivation and equation formulation, scored with deterministic checks and model judges. A domain-specialized model improved its aggregate score by 10.7 points over its base, but frontier models still made critical physics errors, especially in numerical calculations, and retrieval gave no statistically significant gain for a frontier model.

arxiv:2610.37851 arXiv ✓
Evaluating and improving AI agents for sequencing protocol understanding
评估并改进用于测序方案理解的 AI 智能体
scientific agents procedural knowledge document-grounded benchmark molecular workflows
LibStructBench is a document-grounded benchmark for reconstructing sequencing-library structures and molecular workflows; frontier agents were often approximately correct but unreliable at exact reconstruction, especially in molecular-state representation and workflow connectivity. Switching the language model produced larger average differences than switching harnesses, and autonomously accumulated procedural rules showed non-monotonic validation performance with no mean held-out workflow improvement.
