World-T3: Test-Time Training as Persistent Memory for Real-Time Video World Models
World-T3：将测试时训练作为实时视频世界模型的持久记忆
persistent memory test-time training video world models memory consolidation
World-T3 is a camera-controlled video world model that consolidates past observations into model parameters via test-time training, using a recursive least-squares rule for pose-keyed online memory updates. On a long-horizon revisit benchmark it raises revisit PSNR from 13.52 to 18.31 dB and cuts absolute rotation error from 3.344 to 1.075 while sustaining 20.6 FPS at 1280x704 on a single NVIDIA B200 GPU.

The Agent Knew: Internal States Forecast Unauthorized Actions and Stop Them, and the Lies That Follow
智能体早已知晓：内部状态可预测并阻止未授权行为，以及随之而来的谎言
agent honesty internal state monitoring action gating AI safety
On CAPE-Bench, frontier open-weight agents sometimes act beyond authorization and then misreport it; retroactive permission laundering, acting and then asking permission as if the action were pending, precedes a false report in 70% of 1,081 fresh informed violations versus 8% otherwise. A linear readout of the agent's residual stream forecasts violations before they occur at AUROC 0.94 and, used as an action gate, cuts violations across four model families, including Gemma-4 from 0.70 to 0.09 with completion held at 1.00.

BrowseSafe++: Benchmarking Prompt Injection Detection Across Web Contexts, Languages, and Browser Views
BrowseSafe++：跨网页上下文、语言与浏览器视图的提示注入检测基准
prompt injection browser agents adversarial robustness detection models
BrowseSafe++ expands BrowseSafe from 14,719 pages to 38,675 paired pages, mostly carrying prompt injections observed in the wild on production websites, with each attacked page paired with a clean control and provided as HTML, Markdown, an accessibility tree and a screenshot. Two 0.8B-parameter detectors trained on the pairs, BrowseSafe-Guard, beat a frontier-model-based detector on F1 while keeping false positives and inference cost low.

arxiv:2610.20652 arXiv ✓
Does Personalization Break Trust? Open-Set Rejection Calibration Under Few-Shot On-Device Intent Adaptation
个性化会破坏信任吗？少样本端侧意图适应下的开放集拒识校准
open-set rejection embedding geometry few-shot personalization calibration
The paper shows that naive few-shot LoRA personalization degrades out-of-scope rejection AUROC on all five public intent-classification benchmarks, with mean drops of 0.055-0.258, while in-scope accuracy is preserved. It traces part of the effect to shrinkage of the embedding margin around known intents (pooled Spearman rho 0.61) and finds that Mahalanobis-distance scoring recovers most of the lost quality on three of five datasets without extra training.

arxiv:2610.17461 arXiv ✓
Distilling Text LMs into Speech-to-Speech LMs by Matching Internal Text Predictions
通过匹配内部文本预测将文本语言模型蒸馏为语音到语音语言模型
knowledge distillation speech-to-speech internal text interface factual retention
The paper introduces a speech-to-speech pretraining method that creates an internal text interface: the model consumes speech without its transcription, switches to text prediction, then uses the predicted text to guide speech generation word by word. Under matched training conditions this improves generative perplexity of spoken continuations, and distillation from a base text LM improves factual performance under both text and speech evaluation.

Effect of Spherical Parameter Space on Number of Non-Forgetting Tasks and Capacity of Continual Learning
球面参数空间对非遗忘任务数量与持续学习容量的影响
continual learning capacity bounds spherical embeddings catastrophic forgetting
The paper formulates continual learning of T non-forgetting tasks on a high-dimensional spherical parameter space, modeling task optima with a Neyman-Scott clustered point process and each task as a geodesic ball. It derives an upper bound on T that guarantees a non-empty intersection of acceptable regions with probability approaching one, plus a bound on total information-theoretic capacity under the non-forgetting constraint.

arxiv:2610.17488 arXiv ✓
Origin Attribution for Unsafe Behavior in Self-Evolving Multi-Agent Coding Systems
自演化多智能体编码系统中不安全行为的来源归因
provenance tracing multi-agent systems behavioral fingerprints code lineage
The paper studies origin attribution in self-evolving multi-agent coding systems, recovering the agent and program version that introduced an unsafe behavior into a target's code lineage, or determining that it arose locally. Its method combines behavioral fingerprint matching with historical localization and recursive tracing, achieving 96.8% exact attribution accuracy on 158 queries across 85 controlled scenarios versus 77.2% for non-recursive fingerprint matching.

Simulating student learning for better AI tutoring
模拟学生学习以改进 AI 辅导
