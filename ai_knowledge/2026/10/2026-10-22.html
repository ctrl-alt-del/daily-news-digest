Certified Execution Harness for Agentic AI: Safe and Least-Intrusive Petri-Net Supervisory Control Between Reasoning and Action
面向 Agentic AI 的可认证执行框架：推理与行动之间的 Petri 网监督控制
agent safety tool execution Petri nets prompt injection supervisory control
The paper models an LLM agent's tool-execution harness as a Petri-net supervisory control problem in which marks represent capabilities, approvals and budgets, so an injected request with unmet preconditions is refused regardless of wording. It also analyzes a replanning loop in which each retry costs an inference call.

Kolibri: A Sovereign European Model on the Pareto Frontier
Kolibri：位于帕累托前沿的主权欧洲模型
open-weight models mixture-of-experts multilingual knowledge serving cost sovereign AI
Kolibri is an English-German mixture-of-experts transformer with 78.1B total parameters and 3.46B active parameters per token, released as open weights under the Apache 2.0 licence. Trained on 24T tokens with German accounting for more than 20% of the data mix by bytes, it is placed on the quality and serving-cost Pareto frontier among the open models evaluated in the report.

Latent Coherence: Do Frontier Language Models' Probability Judgments Obey the Law of Total Probability?
潜在连贯性：前沿语言模型的概率判断是否遵守全概率定律？
probabilistic coherence calibration latent variables model evaluation
The study tests whether frontier models' zero-shot probability judgments satisfy the law of total probability when a latent variable must be inferred from context, using queries with exact ground-truth probabilities. GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 are the most coherent (mean absolute residuals of 0.08 and 0.12 log-odds), while Claude Haiku 5.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash and DeepSeek V4-Pro show persistent item-specific incoherence (0.27-0.35 log-odds); within every model, larger residuals accompany larger errors against ground truth.

Not Every Agent Needs the Strongest Model: Unified Prompt-Model Optimization for Heterogeneous LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems
并非每个智能体都需要最强模型：异构多智能体系统的提示—模型联合优化
multi-agent systems prompt optimization model routing cost efficiency heterogeneous models
UniMASO jointly adapts role prompts and model assignments in heterogeneous multi-agent systems, using cost-aware Pareto search and topology-aware co-evolution. Across five benchmarks and five execution topologies it improves the average score from 69.79 to 70.40 under centralized execution while reducing token-weighted deployment cost by 85.2% relative to DeepSeek-V4-Pro with MASPO.

How Ground Truth Shapes Retrieval Scores: Evidence-Aware Evaluation of a Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for Breast Lesions
真值如何塑造检索分数：乳腺病变 RAG 系统的证据感知评估
RAG evaluation retrieval metrics cross-encoder reranking clinical knowledge annotation sensitivity
An evidence-aware protocol for a clinical breast-lesion RAG system uses an 18-document corpus and 18 queries, combining dense embeddings, BM25 and cross-encoder reranking; the selected hybrid retriever with 384-token chunks and 20% overlap places accepted evidence in the top three for 17 of 18 queries, with Recall@3, R-Precision, MRR@3 and nDCG@3 each at 0.94. Expanding ground-truth annotations from 25 to 37 evidence spans increased recognized evidence without changing retrieval rankings.

Low-Bit N-Gram Embeddings for Language Models
面向语言模型的低位宽 N-Gram 嵌入
n-gram embeddings quantization memory efficiency model compression validation loss
The authors store n-gram embedding tables at low precision and find that four-bit tables save about 75% of table memory while preserving more than 99% of the validation-loss improvement across nanochat-, Llama- and GPT-2-family models trained from scratch. On DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, low-bit tables save 47-55% of table memory with perplexity increases below 1.2%.

POS-Signature Demonstration Retrieval for In-Context Dependency Parsing
面向上下文依存句法分析的词性签名示例检索
in-context learning demonstration retrieval dependency parsing part-of-speech evaluation
A POS-signature policy represents sentences with weighted part-of-speech statistics and retrieves three length-compatible gold-parsed examples from the labeled training split for in-context dependency parsing. On all 2,077 sentences of the UD English Web Treebank test set it yields the highest labeled attachment score among the tested prompting systems for two open-weight models (72.4 and 73.2), beating semantic retrieval by 1.9 and 2.7 points.

Which Answer Did the Seventeen-Year-Old Write? A Behavioral Case Study of Author-Age Misattribution, Miscalibrated Confidence, and Non-Corrective Self-Explanation in Six Frontier Language Models
哪份答案是十七岁少年写的？六个前沿语言模型作者年龄误判、置信度失准与自我解释无效的行为案例研究
calibration model confidence behavioral case study author attribution stereotyping
A three-item case study across six frontier models finds that three perform at or near chance on matching hand-written exam answers to author age while reporting 73-88% mean confidence, and the youngest writer's answer is misattributed to an adult in up to 90% of trials. Stated confidence is uncorrelated or inversely correlated with correctness for five of six models.

ReCoG: Relational Concept Graph for Training-Free Personalization
ReCoG：面向免训练个性化的关系概念图
concept graph personalization training-free inference-time reasoning benchmark
